    Clogged Shower Drain Solutions in Garden City, KS

    Leaky Shower Head Repair in Garden City

    A dripping shower head wastes water and can be incredibly annoying. Our plumbers can quickly identify the cause, whether it is a worn-out washer, O-ring, or a faulty cartridge. We carry common replacement parts on our trucks to ensure a fast fix. We will stop the leak, saving you money on your water bill and preventing potential water damage in your bathroom.

    Shower Diverter Repair in Garden City, KS

    A diverter sends water from the tub spout to the showerhead. When it wears out, water keeps running from the spout and shower pressure drops. We replace diverter spouts, three-handle diverter stems and multi-function diverter valves. This restores full flow to the showerhead.

    Shower Pan and Wall Leak Repair in Garden City, KS

    Leaks under the shower pan or behind tile can cause rot, mold and stains on ceilings below. We use moisture meters and flood tests to find failed grout, cracked pans or leaking pipe joints. Then we repair the source, whether that means resealing a drain, replacing a valve or rebuilding a pan. On slab homes, these leaks can damage flooring in adjoining rooms.

    Leaky Shower Faucet Repair in Garden City

    A dripping shower faucet can waste hundreds of gallons of water a year and drive up your utility bill. Our plumbers quickly identify the source of the leak, which is often a worn-out O-ring, washer, or cartridge within the faucet assembly. We carry a wide range of replacement parts for common brands like Delta, Moen, Kohler, and Price Pfister, allowing for same-day repair. We will make sure the new components are correctly installed, stopping the drip and saving you water.

    Shower Valve Replacement in Garden City

    If you are experiencing inconsistent water temperature, low water pressure, or difficulty turning your shower on or off, the shower valve may be failing. A faulty mixing valve can lead to scalding or freezing water, which is a safety hazard. We can replace old or broken shower valves with new, dependable models, including pressure-balancing and thermostatic options for improved safety and comfort. This often involves reaching the valve behind the shower wall.

    Faulty Shower Valve Repair in Garden City

    Problems with your shower's water temperature or pressure often point to a malfunctioning mixing valve or cartridge. These components control the hot and cold water flow. Our technicians are skilled in diagnosing valve issues and can repair or replace them, ensuring you get consistent water temperature and pressure every time you shower. We work with all major valve brands.

    Shower Temperature Problem Repair in Garden City

    If your shower turns cold when someone flushes, the pressure-balance spool may be stuck. If you cannot get enough hot water, the anti-scald limit stop may be set too low or the cartridge may be worn. We repair or replace pressure-balance and thermostatic valves and adjust limit stops.
