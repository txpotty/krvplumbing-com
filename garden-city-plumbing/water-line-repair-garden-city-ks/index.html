    Main Water Line Leak Repair in Garden City

    Most main line leaks show up as a soggy strip of yard, a sudden jump in your water bill or a meter dial that moves with every fixture off. We confirm the leak by isolating the house shutoff and watching the meter. Then we use ground microphones to find the exact spot. A single section is cut out and replaced with repair couplings or a new length of pipe, and the line is pressure tested before we backfill.

    Galvanized Water Line Replacement in Garden City, KS

    Many homes built before the 1970s still have galvanized steel service lines that rust from the inside. Water across much of Kansas runs hard, leaving mineral scale in water heaters, faucets, showerheads, and dishwashers. Hard water speeds up scale buildup, which chokes flow and causes rust-colored water. Patching galvanized pipe seldom holds up because the surrounding pipe is just as corroded. We usually recommend replacing the full run from meter to house with copper or PEX, which restores pressure and ends repeat leaks.
