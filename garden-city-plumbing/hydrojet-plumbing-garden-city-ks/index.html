    Hydrojetting vs. Snaking in Garden City, KS

    Snaking pokes a hole through a clog. Jetting cleans the whole pipe. Snaking suits a one-time clog, while jetting fixes a line that clogs repeatedly.

    Whole-House Drain Jetting in Garden City

    Slow drains in several rooms point to buildup in the main or a branch line. The main gets jetted from the cleanout, and branches from vents or fixtures. Most homes take two to three hours.

    Hydrojetting vs Snaking Cost Comparison in Garden City, KS

    Snaking a main line typically costs $200 to $450, while residential hydrojetting usually runs $400 to $900 with a camera inspection. The jet is the bigger bill. Because jetting removes the buildup that causes repeat clogs, many homeowners spend less over a few years than they would on repeated snaking visits for the same line.
