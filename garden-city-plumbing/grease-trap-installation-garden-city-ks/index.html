    Hydromechanical Grease Trap Installation in Garden City

    Hydromechanical grease traps sit under or near sinks and use baffles and flow control to separate grease from water. They are common in small cafes, coffee shops and quick-service restaurants with tight space. We install units from Zurn, Watts, Schier and Endura sized by gallons per minute. Each install includes a flow control fitting and proper venting to keep the trap working as designed. These units need frequent cleaning, so we place them where staff can reach the lid without trouble.

    Gravity Grease Trap Installation in Garden City

    Gravity grease traps are a common and effective solution for many commercial kitchens in Garden City. These passive systems use the natural separation of FOG from water due to density differences. Our plumbers are experts in installing gravity grease traps, ensuring proper sizing, correct inlet and outlet piping, and easy access for future cleaning and maintenance to maximize their efficiency.
