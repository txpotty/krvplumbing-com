    Hard-Water Drain Damage Repair in Garden City, KS

    Water across much of Kansas runs quite hard, leaving mineral scale in water heaters, faucets, showerheads, and dishwashers. Scale grows inside pop-up bodies and on stopper rods until the linkage seizes. The scaled parts all come out. Smooth PVC sheds buildup better.
