    Cast Iron Sewer Cleaning in Garden City, KS

    Many older homes have cast iron drains under the slab or floor. A lot of Kansas housing dates from the mid-century building boom, with galvanized steel supply lines and cast iron drains. Over time the inside of cast iron corrodes, scales and develops a channel along the bottom. Aggressive cutters can break thin spots, so we use descaling chains and careful jetting made for older iron. This restores flow and gives a clear view on camera of the remaining wall. It also helps you decide if lining is a smart option.
