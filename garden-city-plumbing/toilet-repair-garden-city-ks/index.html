    Weak Flush and Clog Repair in Garden City, KS

    If your toilet needs two flushes or clogs often, the problem may be scale in the rim jets, a partial blockage in the trapway or a flush valve that does not open fully. We auger the trap, check the vent if gurgling is present and clean the jets with a descaling treatment. Some low-flow toilets from the 1990s simply flush poorly, and we will tell you honestly if replacement is the better fix.

    Cracked Tank and Supply Line Repair in Garden City, KS

    A hairline crack in the tank or a bulging braided supply line can let go without warning and flood a bathroom. We inspect the tank, tank-to-bowl bolts and gasket, and the supply line during every visit.     Pressure-Assist and Dual-Flush Repair in Garden City

    Leaking Toilet Repair in Garden City

    Toilet leaks can cause significant water damage to your bathroom floor, subfloor, and even the ceiling below. Leaks can originate from the base of the toilet, the tank, or the supply line.
