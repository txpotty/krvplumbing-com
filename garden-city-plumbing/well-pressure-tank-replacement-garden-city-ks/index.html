    Well Pressure Tank Leak Repair vs. Replacement in Garden City

    Leaks at the tank tee, a gauge or a fitting can be fixed. A leak from the tank itself cannot. Bladder tanks with a fixed bladder cannot be rebuilt, so a torn bladder or rusted seam means a new tank, while a leaking tee or relief valve is a simple part swap we can usually do in under an hour.

    Tank Tee, Gauge and Relief Valve Replacement in Garden City

    The tank tee is the brass manifold that connects tank, switch, gauge, drain and relief valve. Old ones leak at threads. The relief valve should open before the system reaches a dangerous pressure, so we replace a crusted or corroded one rather than trust it. Whole set, one visit.

    Commercial Well Pressure Tank Services in Garden City, KS

    Commercial wells often run several tanks in parallel. Some run four or more on a manifold. We check each tank's pre-charge separately, since one bad tank in a bank can still cause short cycling, and we schedule the swap around your business hours to keep the water on. Larger tanks need two people.

    Well-X-Trol Pressure Tank Installation in Garden City, KS

    Amtrol's Well-X-Trol line uses a diaphragm design. It is one of the most common tanks we install. Its stainless or polypropylene water connection keeps water off the steel shell, limiting internal corrosion, and the factory pre-charge still gets checked and adjusted to your switch on install.

    Pressure Tanks for Well Houses and Barns in Garden City, KS

    Tanks in outbuildings face heat, cold and damp. Moisture eats steel tanks from the bottom. We often recommend a composite tank in those spaces, raise it off the floor on a stand, while protecting the switch and pipes from freezing. Simple protection.

    Large Capacity Tanks for Ranch and Commercial Wells in Garden City

    Big properties need big drawdown. Stock tanks, irrigation and barns add up. We install large capacity tanks or banks of tanks on a manifold, sizing them so the pump runs long enough each cycle even under heavy demand. Floor space matters.

    Cost-Effective Well Pressure Tank Upgrades in Garden City, KS

    Upgrading a size often costs less than people think. The difference between two tank sizes is usually modest. A larger tank means fewer pump starts per day, which is the cheapest way to extend the life of your pump, so we often recommend it during a replacement.
