    Indoor Grease Trap Cleaning in Garden City

    Small hydromechanical traps under sinks fill quickly in busy kitchens and often need cleaning every few weeks. We remove the lid, pump or hand-scoop the grease and solids, and scrape the baffles. We check the gasket, flow control fitting and inlet for damage. Then we reassemble the unit and confirm water flows through correctly. This keeps odors down and prevents slow sink drainage.

    Grease Trap Hydro-Jetting in Garden City, KS

    Sometimes, grease traps and their connecting lines develop tough blockages that standard pumping can't fully clear. Our hydro-jetting service uses high-pressure water streams to fully scour the interior of your grease trap and the associated drain lines. This removes hardened grease, scale, and other debris, restoring full flow and preventing recurring clogs in your Garden City, KS commercial property. It's an effective way to reset your system.

    Outdoor Grease Interceptor Pumping in Garden City, KS

    Large in-ground interceptors require a vacuum truck to fully pump out grease, water and settled solids. We open each compartment, pump it down and use high-pressure water to break up hardened grease on the walls. We inspect inlet and outlet tees, baffles and risers while the tank is empty. Warm weather can make grease odors stronger, so we also check lid seals. A complete pump-out brings back full retention capacity.

    Odor Control and Trap Inspections in Garden City

    Persistent odors usually point to a trap that is overdue, a missing seal or a damaged baffle. We inspect gaskets, lids and vent connections during every cleaning. If we find damage, we explain the issue and offer repair options. Sealing problems early keeps odors out of your kitchen and dining room. It also prevents grease from bypassing the trap completely.
