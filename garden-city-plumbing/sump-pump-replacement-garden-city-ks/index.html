    Sewage Ejector Pump Replacement in Garden City

    Basement bathrooms and laundry rooms below the sewer line need sewage ejector pumps instead of sump pumps. These pumps handle solids and must be installed in sealed, vented basins. We replace ejector pumps with models rated for two inch solids. We also check the basin seal, vent and discharge check valve. Proper installation prevents sewer gas odors.

    High-Capacity Sump Pump Upgrades in Garden City

    If your current sump pump struggles to keep up with heavy water flow, especially during intense thunderstorms, it may be undersized. We offer high-capacity sump pump replacements and upgrades designed to handle large water volumes. Our plumbers will assess your water intrusion patterns and recommend a pump with greater horsepower and a higher GPM (gallons per minute) rating. This prevents the pump from cycling too often or failing under stress, common issues in some Garden City properties.

    Wayne Sump Pump Replacement in Garden City

    Wayne sump pumps offer a range of efficient models, from entry-level to professional-grade.     Pedestal Sump Pump Replacement in Garden City, KS

    Pedestal sump pumps have their motor mounted above the sump pit, making them easier to access for maintenance and often more durable. We provide professional pedestal sump pump replacement in Garden City, KS, with proper alignment and secure mounting. This type is often preferred in shallower pits or where noise is not a major concern, offering a robust solution for flood prevention.

    Emergency Sump Pump Replacement in Garden City, KS

    A sudden sump pump failure can lead to immediate flooding, especially during heavy rainfall. KRV Plumbing offers emergency sump pump replacement in Garden City, KS to address urgent situations.     Yard Drainage Pit Pumps in Garden City, KS

    Some properties use pumps in yard drainage pits or below-grade window wells to handle storm runoff. These pumps may sit idle for months, then fail when they are needed most. We replace them with corrosion-resistant models and clear debris from the pit. We can route discharge away from foundations and onto approved areas. Regular testing before storm season is a good habit.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Garden City

    Power outages during severe storms can leave a primary sump pump useless, leading to basement flooding. A battery backup sump pump offers crucial protection during these times. We install and replace complete battery backup systems, including dedicated marine-grade batteries and smart charging units. Our team helps you select a system with sufficient run-time to protect your Garden City home through extended power interruptions. That gives you peace of mind when the lights go out.

    Float Switch and Check Valve Replacement in Garden City

    Many sump pump failures start with a stuck float switch or a leaking check valve. A failed check valve lets water flow back into the pit and causes the pump to short cycle. We install new check valves with every pump replacement and verify the switch moves without binding. Vertical float switches work well in tight pits. Tethered floats work best in larger pits.
