    Sewer Camera Inspection in Garden City, KS

    Many older homes have clay, cast iron or Orangeburg sewer lines that can crack, sag or fill with roots. The camera starts at a cleanout. The video shows offsets, bellies, roots and breaks along with their depth and location, and you keep a copy of the recording for your records or for a contractor.

    Hard Water Damage Assessment in Garden City

    Private wells serve many rural homes, and minerals, sediment, and nitrate levels make periodic well testing a good habit. Hard water leaves scale in pipes, fixtures and water heaters. Signs include crusted aerators, clogged showerheads, slow toilet fill and heater sediment, and we can test hardness during the visit. Then we explain your options. A softener, a filter or a scale-reducing system can extend heater and fixture life, and we tell you which one fits the hardness we measured.

    Water Heater and Softener Inspection in Garden City

    We check the water heater's age by serial number, look for rust, leaks and sediment and test the relief valve. We confirm proper venting. We also look at the expansion tank, drain pan and softener, and a hardness test shows whether the softener is working or just eating salt.

    Water Heater Safety Inspection in Garden City

    A water heater stores a lot of hot water under pressure, so safety items matter. Our checks include the relief valve and its discharge line, the expansion tank and the drain pan. We check gas units for draft. A heater that backdrafts can spill combustion gases into the house, so we test the draft at the vent hood and note any problems right away.

    Sewer Line Camera Inspections in Garden City, KS

    A sewer camera shows the inside of the main drain without digging. The camera goes in at a cleanout and travels to the main or the tank, recording roots, cracks, offsets and bellies along the way. We mark problem spots from above. Slab leaks and broken sewer laterals are common results of shifting clay, especially after long dry spells followed by heavy rain.

    Rental and Commercial Property Inspections in Garden City

    Landlords and property managers use our inspections between tenants or before buying an investment property. We document each unit. We note heaters, fixtures and sewers by unit, and commercial jobs can include backflow and grease lines, giving owners a budget for repairs.

    Water Heater Inspections in Garden City

    Water heaters give clues long before they fail. The serial number tells us the age, and we check for rust, look at the vent and test the relief valve. We check tankless units for scale. Gas heaters get a look at the flame, the drip leg and the vent draft, since a poorly venting heater is a safety issue.

    Pipe Material and Water Quality Review in Garden City, KS

    Pipe material says a lot about what to expect from a house. Our plumber identifies the supply pipe, whether copper, CPVC, PEX, galvanized, poly or Kitec, and explains how each usually fails. Certain types need a plan. Poly and Kitec fail suddenly and galvanized chokes off with rust, so we flag them with replacement options.
