    Gas Water Heater Safety Checks in Garden City, KS

    Gas water heater maintenance involves crucial safety inspections in Garden City, KS. We check for gas leaks, proper venting of exhaust gases, and the function of the pilot light or igniter. We also clean the burner assembly and inspect the thermocouple. These checks are essential to prevent carbon monoxide exposure and keep your gas-fired appliance running safely.

    Electric Water Heater Tune-Up in Garden City

    For electric water heaters, our maintenance service focuses on checking heating elements, thermostats, and electrical connections.     Tankless Water Heater Descaling in Garden City

    Tankless units are especially sensitive to hard water scale, which clogs the heat exchanger and triggers error codes. We isolate the unit with its service valves and circulate a descaling solution with a pump for the recommended time. We also clean the inlet water filter and check the unit's error history. Most manufacturers recommend descaling at least once a year in hard water areas. Regular service keeps flow rates and temperatures consistent.

    Attic and Garage Water Heater Maintenance in Garden City

    Many homes have water heaters in garages or attics, where heat, cold and tight access add stress to the unit. Attic units need working drain pans and drain lines to prevent ceiling damage if the tank leaks. We check the pan, drain line, venting and access platform. Garage units should be raised and protected where required for gas appliances. We confirm your setup meets local code.

    T&P Valve and Safety Testing in Garden City, KS

    The temperature and pressure relief valve keeps your water heater from building dangerous pressure. We test it by lifting the lever and checking that it opens and reseats. A valve that leaks or will not open gets swapped out. We also check that the discharge pipe runs to an approved location. Expansion tanks are checked for proper air charge if you have one.

    Thermostat and Heating Element Checks in Garden City

    For electric water heaters, we test the thermostat and heating elements to make sure they work correctly and hold an steady temperature. Faulty elements or thermostats can lead to too little hot water or wasted energy use. For gas water heaters, we inspect the burner assembly and pilot light. Around Garden City, summer heat keeps irrigation systems and outdoor spigots working hard, so leaks and backflow issues are common. Properly working components are essential for both efficiency and safety.

    Water Heater Anode Rod Inspection in Garden City

    The anode rod is a sacrificial part that protects your water heater tank from corrosion. During maintenance in Garden City, we inspect the condition of the anode rod and recommend replacement if it's heavily depleted. Replacing a worn anode rod can extend the life of your water heater by several years, preventing expensive tank leaks.
