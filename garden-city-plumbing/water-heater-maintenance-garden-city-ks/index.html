    Tankless Descaling in Garden City, KS

    Scale is the tankless unit's main enemy. Water across much of Kansas runs hard, leaving mineral scale in water heaters, faucets, showerheads, and dishwashers. A pump runs descaler through the exchanger for the recommended time, followed by a clean water flush. The inlet screen gets cleaned too. Igniter, burner and venting get a safety check.

    Thermostat, Element and Burner Checks in Garden City

    Wrong temperature means a part is off. Electric units get thermostat and element tests, and gas units get burner, pilot and control checks. Kansas sees hot summers, cold winters, and fast temperature swings, which put constant stress on pipes, fittings, and water heaters. Faulty parts waste energy. You run out sooner.

    Tank Flush and Inspection in Garden City

    Sediment forms from minerals in the water. On a standard tank visit we flush that sediment out, test the temperature and pressure relief valve, check the anode for corrosion and inspect every fitting for leaks, which protects one of the more expensive appliances in the house. The tank heats better. Recovery speeds up.

    Anode Rod Replacement in Garden City

    Some water eats anode rods fast. Water chemistry can wear a rod out in a few years, so we check it every time. We swap worn ones. It keeps rust off the tank lining.

    Anode Rod Checks in Garden City

    The anode rod rusts so the tank does not. It is a long metal rod screwed into the top of the tank, and once it has corroded down to bare wire, the water starts eating the steel tank lining instead. It comes out for inspection. A new anode costs far less than a new tank.

    Relief Valve and Safety Testing in Garden City, KS

    The relief valve is the tank's last line of defense. We lift the lever, confirm water discharges and the valve reseats without dripping, and check that the discharge pipe runs full size to an approved spot within a few inches of the floor or outside. We swap bad ones. We check the expansion tank too.
