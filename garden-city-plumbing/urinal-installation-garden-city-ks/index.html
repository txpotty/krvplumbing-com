    Waterless Urinal Installation in Garden City

    Waterless urinals save the most water and need the most discipline. A cartridge or sealing liquid blocks sewer gas while letting urine through, and it must be replaced on schedule. Your custodial team needs training. We show them how.

    ADA-Compliant Urinal Installation in Garden City

    At least one urinal in a public restroom with more than one usually has to be accessible. That means a rim no higher than 17 inches above the floor, an elongated bowl projecting at least 13-1/2 inches from the wall, and a 30 by 48 inch clear floor space in front. The flush handle has to be easy to reach. We mark it all during rough-in.

    Residential Urinal Installation in Garden City, KS

    Basement and garage bathrooms are where most home urinals go. We tie into the existing drain and vent with a 2 inch line, run a 3/4 or 1/2 inch supply depending on the flush valve, and frame in blocking or a compact carrier behind the wall. Ultra-low-flow models are popular. That barely touches the water bill.

    Office and Restaurant Urinal Installation in Garden City

    Restaurant restrooms see a lot of traffic in short bursts. A single urinal at a busy bar can flush a few hundred times on a Friday night, so we install fixtures and valves that recover quickly and stand up to rough handling without loosening on the wall. We can fit tamper-resistant valves. Night installs keep you open.

    Urinal Permits and Compliance in Garden City, KS

    New urinals usually need a permit. New fixtures mean rough-in and final inspections, and commercial jobs often need plans. Permits are on us. Like-for-like swaps may not need one.

    Urinal Rough-In for New Restrooms in Garden City

    Tenant improvements need rough-in before the walls close. We space urinals 30 inches apart and 15 inches from side walls, then set carriers and run all the lines. Coordination with the GC is constant. Rough inspection comes first.

    Waterless Cartridge Urinals in Garden City

    Each waterless brand uses its own cartridge. Falcon, Waterless Co, Kohler and Zurn units all trap odor differently, and the cartridge or sealing liquid has to be replaced after a set number of uses, which in a busy restroom can mean every few months. A replacement schedule comes with the install. Spare cartridges should stay on site.

    Urinal Installation Cost Factors in Garden City, KS

    Carrier work is the biggest cost swing. A replacement on an existing carrier with good supply and drain lines costs far less than a new install that needs a carrier, a new 2 inch drain and a 3/4 inch supply run through finished walls. Touchless valves cost more than manual. We itemize each piece.
