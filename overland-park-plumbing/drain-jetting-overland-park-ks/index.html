    Frequently Asked Questions About Drain Jetting in Overland Park

    The cost of drain jetting in Overland Park can range from $300 to $800, depending on the severity of the clog, the length and diameter of the pipe, and ease of access. Main sewer line jetting or commercial jobs may be on the higher end due to specialized equipment and more labor. We provide a clear estimate after an initial assessment.

    We use professional compact jetters from well-known drain equipment makers, paired with nozzles designed for grease, scale and debris.

    Drain snaking usually involves pushing an auger through a clog to create a small opening for water to pass. Drain jetting, or hydro jetting, uses high-pressure water to scour the entire pipe wall, removing buildup and leaving the pipe much cleaner. Jetting is more effective for stubborn blockages and preventative maintenance.

    Consumer jetter attachments for pressure washers exist, but they can cause leaks or injuries if misused. A pro has the right equipment and training.

    When performed by trained professionals like KRV Plumbing, drain jetting is usually safe for most common pipe materials, including PVC, cast iron, and clay. However, extremely old, corroded, or damaged pipes may not withstand the high pressure. We always perform a camera inspection first if there's a concern about pipe condition.

    A typical residential drain jetting service can take anywhere from 1 to 3 hours, depending on the complexity and location of the clog. More severe blockages or main sewer line jetting may need additional time for preparation and thorough cleaning.

    Both use pressurized water. Drain jetting focuses on smaller interior lines with compact equipment, while hydro jetting usually refers to main sewer lines with larger units.

    We do not recommend using a home pressure washer for drain jetting. These machines lack the power, specialized nozzles, and safety features of professional equipment. Improper use can damage your pipes, cause serious injury, or push clogs deeper. Always rely on trained professionals for drain jetting.
