    PEX-A vs. PEX-B Installation in Overland Park

    Homeowners ask us which one is better, and it really depends on the house. PEX-A bends tighter, recovers from a kink with a heat gun, and its expansion fittings flow more water, while PEX-B costs less and its crimp joints are just as reliable when they are gauged properly. Both last decades. In a cramped attic, we usually pick PEX-A.

    Uponor PEX System Installation in Overland Park

    Uponor's ProPEX system is PEX-A joined with an expansion tool and a cold-expansion ring. We stretch the pipe and ring, seat the fitting, and within seconds the plastic shrinks back around the barb into a tight seal. There is no crimp gauge to check. The fittings have a wide bore, so flow loss through each joint stays low.
