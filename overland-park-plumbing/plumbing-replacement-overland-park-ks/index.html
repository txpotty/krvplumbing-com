    Frequently Asked Questions About Plumbing Replacement in Overland Park

    Costs vary by job. A tank water heater replacement typically runs $1,400 to $3,000, a water softener $1,500 to $3,500, a water line $1,800 to $5,500 and a sewer line $4,000 to $15,000 or more.

    For water heater replacements, we mainly recommend and install reliable brands such as Rheem, AO Smith, Bradford White and Navien. These manufacturers are known for their durability, energy efficiency and excellent warranty coverage, providing long-term value for your Overland Park home.

    While some minor plumbing repairs can be DIY, major replacements like water heaters, sewer lines or repiping should always be handled by licensed professionals. These jobs involve complex systems, potential safety hazards (gas, high water pressure) and strict code compliance. Improper installation can lead to leaks, damage and even severe safety risks, costing more in the long run.

    We install water heaters from Bradford White, Rheem and A.O. Smith, tankless units from Rinnai and Navien, and quality softeners and well pumps from proven manufacturers.

    Frequent leaks, rusty or discolored water, low pressure, recurring backups and equipment over 10 years old are common signs. We can inspect and give you an honest assessment.

    If you have isolated leaks on newer pipes, a repair might suffice. However, if you have older galvanized, polybutylene or lead pipes, or if leaks are widespread and frequent, replacement is generally recommended. Our plumbers can conduct an inspection, often with camera technology for sewer lines, to provide an accurate assessment and recommendation.

    Most tank water heater replacements take two to four hours. Code upgrades or relocations can take longer.

    Some simple fixture swaps are DIY friendly, but water heaters, well pumps and sewer lines involve gas, electrical and code requirements. A licensed plumber ensures safety and compliance.
