    First-Time Disposal Installation in Overland Park

    Kitchens that never had a disposal need some rework. The sink needs a disposal flange. Then the drain gets reworked, and a switched outlet goes in, with an electrician if there is no power in the cabinet yet. Drain slope to the stack gets checked as well.

    Standard InSinkErator Installation in Overland Park

    A standard install replaces the unit under an existing sink. We take the old disposal down, scrape the old putty off the flange and set a new flange with fresh putty. Then the mount ring goes on. We hang the unit, turn the lower ring until it locks and connect the discharge tube to the trap with a new slip-joint washer so nothing weeps. We connect power through a cord kit or the old hardwire and confirm the ground first.

    Evolution Series Installation in Overland Park

    Evolution units grind in two or three stages and carry extra sound insulation around the motor housing, which is why they sit taller and weigh more than a Badger. Cabinet clearance matters. Before install day we measure the drop from sink to trap arm so the unit fits. The Compact fits tight cabinets, and the Excel needs the most room.

    Air Switch Disposal Installation in Overland Park

    An air switch puts a push button on the countertop or sink deck instead of a wall switch. The button pushes a puff of air down a thin tube. A control box under the sink reads that pulse and flips the power, keeping live wiring well away from the water. We drill the counter or use the spare faucet hole.

    Dishwasher Connection and Air Gap Setup in Overland Park

    Nearly every InSinkErator has a dishwasher inlet with a factory knockout plug inside. The plug gets knocked loose and pulled out of the grind chamber. The hose clamps onto the inlet and runs through an air gap or a high loop, which keeps dirty sink water from siphoning back into the tub. We test both appliances at once.

    Upfront Disposal Installation Pricing in Overland Park, KS

A first-time install that needs a new flange and a new outlet costs more, because that is real labor and sometimes an electrician's visit too. We quote the job before any tools come out.
