    Water Heater Maintenance in Overland Park

    Sediment is the tank's slow killer. Water across much of Kansas runs hard, leaving mineral scale in water heaters, faucets, showerheads, and dishwashers. The tank gets flushed, the anode rod gets checked, the relief valve gets tested and the connections get inspected. Flushing takes about an hour.     Tank and Tankless Heater Tune-Ups in Overland Park

    Mineral-heavy water shortens water heater life.
