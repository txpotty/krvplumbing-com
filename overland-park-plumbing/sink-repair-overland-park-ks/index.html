    Recurring Sink Clog Repair in Overland Park, KS

    Monthly clogs have a cause past the trap. We look past the wall with a longer cable or a camera for a sag or for buildup where the sink line meets the tub. Sometimes the vent is blocked. A sink that gurgles as it drains is the giveaway.

    Pop-Up Stopper Repair in Overland Park, KS

    Stoppers that will not hold water have a linkage problem. Either the clevis slipped on the lift rod or hair has wrapped the stopper so it will not seat. Half the time, a cleaning does it. The other half get a new pop-up drain in a matching finish.

    Supply Line and Angle Stop Replacement in Overland Park, KS

    Braided supply lines have a rubber core that ages. After five to ten years the core can split without warning, and old multi-turn stops often seize open. Lines and stops get replaced together. Quarter-turn stops close in one motion.

    Bathroom Sink Repair in Overland Park, KS

    Bath sinks have small parts that wear. The pop-up linkage sits in a damp cabinet for years, and the pivot nut where the rod enters the drain is the most common leak we find on a bathroom sink. We check pedestal and wall-hung sinks the same way. Vanity sinks just hide the leak better.

    Emergency Sink Repair in Overland Park, KS

    Shut the angle stops first. Turn the two valves under the sink to the right, and if they will not hold, close the main shutoff for the house and then call. We send active leaks to the front of the line. The plumber brings stops and supply lines to stop it on the first visit.

    Dripping Faucet Repair in Overland Park

    A faucet that drips once a second wastes thousands of gallons a year. On compression faucets the fix is a new washer and seat, while cartridge and ceramic disc faucets need the cartridge or its seals replaced. Cartridges for Moen, Delta, Pfister and Kohler are on the truck. Odd brands take a day to order.

    Repair or Replace? Sink Costs in Overland Park, KS

    Repair is cheaper nine times out of ten. Replace the sink only if the bowl is cracked, the glaze is chipped through, or the drain has rusted into the bowl. We quote both ways if it is a close call. Some people repair now and remodel next year.

    Overflow and Drain Flange Leak Repair in Overland Park, KS

    Some leaks only show up when the bowl is full. A leak like that is usually the overflow gasket or the flange putty, which only get wet when the bowl fills. We pull the drain and reseal it. Marble and some granite get silicone, because putty can stain them.
