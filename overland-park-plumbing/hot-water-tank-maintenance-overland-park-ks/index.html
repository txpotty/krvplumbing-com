    Sediment Flush and Descaling in Overland Park

    Hard water leaves calcium carbonate behind every time the burner heats the tank. Over a year that layer can harden into chunks that insulate the water from the flame and cause loud popping. We connect a hose to the drain valve, flush with full line pressure and agitate the bottom until the water runs clear. When scale is severe we use a descaling solution rated for potable systems. A clean tank heats faster and puts less stress on the bottom seam.

    Anode Rod Inspection and Replacement in Overland Park, KS

    The anode rod, often called the sacrificial rod, is essential for preventing corrosion inside your hot water tank. Water chemistry varies from one neighborhood to the next, and it affects the rod's lifespan. Our technicians inspect the anode rod for wear and recommend replacement if it's significantly corroded, typically every 3 to 5 years. Replacing a worn anode rod is a cost-effective way to protect your tank from rust and leaks, saving you from a full tank replacement.
