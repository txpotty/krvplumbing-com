    Disposal Jams and Hums in Overland Park, KS

    A hum without grinding tells us the motor has power and the plate will not turn. That is often ten minutes. We free the plate from below with a hex key, clear the hopper with pliers, never a hand, and reset the overload before we test it with water.     Burst Supply Lines in Overland Park, KS

    Plastic supply lines with plastic nuts crack more often than braided stainless, especially after years of pressure swings. A failed line under the sink can put gallons per minute into the cabinet and the floor below before anyone notices it. Both supply lines get replaced.     Grease Trap Installation and Cleaning in Overland Park

    Grease traps are sized by flow, and a three-compartment sink typically calls for a trap rated at 20 gallons per minute or more. We set the trap level, install the flow control fitting and vent it so it does not siphon. Cleaning schedules depend on volume. Busy fryer kitchens may need it every few weeks.

    Workstation and Farmhouse Sinks in Overland Park

    Farmhouse sinks need a cabinet built to carry them, since a 30 inch fireclay bowl can weigh over 100 pounds before water. The support frame gets checked first. Workstation sinks with ledges and accessories often have an offset drain, so the trap and disposal have to be planned around that drain location before the counter crew arrives.

    New Disposal Installation in Overland Park

    Adding a disposal where there never was one means a new outlet or a switched circuit under the sink, and that part is electrical work. We plumb it and can coordinate the wiring.
