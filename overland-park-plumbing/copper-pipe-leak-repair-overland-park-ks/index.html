    Slab Leak Detection and Repair in Overland Park

    Underground Copper Pipe Leak Repair in Overland Park

    Buried copper service lines can leak for months before anyone notices. The meter gives it away. We locate the leak with acoustic gear and tracer gas, then repair it through a small pit or replace the line by pulling new PEX or Type K copper through the old path. We test before backfill.

    Solder Joint Leak Repair in Overland Park, KS

    Older solder joints can fail from poor original work, thermal expansion or vibration. Look for white or green crust. A leaking joint usually weeps for a while before it drips, and we clean, reflux and resolder the joint or replace the fitting with new pipe on each side when the old copper is too thin. The joint gets tested.

    Thin-Wall Copper Pinhole Repair in Overland Park

    Older copper, especially Type M, pinholes more than heavier pipe. Thin walls wear through first. We locate the leak precisely and either solder in a new section or use a press fitting near framing, replacing the damaged length with Type L copper so the repair has a thicker wall than the pipe it replaced. We check the rest of the run.

    Copper Fitting and Valve Repair in Overland Park

    Angle stops, shutoff valves and copper fittings under sinks and behind toilets can weep or seize. Scale locks them up. Hard water buildup inside a valve can keep it from closing when you need it most, so we replace failing valves and fittings with quarter-turn ball valves that shut off fully and are easy to turn. Every valve gets checked.

    Corrosion-Related Leak Repair in Overland Park

    Corrosion thins copper from the inside. Private wells serve many rural homes, and minerals, sediment, and nitrate levels make periodic well testing a good habit. It shows up as green crust. On exposed pipe you may see blue-green deposits at fittings, and inside the wall the first sign is usually a pinhole or a weeping joint that stains the drywall below it. We replace the corroded length. We explain what drove it.
