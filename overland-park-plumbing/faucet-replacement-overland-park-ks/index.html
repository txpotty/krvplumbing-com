    Frequently Asked Questions About Faucet Replacement in Overland Park

    Labor for a faucet replacement usually runs $150 to $350. With a solid mid-range faucet included, most jobs total somewhere between $300 and $750, depending on the model and the condition of the stops. Touchless faucets can pass $800.

    We work on every major brand. Most of what we pull and install is Delta, Moen, Kohler, American Standard or Pfister, and we know their hardware well. We handle off-brand models as well.

    Count the holes and measure the spacing first. We look at your hole layout and suggest compatible faucets. Many single-hole faucets include a deck plate for three-hole sinks.

    Worn cartridges and O-rings cause most leaks. Water across much of Kansas runs quite hard, leaving mineral scale in water heaters, faucets, showerheads, and dishwashers. Scale hardens seals and scratches cartridge faces, and pressure above about 80 psi wears them faster. Corrosion on the body is the other cause.
