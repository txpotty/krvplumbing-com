    Causes of Sewer Line Damage in Overland Park, KS

    Sewer lines in Overland Park, KS can suffer damage from several factors, including shifting soils that cause pipes to crack or separate. Slab leaks and cracked sewer lines are common results of shifting clay, especially after long dry spells followed by heavy rain. Tree root intrusion is another major culprit, as roots seek out moisture in pipes and grow, causing blockages and structural damage. Age and corrosion, especially in older cast iron or clay pipes, also contribute to deterioration. Our trenchless solutions address these common issues effectively.

    Cured-In-Place Pipe Lining in Overland Park

    Cured-in-place lining creates a new pipe inside the old one. We clean the line, then pull or invert a resin-saturated liner into place and cure it with hot water, steam or UV light. The result is a seamless, jointless pipe that roots cannot penetrate. Lining works well for cracked or root-intruded lines that still hold their form.

    Cured-In-Place Pipe Relining in Overland Park, KS

    CIPP relining creates a new pipe within an existing damaged sewer line. A resin-saturated felt tube is inserted into the old pipe and then inflated, curing in place to form a smooth, durable and watertight liner. This technique is excellent for repairing cracks, leaks and minor offsets without needing to dig up the entire line. It is a quick and effective solution for many common sewer problems faced by Kansas homeowners.

    Root Intrusion Repair in Overland Park, KS

    Tree and shrub roots go after moisture in sewer joints. Once inside, they grow into dense masses that cause repeat backups. We cut roots with a mechanical cutter or hydro jet, then line the pipe to seal the joints. That breaks the cycle of cleaning the line every couple of months.

    Sewer Line Video Inspection in Overland Park

    Before any trenchless repair, we conduct a thorough video inspection of your sewer line. This key step makes sure we choose the most appropriate and effective trenchless method for your Overland Park property, avoiding guesswork.

    Cast Iron Sewer Line Repair in Overland Park, KS

    Cast iron drains and sewer lines corrode from the inside, leaving rough scale, cracks and channel rot along the bottom of the pipe. We descale the pipe with chain knockers or cutters, then line it to restore a smooth, sealed interior. This is especially useful for cast iron running under slabs where excavation is costly. Lining can add many years of service.
