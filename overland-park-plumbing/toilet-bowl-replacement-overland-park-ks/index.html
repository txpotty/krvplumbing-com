    Toilet Bowl Replacement for Older Homes in Overland Park, KS

    Houses built before the 1970s often have a lead closet bend or a cast iron flange caulked with oakum, and those need gentle handling when the bowl comes off. Old pipe cracks easily. We avoid prying on the flange and use repair rings or flange adapters made for cast iron, so the drain stays intact.

    Tank-to-Bowl Leak Repair and Replacement in Overland Park

    Drips from the bolts between the tank and bowl usually mean a flattened gasket or rusted bolts, but sometimes the porcelain around a bolt hole has cracked. The bolt holes get a close look before we quote. A gasket and bolt kit fixes the first case, and a cracked bolt hole means a new bowl with stainless or brass hardware.

    Cracked or Damaged Toilet Bowl Replacement in Overland Park

    Porcelain that cracks below the waterline leaks every time the bowl refills, and the water usually runs down inside the base where you will not see it for weeks. Even a thin crack will leak. We pull the bowl, check the flange and the subfloor, then set a matching bowl on a fresh seal.

    Emergency Bowl Replacement in Overland Park

    Shut the angle stop behind the toilet the moment you see water coming from a cracked bowl, then pull towels around the base to slow the spread. Then call us. We stop the leak, remove the broken bowl safely and install a replacement or a full new toilet if no match is on hand. We also check for water in the floor below.

    Pressure-Assist Toilet Bowl Replacement in Overland Park

    Pressure-assist toilets use a sealed vessel inside the tank, and the bowl is matched to that vessel's flush rate, so a bowl-only swap means finding the exact paired model. Many are not sold separately. When the matching bowl is gone, a full toilet replacement is the better route, and we will price both.

    Wall-Hung Commercial Bowl Replacement in Overland Park, KS

    Wall-hung commercial bowls sit on steel carriers inside the wall, and when one cracks we also check that the carrier bolts and the outlet gasket are still sound. A loose carrier will crack the next bowl. We reconnect and test the flushometer before anyone uses the restroom again.

    Cost-Effective Bowl Swaps in Overland Park, KS

    Swapping only the bowl saves money when the tank is newer, the fill valve and flush valve are in good shape, and the bowl is still made in your color. White is the easiest to match. Discontinued colors are where bowl-only swaps fall apart, and then a full toilet costs about the same.

    Flange Repair During Bowl Replacement in Overland Park, KS

    The flange is the ring that anchors the bowl to the drain, and the only time anyone sees it is when the old bowl comes off. That is the moment to check it. We fix a cracked or sunken flange before the new bowl is set, since a new bowl on a bad flange will rock and leak within months.
