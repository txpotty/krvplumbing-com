    Softener Loop Installation in Overland Park, KS

    Softener loops are the pair of stubbed-out lines near the garage wall or water heater where a softener ties into the main. We can add a loop with shutoff valves and a bypass. Hose bibs stay off it. The drain goes to a laundry box or floor drain with an air gap.

    Brine Tank Replacement in Overland Park

    Salt bridges, crusty layers of salt that form over a hollow, stop the brine from making contact with the water and the softener quits softening. We break up the crust and scoop out the mushy salt underneath. We replace cracked tanks and bad safety floats. Use pellet salt.

    Emergency Softener Replacement in Overland Park, KS

    A softener that sticks in regeneration can run water to the drain for hours, and a cracked bypass or tank can flood a garage floor. Put the unit in bypass first. Most stuck or leaking systems get replaced in a day or two, sooner if the right size is in stock.

    Long-Term Softener Costs in Overland Park, KS

    Long-term cost on a softener is mostly salt and water. Compared with a timer unit, a metered valve set efficiently often uses a third less salt, which matters over a fifteen-year life. We compare brands on that basis.

    TAC Salt-Free Conditioners in Overland Park

    Template-assisted crystallization, or TAC, is the salt-free media with real independent test data behind it, and it is the only kind we install. The tank goes on the main line downstream of the filter, with no power or drain needed. Magnets and clamp-on gadgets are a different story.

    Salt-Free Alternatives in Overland Park, KS

    Some homeowners do not want salt or have no drain near the main line. In those cases a TAC conditioner works, though we are upfront that it stops scale and does not make water feel soft. Low-sodium diets are another reason.

    Replacing Fleck Water Softeners in Overland Park, KS

    Fleck control valves, the 5600SXT and 9100SXT in particular, are on a large share of the softeners we see in Kansas homes. If the valve is old enough that parts are hard to find, a new valve on a new tank is the smarter buy. Current Fleck valves fit standard tanks.

    Urgent Softener Problems in Overland Park

    Not every softener failure is urgent. A unit leaking from the valve body or the brine tank overflowing onto the floor is, and we move those calls up the schedule. Turn the bypass handle and wait for us.
