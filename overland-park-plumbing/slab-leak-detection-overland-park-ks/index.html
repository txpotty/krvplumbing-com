    Cold Water Slab Leak Detection in Overland Park, KS

    Cold water slab leaks do not create warm spots, so they are more difficult to see. They often show up as a running water meter, damp carpet, a musty smell or water seeping at baseboards. We isolate the cold side and use acoustic equipment to listen for the leak through the slab. Line tracers map the pipe route so we know where to listen.

    Post-Tension Slab Leak Detection in Overland Park

    Many newer homes are built on post-tension slabs that use steel cables under high tension. Cutting into one without care is dangerous and can damage the foundation. We locate the cables with scanning equipment before any repair access is opened. This lets us repair the leak without risk or recommend a reroute that avoids the slab entirely.

    Slab Leak Reroutes in Overland Park

    Rerouting abandons the leaking line under the slab and runs new pipe through the walls or attic. It avoids breaking concrete and often costs less than direct access under finished floors. We typically use PEX for reroutes because it handles heat and resists hard-water corrosion. This option is a good fit when the slab is covered in tile or hardwood.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Slab Leak Detection in Overland Park

    Slab leak detection in Overland Park typically costs $150 to $450. Repairs range from about $800 for a simple reroute to $3,000 or more for direct access under finished floors. We provide a written estimate after locating the leak.

    Slab leaks can cause significant damage including foundation cracks, soil erosion beneath the slab, mold and mildew growth in wall cavities or under flooring, damage to flooring materials and increased humidity levels. Over time, it can lead to structural instability and extensive, costly repairs.

    Common signs of a slab leak include unusually high water bills, warm spots on your floor, the sound of running water when no fixtures are in use, visible cracks in the foundation, damp or wet spots on carpets or flooring and a sudden drop in water pressure. Musty odors can also indicate moisture buildup.

    Reroutes and repipes often require a plumbing permit, while small direct repairs may not. Requirements vary by city and county. We take care of permits and inspections when needed.

    For significant slab leak repairs, especially those involving excavation or extensive re-piping, a plumbing permit from the local building department is often required. KRV Plumbing handles all necessary permitting processes so your repair is compliant with local codes and regulations, protecting your investment.

    While you can look for the signs, accurately detecting a slab leak requires specialized equipment and expertise that most homeowners don't have. DIY attempts often lead to more damage or misdiagnosis, making professional detection a more affordable and reliable solution in the long run.

    Most slab leak detection services can be completed within 1 to 3 hours, depending on the severity and location of the leak. Our goal is always to be as efficient and non-invasive as possible, providing a quick diagnosis so repair work can begin soon.

    Some homeowner's insurance policies may cover the cost of accessing and repairing the slab leak, especially if the leak was sudden and accidental. However, coverage often varies, and damage caused by long-term neglect might not be covered. We recommend checking your specific policy details or contacting your insurance provider for details.
