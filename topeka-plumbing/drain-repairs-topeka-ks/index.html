    Tub and Shower Drain Repair in Topeka, KS

    Hair, soap scum and minerals narrow bath drains over time. Gaskets fail too.     Main Line Drain Repair in Topeka

    A problem in the main line shows up at every drain in the house. Basement and first-floor drains go first. Roots, cracks from ground movement and collapsed sections are the common causes, and we confirm which one on camera, then repair it with a spot dig, a trenchless liner or a section replacement depending on what the footage shows.     Emergency Drain Repairs in Topeka, KS

    A drain that bursts under a sink can soak a cabinet in minutes. Shut off the water to it. We answer after hours, talk you through stopping the flow, and send someone out to cut out the broken pipe and fix it the same visit if possible. We also flag any wet drywall.

    Leaking Drain Pipe Repair in Topeka, KS

    Drain leaks are low pressure, so they drip instead of spray. Most go unnoticed for months. Corrosion, shifting framing and cracked fittings are the usual causes, and we trace the leak by running each fixture and watching the joints, then cut out the damaged section and replace it with matching PVC, ABS or cast iron. Repairs meet local plumbing code.

    Bathroom Drain Repair in Topeka

    Hair and soap scum are the main problem in bath drains. Scale speeds it up. We clear sinks, tubs and showers, replace broken pop-up stoppers and worn P-traps, and fix leaking tub shoes and shower drain bodies that let water drip into the ceiling below. Most of these are one-hour jobs.

    Storm Drain Repair in Topeka, KS

    Yard and storm drains carry rain away from the foundation. Flash flooding can push water into basements and crawlspaces, so sump pumps and proper grading matter for many homes. Debris and silt fill them up. We clear the line with a jetter, camera it to look for crushed or separated sections, and repair broken pipe so the runoff reaches daylight instead of soaking the soil next to your foundation. We check the discharge end every time.

    Sewer Drain Line Repair in Topeka

    Every drain in the house empties into one building drain. Trouble there affects everything. Cracks, offsets and collapses in that line cause whole-house backups, so we use a liner when the pipe allows it and a targeted excavation when it does not. We decide after seeing the footage.

    Restaurant and Office Drain Repair in Topeka

A cleaning schedule prevents most of it.
