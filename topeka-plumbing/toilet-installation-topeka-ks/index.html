    Pressure-Assisted Toilet Installation in Topeka

    Pressure-assisted toilets have a sealed vessel inside the tank that uses line pressure to push the flush. Below about 20 psi, they flush poorly. Before suggesting one, we put a gauge on a hose bib, and we mention the noise, because the flush carries through the whole house. They rarely clog.

    Wall-Hung Carrier Systems in Topeka

    In-wall carriers from Geberit and Grohe each mount a little differently, and the instructions matter. We follow the maker's frame height chart exactly. Set the frame too low and the bowl ends up at child height, too high and nobody's feet touch the floor, and neither can be fixed once the wall is tiled. Measure twice here.

    Customer-Supplied Toilet Installation in Topeka, KS

    Bought the toilet yourself? First, look at the rough-in printed on the carton. We unbox it and look over the porcelain for shipping cracks before we pull your old one, since a hairline crack in the bowl or tank means it needs to go back to the store, not onto your floor. We bring the seal and stainless bolts.

    High-Efficiency Toilet Installation in Topeka, KS

    High-efficiency toilets flush on 1.28 gallons or less, against 3.5 gallons or more for toilets from before the early 1990s. Check for the WaterSense label. A MaP rating of 800 grams or better is what we look for, since a weak low-flow toilet that needs double flushing saves no water at all. Dual-flush models add a lighter half flush.

    Two-Piece Toilet Installation in Topeka

    Two-piece toilets come with the tank and bowl separate, joined by a rubber gasket and two or three bolts. The tank goes on before the bowl is set. Tightening the tank bolts evenly, side to side, matters more than how hard they go, because an uneven pull cracks the china or leaves a slow drip at one bolt. It should not wobble.

    Standard Toilet Installation in Topeka

    A standard floor-mount toilet is a two-hour job when the flange is sound. We shut off the stop, drain the tank and pull the old bowl. After scraping the old wax off, we check the flange for cracks, set new stainless bolts, and lower the new toilet straight down onto a fresh seal so it compresses evenly. The old one leaves with us.

    Low-Flow WaterSense Toilet Installation in Topeka, KS

    WaterSense toilets are capped at 1.28 gallons per flush, and some dual-flush models drop to around 0.8 for liquid waste. It adds up quickly. Replacing a single 3.5 gallon toilet in a household of four can save several thousand gallons a year, and some water utilities have offered rebates on high-efficiency models, so it is worth asking yours. Pick a strong MaP rating.

    Smart Toilet and Bidet Seat Installation in Topeka

    Bidet seats like the TOTO Washlet and Bio Bidet bolt onto most existing elongated bowls. A T-valve splits water off the fill line. Heated seats with a warm-air dryer need a nearby GFCI outlet, and when there is none we bring in an electrician first. Non-electric seats take about thirty minutes.
