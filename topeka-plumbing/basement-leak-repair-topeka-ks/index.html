    Groundwater Pressure Relief in Topeka

    Sump Pit and Pump Installation in Topeka

    Installing a new sump means breaking out a section of slab, setting a lined pit, and running the discharge out through the rim joist. We use a cast iron pump in most homes and a 1-1/2 inch discharge with a check valve. Discharge cannot go into the sewer in most places. It has to discharge outside, far enough from the foundation that it does not cycle back into the pit, often ten feet or more.

    Crack Injection for Basements in Topeka

    Injection works on poured concrete walls, not block. Ports go along the crack, the face gets sealed, and resin goes in from the bottom up until it fills the full depth. Epoxy adds strength. For a crack that is actively wet, polyurethane works better since it reacts with water.

    Sump Pump Installation & Repair in Topeka

    A sump pump sits in a pit below the slab and kicks on when a float rises. Most failures come from a stuck float switch, a burned-out motor, or a check valve that lets the discharge water fall back into the pit and short-cycles the pump. We test yours with a bucket. Add a battery backup if storms knock out power.
