    Grease Trap Installation in Topeka

    A new restaurant or a kitchen expansion usually needs a grease interceptor sized from the fixtures that drain to it. Under-sink traps work for small kitchens, while larger operations need in-ground interceptors of 500 gallons or more. Sizing is set by code. We size the unit from the equipment schedule, set it at the right grade and vent it properly so it passes inspection the first time.

    Grease Trap and Interceptor Repair in Topeka, KS

    Food service sites need a grease trap or interceptor to keep fats, oils and grease out of the public sewer, and pretreatment inspectors check them. Odor is the first warning. We repair broken baffles, replace cracked lids and outlet tees, jet the inlet lines and confirm the unit is sized for your fixture count and flow rate. Slow sinks and overflow usually point to a full or damaged trap. Keeping the pumping log current helps at inspection time.

    Commercial Fixture and Faucet Repair in Topeka, KS

    Commercial faucets and toilets are built heavy, but they still wear. Cartridges leak. We repair and replace sensor faucets, metering faucets, wall-hung toilets, mop sinks and three-compartment sinks, and we stock parts for Sloan, Chicago Faucets, Zurn and T and S Brass. Low-flow aerators cut water use without slowing hand washing. Most fixture calls are done in under two hours.

    Slab Leak Detection for Businesses in Topeka

    A slab leak under a store or office floor often shows up as a warm spot, a running water meter with everything off, or a crack in floor tile. Much of Kansas has expansive clay soil that swells when wet and shrinks in drought, which can move foundations and stress buried pipes. We confirm it with a meter test and pressure test, then use acoustic listening gear and thermal imaging to pinpoint the leak within a foot or two. Then we decide. A spot repair through the floor makes sense for some, and an overhead reroute makes more sense for others. You get a price on each option.

    Tenant Improvement and Build-Out Plumbing in Topeka, KS

    New tenants usually need restrooms moved, a break room added or a kitchen built in a shell space. We work from stamped plans, coordinate rough-in with your general contractor and schedule inspections so the plumbing is never what holds up the opening date. Old lines get capped. Abandoned drains and supplies are cut back to the main and capped properly, which prevents dead legs and sewer gas later.

    Commercial Pipe Repair and Repiping in Topeka, KS

    Older commercial buildings often have galvanized supply lines that are rusted nearly shut, or copper that has pinholed along hot water runs. Patching works for a while. Once leaks start showing up in different spots every few months, a sectional repipe in Type L copper or commercial-grade PEX usually costs less than the next three emergency calls. The first step is mapping the risers and branches. Work gets phased by floor or suite so tenants keep their water most of the day.

    Commercial Water Heaters and Boilers in Topeka, KS
