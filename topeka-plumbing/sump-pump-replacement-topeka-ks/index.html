    Zoeller Switch and Pump Repair in Topeka

    Zoeller units with a piggyback float plug the pump cord into the switch cord, and if that piggyback plug fails the pump never gets power. That is the first thing we test. Some calls only need a new switch, while others need the whole pump. You will know which before we quote a pump.

    Pedestal Pumps for Shallow Pits in Topeka, KS

    Older basements sometimes have a narrow, shallow pit poured right into the floor, and a pedestal pump is often the only thing that fits without breaking concrete. We replace the pump and float rod and rerun the PVC up to the check valve. Cheaper than a submersible, too.

    Sump Basin and Pit Replacement in Topeka, KS

    Old clay tile or 18-inch plastic basins crack and fill with silt, and a pump in a basin that small cycles every few minutes during a heavy rain. The fix is to cut the slab, drop in a bigger poly basin with a bolted lid, and patch the concrete. A bigger basin means longer pump cycles. Fewer starts, longer motor life.

    Emergency Sump Pump Swaps in Topeka, KS

    Pit filling, pump not running? Call (316) 229-8459 and we will send someone. Keep the lid off, unplug anything sitting on the wet floor, and use a wet vac to hold the water below the rim until we get there. Common pumps ride on the truck, so most swaps happen right then.     Choosing a Backup Battery in Topeka

    Battery choice decides how long the backup actually runs. A sealed AGM or deep-cycle marine battery in the 75 to 100 amp-hour range will typically handle several hours of intermittent pumping during an outage. We size it to how often your pump runs on a wet day. Batteries need replacing every three to five years.

    Wayne Sump Pump Replacement in Topeka

    Wayne makes everything from a thermoplastic quarter-horse pump at the hardware store to cast iron models with vertical switches, and the cheap ones are what we replace most. The usual failure is a burned-out motor or a float that hangs up on the cord. You get a matching Wayne or a sturdier cast iron model, your call. Takes about two hours.

    Cast Iron Submersible Pumps in Topeka

    Cast iron sheds motor heat into the water around it far better than a plastic housing does, which is a big part of why a Zoeller M53 or Liberty 250 outlasts a hardware-store pump. Most swaps take one to two hours. We also check that the float has room to travel without touching the basin wall.

    Sizing a Submersible Sump Pump in Topeka

    Horsepower matters less than people think. A third-horse pump moves plenty of water for most homes, as long as the vertical lift to the discharge is under about 15 feet. We check the flow chart against your actual lift before we pick a size. A pump that is too big empties the pit too fast and wears itself out.
