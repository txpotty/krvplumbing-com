    Sewage Ejector Pump Replacement in Topeka

    Basement bathrooms and laundry rooms below the sewer line need sewage ejector pumps instead of sump pumps. These pumps handle solids and must be installed in sealed, vented basins. We replace ejector pumps with models rated for two inch solids. We also check the basin seal, vent and discharge check valve. Proper installation stops sewer gas odors.

    Commercial Pit Pump Replacement in Topeka

    Commercial buildings often have elevator pits, parking garage drains and mechanical room sumps. These require pumps rated for higher flow and sometimes oil-sensing controls. We replace commercial pumps and alarms to meet code and insurance requirements. Duplex systems with alternating pumps provide redundancy. We schedule work to avoid interrupting operations.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump System Installation in Topeka

    Flash flooding can push water into basements and crawlspaces, so sump pumps and proper grading matter for many homes. Power outages during severe storms can leave standard sump pumps useless. Our battery backup sump pump system installation in Topeka provides an important layer of protection.     Pedestal Sump Pump Replacement in Topeka, KS

    Pedestal sump pumps have their motor mounted above the sump pit, making them easier to access for maintenance and often more durable.     Float Switch and Check Valve Replacement in Topeka

    Many sump pump failures start with a stuck float switch or a leaking check valve. A failed check valve lets water flow back into the pit and causes the pump to short cycle. We install new check valves with every pump replacement and verify the switch moves without binding. Vertical float switches work well in tight pits. Tethered floats work best in larger pits.

    Combination Sump Pump System Replacement in Topeka, KS

    For added protection, many homeowners in Topeka choose a combination sump pump system, which pairs a primary electric pump with a battery backup pump in one unit. This keeps the system running even if the power fails or the primary pump quits. KRV Plumbing can replace your existing combination system or install a new one, evaluating your needs for groundwater management.     Frequently Asked Questions About Sump Pump Replacement in Topeka

    A standard sump pump replacement typically costs $500 to $1,200 installed. Battery backup systems usually add $800 to $1,800. Sewage ejector pumps usually cost $1,200 to $2,500.

    To maintain your new sump pump, regularly check the pit for debris, test the pump monthly by pouring water into the pit, and make sure the discharge pipe is clear. An annual professional inspection by KRV Plumbing can also help lengthen its lifespan.

    KRV Plumbing provides warranty coverage on both the sump pump unit itself, per the manufacturer's warranty, and our workmanship. We stand behind our installations for your peace of mind and a dependable, long-lasting solution.

    Most sump pump replacements take one to two hours. Battery backups or new discharge lines take longer.

    A battery backup sump pump system is a secondary pump powered by a battery, designed to turn on automatically if your primary sump pump loses power during an outage or fails. This is important for maintaining flood protection during power outages caused by severe weather.

    A primary sump pump is your main defense against basement flooding, powered by your home's electricity. A backup sump pump, usually battery-powered or water-powered, is a secondary system that activates if the primary pump fails, loses power or cannot keep up with heavy water inflow. Many Topeka homes are better off with a backup system due to storm risks.

    Sump pumps usually have a lifespan of 7 to 10 years. Even if they appear to be working, their efficiency can decrease over time. Regular inspection and testing are important, and replacement is advisable as they approach the end of their expected service life.
