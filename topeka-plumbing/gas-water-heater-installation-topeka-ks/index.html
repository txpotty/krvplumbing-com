    Tankless Gas Water Heater Conversion in Topeka

    Converting from a tank to tankless is usually more than a swap. Most conversions need a bigger gas line from the meter, a new vent through the wall or roof, a condensate drain and isolation valves for future descaling. It takes most of a day. We do all of it to code.

    Power Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Topeka, KS

    Power vent heaters use a blower to push exhaust through PVC or other approved pipe. Without needing a chimney, the vent can exit through a side wall within the allowed length. A 120-volt outlet is required. We terminate the vent to spec.

    Proper Sizing and Venting in Topeka, KS

    Sizing and venting decide whether a gas heater runs safe and efficient. We figure your household peak demand from bathrooms and people, then make sure the vent diameter, rise and total length match the manufacturer table and local code. A small vent can backdraft.     Rheem Gas Water Heater Installation in Topeka, KS

    Rheem gas heaters show up in a lot of local homes, from basic atmospheric tanks to power vent models. KRV Plumbing handles every Rheem gas model. Our install covers gas, flue and the Guardian safety system, including the air intake that keeps it working. Then we test draft.

    High-Efficiency Gas Water Heater Installation in Topeka, KS

    High-efficiency gas heaters use condensing technology to pull extra heat out of the exhaust. Because the exhaust comes out cool and wet, they vent through PVC and need a condensate drain, which changes where the heater can go and what the install involves. Rebates may apply. We install Rheem, Bradford White and AO Smith models.

    Gas Tankless Water Heater Installation in Topeka, KS

    Gas tankless units fire at up to 199,000 BTU, far more than a 40,000 BTU tank burner. Private wells serve many rural homes, and minerals, sediment, and nitrates make periodic well testing a good habit. We recommend pre-filtration based on your water.
