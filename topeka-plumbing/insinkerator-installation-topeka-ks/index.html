    Badger Disposal Install in Topeka

    Most replacement disposals in area kitchens are a Badger. These 1/3 to 3/4 HP units handle everyday scraps and fit nearly any standard sink base. The unit hangs on its quick-lock mount, the discharge tube goes on, a cord kit gets added if needed and we finish with a cold-water test grind. About an hour, usually.

    Commercial Disposer Installation in Topeka, KS

    Restaurant disposals are a different class of machine. A commercial disposer runs 1 to 5 HP on its own circuit, and many come with a wall control panel and a water solenoid. We mount the unit and plumb the water feed, and your electrician handles the circuit. Expect an inspector to check it. Local code may also limit where a disposer can discharge relative to the grease interceptor, so we check that rule before the job starts.

    Standard InSinkErator Installation in Topeka

    A standard install goes into a sink that already has a drain and power. The old disposal comes down, the flange gets scraped clean and a new one goes in on fresh putty. Then the mount ring goes on. The unit hangs on the ring, locks with a quarter turn, and the discharge tube meets the trap on a new slip-joint washer. We connect power through a cord kit or the old hardwire and confirm the ground first.

    Dishwasher Connection and Air Gap Setup in Topeka

    Nearly every InSinkErator has a dishwasher inlet with a factory knockout plug inside. The plug gets knocked loose and pulled out of the grind chamber. Then the dishwasher hose goes on with a proper clamp, routed through an air gap or up into a high loop under the counter so sink water cannot siphon back into the dishwasher tub. We test both appliances at once.

    Garbage Disposal Replacement in Topeka

    If your current disposal hums, leaks from the bottom or trips its reset button every week, replacement usually beats repair. A leak from the housing means the internal seal is gone. No one repairs that seal. We disconnect the old unit, cap the dishwasher hose while we work and set the new disposal on the same drain geometry, adjusting the tailpiece if the new one sits at a different height.

    Disposal Replacement and Haul-Away in Topeka, KS

    Old disposals are heavy and awkward to get rid of. They leave on our truck. That includes the old cord, the bent mounting ring if we replaced it and the corroded slip nuts, so nothing is left in a bag under your sink for you to deal with on trash day. Most replacements finish in one visit.

    Septic-Friendly Disposal Installs in Topeka, KS

    Homes on septic can run a disposal, but food solids add load to the tank. Clay soil can slow drain field absorption, which may show up as soggy patches in the yard, odors, or slow drains. InSinkErator's Septic Assist feeds an enzyme solution into the chamber during each run to start breaking down waste before the tank. The enzyme cartridge gets mounted, and we show you the refill. Scrape plates first.

    New Construction Disposal Installs in Topeka

    New builds and gut remodels get the disposal in two trips. At rough-in we set the drain height and confirm a switched outlet is in the sink base. The disposal itself waits. Once the countertop and sink are in and the faucet is set, we come back to hang the unit, connect the dishwasher and test everything with the water on.
