    Shower Pan Leak Repair and Waterproofing in Topeka, KS

    Wet baseboards outside the shower or a stain on the ceiling below usually point to the pan. We plug the drain, fill the pan with an inch or two of water, and mark the level and wait 24 hours. A drop means a leak. Rebuilding means a mortar pre-slope of a quarter inch per foot, a new PVC liner or bonded membrane, and a two-piece clamping drain set on the liner. The new pan gets its own flood test before tile.

    Waterproofing for Older Homes in Topeka, KS

    Houses built before modern drainage rules often have no footing drain at all, or a clay tile drain that collapsed decades ago. Homes from the late seventies through the mid nineties may have gray polybutylene supply pipe, which is prone to sudden failure. Fieldstone and old brick foundations need breathable products. A rigid coating on soft brick traps moisture and makes the face spall off over a few seasons, so we use lime-compatible or vapor-open coatings and pair them with interior drain tile. Old mortar joints get repointed first.

    Interior Walls Waterproofing in Topeka, KS

    Interior drain tile handles water that already got through the wall.     Shower Wall Waterproofing in Topeka
