    Zoeller Sump Pump Installation in Topeka, KS

    Zoeller has built cast iron sump pumps for decades, and parts are easy to get. The M53 and the M98 are the two we set most, depending on lift and inflow, and both come with mechanical float switches that hold up for years. They make matching backup units. We help you pick the model that fits the pit.

    Commercial Sump Pump Solutions in Topeka

    Commercial basements, elevator pits and loading dock drains need pumps rated for more volume. For those jobs we install duplex systems where two pumps alternate, with a control panel and a high water alarm that can tie into the building monitoring. Downtime costs money. Work is scheduled around your operating hours.

    High Water Alarms and Smart Monitoring in Topeka

    A high water alarm is a float switch that sounds when the pit rises past normal. Wi-Fi monitors go further and send a phone alert when the pump runs too long, the power drops or the water climbs past a set level. Rentals need these. We add them to new installs and to pumps already in place.

    Emergency Sump Pump Replacement in Topeka, KS

    A dead pump during a storm means the water is already climbing. We carry common pumps on the truck. Figure on two hours for a typical swap. We test it with a full pit before leaving.

    Primary Sump Pump Installation in Topeka

    A primary sump pump is the one doing the daily work, kicking on every time the float rises in the pit after rain or snowmelt. Sizing comes from two measurements we take on site, the pit diameter and the vertical lift to the discharge. Most homes need 1/3 or 1/2 HP. A 1-1/2 inch discharge line handles the flow from either size without straining the motor.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Topeka, KS

    Backup systems we install use sealed AGM batteries that do not need water. The charger keeps the battery topped off. Batteries need replacing every few years. They matter most below finished rooms.

    Exterior Drainage and Yard Sump Pumps in Topeka

    Sunken patios, below-grade driveways and deep window wells collect water with nowhere to go. We install exterior basins with pumps for those spots. Lots that slope toward the house need this most. The outlet sits well clear of the foundation and the property line.

    Sump Pump Installation Cost in Topeka, KS

    Price depends mostly on whether a pit already exists. Swapping a pump in an existing pit is the cheapest job, while a new pit means saw cutting, digging, gravel and a concrete patch, which roughly doubles the labor. Battery backups and alarms add to the total. Your written quote shows each option and its price.

    Local Sump Pump Installers in Topeka, KS

    Local soil and the way Topeka, KS homes were built both change how a sump system should go in. KRV Plumbing has set pumps in clay, sand and rock. In clay the water stays against the foundation longer, so the pump runs more often and a larger basin helps the motor last. That changes the pump we pick.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Sump Pump Installation in Topeka

    A pump swap in an existing pit usually runs $600 to $1,500. New pits, with saw cutting, excavation and a concrete patch, run about $1,500 to $4,000. Add $500 to $1,500 for a backup.

    Doing it yourself is possible but risky. Getting the pit depth, the check valve orientation, the discharge route and the outlet wiring right takes experience, and one mistake shows up during the next storm. We do it daily.

    Grinding, rattling or a pump that runs with no water in the pit are all warning signs. Rust on the housing is another. If the basement flooded anyway, it is time.

    Swapping a pump in an existing pit takes about two to three hours. A new pit can take a full day.

    Zoeller, Little Giant, Wayne and Liberty make up most of what we install. All four have good parts support. We pick by pit size and lift.

    Discharge should end at least ten feet from the foundation, on ground that slopes away. Plenty of cities ban discharge into the sanitary sewer.

    The pump has its manufacturer warranty, and KRV Plumbing warranties the installation labor. Exact terms depend on the pump model, and they are written into your quote and service paperwork.

    For some homeowners, swapping a pump in a working pit is doable. Anything involving a new pit, a new discharge route or new wiring is a job for a professional.
