    High-Efficiency Water Softener Installation in Topeka

    High-efficiency softeners use upflow brining and metered valves to cut salt and water use. A new metered unit often uses far less salt and water than an older model on a timer, which shows up on both bills. That pays back over time. Several models are available.

    Reverse Osmosis Drinking Water Systems in Topeka

    Reverse osmosis pushes water through a fine membrane that removes most dissolved solids, including the sodium a softener adds. We install RO units under the kitchen sink with a dedicated faucet, and many can also feed a refrigerator water line or an ice maker. It complements a softener. Filters get changed on schedule.

    Water Softener Replacement in Topeka, KS

    Older softeners lose ground as resin wears out, valve seals leak or injectors clog. We remove the old unit and install a new metered model, then check the loop and drain line while everything is apart. Salt use drops. Performance comes back right away.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Installation in Topeka

    Ion exchange is the only method that truly removes hardness from water. We install salt-based softeners with a resin tank sized to your grains per gallon, a brine tank, a bypass valve and a drain line that ends at a code-approved air gap. Then we program it. Regeneration time is usually set for 2 a.m.

    Whole-Home Water Softener Installation in Topeka

    Whole-home softeners treat every gallon that enters the house, right after the main shutoff. Placing it on the main means the water heater, the dishwasher and every fixture get soft water, not just the kitchen. We size before we sell. Your test result and water use decide the tank size.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Systems in Topeka, KS

    Salt-based softeners use ion exchange, swapping calcium and magnesium for sodium on a bed of resin beads. KRV Plumbing sets them up with the right resin and brine tanks. We program regeneration by metered water use so the system only recharges when the resin is actually spent, which saves a lot of salt. Most effective option.

    Ecodyne Water Softener Installation in Topeka

    Ecodyne softeners are sold at many home stores, and we install them for homeowners who already bought one. We follow the Ecodyne manual for the bypass, drain and overflow connections, then program hardness and regeneration settings for your water. We install store-bought units. We set it up for you.

    Water Softener Replacement Services in Topeka, KS
