    Electric Water Heater Repair in Topeka

    Two elements and two thermostats run a standard electric tank, and any one of the four can quit without warning. Lukewarm water that runs out fast usually means the lower element. We test resistance with a meter. The replacement parts ride on the truck, which keeps it to one visit.

    Leaking Water Heater Repair in Topeka, KS

    Not every leak means a dead tank. Relief valve, drain valve and supply line drips are cheap fixes. Water from the tank body or bottom seam usually means the lining has failed, and replacement is the only real fix. We protect your floors either way.
