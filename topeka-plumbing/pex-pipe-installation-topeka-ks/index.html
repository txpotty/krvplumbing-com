    Why Choose KRV Plumbing for PEX Pipe Installation in Topeka, KS?

    PEX does not pit or scale the way copper and galvanized do, and that shows up fastest in houses with hard water. Water across much of Kansas runs hard, leaving mineral scale in water heaters, faucets, showerheads, and dishwashers. We size the lines correctly, too. A three-quarter inch trunk feeding half-inch branches keeps the shower steady when someone starts the dishwasher across the house.

    • Lower Install Cost, PEX costs less per foot than copper and takes fewer labor hours.
    • Quieter Lines, PEX takes the edge off water hammer and transmits less flow noise than copper.
    • Home-Run Option, Each fixture can get its own line off a manifold and its own shutoff.

    Leak Repair with PEX in Topeka, KS

    A single bad section is not always a reason to repipe. We can cut out a split or corroded stretch of copper, transition to PEX with a listed fitting, and have the water back on in an hour or two.     PEX-B Crimp and Clamp Systems in Topeka

    PEX-B is stiffer, is cheaper by the roll, and joins with copper crimp rings or stainless cinch clamps over brass or poly-alloy fittings. A crimp joint is only as good as the tool, so we check every ring with a go/no-go gauge, and a ring that does not pass gets cut out and redone. That takes seconds. Skipping it causes most crimp leaks.
