    What We Fix Under the Sink in Topeka, KS

    Kitchen Sink Repair in Topeka

    Kitchen sinks take the most abuse in the house. Basket strainers loosen and leak around the putty, pull-down sprayer hoses split inside the cabinet, and the disposal flange works loose from years of vibration. The wet part is the one we fix. A cracked composite or porcelain bowl is a replacement, and we will say so.

    Supply Line and Angle Stop Replacement in Topeka, KS

    Braided supply lines have a rubber core that ages. After five to ten years the core can split without warning, and old multi-turn stops often seize open. We swap both in one visit. Quarter-turn stops close in one motion.

    Garbage Disposal Repair in Topeka

    A disposal that hums but will not spin is usually jammed. From below we turn the flywheel with a 1/4 inch hex key, hit the reset button and look for a bent impeller. A leaking unit is another matter. A cracked housing means a new disposal, usually an InSinkErator or a Waste King.

    P-Trap Repair and Replacement in Topeka, KS

    The trap holds a plug of water that keeps sewer gas out of the room. Most codes want 2 to 4 inches of trap seal, and a leaking or cut-down trap can lose it and let sewer odor into the house. We replace bad traps with a matched kit. Chrome traps that have rusted thin get swapped for PVC or brass.

    Slow or Clogged Sink Drain Repair in Topeka, KS

    Slow drains almost always clog at the trap or the first few feet past the wall. Kitchen clogs are grease and food that cooled and stuck, and bathroom clogs are hair wound around the stopper with soap scum holding it together. We clear it with a hand auger. Hydro-jetting is for main lines, not sink arms.

    Faucet Base Leak Repair in Topeka

    Water pooling around the faucet base is a different leak than a drip from the spout. It usually means worn O-rings on a swivel spout or a failed base gasket, and on some faucets it means the body has cracked and the whole faucet needs replacing. Each faucet style rebuilds its own way. We identify yours before ordering parts.

    Emergency Sink Repair in Topeka, KS
