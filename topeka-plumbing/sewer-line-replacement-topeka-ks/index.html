    Trenchless Sewer Line Replacement in Topeka, KS

    To minimize disruption, trenchless sewer line replacement replaces pipes without extensive digging. We offer pipe bursting, where a new pipe is pulled through the old one, fracturing the old pipe outward. This is ideal when protecting landscaping or driveways is a priority. The method works well for many homes in Topeka and can often be finished faster than traditional methods, using high-density polyethylene (HDPE) pipe known for its long life.

    Emergency Sewer Line Replacement in Topeka, KS

    Sewer line emergencies demand immediate attention. A complete sewer line collapse or severe blockage can cause raw sewage to back up into your property. KRV Plumbing offers emergency sewer line replacement across Topeka, KS, responding quickly to limit damage and restore your system.     Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Topeka

    Cast iron drains in older homes corrode from the inside, particularly along the bottom of the pipe. Over time this leaves cracks, holes and a rough surface that catches waste. Replacing the cast iron sewer outside the home is often the first step. We can also evaluate under-slab cast iron and discuss lining or rerouting choices. New PVC or HDPE will not rust or scale the same way.

    Open-Trench Sewer Replacement in Topeka, KS

    Traditional excavation is sometimes the best option, especially when the old line has a severe belly or the slope must be corrected. We dig along the line, remove the old pipe and set new PVC on compacted bedding at the correct grade. Rocky or hard-packed soil can slow digging, and we account for that in the estimate. Each joint is glued and checked before backfill. The inspector sees the open trench before we cover it.

    KRV Plumbing focuses on residential sewer line replacement for Topeka, KS homeowners.     Partial Sewer Line Repair in Topeka, KS

    Not every damaged sewer needs full replacement. If the camera shows a single break or one root-damaged joint, a spot repair may solve the problem. We locate the exact spot and depth, excavate a small area and replace that section. This costs much less than replacing the whole lateral. We only recommend it when the rest of the line is in good condition.

    Clay and Orangeburg Line Replacement in Topeka, KS

    Clay pipe joints let roots in, and Orangeburg fiber pipe flattens and blisters with age. Both are near the end of their service life in many older properties. When the camera shows multiple failures, replacement is typically the best value. We remove or burst the old line and install modern pipe with a long expected service life. You get a smooth, root-resistant line to the street.
