    Commercial and Multi-Unit Sump Installations in Wichita

    Office buildings, parking structures and multi-unit properties with below-grade levels often need duplex sump systems with two pumps and an alternating control panel. The panel switches lead and lag pumps on each cycle so wear stays even, and a high water alarm notifies building staff. We coordinate electrical with your electrician and follow manufacturer specs for basin size and float settings. Property managers get a simple maintenance schedule to keep both pumps exercised.

    Pedestal Sump Pumps in Wichita

    Pedestal pumps keep the motor above the pit, which makes them easier to service and a solid fit for narrow pits in older homes. They usually last a long time because the motor never sits in water. The tradeoff is more noise and a less finished look, so we usually recommend them for utility rooms rather than living spaces. We install them with a rigid discharge, a quality check valve and a float rod adjusted to your pit depth.

    Discharge Line and Check Valve Installation in Wichita

    A great pump is useless if the water it moves runs right back toward the foundation. We route rigid PVC discharge pipe out of the basement and extend it at least 10 feet from the house or into a pop-up emitter or dry well. A spring or swing check valve keeps the column of water from dropping back into the pit after every cycle, which saves wear on the motor. Many Kansas homeowners let faucets drip and open cabinet doors on the coldest nights to keep water moving. Where lines can freeze, we add a freeze-resistant outlet so ice cannot block the discharge.

    Sump Pump Basin and Discharge Line Installation in Wichita

    A complete sump pump system requires a properly installed basin and discharge line. We handle the excavation and placement of the sump basin, setting it at the lowest point of your basement floor so it collects water well. We also install the discharge line, ensuring it is correctly sloped, sealed and routed away from your foundation to a suitable drainage area, so water does not find its way back into your Wichita property.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Wichita

    Power outages during heavy storms can render a primary sump pump useless, leading to basement flooding. A battery backup sump pump provides vital protection when the power goes out. We install dependable backup systems that activate automatically, keeping water moving even without electricity. This is an important safeguard for homes in Wichita that lose power during storms, offering peace of mind in severe weather. We integrate these systems seamlessly with your existing plumbing.

    New Sump Pit Excavation in Wichita

    If your basement has never had a pump, we start by locating the low point where water collects. We saw cut the slab, excavate a pit roughly 18 to 24 inches wide and 24 to 30 inches deep, and set a perforated or solid liner depending on whether we are collecting groundwater or surface seepage. Gravel goes around the liner to help water flow in while keeping fine soil out. We then patch the slab flush with new concrete so the floor stays clean and level.

    Sump Pump Installation Cost Comparison in Wichita, KS

    Replacing a pump in an existing pit is the least expensive option, typically a few hundred dollars plus the pump. Digging a new pit through the slab adds labor for saw cutting, excavation and concrete patching. Adding a battery backup raises the total but often costs less than one cleanup after a flooded basement. We give you an itemized quote so you can compare a basic primary pump against a full primary and backup system before we start.
