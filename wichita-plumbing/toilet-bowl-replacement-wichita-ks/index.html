    Flange Repair During Bowl Replacement in Wichita, KS

    The flange is the ring that anchors the bowl to the drain, and the only time anyone sees it is when the old bowl comes off. Look at it then or not at all. A cracked or sunken flange gets repaired before the new bowl goes down, since a new bowl on a bad flange will rock and leak within months.

    Pressure-Assist Toilet Bowl Replacement in Wichita

    Pressure-assist toilets use a sealed vessel inside the tank, and the bowl is matched to that vessel's flush rate, so a bowl-only swap means finding the exact paired model. Some bowls are not sold on their own. When the matching bowl is gone, a full toilet replacement is the better route, and we will price both.

    Bowl Replacement Versus Full Toilet Replacement in Wichita, KS

    Bowl-only swaps make sense when the tank is newer and the bowl is still made, since a matched bowl often costs $100 to $350 while a new basic toilet starts around $200. The labor is about the same for both. If the tank is old or the model is discontinued, a full replacement usually wins, and we show you both prices.

    Tank-to-Bowl Leak Repair and Replacement in Wichita

    Drips from the bolts between the tank and bowl usually mean a flattened gasket or rusted bolts, but sometimes the porcelain around a bolt hole has cracked. We look closely before quoting. For a worn gasket, a new bolt kit fixes it, and a cracked bolt hole means a new bowl with stainless or brass hardware.

    Toilet Bowl Replacement for Older Homes in Wichita, KS

    Houses built before the 1970s often have a lead closet bend or a cast iron flange caulked with oakum, and those need gentle handling when the bowl comes off. Old cast iron can crack if you pry on it. We avoid prying on the flange and use repair rings or flange adapters made for cast iron, so the drain stays intact.

    Chipped or Stained Bowl Replacement in Wichita, KS

    Deep chips in the glaze collect bacteria, and hard water rings that have etched the surface will not come out with any cleaner. A new bowl fixes both. We match the color to your tank, which is easy for white and harder for biscuit or bone fixtures that have faded over the years.

    Brands We Match in Wichita

    American Standard, Kohler, TOTO and Gerber sell bowls separately for many two-piece models, but the tank bolts, gasket style and bolt spacing have to match exactly. Close is not good enough. We confirm the pairing from the model number before ordering, which avoids a second trip.

    Cracked or Damaged Toilet Bowl Replacement in Wichita

    Porcelain that cracks below the waterline leaks every time the bowl refills, and the water usually runs down inside the base where you will not see it for weeks. Even a thin crack will leak. We pull the bowl, check the flange and the subfloor, then set a matching bowl on a fresh seal.
