    Dependable Trenchless Sewer Line Repair Services in Wichita, KS

    Cured-In-Place Pipe Relining in Wichita, KS

    CIPP relining creates a new pipe within an existing damaged sewer line. A resin-saturated felt tube is inserted into the old pipe and then inflated, curing in place to form a smooth, durable and watertight liner. This technique is excellent for repairing cracks, leaks and minor offsets without needing to dig up the entire line. It is a quick and effective solution for many common sewer problems faced by Kansas homeowners.

    Pipe Bursting in Wichita, KS

    Pipe bursting replaces a badly damaged line by pulling a bursting head through the old pipe. The head breaks the old pipe outward while new HDPE pipe follows behind it. Bursting is best for collapsed or severely offset lines that cannot be lined. It usually requires an entry pit and an exit pit.

    Perma-Liner and NuFlow Trenchless Technologies in Wichita

    KRV Plumbing uses proven trenchless technologies from trusted brands like Perma-Liner and NuFlow. These systems are known for their durability, effectiveness and adherence to plumbing standards. Using these materials and equipment means your trenchless sewer line repair in Wichita is not only minimally invasive but also a high-quality, long-lasting solution, backed by manufacturer warranties.

    Pipe Bursting Sewer Repair in Wichita

    Pipe bursting is a trenchless method where a new pipe is pulled through the old one while the old pipe is fractured outward. This creates a new, seamless and often larger diameter sewer line without the need for long trenching. It is ideal for severely damaged or collapsed pipes in Wichita that require full replacement rather than just relining.

    Cured-In-Place Pipe Lining in Wichita

    Cured-in-place lining creates a new pipe inside the old one. We clean the line, then pull or invert a resin-saturated liner into place and cure it with hot water, steam or UV light. The result is a seamless, jointless pipe that roots cannot penetrate. Lining works well for cracked or root-intruded lines that still hold their form.

    Sewer Camera Inspections for Trenchless Repair in Wichita

    Before any trenchless repair, a detailed sewer camera inspection is essential. Our high-definition cameras travel through your sewer lines, providing real-time video of the pipe's interior. This allows us to accurately diagnose the problem, identify its exact location and determine the most appropriate trenchless method, whether it is pipe bursting or CIPP. This diagnostic step makes for a precise and lasting repair in Wichita.

    Root Intrusion Repair in Wichita, KS

    Tree and shrub roots go after moisture in sewer joints. Once inside, they grow into dense masses that cause repeat backups. We cut roots with a mechanical cutter or hydro jet, then line the pipe to seal the joints. That breaks the cycle of cleaning the line every few months.
