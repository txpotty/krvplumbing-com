    Diagnosing Low Water Pressure in Wichita

    Low water pressure from a well system can be a frustrating problem, often indicating issues beyond just the pump itself. This could be due to a failing pressure switch, a partially clogged well screen, a leak in the supply line, or even a pump that is no longer meeting demand. Our team conducts careful diagnostics to pinpoint the exact cause of low pressure in your Wichita home, ensuring a targeted and effective repair solution.

    Pressure Switch Replacement in Wichita

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop. Pitted contacts, clogged sensing tubes and insect damage are frequent causes of failure. We replace the switch, clean or replace the tube and set the cut-in and cut-out pressures to match your tank. This is one of the most budget-friendly well repairs.
