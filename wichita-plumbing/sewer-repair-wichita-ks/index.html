    Sewer Vent Pipe Repair in Wichita, KS

    A plugged vent makes drains gurgle and traps run dry. Then the sewer smell shows up.     Sewer Pipe Replacement Options in Wichita, KS

    Replacement makes sense when the camera shows failures every few feet. Spot repairs will not keep up. We price open trench replacement, pipe bursting and full-length lining side by side for your Kansas property, showing what each method costs and what it does to the yard.     Orangeburg and Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Wichita

    Orangeburg is wood fiber soaked in coal tar. It flattens with age. Cast iron from the same era rusts from the inside, builds scale and eventually splits along the bottom where the sewage runs, so by the time a camera shows widespread damage, spot repairs are a poor investment. PVC goes in, usually end to end. Trenchless bursting is often an option.

    Bellied Sewer Line Correction in Wichita, KS

    A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged and holds standing water. Waste collects in the dip. Slab leaks and broken sewer laterals are common results of shifting clay, especially after long dry spells followed by heavy rain. We measure the depth of standing water on camera, dig out that section, re-bed the pipe on compacted gravel and reset the slope to the quarter inch per foot most codes call for on residential laterals. A shallow belly sometimes just needs regular cleaning.

    After-Hours Sewer Repair in Wichita

    Main lines like to fail on holidays. Our phones stay on. An after-hours call gets a tech with a cable machine and a camera, which is enough to restore flow in most cases and to tell you if you have a clog or a broken pipe. If it is structural, we schedule the dig for the earliest safe window. We avoid digging blind at night near gas and electric lines.

    Cracked and Broken Pipe Repair in Wichita

    Clay and cast iron crack from settling soil, tree roots and vehicles parked over the line. The crack lets soil in and sewage out. We excavate at the marked spot, cut out the damaged length and install new Schedule 40 PVC with shielded stainless couplings rated for the transition between materials, then run water through it and check it on camera before backfill. Most of these are one-day jobs.

    Root Intrusion Repair in Wichita, KS

    Cutting roots treats the symptom. The joint is the real problem. After clearing the line, we look at each rooted joint on camera and decide between a short sectional liner, a full liner or cutting in new pipe, based on the number of bad joints and the spacing between them. One or two joints is a small job. Ten or twelve usually justifies lining the run.
