    Whether gas or electric, your hot water tank benefits significantly from annual maintenance.     Leak Detection and Slab Leak Checks in Wichita

    A lot of Kansas housing dates from the mid-century building boom, with galvanized supply lines and cast iron drains. Soil movement and hard water can wear buried or under-slab water lines until they leak. We check your water meter for hidden flow, test fixture valves and use acoustic and thermal tools to find leaks. Warm spots on floors, a running meter and unexplained high water bills are common signs. Finding a leak early prevents structural damage and mold.

    Gas Line Inspection and Repair in Wichita

    Gas lines feed water heaters, furnaces, ranges, dryers and outdoor grills or fire pits. We inspect visible gas piping, test connections for leaks and check that appliance shutoffs work. Flexible connectors that are old or kinked get replaced. Any gas repair is done to local code with pressure testing where required. If you ever smell gas, leave the home and call your gas utility right away.

    Sump Pump Maintenance in Wichita, KS
