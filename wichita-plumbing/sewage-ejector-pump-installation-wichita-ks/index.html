    Frequently Asked Questions About Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Wichita

    The cost for sewage ejector pump installation in Wichita usually ranges from $2,500 to $5,000.     New ejector installations typically require a plumbing permit and inspection. Straight pump replacements may not, and we confirm local requirements for each job.

    Yes, in most cases a plumbing permit from the City of Wichita is required for sewage ejector pump installation. This ensures the work complies with local building and plumbing codes. KRV Plumbing will assist you with securing the necessary permits and make sure all work passes inspection.

    A sump pump is designed to remove groundwater from a basement or crawl space, preventing flooding. A sewage ejector pump, however, is built specifically to handle wastewater containing solids from toilets, sinks and showers in areas below the main sewer line. They are not interchangeable and serve different purposes.

    We mainly install high-quality, reputable brands such as Liberty Pumps and Zoeller. These manufacturers are known for their durability, reliability and efficient performance, ensuring a long-lasting solution for your home. We can discuss the best brand and model for your needs.

    Signs that you might need a new sewage ejector pump include unusual noises from the pump or pit, frequent cycling on and off, slow drainage from basement fixtures or a sewage odor coming from the area. If you notice any of these issues, it is best to have a professional inspect your system to prevent a complete failure and costly backups.

    With proper installation and regular maintenance, a high-quality sewage ejector pump can last 7 to 15 years. Factors such as usage frequency, the type of waste it handles and water quality can affect its lifespan. Annual inspections can help maximize its longevity.

    Avoid flushing wipes, paper towels, feminine products, grease and cat litter. These items clog pumps and cause early failure.
