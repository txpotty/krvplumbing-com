    Low Shower Pressure Repair in Wichita, KS

    Low pressure at the shower with good pressure at the sink narrows it down fast.     Weak Shower Spray Repair in Wichita, KS

    Most weak showers come down to the head. Scale packs the nozzles and the restrictor screen behind them, and the spray drops off a little at a time until one day it feels like a garden mister. A soak can bring it back. If the cartridge is clogged too, we replace it and test the flow again.

    Showerhead Repair and Replacement in Wichita

    Showerheads clog from the inside out. Minerals collect behind the face and inside the restrictor, which turns the spray patchy or weak with the valve fully open. A vinegar soak can work. Otherwise we put on a new head with rubber nozzles that rub clean with your thumb.
