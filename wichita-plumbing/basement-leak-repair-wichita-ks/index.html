    How We Repair Basement Leaks in Wichita, KS

    Foundation Crack Repair in Wichita

    Hairline cracks in a poured wall are normal and usually dry, but a crack that seeps after rain needs attention. Slab leaks and broken sewer laterals are common results of shifting clay, especially after long dry spells followed by heavy rain. We inject leaking cracks from the inside with polyurethane foam, which expands into the crack and stays flexible as the wall moves. Most injections take an hour or two.

    Wall Leak Solutions in Wichita, KS

    Damp walls without a crack often point to water soaking through porous block or concrete. A simple foil test tells you whether it is seepage or condensation: tape a square of foil to the wall for a day and see which side gets wet. Condensation needs a dehumidifier. Seepage needs drainage outside or a channel inside.

    Interior Drainage Systems in Wichita

    Where digging outside is not practical, we handle the water from inside instead. We cut the slab along the perimeter, lay perforated pipe in washed stone, and slope it to a new or existing sump pit before patching the floor with fresh concrete. The wall gets a dimpled membrane. Seepage runs down behind it and into the pipe.

    New Sump Pump Installation in Wichita

    Installing a new sump means breaking out a section of slab, setting a lined pit, and running the discharge out through the rim joist. Most of our installs get a cast iron pump, a check valve and 1-1/2 inch discharge. Discharge cannot go into the sewer in most places. It has to discharge outside, far enough from the foundation that it does not cycle back into the pit, often ten feet or more.

    Hydrostatic Pressure Management in Wichita, KS

    Water pressing against the outside of a basement wall finds the weakest point, usually the cove joint where wall meets floor. Flash flooding can push water into basements and crawlspaces, so sump pumps and good grading matter for many homes. An interior drain channel along that joint relieves the pressure by giving the water a path to the sump. No pressure, no seepage.

    Clogged Drain Tile Repair in Wichita

    Footing drains clog with silt and roots over the years, and once they stop flowing the water table at the wall rises. A camera run from the sump inlet shows us where the tile is plugged. A jetter usually opens it. Crushed clay tile has to be dug up and replaced with perforated PVC or corrugated pipe in a sock.
