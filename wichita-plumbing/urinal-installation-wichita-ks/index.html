    Waterless Urinal Installation in Wichita

    Waterless urinals save the most water and need the most discipline. They use a cartridge or a layer of sealing liquid that lets urine pass while blocking sewer gas, and the cartridge has to be swapped on schedule or the restroom starts to smell. Your custodial team needs training. We show them how.

    Residential Urinal Installation in Wichita, KS

    Basement and garage bathrooms are where most home urinals go. A 2 inch drain, a vent tie-in and a supply sized to the valve go in, with blocking or a compact carrier. Many use 0.125 gallons per flush. That barely touches the water bill.

    Residential Urinal Plumbing in Wichita, KS

    Home urinals use the same rules as commercial ones. Expect a 2 inch drain, a built-in trap, a vent and a supply sized to the flush valve. Permit rules apply too. We check that first.

    Flush Valve Urinal Installation in Wichita, KS

    Flush valve urinals are the commercial standard. Tankless flushometers deliver a set volume from a 3/4 inch line and recover instantly for the next user. We set the volume to the bowl. Wrong settings stain bowls.

    Rough-In Plumbing for Urinals in Wichita, KS

    Rough-in decides whether the finish goes smoothly. We set the carrier, run a 2 inch waste line to the outlet height on the spec sheet, stub the 3/4 inch supply where the flush valve will land, and tie the vent in before anyone hangs drywall. Inspectors see it open. Then the walls close.

    Home Gym and Garage Urinals in Wichita, KS

    A garage urinal saves tracking dirt into the house. The drain ties to the nearest stack, a vent or AAV goes in where allowed, and a compact bowl fits the space. Freeze protection matters in garages. Ask us about the layout.

    Flushometer Urinal Installation in Wichita
