    Crawl Space Encapsulation in Wichita

    Encapsulation seals the whole crawl space, walls and floor, with a reinforced poly liner. We run 12 to 20 mil liner up the walls, tape every seam, seal around piers and pipe penetrations, and often add a dehumidifier to keep relative humidity below 60 percent. Strong winds and dry spells can draw moisture out of the soil, while sudden cold fronts can drop temperatures sharply overnight. The joists stop rotting. Drier air under the floor also means less musty air upstairs.

    Commercial Walls Waterproofing in Wichita

    Commercial basements, elevator pits and mechanical rooms in Wichita take on water the same way houses do, just with more at stake. One wet electrical room can close a business for days. We schedule around operating hours, work with property managers on access, and follow the waterproofing specs written into the building plans for below-grade walls. We keep downtime short.

    Exterior Block Wall Waterproofing in Wichita, KS

    White crust on block or stem walls is efflorescence, salt left behind as water moves through the masonry and dries. The usual cause is a sprinkler head aimed at the house instead of the bed, or soil graded toward the foundation. We fix the water first. After that the wall gets cleaned, dried and coated with a breathable masonry sealer or elastomeric coating.

    Foundation Crack Repair & Sealing in Wichita

    Hairline cracks in poured concrete walls leak more water than their size suggests. We inject them with low-pressure polyurethane, which expands to fill the crack from the inside face to the soil side, or with epoxy when the crack also needs structural bonding. Horizontal cracks are different. They can mean the wall is bowing from soil pressure, so we check for movement with a level before we seal anything. Most vertical cracks get sealed in one visit.

    Sump Pump Installation and Repair in Wichita, KS

    A sump pump is the part of an interior system that actually moves the water. We set a lidded basin below slab level, install a cast iron pump sized to the water coming in, and add a battery backup or water-powered backup for the night the power goes out during a storm. Check valves matter. A missing check valve lets the discharge line drain back into the pit and the pump short-cycles. The discharge line gets routed far enough out that it drains away from the house.

    Basement Wall Sealing in Wichita, KS

    Concrete block is porous, and its hollow cores fill with water like a sponge. Strong winds and dry spells can pull moisture out of the soil, while sudden cold fronts can drop temperatures sharply overnight. We drill weep holes at the bottom course to drain the cores, then coat the face with a masonry waterproofing product that lets vapor out but stops liquid water. The musty smell usually fades within weeks. Those weep holes empty into the interior drain if the house has one.

    Interior Walls Waterproofing in Wichita, KS

    Interior drain tile handles water that already got through the wall. We cut a channel in the slab along the perimeter, lay perforated pipe in washed stone, and tie it into a sump pit so the pump can push the water outside. The wall still gets wet. The difference is that the water no longer ends up on your floor. This route costs less than digging outside and works where a driveway or deck blocks the wall.

    Shower Wall Waterproofing in Wichita

    Tile and grout are not waterproof. Water passes through grout every time you shower, so the layer behind the tile, a membrane over cement board or a foam board system, is what actually keeps the studs dry. We tear out failed tile, install new backer, and apply Schluter Kerdi, RedGard or Hydro Ban at the thickness the manufacturer calls for. We reinforce corners, niches and valve penetrations with fabric or preformed pieces.
