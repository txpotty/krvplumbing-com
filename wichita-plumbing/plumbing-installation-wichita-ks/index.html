    Sump Pump and Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Wichita

    Basements and crawl spaces that take on water need a sump pump, and fixtures below the sewer line need an ejector. They are different pumps for different water, and one cannot stand in for the other. We size before we sell. We match pump capacity to how fast the pit fills, put the pump on its own circuit and run the discharge with a check valve.

    PEX Repiping in Wichita

    Copper repipes cost more and take longer, which is why most whole-house repipes today use PEX-A or PEX-B tubing with brass or poly fittings. It runs quieter than copper, with less water hammer banging in the walls. It also handles a freeze better. Hard freezes happen every winter in Kansas, and pipes in exterior walls, garages, and crawlspaces are the most at risk. We use fittings matched to the tubing brand.

    Whole-House Water Softener Installation in Wichita

    Water across much of Kansas runs quite hard, leaving mineral scale in water heaters, faucets, showerheads, and dishwashers. Softener sizing comes down to hardness times daily gallons, which tells us how many grains the resin has to remove between regenerations. A unit sized too small regenerates constantly and wastes salt. The drain line gets a proper air gap. Programming follows, set to your hardness, and we run one test cycle.

    Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Wichita, KS

    A basement bathroom sits below the sewer line, so gravity cannot carry the waste out. An ejector pump in a sealed basin lifts it up to the main drain. We size the pump and basin to the fixtures it serves, vent the basin to code, and put a check valve and a full-port shutoff on the discharge so the pump can be pulled for service. We usually install Liberty or Zoeller pumps.

    Water Service Line Installation in Wichita, KS

    New water service lines run from the meter to the house, and the old one usually comes out in the same trench. Much of Kansas has expansive clay soil that swells when wet and shrinks in drought, which can move foundations and stress buried pipes. The trenching equipment we bring depends on what we find on site. We add a new main shutoff at the entry point. Depth and bedding protect the pipe. Pressure and flow get tested before the trench is closed.

    PEX Pipe Installation and Repiping in Wichita

    Pinhole leaks in copper keep coming back once the pipe walls start to thin. Repiping in PEX stops the cycle. We run either a manifold with home runs to each fixture, which balances pressure, or a trunk-and-branch layout when the walls make home runs impractical. Access holes are kept small and patched, or left square and clean for your drywall contractor.

    Water Heater Replacement in Wichita

    Most tank heaters give out between 8 and 12 years, sooner with heavy sediment. Our stock covers 40 and 50 gallon tanks and 75 gallon units for larger families, and each install includes new connectors, a code relief line and a vent check. We also check whether you need an expansion tank. One visit covers most installs.

    Water Heater Installation in Wichita
