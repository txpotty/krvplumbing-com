    Copper Repiping in Derby, KS

    Some homeowners prefer copper for its long track record and rigidity. We install Type L copper, which has a thicker wall than the Type M used in many older homes. Joints are soldered with lead-free solder or pressed with ProPress fittings. Copper repipes typically cost more and take a bit longer than PEX.

    Emergency Repipe Services in Derby, KS

    Slab Leak Reroutes in Derby

    When one line under the slab leaks, a full repipe is not always the first step. We can reroute a single hot or cold branch overhead and abandon the leaking pipe in the slab. This is a good option for newer homes or a first-time leak. If the home has had multiple leaks, we will explain why a full repipe may save money.

    Slab Leak Repiping Solutions in Derby

    Slab leaks are a serious and damaging problem for homes built on concrete foundations. Much of Kansas has expansive clay soil that swells when wet and shrinks in drought, which can move foundations and stress buried pipes. These leaks often require rerouting or repiping entire sections of your plumbing system to bypass the compromised pipes under the slab. Our repipe specialists use advanced detection tools to pinpoint slab leaks and then reroute new PEX or copper lines through your attic or walls, completely bypassing the problematic underground sections. This prevents further foundation damage and restores water service without breaking your slab.

    Condo, Townhome and Multi-Family Repipes in Derby

    Shared walls and HOA rules make repiping attached homes more complex. We coordinate with HOAs, schedule water shutoffs and protect neighboring units. For property managers we offer building-wide repipes done in phases to keep residents at home. Each unit gets clear communication and daily cleanup.

    PEX-A Whole-Home Repiping in Derby

    PEX-A tubing is flexible, resists scale buildup and handles thermal expansion well, which suits hard water. We use Uponor or similar PEX-A with cold expansion fittings that open up the full pipe diameter. Long continuous runs mean fewer joints in walls and attics. Most homes are repiped with PEX in two to four days.

    Kitec and Polybutylene Replacement in Derby

    Kitec, with its orange or blue PEX-AL-PEX pipe and brass fittings, and gray polybutylene pipe are both known for failures. Many homes built in the 1980s through early 2000s contain one or the other. We replace these systems completely so your home is easier to insure and sell. Visible pipe at the water heater often tells us which type you have.
