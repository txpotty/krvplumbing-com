    What Our Repipe Crews Do in Derby, KS

    Repiping for Hard Water in Derby, KS

    Many Kansas homeowners use water softeners to extend the life of their tank water heaters and fixtures against heavy mineral buildup. Hard water leaves calcium and magnesium scale inside pipe, and over years it narrows the bore and speeds up corrosion in metal lines. Pressure suffers. For homes with hard water, we usually recommend PEX-A, which resists scale and does not corrode, and we can add a softener loop or a whole-house filter during the repipe. Adding the loop now costs less than later.

    Slab Leak Reroutes in Derby

    When one line under the slab leaks, a full repipe is not always the first step. Rerouting one branch overhead and leaving the bad pipe in the slab is often cheaper than both a repipe and a jackhammer repair. This works well for a first leak. If the house has had several leaks, we will walk through why a full repipe usually costs less over time.

    Urgent Repipes After Repeated Failures in Derby, KS

    A widespread pipe failure sometimes forces a repipe sooner than you planned. The first step is temporary repairs so you have water, and then we book the repipe as quickly as the permit and schedule allow. We move fast. Even urgent repipes usually take just two to four days after we begin.

    PEX Versus Copper Repipe Pricing in Derby, KS

    PEX repipes usually cost less than copper because the material is cheaper and installation goes faster. Copper costs more. Copper commonly costs 20 to 40 percent more than PEX, and some owners prefer it anyway for its long history. Our quotes for either include permit, fittings, patching and cleanup.

    Slab Leak Repiping in Derby

    A slab leak shows up as a warm spot on the floor, a running meter or a wet patch of carpet with no visible cause. Basements are common in the eastern part of Kansas, while slab and crawlspace homes are more typical in many other areas. Instead of breaking concrete to patch one pipe, we reroute new supply lines overhead through the attic and down interior walls, then abandon the old lines in the slab. No jackhammer needed. It ends this leak and takes the rest of the slab plumbing out of the picture.

    Galvanized Pipe Replacement in Derby, KS

    Galvanized steel rusts from the inside, and over decades the inside diameter closes down to a fraction of what it was. Pressure drops slowly. Rust-colored water in the morning, poor pressure upstairs and dripping threaded joints all point to failing galvanized. We replace it with PEX or copper. Owners usually notice stronger flow the first day.

    Kitec and Polybutylene Replacement in Derby

    Kitec, an orange or blue PEX-AL-PEX pipe with brass fittings, and gray polybutylene are both known for failures. Both still turn up in older homes. The pipe at the water heater usually tells us which one you have, and we replace the full system so the home is easier to insure and to sell.

    Copper Repiping in Derby, KS

    Some homeowners prefer copper for its long track record and rigidity. We install Type L copper, which has a thicker wall than the Type M used in many older homes, with lead-free solder or ProPress fittings. Expect a slightly longer job. Copper repipes usually cost more and take a day longer than PEX, mostly because every turn needs a fitting.
