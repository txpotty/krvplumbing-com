    Double Check Valve Assembly Installation in Derby

    Double check assemblies are used for low hazard connections such as many fire sprinkler systems and some irrigation lines. They have two spring-loaded check valves and test cocks for annual certification. Unlike an RPZ, they do not discharge water, so they can be installed in vaults or below grade where local rules permit. We size them to avoid excessive pressure loss on your system.

    Backflow Enclosures and Freeze Protection in Derby, KS

    Above-ground assemblies sit in direct sun in summer and can freeze on cold nights. Sudden arctic fronts can drop temperatures quickly, so disconnecting hoses and protecting outdoor spigots before winter is a smart routine. We install insulated enclosures, freeze blankets or heated cabinets based on your location. Enclosures also discourage theft, since brass backflow assemblies are a common target for scrap thieves. We choose enclosures with enough room for testing and service.
