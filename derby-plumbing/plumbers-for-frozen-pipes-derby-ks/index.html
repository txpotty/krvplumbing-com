    Frozen Water Line Diagnosis in Derby, KS

    Finding the frozen spot takes the most skill. Thermal cameras make it faster. By checking which fixtures work and which do not, then scanning walls and ceilings with the camera, we can usually narrow the ice to a few feet of pipe and thaw it without cutting into the wall.

    Heat Cable for Exposed Pipes in Derby

    Some pipes cannot be rerouted or insulated enough on their own, like a supply line under a manufactured home or a pipe through an unheated porch. Heat cable handles those. We replace old constant-wattage tape with listed self-regulating cable.

    Thawing Drain Lines and Sewer Pipes in Derby

    Drain lines can freeze too, usually where a line runs through an unheated crawlspace or an exterior wall. A frozen drain backs up. Hot water through the cleanout thaws it, and then we check for a sag that holds water.

    Water Heater Line Thawing and Repair in Derby

    Water heaters in garages or attics have exposed supply lines that can freeze. Tankless units are especially vulnerable. Freeze protection on tankless units needs power, so after an outage we check the heat exchanger and connectors.

    Pipe Insulation and Heat Cable Installation in Derby, KS

    Insulation slows heat loss, but it does not add heat. For truly exposed runs, we add heat cable.
