    A French drain collects water that soaks into soil and is best along foundations and low areas. A channel drain catches water flowing across hard surfaces like driveways and patios. Plenty of properties benefit from both.

    Drain snaking uses a flexible auger to mechanically break through or retrieve a blockage.     We regularly install and service quality sump pump brands such as Zoeller, Little Giant, Wayne, and Liberty Pumps. Our technicians can help you choose the best model for your specific needs, considering factors like horsepower, pump type, and reliability for Kansas weather conditions.

    Common signs include slow draining sinks or tubs, gurgling sounds from drains, foul odors coming from drains, water backing up into fixtures, and soggy spots in your yard. If you notice any of these, it's wise to call a professional plumber.
