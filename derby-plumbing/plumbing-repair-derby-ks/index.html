    Slow Drain Repair in Derby

    Gurgling toilets and a tub that drains slow often trace to a plugged roof vent or a sagging drain line. After clearing it, we camera the line and fix whatever is broken. Common fixes include replacing corroded cast iron, cutting out failed ABS joints, rebuilding a broken trap arm and resetting toilets on a new wax ring and flange. The goal is a drain you stop thinking about.

    Drain Repairs in Derby

    Slow drains that come back a week after snaking usually mean the pipe itself is damaged. Cracked cast iron or a sagging run will hold debris every time. We run a camera to find the spot, then replace that section or hydro-jet the line if buildup is the only problem. Grease is the usual buildup.

    Tank and Tankless Water Heater Repair in Derby

    Tank and tankless heaters fail in different ways. KRV Plumbing repairs both, including the major brands, gas and electric. Tank calls usually come down to the thermocouple, the gas valve, an element or the anode. On a tankless unit, the culprit is often scale in the heat exchanger or a dirty inlet filter, so we flush and clean before swapping parts.

    Under-Slab Leak Repair in Derby, KS

    A lot of Kansas housing dates from the mid-century building boom, with galvanized supply lines and cast iron drains. Copper or PEX under the slab can run for decades until a fitting or a rubbed spot gives out. Our leak tech pins down the spot with acoustic and thermal gear, and you get each repair option priced next to the others. Epoxy lining is one choice. A reroute through the attic or walls is often the cleaner one.

    Well Pump Repair in Derby, KS

    No water at the tap on a well system points to a short list of parts. It is usually the pressure switch, the capacitor in the control box, the pump motor or a waterlogged pressure tank. We check voltage and amp draw at the control box before pulling anything out of the well. A submersible pull takes two techs and a truck.

    Gas Pipe Repair in Derby, KS

    Leave the house first if you smell gas, and call the gas utility from outside. Once the utility clears it, we handle the repair. The system gets a pressure test, the leak gets traced with a detector and bubble solution, and the failed connector or pipe gets replaced. Nothing is relit until the test holds.

    Sewer Line Repair in Derby, KS

    Roots are the usual cause of a sewer line that backs up every few months. They find a cracked joint, grow inside and catch paper and grease. We camera the line to see the damage and mark the depth on the surface, then choose between an open dig and a trenchless method. Clay pipe usually goes first.

    Gas Line Leak Repair in Derby

    Gas work is the one job no homeowner should try. A sulfur smell or a hissing fitting means leave first and call from outside. KRV Plumbing finds and fixes gas leaks in Derby with a combustible gas detector, then pressure tests the line to code before the meter is turned back on. Flex connectors fail most often.
