    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Derby, KS

    Battery backups earn their keep during outages. Flash flooding can push water into basements and crawlspaces, so sump pumps and proper grading matter for many homes. We add a second pump in the same pit with its own float, wired to a sealed AGM battery on a smart charger that tests itself and beeps when the battery gets weak.     Discharge Line and Check Valve Installation in Derby

    Rigid PVC carries the water from the pump out through the rim joist and at least 10 feet from the house. A spring check valve stops the column from falling back into the pit, which cuts motor run time and the loud thunk you hear after every cycle. Sudden arctic fronts can drop temperatures fast, so disconnecting hoses and protecting outdoor spigots before winter is a smart routine. We fit a freeze-resistant outlet.

    High-Capacity Sump Pump Systems in Derby, KS

    Some lots sit in a high water table and fill a standard pit every few minutes during spring. Those basements need a 1/2 or 3/4 horsepower pump in a larger basin, all sized after we time how fast your pit refills. Timing the refill comes first.

    New Sump Pit Excavation in Derby

    No pit yet? First we locate the low point where water gathers, saw cut the slab, dig roughly 24 to 30 inches deep, and set a liner surrounded by washed gravel so water can enter while fine soil stays out. New concrete goes back flush and level.
