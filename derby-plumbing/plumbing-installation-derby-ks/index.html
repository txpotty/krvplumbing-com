    Expansion Tank and Sewage Ejector Installs in Derby, KS

    A closed water system has nowhere for heated water to expand, so pressure climbs every single time the heater cycles. An expansion tank absorbs that swing and keeps the relief valve from dripping. A PRV usually means you need one. For basement baths and guest suites below the sewer, we also install ejector systems with a sealed basin, check valve and vent.

    Sump Pump and Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Derby

    Basements and crawl spaces that take on water need a sump pump, and fixtures below the sewer line need an ejector. They are different pumps for different water, and one cannot stand in for the other. Sizing comes first. We match pump capacity to how fast the pit fills, put the pump on its own circuit and run the discharge with a check valve.

    Tank and Tankless Water Heater Installs in Derby

    Bradford White and Rheem both make good tanks, and the brand matters less than the fit. Demand, available space and the cost of gas versus electric at your address decide the model we recommend. Then we check the Kansas code items. On a closed system with a check valve or pressure reducing valve, an expansion tank is usually required, and we set it with the heater.

    Gas Line Installation for New Appliances in Derby, KS

    Water heaters, ranges, dryers, fireplaces and pool heaters all pull from the same gas service. We add up the BTU demand of everything on the line, measure the run, and size each branch so the furthest appliance still gets full pressure. New lines get pressure tested, always. Bigger loads sometimes need a meter upgrade, and we set that up with the gas utility.

    Water Heater Replacement in Derby

    Most tank heaters give out between 8 and 12 years, sooner with heavy sediment.     Natural Gas and Propane Lines in Derby, KS

    Natural gas and propane run at different pressures, and the pipe sizing changes with them. We size every run from the appliance BTU ratings and total pipe length, and use black iron or CSST depending on the path. CSST must be bonded. Skip the bond and a nearby lightning strike can arc through the tubing, which is one more reason gas work is not a weekend project.

    PEX Repiping in Derby

    Copper repipes cost more and take longer, which is why most whole-house repipes today use PEX-A or PEX-B tubing with brass or poly fittings. It runs quieter than copper, with less water hammer banging in the walls. It also handles a freeze better. Hard freezes happen every winter in Kansas, and pipes in exterior walls, garages, and crawlspaces are the most at risk. Fittings are matched to the tubing manufacturer.

    Water Line Installation and Replacement in Derby

    Corroded galvanized and brittle polybutylene are the two pipes we replace most. We run new lines in PEX or Type L copper, using crimp or expansion fittings on the PEX and soldered joints on the copper. Slab leaks and broken sewer laterals are common results of shifting clay, especially after long dry spells followed by heavy rain. We bed buried pipe in sand at the right depth so stones and settling soil cannot chafe it over time. Street pressure over 80 psi gets a PRV.
