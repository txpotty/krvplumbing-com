    Shower Diverter Repair in Derby, KS

    Diverters send water up to the head instead of out the tub spout. A worn spout gate or a tired diverter stem lets water keep running from the spout, and the head pressure drops off. Usually a new spout fixes it. Spout replacements take about twenty minutes.

    Hot Water Pressure Problems in Derby, KS

    Weak hot water with strong cold usually rules out the showerhead. It comes down to the hot side, whether that is a sediment-choked heater outlet or a cartridge with scale packed in the hot port. We test both sides. Good hot flow at the sinks points us straight at the shower cartridge.

    Shower Valve Replacement in Derby

    Temperature that swings or a handle that fights you points to the valve. If the valve body is pitted or parts no longer exist, we swap the valve, often through an access opening from the closet side. The new valve will have scald protection. Thermostatic valves are an option if you want exact temperature control.

    Shower Handle Leak Repair in Derby

    Leaks at the handle or behind the trim plate are easy to miss. Water leaking behind the trim slips into the wall cavity at the valve opening and soaks the drywall while the tile face stays dry. We pull the trim and look. It is usually a loose bonnet nut or a worn O-ring.

    Shower Temperature Swing Repair in Derby

    Cold blasts when a toilet flushes mean the pressure-balance spool is stuck. That spool is supposed to slide back and forth to keep the hot and cold mix even when pressure drops on one side, and scale can freeze it in place. A new cartridge fixes it. If you cannot get the water hot enough, the anti-scald limit stop may just need adjusting.

    Moen and Delta Shower Repair in Derby, KS

    Moen 1222 and Delta RP46074 cartridges sit inside a huge share of the showers we open. The Moen 1222 runs the Posi-Temp line, and the Delta RP46074 runs the Monitor 13 and 14 series, and both wear out on a schedule you can almost set a calendar by. We stock both. Many of these carry a lifetime warranty to the original owner.

    Tub and Shower Combo Repair in Derby, KS

    Tub and shower combos share one valve, one drain and a diverter between them. That means a worn diverter steals pressure from the head, a clogged tub drain slows the shower and a leaky valve shows up at both the spout and the head. We check all of it. The overflow gasket gets a look too, since it is a common hidden leak.

    Shower Drain Body Repair in Derby

    Drain bodies can crack or work loose under the pan. When that happens, water goes under the shower instead of down the pipe, and the first sign is often a soft spot in the floor next to the shower or a stain in the ceiling below. We clear and test the drain. Loose drains get resealed, and cracked ones get replaced.
