    Freestanding and Deck-Mount Tub Filler Installation in Derby

    Freestanding tubs need a floor-mount filler. Supplies have to rise through the floor, from a slab cut or a crawlspace, with a bracket anchored below. Deck fillers mount on the tub deck. Tub-only fillers need a temperature limit.

    Tub Faucet Style Upgrades in Derby

    Wall-mount tub fillers are a popular upgrade. A long-reach filler or a slide-bar handheld changes the tub, and both usually need a new valve or diverter. Rough-in gets planned before trim. The trim goes on last.

    Moen Tub Faucet Replacement in Derby

    Moen Posi-Temp valves use the 1222 cartridge. A worn 1222 causes drips or weak hot water, and replacing it takes about an hour with a puller. Moentrol valves use a different part. We carry both.

    Diverter Valve Repair and Replacement in Derby, KS

    Water running from the spout during a shower means a failing diverter. On a single-handle setup the diverter is usually the lift gate in the spout itself, and on a three-handle faucet it is the middle stem, and both wear out until water splits between the spout and the head. A new diverter spout takes minutes. Middle stems need a seat too.

    Tub Faucets in Older Homes in Derby

    Older homes hide odd tub plumbing. Expect rusted galvanized supplies, valves with no stops, and steel spout nipples that snap. Adapters for old pipe ride along. Replacing a short run of galvanized is common.

    Dripping Tub Faucet Replacement in Derby

    Drips at the spout come from inside the valve. On a single-handle faucet the cartridge seals have worn, and on a two- or three-handle faucet the stem washers and brass seats have worn, and either way the drip gets worse every month. New internals end the drip. Cracked bodies get a new valve.

    Hard Water Tub Faucet Replacement in Derby

    Water across much of Kansas runs hard, leaving mineral scale in water heaters, faucets, showerheads, and dishwashers. Scale packs into cartridges and spout diverters until handles stiffen and the diverter will not lift. Soaking and cleaning buys time on a lightly scaled valve, but once the brass is pitted or the cartridge has fused to the body, replacing the faucet is the only fix that lasts. One cartridge pull tells us.
