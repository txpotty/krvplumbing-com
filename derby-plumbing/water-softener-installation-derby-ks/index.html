    Long-Lasting Water Softener Installs in Derby, KS

    Whole-Home Water Softener Installation in Derby

    Whole-home softeners treat every gallon that enters the house, right after the main shutoff. Placing it on the main means the water heater, the dishwasher and every fixture get soft water, not just the kitchen. We size before we sell. Your test result and water use decide the tank size.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Installation in Derby

    Ion exchange is the only method that truly removes hardness from water. Every salt-based install includes a sized resin tank, a brine tank, a bypass and a drain to an air gap. Programming is the last step. Regeneration time is usually set for 2 a.m.

    Softener Loop Installation in Derby

    No loop? A loop is the plumbing that routes the main line through a softener, and many older homes were built without one. We add the loop. Outdoor taps can stay on hard water, which saves salt. Future service gets easier too.

    Fleck Water Softener Installation in Derby, KS

    Fleck valves, especially the 5600SXT and 7000SXT, are on a lot of the softeners we install. Their parts are easy to find and the valves can be rebuilt instead of replaced, so the system lasts. Settings match your test. We confirm the setup before leaving.

    Pelican Water Softener Installation in Derby, KS

    Pelican sells both salt-based softeners and salt-free conditioners, often direct to homeowners. If you bought a Pelican system for your home, we install it with secure connections, a proper bypass and the startup steps the manual lists for flushing the media. Skipping the flush causes trouble. We follow it step by step.

    High-Efficiency Water Softener Installation in Derby

    High-efficiency softeners use upflow brining and metered valves to cut salt and water use. A new metered unit often uses far less salt and water than an older model on a timer, which shows up on both bills. The savings add up. Several models are available.

    Water Hardness Testing and Programming in Derby

    Testing is what makes the settings right. We test for hardness, iron and chlorine before installing, then program the regeneration frequency and salt dose so you get soft water without wasting salt. Iron changes the math. You get a walkthrough of the settings.

    Water Softener Replacement & Upgrades in Derby, KS

    Upgrading an old timer-based softener is one of the easiest ways to cut salt use. Newer valves meter actual water use, some connect to Wi-Fi for salt alerts and higher-capacity tanks regenerate less often for larger households. Salt bills drop. We swap it out in a few hours.
