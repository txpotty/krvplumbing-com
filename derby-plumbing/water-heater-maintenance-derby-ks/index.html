    Tankless Water Heater Descaling in Derby

    Tankless units are particularly sensitive to hard water scale, which clogs the heat exchanger and triggers error codes. We isolate the unit with its service valves and circulate a descaling solution with a pump for the recommended time. We also clean the inlet water filter and check the unit's error history. Most manufacturers recommend descaling at least once a year in hard water areas. Regular service keeps flow rates and temperatures consistent.

    Standard Tank Water Heater Maintenance in Derby

    Our standard tank water heater maintenance includes flushing the tank to remove sediment buildup. Private wells serve many rural homes, and minerals, sediment, and nitrate levels make periodic well testing a good habit. We also inspect the anode rod, test the temperature and pressure relief valve, and check for any leaks or corrosion on the tank and connections. This service helps prevent early failure and improves heating efficiency, keeping your energy bills down.

    Maintenance Cost Versus Replacement in Derby, KS

    A water heater tune-up costs a fraction of a new unit, which can run well over a thousand dollars installed.     Electric Water Heater Element Service in Derby, KS
