    Toilet Brands We Install in Derby

    We install Kohler, TOTO, American Standard, Gerber and most other brands sold at supply houses and home centers. Every brand has its quirks. Some skirted TOTO models use a Unifit adapter, and certain Kohler tanks take a gasket that hardware stores rarely stock. We keep those parts on hand.

    Comfort-Height and ADA Toilets in Derby

    Aging in place usually starts in the bathroom. A taller toilet and a grab bar go a long way. If the drywall is already off, blocking gets added between studs so a grab bar can screw into wood, not hollow-wall anchors. We also center the bowl to ADA spacing.

    Comfort Height Toilet Installation in Derby, KS

    Comfort height bowls stand about two inches taller than standard ones. Think about who will use it before you buy. Shorter adults and kids sometimes find the taller seat leaves their feet dangling, while anyone with bad knees or a hip replacement usually finds it much easier to sit and stand. Installation is the same as a standard bowl.

    Standard Toilet Installation in Derby

    A standard floor-mount toilet is a two-hour job when the flange is sound. We shut off the stop, drain the tank and pull the old bowl. Once the old wax is scraped off, the flange gets checked for cracks, new stainless bolts go in, and the new bowl is lowered straight onto a fresh seal. The old one leaves with us.

    Wall-Hung Carrier Systems in Derby

    In-wall carriers from Geberit and Grohe each mount a little differently, and the instructions matter. The frame height chart from the maker gets followed to the letter. A frame set wrong puts the bowl too low or too high, and once tile is up there is no adjusting it. Measure twice here.

    New Construction Toilet Installation in Derby

    On new builds we set toilets after the finish floor is down, never before. The flange should sit on top of the finished floor. An early set means the tile goes around the flange, leaving it low, and that calls for an extender ring later. Each bowl gets a fresh seal.

    Wall-Hung Toilet Installation in Derby, KS

    Wall-hung toilets need a wall at least 2 by 6 deep, or a furred-out chase built in front of an existing 2 by 4 wall. The floor underneath stays open. Cleaning gets easier, and the bowl height can be set anywhere within the carrier range, useful for a tall family. Service happens through the flush plate.

    High-Efficiency Toilet Installation in Derby, KS

    High-efficiency toilets flush on 1.28 gallons or less, against 3.5 gallons or more for toilets from before the early 1990s. Check for the WaterSense label. We steer customers toward models with a MaP score of 800 grams or higher, because a cheap low-flow bowl that needs two flushes saves nothing. Dual-flush models add a lighter half flush.
