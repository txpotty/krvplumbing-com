    From Tank to Drain Field: Septic Repair in Lenexa, KS

    Repair Versus Replace in Lenexa, KS

    Most septic systems that fail can be repaired for a fraction of the cost of a new one. You see the repair and the replacement priced next to each other, with our honest pick for a house like yours. A new field is sometimes the right call. Often it is not.

    Inlet and Outlet Baffle Replacement in Lenexa, KS

    Baffles hold back the scum layer on top and the sludge on the bottom so only clear effluent leaves the tank. Concrete ones get eaten by sulfuric acid from sewer gas and eventually fall off into the tank. With no outlet baffle, sludge flows right out to the leach lines. We replace them with PVC sanitary tees. Usually a short job.

    Effluent Pump Replacement in Lenexa, KS

    Effluent pumps in a dosing chamber last roughly 8 to 15 years, and they usually quit without much warning at all. Humming with no flow points to a jammed impeller or a failed capacitor. We pull it and test the discharge line before setting a new one. Floats get replaced at the same time.

    Drain Field Repair in Lenexa, KS

    Green stripes over the trenches and a wet lawn in dry weather are the classic signs of a field in trouble. The cause is usually biomat, a black layer of slime that seals the soil. Compaction and hydraulic overload from a running toilet can do it too. Testing always comes first. Resting or jetting can bring a field back.

    Drain Field Line Jetting in Lenexa

    Distribution laterals clog from the inside with solids and slime, especially after years without pumping. A jetter goes into each lateral from the D-box or an end cleanout, and the water that comes back tells us how bad it is. Roots get cut. If the soil itself has sealed, jetting will not fix it, and we say so.

    Septic Line Clog Clearing in Lenexa

    A slow toilet and a gurgle in the tub usually point to a clog between the house and the tank. We run a camera down the line first, then clear it with a jetter set up for a 4-inch building sewer. Grease and wipes cause most of these, and the fix after clearing depends on which one we pull out. Roots mean a joint has opened.

    Septic Pump and Alarm Repair in Lenexa

    Pumped systems move effluent uphill or to a far field, and the pump sits in its own chamber with a float tree and an alarm. The alarm sounds because liquid climbed past the high float. Our tech meters the pump and tests each float before replacing anything. Zoeller and Liberty parts ride on the truck.

    Camera Inspection of Septic Baffles in Lenexa, KS

    Some tanks have no riser, so we run a push camera through the small inspection port to see the baffles. That saves digging up the whole lid. If the outlet tee is cracked or missing, we dig at that end only, then cut in a new PVC tee and an effluent filter housing. Concrete lids get a riser while they are open.
