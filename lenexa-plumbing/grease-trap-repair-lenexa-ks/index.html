    Grease Interceptor Clog Removal in Lenexa

    Grease does not stay liquid for long once it cools in a pipe. It sets up like candle wax on the crown of the line.     Damaged Baffle Repair in Lenexa

    Baffles are the walls inside the trap that slow the water down long enough for grease to float and solids to sink. Once one cracks or falls loose, the trap is just a box that grease passes through. We pump it out and pull the old baffle. On plastic units we order the factory part for that model number. Steel traps with a rusted-out baffle may need a fabricated piece, and sometimes a new trap is the cheaper answer.

    Commercial Grease Interceptor Repair in Lenexa, KS

    In-ground gravity interceptors run from about 500 gallons to several thousand, usually precast concrete with two compartments.     Clogged Grease Trap Repair in Lenexa

    Hardened grease in the inlet tee is the usual culprit when a trap backs up. The fix starts with pumping the trap down and jetting the inlet line until it flows again.
