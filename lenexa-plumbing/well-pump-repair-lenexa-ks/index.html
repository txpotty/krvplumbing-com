    Jet Pump System Repair in Lenexa, KS

    Jet pumps, typically found above ground, use an ejector assembly to draw water from the well. Typical problems include air leaks in the suction line, clogged nozzles, or a failing motor. These issues can lead to intermittent water flow or a pump that runs continuously without building pressure. KRV Plumbing can effectively troubleshoot and repair these components, ensuring your jet pump system in Lenexa operates at peak efficiency. We also check for proper priming, which is essential for jet pump function.

    Pressure Tank Repair in Lenexa, KS

    A waterlogged pressure tank makes the pump short cycle, turning on and off every few seconds. That rapid cycling burns out pumps early. We check the air charge, inspect the bladder and recharge or replace the tank. Correct tank sizing protects the pump and gives more even pressure at the tap.
