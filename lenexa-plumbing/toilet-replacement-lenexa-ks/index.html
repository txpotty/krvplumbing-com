    Frequently Asked Questions About Toilet Replacement in Lenexa

    Most toilet replacements, including a quality standard toilet, labor and disposal, cost $450 to $950. Premium one-piece or smart toilets can raise the total to $1,200 to $2,500. Flange repairs, if needed, usually add $100 to $250.

    We recommend reliable brands known for their quality, efficiency, and availability of parts. Popular choices include Kohler, American Standard, Toto, Delta, and Gerber. Each offers a range of styles and features, from basic models to advanced low-flow or comfort height options. We can help you select the right fit for your needs.

    Older toilets can use 3.5 to 7 gallons per flush, while WaterSense toilets use 1.28 gallons or less. For a average household, that can mean thousands of gallons saved each year.

    Signs of failing internal toilet parts include a toilet that runs constantly, a weak or incomplete flush, a handle that needs jiggling, or visible wear and tear on components like the flapper, fill valve, or flush valve. While some parts can be replaced one at a time, an older toilet with multiple failing components might benefit more from a full replacement.

    The rough-in measurement refers to the distance from the finished wall behind the toilet to the center of the drainpipe (the bolts that secure the toilet). Standard rough-ins are usually 12 inches, but 10-inch and 14-inch options exist. Knowing your rough-in is essential to make sure your new toilet fits correctly in the existing space.

    In most residential cases, replacing an existing toilet with a new one of similar type does not require a plumbing permit in Lenexa. However, if the replacement involves altering the drain lines or significant structural changes, a permit might be necessary. We can explain specific requirements.
