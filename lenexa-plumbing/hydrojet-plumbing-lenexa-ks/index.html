    Main Sewer Line Hydrojetting in Lenexa

    The main sewer carries every drain in the house, so a blockage there backs up the lowest fixture first. We jet the full main to the street tap. Four-inch mains get a heavy nozzle and high flow to push debris downstream.

    Kitchen Grease Line Jetting in Lenexa

    Kitchen drains collect grease that cools and hardens along the pipe wall, especially in long horizontal runs under a slab. A cable cuts a channel through it. A spinning nozzle removes the grease all around the pipe so it flushes away cleanly.

    Emergency Hydrojet Services in Lenexa, KS

    Grease Trap Line Cleaning in Lenexa, KS

    Restaurant kitchen lines carry hot grease that hardens as it cools. By the time it reaches the interceptor, a lot of it has already coated the pipe. We jet the line from the kitchen to the interceptor in short passes, which keeps loosened grease from plugging the pipe farther down, and we coordinate with your pump-out company.     Sewer Camera Inspection Before Hydrojetting in Lenexa

    Jetting a broken pipe can make the break worse. We camera every line before we set up the jetter. The video shows the pipe material, the condition of each joint, and any cracks, offsets or sags, which tells us the nozzle and pressure to use or whether we should not jet that line at all.

    Chemical-Free Drain Cleaning in Lenexa, KS

    Hydrojetting uses only water, so nothing caustic goes into your pipes or septic tank. Caustic cleaners can harm old pipe. Jetting removes the buildup physically, and the debris flushes to the sewer or septic tank as normal waste, so there is nothing left in the pipe to eat at joints or harm the bacteria a septic system depends on.
