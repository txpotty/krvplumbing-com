    Why Choose KRV Plumbing for Grease Trap Cleaning in Lenexa, KS?

    Skipped pump-outs usually announce themselves at the floor drain first, with gray water pooling around the grate after the dish machine drains. The smell shows up next. A rancid, sour odor means the grease cap has started to break down and give off hydrogen sulfide. The same gas corrodes concrete, which shortens the life of an in-ground interceptor.

    • Compliance Records, Each visit gets a dated record with gallons removed and the condition of the trap.
    • Code Compliance, Most cities, Lenexa included, set a pumping rule for food service kitchens, often the 25 percent rule, and inspectors check the log.
    • Extended Lifespan, Less hydrogen sulfide in the tank means slower corrosion of concrete and baffles.

    Citywide Coverage

    School cafeterias and church kitchens around Lenexa usually have small traps that get forgotten for a year at a time. We put those on a schedule too. For a kitchen that serves lunch on weekdays and shuts down for summer, pumping once a quarter is usually plenty.

    Experienced Team

    Our techs have pumped enough traps to spot a cracked baffle by the way the grease cap sits. Experience shows up in small things. An uneven cap tells them a baffle has come loose, and grease in the outlet chamber tells them the outlet tee is gone or plugged.

    Grease trap waste goes to a permitted receiving facility, never down a manhole or a storm drain. We log every load. The log ties the gallons we pumped at your kitchen to the facility that accepted them, which is the paper trail inspectors and haulers both rely on.

    Scheduled Grease Trap Maintenance Plans in Lenexa

    Most pretreatment programs use the 25 percent rule: pump before floating grease and settled solids together take up a quarter of the trap's liquid depth. Some cities set a fixed interval instead, often 90 days for interceptors. We work to whichever rule applies. Changes are easy.

    Grease Trap Inspection and Repair in Lenexa

    An empty trap is the only time anyone can see what is wrong with it. Cracked baffles and missing outlet tees are the usual finds. A missing outlet tee lets the floating grease cap pour straight into the sewer, and it is one of the most common failures we find on older concrete interceptors. Small fixes can often happen the same visit.

    Grease Trap Hydro-Jetting in Lenexa, KS

    Some traps have years of grease baked onto the walls and inlet piping. That crust survives a normal pump-out. We jet the inside of the empty trap and the line feeding it, which takes the unit back to bare walls and restores the capacity the crust was eating up. It adds about an hour.

Most smells get fixed that visit.
