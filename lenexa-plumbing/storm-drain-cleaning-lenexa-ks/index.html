    Channel and Trench Drain Cleaning in Lenexa

    Channel drains across driveways and garage entries catch water before it reaches the house. Their narrow channels fill quickly with sand, leaves and debris. We remove the grates, clean the channel and jet the outlet pipe. Cracked or loose grates can be replaced while we are there. A clean trench drain keeps water out of your garage during a downpour.

    Commercial Storm Drain Maintenance in Lenexa, KS

    For businesses in Lenexa, KS, a clogged storm drain can mean lost operating time and potential liability from flooding. We offer full-service commercial storm drain cleaning, often involving larger diameter pipes and more extensive drainage networks. Our technicians are equipped to handle the demands of commercial properties, including parking lot drains and industrial runoff systems, supporting compliance with local environmental rules and business continuity.

    Yard and Area Drain Cleaning in Lenexa

    Yard drains and area inlets collect runoff from lawns, rock landscaping and patios. Over time the grates fill with leaves and the pipes fill with silt and small gravel. We pull the grates, vacuum the inlet box and jet the pipe toward its outlet. This restores the line so it can handle a full storm. We also check that grates are secure and seated.

    Hydro Jetting for Storm Drains in Lenexa, KS

    Hydro jetting is a proven solution for deeply clogged storm drains in Lenexa, KS. This method uses high-pressure water streams to wash out stubborn obstructions like compacted soil, heavy silt, roots, and grease buildup. It is very effective for thoroughly cleaning the inside of the pipe walls, restoring the drain to its full capacity and preventing a quick return of blockages, a common issue with older drainage systems.

    Catch Basin Cleaning in Lenexa, KS

    Catch basins are key parts of any storm drainage system in Lenexa, KS, designed to collect runoff and trap debris before it enters the main drain lines. Regular cleaning of catch basins is essential to prevent them from becoming overloaded and causing blockages further down the system. Our service includes removing built-up leaves, sediment, trash, and other debris from catch basins, so they work correctly and protect your entire drainage system. This preventative maintenance is key to avoiding larger issues.

    Dry Well Cleaning and Inspection in Lenexa, KS

    Dry wells let storm water soak into the ground instead of flowing to a street. They are found on commercial sites, HOA common areas and some residential lots. Silt slowly seals the settling chamber and the drainage rock, slowing how fast water drains. We vacuum the chamber, inspect the inlet and outlet, and check drain-down time. If the dry well no longer drains properly, we explain options for rehabilitation or replacement.

    Root Removal from Storm Drains in Lenexa

    Tree roots are a common cause of severe storm drain blockages in Lenexa, especially in older neighborhoods with mature trees. Roots seek out moisture and can work into drain pipes through tiny cracks or loose joints, growing and eventually completely blocking water flow. KRV Plumbing uses specialized root-cutting attachments on our drain snakes and hydro jetting equipment to cut through and remove root intrusions. After removal, we can suggest further steps to prevent future root problems.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Storm Drain Cleaning in Lenexa

    Residential storm drain cleaning in Lenexa typically runs $200 to $500 depending on the number of inlets and pipe length. Dry well cleaning and commercial systems typically cost more and are quoted after a site visit.

    Common signs include water pooling around your property after rain, slow drainage from downspouts, visible debris collecting at catch basin grates, and unusual odors coming from the drains. If you notice any of these, it is a good idea to have your storm drains checked.

    To prevent storm drain clogs, regularly clear leaves and debris from grates and catch basins, avoid planting trees directly over drain lines, and keep landscaping in good shape to prevent soil erosion. Consider installing grates with smaller openings in high-debris areas and schedule yearly professional cleanings.

    While you can remove surface debris from drain grates, deeply clogged storm drains need professional tools like hydro jetters and camera equipment for effective cleaning. DIY attempts can sometimes push debris further down or damage the pipes, leading to more costly repairs. It is safer and more reliable to hire a professional.

    It is generally recommended to have your storm drains inspected and cleaned at least once a year. Properties with many trees or those that see frequent heavy rains may benefit from twice-yearly cleaning to prevent buildup and blockages.

    Usually, permits are not required for routine storm drain cleaning and maintenance. However, if the service involves excavation for major repairs or replacement of storm drain lines, then local Lenexa building and plumbing permits may be necessary. KRV Plumbing handles all necessary permitting for larger projects.

    Cleaning does not need a permit. New dry wells or major drainage changes may require permits or registration, and we can help you.
