    First-Time Disposal Installation in Lenexa

    Dishwasher Connection With Disposal Installation in Lenexa

    New disposals come with a knockout plug in the dishwasher inlet. We knock it out with a screwdriver and hammer, fish the plug out of the chamber and connect the hose with a new clamp. Forgetting the plug floods the dishwasher. An air gap or high loop goes in too, which keeps sink water from running back into the dishwasher.

    Switching From Batch-Feed to Continuous-Feed in Lenexa, KS

    Going from batch-feed to continuous-feed means adding a way to switch it on. That can be a wall switch, an air switch button on the sink deck or a switched outlet under the sink, and the choice depends on how hard it is to run wire to the spot you want. An air button skips the wall wiring. The drain usually stays the same.

    Standard Disposal Installation in Lenexa

    Most households in Lenexa do well with a continuous-feed unit between 1/3 and 1/2 horsepower. We remove the old unit if there is one, set a new flange in fresh plumber's putty, hang the disposal on a new mount and connect it to the trap with a new discharge tube and gasket. Wiring and testing come last. A smooth spin and a clean drain tell us it is right.

    Disposals in Remodels and New Builds in Lenexa

    On new builds and remodels in Lenexa, we work with your contractor so the disposal, the sink and the dishwasher are planned together.     High-Capacity Disposal Installation in Lenexa, KS

    Busy kitchens get the most from a 1 HP unit, like an InSinkErator Evolution or a Waste King Legend model.     Disposal Installation for Double Sinks in Lenexa, KS

    Double bowl sinks put the disposal on one side and a continuous waste or end outlet tee on the other. The layout keeps both bowls draining and stops the disposal from pushing water into the second bowl, a problem when the tee is too close to the discharge. Heavy-wall fittings last longer.
