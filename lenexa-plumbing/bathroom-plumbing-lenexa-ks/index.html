    Simple repairs like flapper or cartridge replacement usually cost $150 to $350. Toilet replacement usually runs $350 to $900 including the toilet, and shower valve replacement ranges from $450 to $1,500 depending on access and brand.

    Common signs include dripping faucets, running toilets, slow or clogged drains, reduced water pressure in showers, strange noises from pipes, and visible leaks around fixtures or under sinks. Any of these symptoms call for a professional inspection to prevent further damage.

    If the toilet is cracked, uses more than 1.6 gallons per flush or needs frequent repairs, replacement is often better. A new WaterSense toilet can reduce your water bill.

    Fixture repairs and like-for-like replacements usually do not need a permit. Moving drains, adding fixtures or remodeling often does. We handle permits when required.

    A failing pressure-balance cartridge often causes temperature swings when someone flushes a toilet or runs a faucet. Replacing the cartridge usually fixes it.

    Most common bathroom plumbing repairs, such as fixing a leaky faucet or clearing a simple toilet clog, can be completed within 1 to 3 hours.     Regular maintenance is key. Avoid flushing anything but toilet paper, use drain strainers to catch hair in sinks and showers, and fix minor leaks promptly. Consider a water softener if you have hard water, to reduce mineral buildup in fixtures and pipes.
