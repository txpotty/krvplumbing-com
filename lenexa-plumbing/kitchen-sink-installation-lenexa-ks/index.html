    Composite Sink Installation in Lenexa

    Granite and quartz composite sinks are tough on top but can chip at the drain opening if a strainer is overtightened. Hand tight comes first.     Apron-Front Sinks in Lenexa, KS

    Apron sinks need a cabinet cut to fit the front panel, and the bowl must be set before the counter gets measured. We set the sink and frame early, so the fabricator measures from the actual bowl instead of a spec sheet. It prevents a crooked reveal. Cast iron versions can top 150 pounds, so we bring two people.

    Top-mount Sink Installation in Lenexa

    Drop-in sinks are common in Kansas homes with laminate or tile counters. The rim carries the weight. After checking the cutout, we lay silicone under the lip and draw the clips down evenly so the whole rim sits flat. Then the drain and faucet get connected and tested.

    Farmhouse Sink Plumbing in Lenexa, KS

    Fireclay and cast iron farmhouse sinks can weigh well over 100 pounds empty, before a single drop of water goes in. We build a support frame inside the cabinet and set the sink level before the counter is installed. Deep bowls need a lower trap. The drain gets planned for that depth from day one.

    Drop-In Kitchen Sink Installation in Lenexa

    Sitting on the counter by their rim, drop-in sinks are a natural fit for laminate, butcher block and tile. Usually the fastest upgrade. After checking the cutout, we seal the rim and draw the clips down evenly, and in older homes and rentals it is often a two-hour job.

    Workstation Sink Installation in Lenexa, KS

    Workstation sinks have a ledge for cutting boards, colanders and drying racks, and many are deep single bowls with the drain off center. We plan the trap around that offset drain so the disposal fits and the cabinet keeps usable space on one side. Check the faucet reach as well.
