    Tankless water heaters need specific maintenance, mainly descaling to remove mineral buildup from the heat exchanger. This is particularly important where the water is hard.     Water Heater Maintenance in Lenexa

    Regular water heater maintenance is crucial to preventing sediment buildup. Water across much of Kansas runs hard, leaving mineral scale in water heaters, faucets, showerheads, and dishwashers. Our service includes flushing the tank, inspecting the anode rod for corrosion, checking the temperature and pressure relief valve, and examining all connections and heating elements. This extends the life of your unit, boosts efficiency, and helps avoid sudden cold showers or leaks.

    Water Softener Maintenance in Lenexa

    Water softeners fight hard water, protecting your pipes and appliances from mineral scale. Our maintenance service includes checking salt levels, cleaning the brine tank, confirming the regeneration cycle works correctly, and inspecting valves and resin beds. Proper softener maintenance prolongs its life and keeps soft water flowing to your home, preventing expensive scale damage.
