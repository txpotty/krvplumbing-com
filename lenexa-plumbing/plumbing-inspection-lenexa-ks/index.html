    Water Heater Inspections in Lenexa

    During a full inspection, we look at the visible gas piping and the connectors to each appliance. Most of our gas findings are in older homes, often at original appliance connectors.

    Whole-Home Plumbing Inspections in Lenexa

    Water Pressure and Supply Pipe Review in Lenexa, KS

    We measure static water pressure and check the pressure reducing valve if one is installed. High pressure ages fixtures fast. Pressure above 80 psi shortens the life of fixtures, supply hoses and water heaters, and we also identify the supply pipe material, from copper and CPVC to polybutylene, Kitec and galvanized.

    Fixture, Drain and Shutoff Valve Check in Lenexa, KS
