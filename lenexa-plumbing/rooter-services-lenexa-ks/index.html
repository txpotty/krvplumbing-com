    We run RIDGID, Spartan and Gorlitz augers, plus sewer cameras and jetters from the same tier of commercial makers. These machines take daily abuse and keep working.     Slow drains, gurgling pipes and sewer smell are the early ones. If the tub fills when the toilet flushes, the main is blocked. That one needs a call. More than one fixture draining slow at the same time usually means the main, not a branch.

    Most single-drain clogs take 1 to 3 hours from arrival to flow test. Main lines with heavy roots, or jobs that need a jetting pass, can stretch to half a day or more. Access is the wild card. A buried or missing cleanout means pulling a toilet first, which adds about an hour.

    Drain snaking, also called augering, uses a flexible cable and cutting head to break up or pull out the clog. It works well on hair, wipes and roots.
