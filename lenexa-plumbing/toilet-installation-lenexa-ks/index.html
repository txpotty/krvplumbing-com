    Kinds of Toilet Installation in Lenexa, KS

    Wall-Hung Toilet Installation in Lenexa, KS

    Wall-hung toilets need a wall at least 2 by 6 deep, or a furred-out chase built in front of an existing 2 by 4 wall. Floors stay clear underneath. Cleaning gets easier, and the bowl height can be set anywhere within the carrier range, useful for a tall family. Service happens through the flush plate.

    Commercial Toilet Installation in Lenexa, KS

    Commercial restrooms take a beating, so we install vitreous china bowls with flushometer valves rather than tank toilets. Flushometers want a 1 inch supply line and steady pressure. We confirm the building line can deliver the valve's rated flow, often 20 to 25 gallons a minute, before any fixture goes up. Fixture counts follow code.

    Two-Piece Toilet Installation in Lenexa

    Two-piece toilets come with the tank and bowl separate, joined by a rubber gasket and two or three bolts. We assemble them on the floor before setting. Tightening the tank bolts evenly, side to side, matters more than how hard they go, because an uneven pull cracks the china or leaves a slow drip at one bolt. It should not wobble.

    Customer-Supplied Toilet Installation in Lenexa, KS

    Bought the toilet yourself? Check the rough-in printed on the box first. Before the old toilet comes out, we inspect the new one for shipping cracks, because cracked china goes back to the store, not onto your flange. We bring the seal and stainless bolts.

    Comfort-Height and ADA Toilets in Lenexa

    Aging in place usually starts in the bathroom. A grab bar and a taller bowl help a lot.     Rough-In Toilet Installation in Lenexa, KS

    Rough-in is the plumbing that goes in before walls and floors close up. On a toilet, that is a vented 3 inch drain and a cold water stub. The drain goes 12 inches out from the finished wall line, which means we add the drywall thickness to the framing measurement. Then the inspector sees it open.
