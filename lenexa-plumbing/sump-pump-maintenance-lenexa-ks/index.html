    Crawlspace and Window Well Pumps in Lenexa, KS

    Pre-Storm Season Pump Testing in Lenexa

    Drought and water restrictions also affect Kansas, and dry clay soil pulling away from pipes can lead to cracks and leaks.     Submersible Sump Pump Service in Lenexa

    Float Switch Inspection and Adjustment in Lenexa, KS

    The float switch is a critical component that tells your sump pump when to turn on and off. During maintenance, we inspect the float switch for freedom of movement and proper calibration, making adjustments as needed to support reliable operation for your home in Lenexa, KS.
