    InSinkErator makes the Badger line and the Evolution series, and we repair both.     Flange and Inlet Leak Repair in Olathe, KS

    Leaks show up at the flange, dishwasher inlet, discharge tube or body. We trace the leak, reseal the flange with plumber's putty and replace gaskets or tubes as needed. Water from the bottom usually means a failed internal seal, which makes replacement the smarter move. That seal cannot be replaced.

    Commercial Disposal Repair in Olathe

    Restaurant disposals run 1 to 5 horsepower and usually include a control box and a solenoid valve for water. When one stops, the dish line backs up fast. Controls get checked first. A failed solenoid or a tripped overload in the control panel stops the unit just as completely as a jam, and it is a much faster fix.

    Preventive Care and Longevity in Olathe, KS

    Run cold water before, during and after grinding, and let it go about 15 seconds after the motor stops so the food clears the trap.     Leak Detection and Repair in Olathe, KS

    There are four usual leak points on a disposal: the sink flange, the dishwasher inlet, the discharge tube and the housing itself. We dry everything, run water with a little food coloring and watch to see which joint shows color first, since water runs down the outside of the unit and hides the real source. Most leak points can be repaired. A leak from the housing means it is time for a new unit.

    Noise and Odor Diagnosis in Olathe

A steady whine usually points to failing motor bearings, and the noise keeps building until the motor locks up. Most smells start at the splash guard. The guard comes out, the chamber gets cleaned and a torn guard gets replaced.
