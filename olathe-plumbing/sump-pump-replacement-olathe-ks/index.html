    Sewage Ejector Pump Replacement in Olathe

    Basement bathrooms and laundry rooms below the sewer line need sewage ejector pumps instead of sump pumps. These pumps handle solids and must be installed in sealed, vented basins. We replace ejector pumps with models rated for two inch solids. We also check the basin seal, vent and discharge check valve. Proper installation stops sewer gas odors.

    Submersible Sump Pump Replacement in Olathe

    Submersible sump pumps are popular in Olathe for their quiet operation and ability to sit fully submerged in the sump pit. When replacing a submersible pump, we carefully remove the old unit, clean the pit and install a new, properly sized pump. We make sure the check valve and discharge piping are correctly connected to prevent backflow and improve efficiency. That is crucial for handling heavy rainfall.

    Wayne Sump Pump Replacement in Olathe

    Wayne sump pumps offer a range of efficient models, from entry-level to professional-grade. If your Wayne sump pump is failing, KRV Plumbing provides prompt Wayne sump pump replacement in Olathe. We make sure the new pump is correctly installed with proper discharge and check valve components, restoring your home's flood protection fast and efficiently.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Olathe

    Power outages during severe storms can leave a primary sump pump useless, leading to basement flooding. A battery backup sump pump offers crucial protection during these times. We install and replace complete battery backup systems, including dedicated marine-grade batteries and smart charging units. Our team helps you select a system with enough run-time to protect your Olathe home through extended power interruptions. You get peace of mind when the lights go out.

    Combination Sump Pump System Replacement in Olathe, KS

    For maximum protection, many homeowners in Olathe choose a combination sump pump system, which pairs a primary electric pump with a battery backup pump in one unit. This keeps the system running even if the power fails or the primary pump quits. KRV Plumbing can replace your existing combination system or install a new one, evaluating your needs for groundwater management. We make sure the components are properly matched for strong performance and longevity.

    Commercial Pit Pump Replacement in Olathe

    Commercial buildings often have elevator pits, parking garage drains and mechanical room sumps. These require pumps rated for higher flow and sometimes oil-sensing controls. We replace commercial pumps and alarms to meet code and insurance requirements. Duplex systems with alternating pumps provide redundancy. We schedule work to avoid interrupting operations.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Replacement in Olathe, KS

    Pedestal sump pumps have their motor mounted above the sump pit, making them easier to access for maintenance and often more long-lasting. We provide professional pedestal sump pump replacement in Olathe, KS, with proper alignment and secure mounting. This type is often preferred in shallower pits or where noise is not a major concern, offering a robust solution for flood prevention.

    Float Switch and Check Valve Replacement in Olathe

    Many sump pump failures start with a stuck float switch or a leaking check valve. A failed check valve lets water flow back into the pit and causes the pump to short cycle. We install new check valves with every pump replacement and verify the switch moves freely. Vertical float switches work well in tight pits. Tethered floats work best in larger pits.
