    For light use, a 1/3 HP or 1/2 HP unit is usually enough. For regular use and tough food scraps, a 3/4 HP or 1 HP unit is recommended. Our plumbers can help you weigh your needs and choose the best fit for your Olathe home.

    In most cases, a simple like-for-like garbage disposal replacement in Olathe does not require a permit. However, if significant plumbing modifications or electrical work beyond a simple swap are needed, a permit might be required. We'll let you know if this is the case.
