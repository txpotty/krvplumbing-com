    Commercial Drain Cleaning and Hydro-Jetting in Olathe

    Commercial drains carry grease, paper towels and food waste in volumes no house ever sees. A cable just pokes a hole. A jetted line stays open far longer than a cabled one. Restaurants, schools and apartment stacks are where we see the biggest difference.

    Backflow Assembly Testing and Rebuilds in Olathe

    Most water providers require annual tests on commercial backflow assemblies, and many send late notices that come with fines. Test early. Failed devices get rebuilt on the spot when we have the kit.

    Commercial Water Heater Repair and Installation in Olathe, KS

    A commercial water heater is judged by recovery rate, the gallons it can reheat per hour, not by tank size alone. Undersized heaters run cold by mid-shift.     Backflow Testing and Certification in Olathe

    Each backflow test takes about 30 minutes per assembly and requires a short water shutoff to that line. Plan for a brief outage. Our tester opens each test cock in order, records the check valve and relief valve readings on a calibrated gauge and compares them against the pass limits printed on the test form. Passing devices get tagged. We file the report with your water provider and keep a copy on our side for next year.
