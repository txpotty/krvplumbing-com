    Pre-Storm Season Pump Testing in Olathe

    Severe thunderstorms are common in Kansas, and heavy downpours can overwhelm gutters, storm drains, and older sewer lines.     Float Switch Inspection and Adjustment in Olathe, KS

    The float switch is a key component that tells your sump pump when to turn on and off. A stuck or improperly adjusted float switch can lead to the pump running continuously or failing to activate at all. During maintenance, we inspect the float switch for freedom of movement and proper calibration, making adjustments as needed to support reliable operation for your home in Olathe, KS.

    Battery Backup System Testing in Olathe, KS

    A power outage during a heavy storm can leave your primary sump pump useless. Our maintenance service includes complete testing of your battery backup sump pump system. We check the battery charge, verify the charger's function and ensure the backup pump activates when the main power is interrupted. This dual protection is vital for homes in Olathe, KS where power interruptions can coincide with severe weather.

    Submersible Sump Pump Service in Olathe

    Our team is skilled in maintaining submersible sump pumps, a popular choice in Olathe due to their quiet operation and compact design.
