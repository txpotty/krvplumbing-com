    Bypass Valve and Drain Line Repair in Olathe, KS

    A leaking bypass valve can let hard water slip past the softener without you noticing. We test and rebuild bypass valves and replace cracked fittings. We also inspect the drain line for kinks and proper air gap and the brine overflow line for correct routing. These small fixes head off leaks and backflow problems.

    Routine Water Softener Inspections in Olathe

    Regular inspections are the cornerstone of effective water softener maintenance. Our technicians will examine your entire system, including the brine tank, resin tank, control valve and all connecting pipes. We check for leaks, corrosion, proper salt levels and any signs of wear that could lead to future problems. This preventive approach helps extend the life of your unit and keeps performance consistent, especially since water hardness varies from place to place.

    Salt-Free Conditioner Maintenance in Olathe

    Template assisted crystallization and other salt-free conditioners do not remove hardness but change how it behaves. They still need media replacement every few years and sediment prefilter changes much more often. We inspect the media tank, replace prefilters and check bypass valves. We also explain honestly how these systems differ from a true softener.

    Resin Bed Cleaning and Replacement in Olathe

    Softener resin can foul with iron, manganese or sediment and lose capacity over time. Chlorine and chloramine in city water also slowly break resin down. We run a resin cleaner through the system and test performance afterward. If the resin is exhausted, we rebed the tank with fresh high-capacity resin, which costs far less than a new system.

    Maintenance Versus New Softener Costs in Olathe, KS

    A maintenance visit typically costs far less than a new system, which often runs $1,200 to $3,500 installed. Resin rebedding typically costs $300 to $700 and can bring an older tank back to full capacity. Most quality softeners last 10 to 15 years with service. We will tell you when a repair makes sense and when a replacement is the better investment.

    Regeneration Settings and Hardness Testing in Olathe, KS

    Many softeners are set incorrectly from day one or never updated when household size changes. We test your raw water hardness and program the valve to match your actual use. Metered systems regenerate based on gallons used, which saves salt compared to time clock models. Correct settings keep water soft without squandering salt or water.

    Resin Bed Cleaning and Regeneration Optimization in Olathe, KS

    The resin beads are responsible for ion exchange, removing hard minerals from your water. Over time, these beads can become fouled with iron or other contaminants, reducing their capacity. Our maintenance includes cleaning the resin bed and adjusting the regeneration cycle settings for full softening capacity. We make sure your system is regenerating efficiently for your water quality, providing truly soft water.
