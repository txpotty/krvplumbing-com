    Leaking Drain Pipe Repair in Olathe, KS

    Drain leaks are low pressure, so they drip instead of spray. Most go unnoticed for months.     Broken drain pipe usually comes from settling, roots or age. Poor original work causes some. We find the break with a camera and a locator, then replace that section with new pipe joined by shielded couplings that fit the old and new outside diameters. We test it before closing up.
