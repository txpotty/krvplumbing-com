    PEX Pipe Repair & Installation in Olathe

    PEX is flexible and handles freezing better than rigid pipe. Fittings let go. Most PEX leaks happen at a crimp ring that was not fully seated, an early brass fitting that dezincified or a section damaged by sunlight in an attic, and we cut out the bad fitting and replace it with a crimp, clamp or expansion connection rated for the pipe. Usually a quick fix.

    PEX and Copper Pipe Repair in Olathe, KS

    PEX and copper fail in different ways. Copper gets pinholes. Copper thins from the inside and develops pinholes, while PEX usually fails at fittings or where it was kinked during installation, so the truck carries tools and fittings for both. Each repair is pressure tested.

    Buried Line Repair in Olathe

    Buried pipes can be damaged by settling, roots or deep frost. Sudden arctic fronts can drop temperatures quickly, so disconnecting hoses and protecting outdoor spigots before winter is a smart routine. Water mains are common. We locate the leak without guessing, and either fix it at the mark or pull a new line through the old route. Sewer lines get a camera.

    Frozen and Burst Line Repair in Olathe

    Pipes in exterior walls, attics and crawlspaces freeze first. Frozen water cracks it. The split usually does not leak until the ice thaws, so a pipe that froze overnight may start spraying the next afternoon once the house warms up. We repair the split and add insulation. Heat tape is an option on problem runs.

    Galvanized Pipe Replacement in Olathe

    Older homes were plumbed with galvanized steel, which rusts from the inside out. Flow drops slowly. Every time a fitting is disturbed, the next one tends to leak, so repairs on galvanized are usually short-term and replacing the run with PEX or copper is the lasting fix. We use dielectric unions at transitions.

    Drain and Waste Pipe Repair in Olathe

    Drain pipe made of ABS, PVC or cast iron can crack, separate or leak at joints. Stains give it away. A leaking drain often shows up as a stain below a bathroom or a musty smell in a cabinet, and we locate the leak, replace the damaged section and test the drain under full flow before closing up. We look at venting as well.

    Emergency Pipe Repair in Olathe, KS

    Slab Leak Pipe Repair in Olathe, KS

    Slab leaks start when pipe under the concrete wears through or gets stressed by soil movement. Much of Kansas has expansive clay soil that swells when wet and shrinks in drought, which can move foundations and stress buried pipes. Warm floors are a clue. We isolate the leaking line with pressure tests and find it with acoustic and thermal equipment, then choose between a small slab cut at the leak and rerouting the line through the walls or attic. Both options get a price.
