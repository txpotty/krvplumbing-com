    How We Install Showers in Olathe, KS

    Shower Valve and Fixture Installation in Olathe, KS

    Pressure-balancing valves are the baseline for any new shower. Thermostatic valves hold a set temperature and add volume control, which works well with rain heads and handhelds. Both prevent scalding. We help you choose based on how many outlets you plan.

    Walk-In Shower Installation in Olathe

    Walk-in showers depend on floor slope. With no curb, the floor needs a steady quarter-inch-per-foot pitch to the drain and waterproofing that extends past the opening. We set that slope. Grab bar blocking goes in while the walls are open, so bars can be added later without hunting for studs.

    Commercial Shower Installation in Olathe, KS

    Locker rooms need showers that take hundreds of uses a day. We install commercial valves with tamper-resistant trim, vandal-resistant heads and drains sized for several showers running at once, all to commercial code. We work around your business. A master mixing valve can protect the whole room from scalding.

    Shower Stall Installation in Olathe

    A one-piece stall needs a level, fully supported base. We set fiberglass and acrylic bases in a bed of mortar or setting compound so the floor of the unit does not flex underfoot, since flex is what cracks a base within a couple of years. The drain connects from below. Flow and temperature get tested after trim.

    Emergency Shower Replacement in Olathe

    A cracked pan or a failed valve can force a replacement fast. If water is reaching the subfloor or the ceiling below, we remove the old unit and set a standard stall to get the bathroom working quickly. Custom work can wait. We check the framing before the new unit goes in.

    Curbless Shower Installation in Olathe

    Curbless showers need the floor lowered in the shower area. A wood floor gets dropped joists or a recessed pan, while a slab gets cut and lowered so the floor flows straight in. A linear drain often works best. We plan the slope with your contractor before demo.

    Shower Valve and Trim Installation in Olathe, KS

    The valve sits behind the tile for decades. Each valve is set to the depth its trim requires, and we include integral stops whenever the valve supports them. Trim goes on after tile. Waiting keeps the trim clean during tiling.

    Tub-to-Shower Conversion in Olathe, KS

    Tub-to-shower conversions are one of our most common jobs. The tub comes out, the drain shifts to the pan location and the valve moves up to about 48 inches. On a slab, we cut concrete. The new shower is safer to step into.
