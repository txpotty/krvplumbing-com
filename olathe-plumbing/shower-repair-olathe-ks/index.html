    Shower Diverter Repair in Olathe, KS

    A diverter sends water from the tub spout to the showerhead. When it wears out, water keeps running from the spout and shower pressure drops. We replace diverter spouts, three-handle diverter stems and multi-function diverter valves. This restores full flow to the showerhead.

    Showerhead Repair and Replacement in Olathe

    A malfunctioning showerhead can spray erratically, have reduced pressure, or even leak. Often, mineral deposits from the water supply can clog the nozzles, or internal components can wear out. We can clean, repair, or replace your showerhead with a new, efficient model. We offer options from standard to rain showerheads, providing proper installation for strong performance and water conservation.

    Leaking Shower Valve Repair in Olathe

    A shower that drips after it is turned off usually has a worn cartridge or seals. Hard water scale scratches cartridge surfaces and breaks down O-rings faster than soft water does. We replace the cartridge with a genuine part and lubricate the valve with silicone grease. This stops the drip and makes the handle turn smoothly again.

    Shower Temperature Problem Repair in Olathe

    If your shower turns cold when someone flushes, the pressure-balance spool may be stuck. If you cannot get enough hot water, the anti-scald limit stop may be set too low or the cartridge may be worn. We repair or replace pressure-balance and thermostatic valves and adjust limit stops. Your shower will hold a steady, comfortable temperature.

    Faulty Shower Valve Repair in Olathe

    Problems with your shower's water temperature or pressure often point to a malfunctioning mixing valve or cartridge. These components control the hot and cold water flow. Our technicians are experienced at diagnosing valve issues and can repair or replace them, ensuring you get consistent water temperature and pressure every time you shower. We work with all major valve brands.

    Shower Pan and Wall Leak Repair in Olathe, KS

    Leaks under the shower pan or behind tile can cause rot, mold and stains on ceilings below. We use moisture meters and flood tests to find failed grout, cracked pans or leaking pipe joints. Then we repair the source, whether that means resealing a drain, replacing a valve or rebuilding a pan. On slab homes, these leaks can damage flooring in nearby rooms.
