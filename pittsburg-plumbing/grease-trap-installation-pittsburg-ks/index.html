    Hydromechanical Grease Trap Installation in Pittsburg

    Hydromechanical grease traps sit under or near sinks and use baffles and flow control to separate grease from water. They are common in small cafes, coffee shops and quick-service restaurants with tight space. We install units from Zurn, Watts, Schier and Endura sized by gallons per minute. Each install includes a flow control fitting and proper venting to keep the trap working as designed. These units need frequent cleaning, so we place them where staff can reach the lid without trouble.

    Gravity Grease Trap Installation in Pittsburg

    Gravity grease traps are a common and effective solution for many commercial kitchens in Pittsburg. These passive systems use the natural separation of FOG from water due to density differences. Our plumbers are experienced with gravity grease traps, ensuring proper sizing, correct inlet and outlet piping, and easy access for future cleaning and maintenance to get the most from their efficiency.

    Large Outdoor Grease Interceptor Installation in Pittsburg

    For high-volume commercial kitchens in Pittsburg, large outdoor grease interceptors are often required by code.
