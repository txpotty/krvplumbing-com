    Frequently Asked Questions About Well Pump Replacement in Pittsburg

    The cost of well pump replacement in Pittsburg, KS can vary widely, typically ranging from $1,500 to $5,000 or more. This price depends on factors like the type of pump, submersible or jet, well depth, horsepower and any additional components needed like pressure tanks or control boxes. We provide a written, upfront estimate after diagnosing your situation.

    Permit requirements vary by county. We follow local requirements for all well work.

    Common signs include no water from your faucets, weak or inconsistent water pressure, strange noises from the well or pressure tank area, short cycling where the pump turns on and off frequently, or cloudy or muddy water. If you notice any of these, it is time to call a professional for an inspection.

    Jet pumps sit above ground and pull water up from shallower wells, typically less than 25 feet. Submersible pumps are placed directly inside the well casing, submerged in water, and are used for deeper wells, pushing water up to the surface. Each has its own applications and benefits.

    If your pressure tank is old or waterlogged, replacing it at the same time can protect the new pump. A failed tank causes rapid short cycling, which is one of the most common reasons submersible motors burn out early.

    Common signs of a failing well pump include no water, sputtering faucets, reduced water pressure, discolored water or unusually high electricity bills. If your pump runs constantly or cycles on and off too frequently, these are also signs that replacement might be necessary.

    It is often a good idea to consider replacing your pressure tank at the same time as your well pump, especially if both are of similar age. A new pressure tank can improve the efficiency and lifespan of your new pump by reducing cycling. It also cuts labor costs to do both jobs at once.

    Most new well pumps come with a manufacturer's warranty, typically ranging from 1 to 5 years, covering defects in materials and workmanship. KRV Plumbing also backs our installation work with our own labor warranty for complete peace of mind.
