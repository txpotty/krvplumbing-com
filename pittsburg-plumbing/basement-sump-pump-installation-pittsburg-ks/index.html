    High-Capacity Sump Pump Systems in Pittsburg, KS

    Some lots sit in a high water table and fill a standard pit every few minutes during spring. Those basements need a 1/2 or 3/4 horsepower pump in a larger basin, all sized after we time how fast your pit refills. We use a stopwatch.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Pittsburg, KS

    Battery backups earn their keep during outages. Drought and watering limits also affect Kansas, and dry clay soil pulling away from pipes can lead to cracks and leaks. We add a second pump in the same pit with its own float, wired to a sealed AGM battery on a smart charger that tests itself and beeps when the battery gets weak.     Discharge Line and Check Valve Installation in Pittsburg

    Rigid PVC carries the water from the pump out through the rim joist and at least 10 feet from the house. We add a spring check valve so the water in the pipe cannot fall back, which cuts motor run time and the loud thunk you hear after every cycle. Hard freezes happen every winter in Kansas, and pipes in exterior walls, garages, and crawlspaces are the first to burst. Where lines can freeze, we fit a freeze-resistant outlet.

    Sump Pit Installation in Pittsburg, KS

    Pit size controls how often the pump starts. Too small a pit means the pump short cycles in heavy flow, so we usually install an 18 by 22 inch or 18 by 30 inch basin with a solid bottom and perforated sides wrapped in filter fabric. Less cycling, longer life.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Pittsburg

    Pedestal pumps put the motor on a column above the water. The motor stays dry and you can reach it without pulling the pump, which is why they suit narrow pits under 14 inches across, and why they often outlast cheap submersibles in Pittsburg homes. They are louder, though.

    Wayne Sump Pump Installation in Pittsburg, KS

    Wayne builds everything from basic utility pumps to combo units with a built-in backup.     Quiet Submersible Pumps for Finished Basements in Pittsburg

    Finished basements call for a quiet pump and a sealed lid. Pairing a submersible with a gasketed cover cuts noise and odor, and we route the discharge behind framing or through a closet so the pipe does not run across a wall you just painted in your Pittsburg home. We patch the slab flush.
