    Flex-Lite Pressure Tank Replacement in Pittsburg

    Flex-Lite tanks have a composite shell. Rust is not a concern. That makes them a good pick for damp basements, crawlspaces and well houses where steel tanks tend to corrode from the bottom up, and they are lighter to carry into tight spaces. Same plumbing as steel.

    Pressure Switch and Gauge Integration in Pittsburg, KS

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop. Switch and tank are a team. A switch set at 40/60 needs a tank pre-charged to 38 psi, and a gauge that reads correctly so you can see what the system is doing, so we replace a worn gauge rather than trusting a sticky needle. Small parts, big role.

    Preventative Well Pressure Tank Maintenance in Pittsburg, KS

    Check the air charge once a year. Shut off the pump, drain the system to zero, and put a tire gauge on the valve at the top of the tank, topping it up to two psi below the cut-in if it reads low. Water out of the valve means a torn bladder.

    Same-Day Tank Swaps in Pittsburg

    We keep common tank sizes on our trucks. That means a call in the morning can often end with a new tank installed by afternoon, including new tank tee, gauge and relief valve, without waiting on a parts order. Bigger tanks may take a day to order.

    Cost-Effective Well Pressure Tank Upgrades in Pittsburg, KS

    Upgrading a size often costs less than people think. Going one size larger adds only a little. A larger tank means fewer pump starts per day, which is the cheapest way to extend the life of your pump, so we often recommend it during a replacement.

    Residential Well Pressure Tank Replacement in Pittsburg

    Most homes on a well have a tank between 20 and 86 gallons sitting near the pressure switch. First step, kill the breaker to the pump.     Galvanized Steel Pressure Tank Upgrades in Pittsburg

    Old galvanized tanks have no bladder at all. Air and water share the same space. Over time the water absorbs the air, the tank waterlogs, and someone has to drain and recharge it every few months. A sealed bladder tank ends that chore.
