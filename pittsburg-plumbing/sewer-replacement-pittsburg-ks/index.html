    Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Pittsburg

    Cast iron sewer pipe can corrode from the inside, creating rough surfaces and holes. Scale buildup catches debris and causes clogs. We replace cast iron with PVC or HDPE. This restores flow and ends corrosion. Many homes built before 1975 have cast iron lines.

    Sewer Pipe Material Upgrades in Pittsburg, KS

    Many older homes in Pittsburg, KS still have original sewer lines made of clay, cast iron, or Orangeburg pipe, which are prone to degradation, cracking, and root intrusion over time. When we perform a sewer replacement, we often suggest upgrading to modern, durable materials like PVC (polyvinyl chloride) or ABS (acrylonitrile butadiene styrene). These materials are more flexible, resistant to corrosion and roots, and have a much longer lifespan, providing a significant upgrade to your home's plumbing infrastructure.

    Traditional Trench Sewer Replacement in Pittsburg

    For severely damaged or collapsed sewer lines, traditional trenching is often the most effective solution. This involves excavating a trench along the sewer line's path to allow for direct access and replacement of the entire pipe. While more invasive, it provides a complete overhaul and is often required for older homes with clay or cast iron pipes that have reached the end of their lifespan. We ensure thorough planning and execution to minimize disruption to your yard.

    Trenchless Pipe Bursting in Pittsburg

    Pipe bursting pulls a new HDPE pipe through the old line while breaking the old pipe apart. It needs only small access pits at each end. This protects landscaping, driveways and patios. It works well for clay, cast iron and Orangeburg pipe. Most jobs are finished in one day.

    Traditional Sewer Line Replacement in Pittsburg
