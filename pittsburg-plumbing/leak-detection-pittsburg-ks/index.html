    Main Water Line Leak Detection in Pittsburg

    The service line between your meter and house can leak underground for months, especially in older galvanized or copper lines. Wet spots in the yard, unusually green grass or a sinking area near the front of the house are clues. We use line tracers to map the pipe and ground microphones to hear the leak. That keeps excavation to a small, focused hole.

    Pool Leak Detection in Pittsburg

    A leaking pool can lead to wasted water and higher utility bills. Our specialists are skilled in detecting leaks in pool liners, plumbing and equipment. We use dye testing, pressure testing and listening devices to find the precise source of the leak, whether it's in the skimmer, main drain or return lines. Protect your investment and enjoy your pool without endless refilling in Pittsburg.

    Water Leak Detection in Walls & Ceilings in Pittsburg, KS

    Water leaks behind walls or in ceilings can go unnoticed for long periods, leading to extensive drywall damage, wood rot and mold. We use non-invasive methods like moisture meters, thermal cameras and borescopes to precisely pinpoint the source of these leaks. This approach minimizes the need for destructive investigation, saving you time and money on repairs to your home's walls and ceilings.

    Hidden Wall and Ceiling Leak Detection in Pittsburg

    Leaks inside walls and ceilings often show up as stains, bubbling paint, soft drywall or a musty smell. We use thermal cameras to spot temperature differences caused by water, then confirm with moisture meters. Acoustic tools help when the leak is on a pressurized line. Pinpointing the source lets us cut a small, clean access opening.

    Underground Water Line Leak Detection in Pittsburg, KS

    Leaks in underground water lines, often leading from the meter to your home, can result in significant water loss and property damage. We employ ground microphones and tracer gas detection to locate these buried leaks precisely. Factors like tree roots, ground shifting and corrosion in older galvanized pipes can contribute to these issues, making professional detection important.
