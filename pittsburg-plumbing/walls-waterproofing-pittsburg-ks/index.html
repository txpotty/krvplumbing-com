    Exterior Block Wall Waterproofing in Pittsburg, KS

    Block and stem walls often show efflorescence, which is the white mineral crust left when water passes through masonry. Irrigation and sprinklers aimed at the house are common culprits.     Shower Pan Leak Repair and Waterproofing in Pittsburg, KS

    A cracked or poorly sloped shower pan often causes wet baseboards and stained ceilings below. We test the pan by plugging the drain and flooding it to confirm the leak. Then we rebuild the pan with correct pre-slope, a new liner or bonded membrane and a properly clamped drain. A second flood test proves the pan is watertight before tile.

    Shower Wall Waterproofing in Pittsburg

Seams, corners and niches get extra reinforcement before new tile is set.

It's an affordable way to control humidity and prevent wood rot.
