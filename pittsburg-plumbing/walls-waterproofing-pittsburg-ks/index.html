    Crawl Space Encapsulation in Pittsburg

    Encapsulation seals the whole crawl space, walls and floor, with a reinforced poly liner. We run 12 to 20 mil liner up the walls, tape every seam, seal around piers and pipe penetrations, and usually add a dehumidifier to hold humidity under 60 percent. Strong winds and dry spells can draw moisture out of the soil, while sudden cold fronts can drop temperatures sharply overnight. Wood rot slows to a stop. Drier air under the floor also means less musty air upstairs.

    Waterproofing Costs vs. Repeat Water Damage in Pittsburg, KS

    Paying once beats paying every two years. Exterior block sealing depends on wall length. We write up each option with its price so you can compare.

    Laundry Room and Utility Wall Waterproofing in Pittsburg, KS

    Rubber washing machine hoses burst.     Foundation Crack Repair & Sealing in Pittsburg

    Hairline cracks in poured concrete walls leak more water than their size suggests. We inject them with low-pressure polyurethane, which expands to fill the crack from the inside face to the soil side, or with epoxy when the crack also needs structural bonding. Horizontal cracks are different. They can mean the wall is bowing from soil pressure, so we check for movement with a level before we seal anything. Most vertical cracks get sealed in one visit.

    Shower Pan Leak Repair and Waterproofing in Pittsburg, KS

    Wet baseboards outside the shower or a stain on the ceiling below usually point to the pan. We plug the drain, fill the pan with an inch or two of water, and mark the waterline and check it a day later. A drop means a leak. Rebuilding means a mortar pre-slope of a quarter inch per foot, a new PVC liner or bonded membrane, and a two-piece clamping drain set on the liner. The new pan gets its own flood test before tile.

    Below-Grade and Basement Wall Waterproofing in Pittsburg

    Homes from the late seventies through the mid nineties may have polybutylene supply pipe, which is prone to sudden failure. Any wall below grade sits in soil that gets saturated after heavy rain or snowmelt, and that water pushes against the wall with real hydrostatic pressure. We inject cracks, coat the wall inside or out, and install drain tile where water pools along the footing. A sump goes in if groundwater sits high. Not every basement needs one.

    Foundation Crack Repair in Pittsburg

    Crack injection takes about two hours per crack. We set injection ports every 8 to 12 inches along the crack, seal the surface with epoxy paste, and pump in resin from the bottom port up until it pushes out the next port. Polyurethane flexes a bit if the wall shifts, and epoxy bonds the two sides back together. The surface paste gets ground off once it cures.
