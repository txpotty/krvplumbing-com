    Bradford White and Rheem Gas Water Heater Repair in Pittsburg, KS

    Bradford White and Rheem tanks make up a big share of what we repair in Pittsburg, KS. Most Rheem gas units run a White-Rodgers or Honeywell valve with a blinking status LED.     Flammable Vapor Sensor Repair in Pittsburg

    Since about 2003, most residential gas tanks have been built with a flammable vapor ignition resistant combustion chamber. A flame arrestor screen at the base lets air in and keeps flame from getting out. When lint, dust or pet hair coats that screen, the burner starves, the chamber overheats and a thermal switch shuts the whole heater down, sometimes several times a week. The screen and the chamber both get cleaned. A tripped thermal switch gets replaced.

    Pilot Light & Burner Issues in Pittsburg

    A pilot that lights, holds for ten seconds and then goes out is almost always a thermocouple or a weak magnet in the gas valve. A pilot that will not light at all is more often a clogged orifice or a dead igniter. Main burner problems show up another way. The pilot stays lit but the main flame never comes on, which points to the thermostat side of the valve, a tripped thermal cutoff or a flame arrestor so clogged with lint that the burner starves for air. Each part gets tested in that sequence.

    Tank Leaks & Anode Rods in Pittsburg

    Corrosion starts at the anode rod and works its way into the steel once the rod is spent. Many Kansas homeowners use water softeners to extend the life of their tank water heaters and fixtures against heavy mineral buildup. The tech pulls the anode, checks what metal remains and swaps it if bare core wire shows. A fresh anode, a drained tank and a relief valve that seats properly can add years to a heater in a Pittsburg home that has not started leaking from the shell. A wet bottom seam is different. That tank needs replacing.

    Power Vent Water Heater Repair in Pittsburg

    Power vent heaters push exhaust through PVC with a blower on top of the tank. When one quits, it is usually the blower motor, the pressure switch or the hot surface igniter. Condensate pooling in a vent with the wrong slope can also trip the pressure switch, so we check the pipe run all the way to the termination before we sign off on a part. The control's error code is our starting point. Then each part gets a meter test.

    All Major Gas Water Heater Brands Serviced in Pittsburg

    Every brand label we see in garages and closets uses one of a handful of gas control designs. Rheem and Ruud share most parts, and State shares a lot with A.O. Smith.
