    Open-Trench Sewer Replacement in Pittsburg

    A collapsed run or a long belly usually means we dig. We open a trench along the old line, pull the broken pipe and lay new PVC on compacted bedding at a steady fall of an eighth to a quarter inch per foot. Sod gets cut and rolled aside first when the lawn allows it. The inspector sees open pipe. We compact the backfill in layers, which keeps a dip from forming along the trench.

    Keeping a New Sewer Line Healthy in Pittsburg

    New pipe still needs a little care. Plant thirsty trees away from the line, since big roots can push on even a sound pipe. Much of Kansas has expansive clay soil that swells when wet and shrinks in drought, which can shift foundations and stress buried pipes. Wipes do not break down. On older homes, a quick camera look every few years spots trouble at the tap before it backs up.

    Commercial Sewer Lateral Work in Pittsburg, KS

    Apartment buildings, offices and restaurants in Pittsburg, KS need lateral work too.     Cured-in-Place Sewer Lining in Pittsburg

    Lining builds a new pipe inside the old one. After cutting roots and jetting the line, we pull in a resin-filled liner and inflate it until it cures against the old pipe wall. Collapsed pipe will not work. Lining shines under finished basements and slabs. We say plainly whether your line qualifies.

    Trenchless Sewer Replacement in Pittsburg, KS

    Two holes instead of one long trench. With bursting, a steel head splits the old pipe into the soil and pulls new HDPE into the same path. The other option is a cured-in-place liner.
