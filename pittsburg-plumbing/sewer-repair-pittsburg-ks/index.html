    Orangeburg and Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Pittsburg

    Some older homes still have Orangeburg pipe, a tar-paper material that flattens and blisters over time.     Sewer Pipe Relining in Pittsburg, KS

    Sewer pipe relining is a trenchless repair technique where a new, durable pipe is created within the existing damaged one. A flexible, epoxy-impregnated liner is inserted into the old pipe and inflated, curing in place to form a seamless, strong and corrosion-resistant new pipe. This method is effective for addressing cracks, minor breaks and root intrusion without major digging. It extends the life of your sewer system and is an excellent option for preserving landscapes in Pittsburg.
