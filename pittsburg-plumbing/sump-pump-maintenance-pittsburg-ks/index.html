    Float Switch Repair and Adjustment in Pittsburg, KS

    The float switch is the most frequent reason a sump pump fails to start. Tethered floats can tangle on the pump body, and vertical floats can stick from mineral crust or debris. We clean or replace the switch and set the on and off levels so the pump does not short cycle. Electronic sensor switches are an option for tight pits where floats get stuck.

    Sump Pump Cleaning and Debris Removal in Pittsburg

    Frequently Asked Questions About Sump Pump Maintenance in Pittsburg

    A standard sump pump inspection and cleaning typically runs $125 to $250. Replacing a float switch or check valve usually adds $75 to $250 depending on parts.

    A standard sump pump maintenance appointment usually takes about 30 to 60 minutes. This time allows for a complete inspection, cleaning and testing of all components. If minor repairs are needed, the visit might run a little longer.

    Most quality pumps last seven to ten years. Pumps that sit dry for long periods can seize sooner, which is why testing matters during dry months.

    A standard sump pump maintenance visit typically takes about 30 minutes to an hour. If we find issues requiring repair, the time might be extended, but we will always explain this upfront.

    While you can perform basic checks like making sure the pump is plugged in and the discharge pipe isn't blocked, professional maintenance goes much deeper.
