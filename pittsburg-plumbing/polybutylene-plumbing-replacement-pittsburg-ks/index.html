    Understanding Polybutylene Failure in Pittsburg

    Polybutylene's inherent weakness stems from its chemical reaction with oxidants like chlorine, commonly used in municipal water treatment. Over time, this reaction causes the pipes and their plastic fittings to become brittle, leading to hairline cracks and eventual rupture. Private wells serve many rural homes, and minerals, sediment, and nitrates make periodic well testing a good habit. Hard water can also add stress to pipe that is already breaking down. Understanding this failure mechanism is crucial for diagnosing issues and recommending the most effective and permanent replacement strategy for your home.
