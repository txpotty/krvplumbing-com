    Every Kind of Disposal Install, Done Properly in Pittsburg, KS

    Standard Disposal Installation in Pittsburg

    Most households in Pittsburg do well with a 1/3 to 1/2 horsepower continuous-feed unit. We remove the old unit if there is one, set a new flange in fresh plumber's putty, hang the disposal on a new mount and connect it to the trap with a new discharge tube and gasket. Then we wire it and test it. A smooth spin and a clean drain tell us it is right.

    Batch-Feed Disposal Installation in Pittsburg, KS

    Batch-feed units only run with the lid in place, and the lid holds a magnet that closes a switch inside the unit. No wall switch is needed, which makes them handy in kitchens where running a new switch would mean opening finished walls. They are slower to use. Parents of young kids often think that is worth it.

    Disposal Installation for Double Sinks in Pittsburg, KS

    Double bowl sinks put the disposal on one side and a continuous waste or end outlet tee on the other. The layout keeps both bowls draining and stops the disposal from pushing water into the second bowl, a problem when the tee is too close to the discharge. We use heavy-wall fittings.

    Commercial Disposal Setup in Pittsburg

    Restaurants and commercial kitchens in Pittsburg need disposals built for constant use. They usually need their own circuit, a bigger drain and a panel that runs water on its own, and some sewer districts restrict them, so we confirm the rules first. Downtime costs money.

    First-Time Disposal Installation in Pittsburg

    For kitchens that have never had a disposal, we start by checking the sink flange, drain height and electrical supply. The mount goes on, the unit gets hung and the discharge tube is connected to a new trap, with the wall stub lowered if it sits too high. An electrician adds power if needed.

    High-Horsepower Disposal Installation in Pittsburg

    A 3/4 or 1 HP unit takes up more room and weighs more than a basic unit, so we measure the cabinet first. Taller units can push the discharge outlet below the wall stub, and when that happens we either lower the drain at the wall or pick a model with a higher outlet so water does not sit in the chamber. Power needs a check too. Bigger motors draw more at startup.

    Disposal Installation on an Existing Sink in Pittsburg

    For a common 1/2 or 3/4 HP disposal, we remove the old unit if there is one and install the new one on the existing sink. We connect it to the drain, set the P-trap and wire it, then check the clearance under the sink, which can be tight in older Pittsburg homes with shallow cabinets or a water filter mounted on the back wall. Cramped cabinets take some planning. Sometimes a compact model is the better fit.

    Dishwasher Connection With Disposal Installation in Pittsburg

    Every new disposal has a plug sealing the dishwasher inlet. We knock it out with a screwdriver and hammer, fish the plug out of the chamber and connect the hose with a new clamp. A plug left in backs up the dishwasher. We also install an air gap or high loop, so dirty sink water cannot flow back into the dishwasher while the disposal is draining.
