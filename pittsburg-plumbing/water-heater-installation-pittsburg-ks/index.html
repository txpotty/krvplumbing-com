    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Installation in Pittsburg

    Common signs that you might need a new water heater include a lack of hot water, rusty or discolored water coming from the hot tap, banging or rumbling noises from the tank, visible leaks around the base of the unit and an age of 10 to 15 years or more. If you notice any of these issues, it is wise to have a professional inspection.

    Manufacturer warranties typically range from 6 to 12 years on the tank and parts. We also warranty our installation labor.

    A standard tank replacement usually takes two to four hours. Tankless installs or relocations can take most of a day.

    A traditional tank water heater typically lasts between 8 and 12 years with proper maintenance. Tankless water heaters often have a longer lifespan, ranging from 15 to 20 years, thanks to their durable components and on-demand operation.

    New water heaters usually come with a manufacturer's warranty on the tank and components, usually ranging from 6 to 12 years. KRV Plumbing also provides a labor warranty on our installation work, giving you peace of mind. We will discuss exact warranty details for your chosen unit during the consultation.

KRV Plumbing handles all necessary permit applications and inspections in Pittsburg as part of our service.
