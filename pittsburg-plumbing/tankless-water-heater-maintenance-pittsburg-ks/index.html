    Noritz Descaling in Pittsburg, KS

    Rinnai Tankless Service in Pittsburg

    Rinnai units tell you a lot through the controller. Code 11 means the burner never lit, and code 12 means the flame went out after it did. We clean the Rinnai air intake filter and look over the flame rod for soot or cracks.     Condensate and Neutralizer Service in Pittsburg

    Condensing tankless heaters drip acidic condensate, often around pH 3 to 4, and it needs to pass through a neutralizer before it reaches a drain. We clean the trap and look at the neutralizer media. Spent media gets replaced. An outdoor run of condensate line gets checked for support and protection.

    Maintenance for Every Tankless Brand in Pittsburg, KS

    We service Bosch, Rheem, Takagi and Navien heaters around Pittsburg, and each one has its own filter locations and code list in the manual. The manufacturer's steps come first, not a generic checklist. That matters for warranty claims down the road.

    Inlet Screen and Air Filter Cleaning in Pittsburg

    Grit gets in. A small screen at the cold inlet catches sand and scale flakes, especially after a main break. On units with an air intake filter, dust and lint load it up and the burner runs rich or throws a combustion code. We clean both on every visit.

    Tankless Error Code Diagnosis in Pittsburg, KS

    An error code is the heater telling you what it saw. On a Rinnai an LC code means scale, on a Navien E003 means no ignition, and on a Noritz a 90 usually means an air or exhaust problem. We pull the code history before touching anything. If a part has failed, you hear the repair and the price before we start.

    Recirculation and Combi Unit Service in Pittsburg, KS

    Built-in recirculation pumps and buffer tanks get hot water to the tap faster, but they also push more water through the exchanger every day, so those units scale sooner. We flush the recirculation loop along with the exchanger. Next we confirm crossover valve or dedicated return, and set the pump schedule to fit.

    Yearly Tankless Inspection in Pittsburg

    A yearly inspection costs less than one service call for a dead heater. We soap-test the gas connections and try the pressure relief valve. We pull the air filter and check the igniter. Anything that is wearing out goes on a written list with a plain price, and you decide what gets done now and what can wait until next year.
