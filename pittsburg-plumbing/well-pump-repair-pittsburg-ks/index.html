    Control Box and Electrical Repair in Pittsburg, KS

    Three-wire submersible pumps rely on a control box with a start capacitor and relay. Severe thunderstorms are common in Kansas, and heavy downpours can swamp gutters, storm drains, and older sewer lines. Lightning and power surges frequently damage these components. We test capacitors, relays and overload protection and replace failed parts. A surge protector at the control box can help prevent repeat failures.

    Pressure Tank Repair in Pittsburg, KS

    A waterlogged pressure tank makes the pump short cycle, turning on and off every few seconds. That rapid cycling burns out pumps prematurely. We check the air charge, inspect the bladder and recharge or replace the tank. Correct tank sizing protects the pump and gives more even pressure at the tap.
