    What Our Gas Line Plumbers Fix in Pittsburg, KS

    Gas Leak Detection in Pittsburg

    A rotten egg smell, hissing near a line, dead grass in a stripe across the yard or a gas meter that moves with everything off are the common leak signs. Trust your nose. Our plumbers isolate the system section by section, sweep it with an electronic detector and confirm the exact spot with soap solution. After we locate the leak, we price the repair in writing.

    Gas Line Relocation and Upgrades in Pittsburg, KS

    A kitchen remodel or a new tankless water heater can push a gas system past what its original pipe can deliver. Tankless units need a lot of gas. Many tankless heaters are rated at 150,000 to 199,000 BTU, which can be more than double what an old tank heater used, so the line feeding it often has to be upsized back toward the meter. We calculate the full load first. Then we size the new piping so every appliance in the house still gets full pressure at the same time.

    Interior Black Iron Pipe Repair in Pittsburg

    Threaded black iron pipe can leak at joints after years of vibration or settling. We tighten, reseal or replace fittings and sections with new pipe and gas-rated thread sealant. Rusted pipe goes too. Heavily rusted pipe in garages, crawlspaces or outside walls is replaced before it fails, and we pressure test the full system afterward.

    Appliance Connector and Shutoff Repair in Pittsburg

    Old flexible connectors, especially uncoated brass ones, crack and leak with age. We replace them with new coated stainless connectors sized for the appliance and add an accessible shutoff valve if the old one is missing or seized. This is a quick fix. Ranges, dryers, water heaters and furnaces all need proper connections, and most also need a sediment trap ahead of the appliance gas valve.

    Gas Permits and Inspections in Pittsburg

    Repairs that change piping, and all new gas lines, usually need a permit and inspection from Pittsburg building officials. We file the permit, schedule the inspection and are on site when the inspector checks the work and the test gauge. Inspection usually happens within a few days. After it passes, the gas company can restore service, and we can return to relight the pilots.

    Natural Gas Leak Detection and Repair in Pittsburg

    Finding a gas leak starts at the meter. With every appliance off, a moving test dial on the meter tells us gas is escaping somewhere, and then we isolate branches to narrow it down. An electronic detector does the rest. A spray of soap solution on the fitting shows bubbles at the leak, and we fix that joint or section and retest the whole system.

    Pool Heater, Fire Pit and Grill Lines in Pittsburg, KS

    Backyard gas lines for pool heaters, fire pits and built-in grills face sun, weather and irrigation water that shorten their life. Pool heaters are the big load, often 250,000 to 400,000 BTU, so a line that was fine for a grill can starve the heater and the house appliances at the same time. We size outdoor lines for the full load. Repairs and new runs are tested, buried at code depth and marked with tracer wire.
