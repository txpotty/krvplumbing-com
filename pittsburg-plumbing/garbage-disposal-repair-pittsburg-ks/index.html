    Electrical Issues and Motor Repair in Pittsburg

    A disposal that hums but will not grind has power but cannot turn, while one that is silent has no power at all. The two need different fixes. For a silent unit we test the breaker, reset and switch, and for a humming one we check the flywheel and motor before deciding if it is worth repairing.

    InSinkErator Disposal Repair in Pittsburg, KS

    InSinkErator makes the Badger line and the Evolution series, and we repair both.     Commercial Disposal Repair in Pittsburg

    Restaurant disposals run 1 to 5 horsepower and usually include a control box and a solenoid valve for water. When one stops, the dish line backs up fast. We check the controls first.     Electrical Problems and Resetting in Pittsburg

    No hum at all usually means no power reaching the motor.     Slow Draining Disposal Repair in Pittsburg, KS

    Good grinding but slow draining usually means the discharge tube or the trap arm is packed. Rice and coffee grounds are the usual culprits. We pull the trap, clear the tube and the arm with the right size cable, and check that the arm has at least a quarter inch of fall per foot toward the wall.

    Jammed Disposal Repair in Pittsburg

    Jams usually trace back to one hard object, like a peach pit, a chicken bone or a twist tie wrapped around the impeller. We free the flywheel from below with the hex key and pull the object with pliers, never a hand, then run it with water to make sure nothing else is stuck. The reset button may need a press. Check the trap arm if it jams again.
