    A maintenance check is a lighter version of a full inspection for owners who want to stay ahead of problems.     Leak Detection and Slab Screening in Pittsburg

    A spinning water meter with all fixtures off is a clear sign of a hidden leak. We also check for warm floor spots.     We measure static water pressure and check the pressure reducing valve if one is installed. High pressure ages fixtures fast.
