    Pipe Bursting and Pulling in Pittsburg

    Pipe bursting means splitting the old line in place while the new one follows behind it. It works best on straight runs. A cone-shaped head breaks the old pipe outward, while new HDPE comes through right behind the head. You end up with a fresh line and two small pits, and the lawn, sidewalk and driveway in between stay where they are, which is the whole reason people pick this method over a trench.

    After-Hours Water Line Breaks in Pittsburg, KS

    Water lines tend to break on a Saturday night. Ours is a 24 hour line. KRV Plumbing sends a plumber who can shut off the curb stop, pump the hole and make a permanent repair on the spot, which saves you from a weekend on a temporary patch. If the break is on the utility side of the meter, we tell you right away and help you get the provider out. That happens more than you would think.

    Underground Water Line Leak Repair in Pittsburg

    Underground leaks hide well. Many people find out from the bill before they see anything in the yard. Others notice a patch of grass that grows greener than the rest, or a spot near the walk that stays muddy days after rain. We confirm the leak at the meter, trace the pipe route and listen along it. The dig follows the sound.

    Trenchless Water Line Replacement in Pittsburg

    A trench through a paver driveway or a mature garden can cost more to restore than the pipe itself. Trenchless avoids most of that. We dig a pit at each end and pull HDPE or PEX through with a boring tool or pipe puller, either along the old route or through fresh soil next to it. Most replacements finish in a day. The new pipe carries its own manufacturer warranty.

    PEX Water Line Repair in Pittsburg, KS

    PEX itself rarely fails underground, but the joints and the first few feet near the house take a beating. Rodents chew it. A shovel or a fence post auger can nick it, and sunlight exposure before burial weakens the plastic over time. We cut out the damaged section and install new pipe with crimp, clamp or expansion fittings, matched to the PEX brand already in the ground.

    Slab Leak Water Line Repair in Pittsburg

    A slab leak is a supply pipe leaking under the concrete floor of the house. Newer construction mostly uses PEX and plastic drains, while older homes often mix copper, galvanized, and plastic from past repairs. Signs include a warm spot on the floor, the sound of running water and a meter that moves with everything off. Acoustic gear and a thermal camera tell us where it is. From there you have three choices: break the slab at the spot, reroute the line through the walls or attic, or abandon the old pipe under the floor and run new PEX above it. You get a price on every option.

    Underground Leak Detection in Pittsburg, KS

    A ground microphone picks up the hiss of water escaping a pressurized pipe, even through a couple of feet of dirt. Quiet leaks are harder. For those we push a hydrogen and nitrogen tracer gas into the drained line and sniff for it at the surface, which finds leaks the microphone cannot hear. Slab leaks and broken sewer laterals are common results of shifting clay, especially after long dry spells followed by heavy rain. You get a written price as soon as the leak is marked on the ground.

    PEX and Copper Water Line Repair in Pittsburg

    Copper and PEX fail in different places. Copper tends to pinhole where a rock rubs it or where the water chemistry eats the wall from inside. PEX usually fails at a fitting. We cut out the bad section of copper and sweat or press in a new length, while PEX gets a fresh crimp ring or expansion fitting with the right tool for the brand.
