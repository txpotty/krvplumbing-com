    Pool Water Leak Detection in Pittsburg, KS

    Pools lose a lot of water to evaporation in summer heat, so a leak can hide behind normal loss. We use a bucket test to compare evaporation with actual water loss. Then we pressure test pool plumbing and dye test fittings, lights and skimmers. Finding the leak protects the pool and the soil around it.

    Underground Water Line Leak Detection in Pittsburg

    Leaks in underground water lines, from your meter to your home, can go unnoticed for long periods, wasting water and causing yard erosion. Signs include oddly green patches in your lawn, persistent wet spots or a constantly running water meter. KRV Plumbing uses ground-penetrating radar and tracer gas detection methods to accurately map out and pinpoint leaks in buried pipes, allowing for targeted excavation and repair rather than digging up your entire yard.

    Water Heater and Softener Leak Detection in Pittsburg

    Water heaters and softeners can leak at fittings, valves and drain lines. A softener stuck in regeneration can send water down the drain continuously, and a dripping relief valve on a water heater wastes water too. We check these units carefully and repair or replace failed parts. That often explains a mystery bill in homes with water softeners.
