    Wayne Sump Pump Service in Hutchinson, KS

    Some Wayne models draw water through a top suction intake, which helps them avoid pulling in the sludge that settles at the bottom of a pit. That intake gets cleaned, and so does the float mechanism. Any brand can rattle its discharge fittings loose over time. Easy fix.

    Commercial Building Sump Pumps in Hutchinson, KS

    Parking garages, mechanical rooms and loading docks often drain to large pits with pumps rated at a horsepower or more. They need checking more often than anything in a house. Our crew tests the controls, the alarm panel and each pump in a duplex station, and records amp draw every visit so a slow decline gets caught. Downtime costs money.

    Battery Backup System Testing in Hutchinson, KS

    Storms knock out power when the pit fills fastest. A backup pump exists for exactly that hour. We check the battery date, clean the terminals and confirm the charger is actually charging, since a dead charger quietly drains the battery to nothing over a few months.

    Home Sump Pump Maintenance in Hutchinson

    A typical house pit is about 18 inches across and two feet deep, and most of what goes wrong happens inside it. Our plumber lifts the pump out, cleans the screen and empties the sediment from the bottom. Then we run a full water test. Much of Kansas has expansive clay soil that swells when wet and shrinks in drought, which can move foundations and stress buried pipes.

    Elevator Pit and Garage Pumps in Hutchinson

    Elevator pits, parking garages and mechanical rooms all collect water that has nowhere to go by gravity, so a pump has to lift it out. In elevator pits, the pump usually needs an oil-sensing control so it will not discharge hydraulic fluid. We test duplex systems and high-water alarms on a set schedule. The property manager gets a copy of every log.

    Emergency Sump Pump Diagnostics in Hutchinson, KS

    Grinding, clicking and a hum with no water moving each point to a different failure. We start with the outlet and the cord, then the float, then the motor. A tripped GFCI is a quick fix. A seized motor calls for a new pump, so we carry replacements and never leave the pit unprotected overnight.

    Float Switch Repair and Adjustment in Hutchinson, KS

    Float trouble causes more no-start calls than anything else. A tethered float can tangle on the pump body or catch on the side of the basin. Vertical floats hang up once minerals coat the rod. In tight pits where nothing has room to swing, we can switch you to an electronic sensor switch with no moving float at all.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Maintenance in Hutchinson, KS

    Backup pumps sit in the pit next to the main pump and run off a 12-volt battery when the power drops or the primary fails. Our plumber load tests the battery instead of just reading its voltage. Next, we cut power to the main pump and watch the backup clear the basin by itself. Alarms get tested too.
