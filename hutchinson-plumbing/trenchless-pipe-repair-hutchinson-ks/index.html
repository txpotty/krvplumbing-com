    Trenchless Water Line Replacement in Hutchinson, KS

    Main water lines can also be replaced without a trench. Bursting or boring works. New HDPE or PEX goes in along the old path by bursting, or along a new one by directional boring, leaving lawns and drives alone. The line is pressure tested before service is restored.

    Perma-Liner Trenchless Systems in Hutchinson

    Perma-Liner is one of the CIPP systems our crews install. It's built for sewer use. Its liners cure into a structural pipe that holds even if the old host pipe keeps falling apart around it. They handle harsh soil and flow.

    Pipe Bursting in Hutchinson, KS

    Bursting is used when the old pipe is too damaged to line or needs a larger diameter. You'll see an entry and exit pit. The head fractures the old pipe as it travels, with new HDPE trailing behind, fused into one length with no joints.

    Same-Day Sewer Assessment in Hutchinson, KS

    Sewer lines that fail suddenly can often be cleared and scoped the same day. Then we schedule the fix. When lining fits, the repair gets booked fast, and until then we keep it flowing and tell you what not to send down the drain.

    CIPP Lining for Clay and Cast Iron in Hutchinson

    Older Hutchinson homes often have clay or cast iron sewer pipe. Both fail with age. Clay joints let roots in and cast iron scales and rusts through, and a liner seals both problems with a new jointless pipe that often outlasts the original material by decades. Only one or two access points are needed.

    Trenchless vs Traditional Costs in Hutchinson, KS

    On paper, the two methods can price out close. Then add restoration. A trench under a driveway or through an established garden means paying to replace concrete, plants and irrigation after the plumbing is done, and trenchless avoids most of those costs. We price both side by side.

    Cured-in-Place Pipe (CIPP) Lining in Hutchinson

    A CIPP liner is a felt tube soaked in resin. The resin cures in place. We pull or invert the tube into the cleaned line, inflate it against the pipe wall and cure it with hot water, steam or UV light, which leaves a jointless pipe inside the old one. It fixes cracks, breaks and roots without digging up your Hutchinson yard.
