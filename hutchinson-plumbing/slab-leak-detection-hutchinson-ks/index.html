    Acoustic Slab Leak Detection in Hutchinson

    Water escaping a pressurized pipe makes a hiss that carries up through concrete. A ground microphone picks it up.     Supply and Drain Leaks Under the Slab in Hutchinson, KS

    A supply leak and a drain leak show different signs. Supply leaks are pressurized, so they move the meter, warm the floor on the hot side and run whether or not anyone uses water. Drain leaks only leak when water goes down, showing up as smells, sluggish drains or wet spots near bathrooms. We test both kinds in your Hutchinson, KS home.

    Non-Invasive Slab Leak Locating in Hutchinson, KS

    Post-Tension Slab Leak Detection in Hutchinson

    Many newer homes sit on post-tension slabs. These slabs have steel tendons stretched under tens of thousands of pounds of force, and cutting one by mistake is dangerous and can damage the foundation. We scan for tendons with ground-penetrating radar or a rebar locator before opening anything, or recommend a reroute that never touches the concrete.
