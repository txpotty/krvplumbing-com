    Electronic Acoustic Leak Detection in Hutchinson

    Our team uses electronic listening devices to pinpoint the exact location of a slab leak.     Repiping After Multiple Slab Leaks in Hutchinson, KS

    If your home has had two or more slab leaks, the remaining pipe is likely corroded too. Repiping replaces all supply lines with new PEX or copper run through the walls and attic. It is a bigger project, but it ends the cycle of repeat slab leaks. We can usually complete a full repipe in two to four days.

    Electronic Leak Detection in Hutchinson

    For hard-to-find leaks, our electronic leak detection equipment sends a signal through your pipes and measures changes that indicate a breach. This technology allows us to locate leaks in various pipe materials, including copper, PEX and PVC, commonly found in Hutchinson homes. It helps prevent exploratory digging, saving you time and money.

    Slab Leak Repair Through Direct Access in Hutchinson, KS

This technique is particularly useful for hot water line leaks and helps us narrow down the search area significantly without any physical disruption to your property in Hutchinson.
