    Preventive Pipe Winterization in Hutchinson

    Winterizing before the first hard freeze costs far less than a burst pipe repair. We walk the house and mark every exposed run. We insulate attic lines, cover vents, upgrade outdoor faucets and drain irrigation lines.

    Outdoor Faucet and Sprinkler Line Freezing in Hutchinson, KS

    Outdoor plumbing is the first to freeze, especially a spigot with a hose still attached. The hose traps water in the valve. Irrigation backflow preventers sit above ground with no insulation, and their brass bodies and test cocks crack easily, so we drain or cover them before the first freeze and repair them when they split.

    Pipe Insulation and Heat Cable Installation in Hutchinson, KS

    Insulation slows heat loss, but it does not add heat. For truly exposed runs, we add heat cable. We install self-regulating cable that adjusts its output to the pipe temperature, plugged into a GFCI outlet, and cover it with insulation so it uses less power and keeps the pipe above freezing on the coldest nights.

    Backflow Preventer and Irrigation Freeze Repair in Hutchinson

    Backflow preventers and irrigation valves sit above ground and take the coldest air directly. Their brass bodies split easily. We repair or replace the damaged assembly, have it tested if your water utility requires it, and show you how to drain it or fit an insulated cover before the next freeze.
