    Dependable InSinkErator Installation in Hutchinson, KS

    Standard InSinkErator Installation in Hutchinson

    A standard install goes into a sink that already has a drain and power. The old disposal comes down, the flange gets scraped clean and a new one goes in on fresh putty. Then the mount ring goes on. The unit hangs on the ring, locks with a quarter turn, and the discharge tube meets the trap on a new slip-joint washer. We connect power through a cord kit or the old hardwire and confirm the ground first.

    New Construction Disposal Installs in Hutchinson

    New builds and gut remodels get the disposal in two trips. At rough-in we set the drain height and confirm a switched outlet is in the sink base. We hold the unit back for now. Once the countertop and sink are in and the faucet is set, we come back to hang the unit, connect the dishwasher and test everything with the water on.

    Badger Series Installation in Hutchinson, KS

    The Badger line runs from the 1/3 HP Badger 1 up to the Badger 5XP. Every Badger shares one quick-lock mount. Because of that, a Badger-for-Badger swap can take under an hour, and the flange often stays if it is still clean. Badgers ship without a cord, so we bring the cord kit.

    Evolution Series Installation in Hutchinson

    Evolution units grind in two or three stages and carry extra sound insulation around the motor housing, which is why they sit taller and weigh more than a Badger. Clearance under the bowl matters here. Before install day we measure the drop from sink to trap arm so the unit fits. Tight cabinets suit the Compact, while the Excel wants the most space.

    Evolution Upgrade Installs in Hutchinson, KS

    Quiet is the main thing people notice after an Evolution install. Insulation around the motor drops the noise a lot. What they notice later is fewer clogs downstream, because the multi-stage grind breaks fibrous scraps into much smaller pieces before they reach the trap and the branch line. With the motor running, we check the mount for vibration.

    Air Switch Disposal Installation in Hutchinson

    An air switch puts a push button on the countertop or sink deck instead of a wall switch. The button pushes a puff of air down a thin tube. That pulse trips a switch in a control box plugged in under the sink, so no live wiring sits anywhere near the water, which is the main reason people like them. We drill the counter or use the spare faucet hole.

    Hard Water and Disposals in Hutchinson

    Many Kansas homeowners use water softeners to extend the life of their tank water heaters and fixtures against heavy mineral buildup. Scale builds up on grind rings and drain tailpieces over the years. Scale makes a disposal noisy. It also drains slower, because mineral crust narrows the tailpiece and the trap, so we look inside the old drain parts before we connect anything new to them. If they are crusted badly, we swap them while we are there.

    Garbage Disposal Replacement in Hutchinson

    If your current disposal hums, leaks from the bottom or trips its reset button every week, replacement usually beats repair. A leak from the housing means the internal seal is gone. That seal cannot be fixed. We disconnect the old unit, cap the dishwasher hose while we work and set the new disposal on the same drain geometry, adjusting the tailpiece if the new one sits at a different height.
