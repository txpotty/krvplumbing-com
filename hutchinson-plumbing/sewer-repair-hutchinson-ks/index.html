    Orangeburg and Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Hutchinson

    Some older homes still have Orangeburg pipe, a tar-paper material that flattens and blisters over time. Cast iron from the same era corrodes from the inside and scales up until little flow remains. When a camera shows widespread failure, patching is a waste of money. We replace the line in sections or end to end with PVC, and we can usually use trenchless methods to avoid tearing up the whole yard.

    Sewer Line Backups & Blockages in Hutchinson

    Sewer backups are often caused by severe blockages from grease buildup, foreign objects or extensive tree root intrusion. Our team uses advanced sewer camera inspections to identify the exact cause and location of the blockage. We then use hydro-jetting or mechanical snaking to clear the obstruction, restoring proper flow to your system and preventing further damage. This approach is very effective for the stubborn clogs often found in older Kansas homes.

    Sewer Repair Cost Comparison in Hutchinson, KS

    A single spot repair is the most affordable fix when the rest of the line is sound. Pipe lining costs more per foot but avoids excavation and works well for long runs under driveways or landscaping. Full replacement is the right call when the pipe is Orangeburg, badly bellied or broken in several places. We lay out all three options with real numbers so you can compare cost against how long the repair will last.

    Bellied Sewer Line Correction in Hutchinson, KS

    A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged and holds standing water and solids. Bellies often form where the original bedding washed out or where the soil settled after heavy rain. Much of Kansas has expansive clay soil that swells when wet and shrinks in drought, which can shift foundations and stress buried pipes. We confirm the depth of standing water on camera, then re-bed and re-grade that section to restore proper fall. Correct slope is typically a quarter inch per foot for a typical residential line.
