    Whole-Home PEX Replacement in Hutchinson, KS

    PEX-A is the most common replacement for polybutylene because it is flexible, scale resistant and easy to route. Our crews use Uponor PEX-A and full-bore fittings.     Finding Polybutylene Leaks in Hutchinson

    A poly leak inside a wall or ceiling can run for days before you see a stain. The meter often tells first. With every fixture off, a spinning leak indicator on the meter means water is going somewhere, and we use acoustic listening gear and thermal imaging to find it without opening walls at random. Finding it lets us make a temporary repair and plan the full replacement.

    Yard Service Line Replacement in Hutchinson, KS

    Many poly-era homes also have a blue or black polybutylene service line running from the water meter to the house. Buried poly service lines split, run up bills and saturate the yard.
