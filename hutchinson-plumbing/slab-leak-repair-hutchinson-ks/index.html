    Direct Slab Leak Repair in Hutchinson, KS

    When a slab leak is localized and accessible, direct repair is often the most efficient solution.     Pipe Rerouting and Repiping in Hutchinson

    For more extensive damage, multiple leaks, or pipes that are too old and corroded to repair reliably, pipe rerouting is a long-term solution. This involves abandoning the leaking line under the slab and installing a new pipe through the walls or attic. This method skips the problem area entirely, providing a fresh, durable plumbing line. It is a common solution for aging homes in Hutchinson with original copper or galvanized plumbing.
