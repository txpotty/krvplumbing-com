    Whether your home has modern PEX piping or traditional copper lines, our technicians are skilled in repairing both.     Pressure Regulator and Shutoff Repair in Hutchinson

    Many water line problems are made worse by high incoming pressure.     Galvanized Water Line Replacement in Hutchinson, KS

    Many homes built before the 1970s still have galvanized steel service lines that rust from the inside. Water across much of Kansas runs quite hard, leaving mineral scale in water heaters, faucets, showerheads, and dishwashers. Hard water speeds up scale buildup, which chokes flow and causes rust-colored water. Patching galvanized pipe seldom holds up because the surrounding pipe is just as corroded. We usually recommend replacing the full run from meter to house with copper or PEX, which restores pressure and ends repeat leaks.
