    Wall-Hung Toilet Installation in Hutchinson, KS

    Wall-hung toilets mount to a steel carrier inside the wall, with the tank hidden behind an access plate. They free up floor space and make cleaning simple, but they need a wall at least 2 by 6 deep or a furred-out chase. We install Geberit, Grohe and Kohler carrier systems and set the bowl height to your preference. This work is best done during a remodel when the wall is open.

    Comfort-Height and ADA Toilet Installation in Hutchinson

    Comfort-height toilets put the seat at about 17 to 19 inches, which is easier on knees and backs. For accessible bathrooms, we follow ADA guidelines for seat height, centerline distance from the side wall and grab bar clearance. We can install elongated bowls and add blocking for grab bars while the wall is open. These upgrades are popular with homeowners planning to age in place.

    Comfort Height Toilet Installation in Hutchinson, KS

    Most standard toilet installations can be completed within 1 to 3 hours. More complex installations, such as wall-mounted or smart toilets, can take longer due to additional plumbing or electrical work.

    Common signs you might need a new toilet include frequent clogs, cracks in the porcelain bowl or tank, constant running water, a wobbly base, or a toilet that is very old and inefficient. Upgrading can save water and improve the look of your bathroom.

    A one-piece toilet has the bowl and tank integrated into a single unit, offering a sleek look and easier cleaning. A two-piece toilet has a separate bowl and tank, which are bolted together during installation. Both types are widely available and perform well.
