    Garbage Disposal Problems We Fix in Hutchinson, KS

    Humming Disposal Repair in Hutchinson

    Humming with no spin usually means something is stuck. Bones, pits, glass and spoons are the usual suspects. With the power off, we free the flywheel using the hex socket underneath, pull the object, and test the motor and reset with water running.

    Flange and Inlet Leak Repair in Hutchinson, KS

    The flange, dishwasher inlet, discharge tube and body are where leaks start. We trace the leak, reseal the flange with plumber's putty and replace gaskets or tubes as needed. A leak from the bottom of the unit usually means the internal seal has failed, and in that case replacement is the better option. It is not a repairable part.

    Noisy Disposal Repair in Hutchinson

    Grinding, rattling or a hum that sounds rough usually mean trouble inside the unit. Loose mounting rings rattle too. We check the mount first, then the chamber for debris, then listen to the motor with the chamber empty, because a motor that whines with nothing in it has bad bearings.

    Electrical Problems and Resetting in Hutchinson

    No hum at all usually means no power reaching the motor. We start at the red reset button on the bottom, then check the breaker, the wall switch and the outlet or junction box under the sink with a meter. Cabinets are damp places. Corroded wire nuts and loose cord connections are common in that damp space, and we replace them rather than just tightening them back down.

    Grinding Noise Repair in Hutchinson

    Loud metal-on-metal sounds may mean a loose mount, a foreign object or worn impellers. We inspect the chamber, tighten the mounting ring and remove any debris, then run the unit empty to hear whether the motor itself is making the noise. Failing bearings are not worth fixing.

    Emergency Disposal Repair in Hutchinson, KS

    Water on the floor beats every other problem. Turn the disposal off, stop the dishwasher if it is running, and close the angle stops under the sink so the faucet cannot add more water to a backed up bowl. After that, call. We can usually stop the leak the same day and either repair the unit or quote a replacement before we leave.

    Jam Clearing and Motor Repair in Hutchinson

    Bones, fruit pits and bottle caps wedge between the impeller lugs and the grind ring, and the motor stalls. We shut off power, insert a 1/4 inch hex key into the socket on the bottom of the unit and work it back and forth until the flywheel turns freely. Then the object comes out with pliers. A motor that stalled for a long time may have overheated, so we test it under load before calling the job done.

    Cost-Effective Disposal Repair in Hutchinson, KS

    Paying half the price of a new disposal for a repair seldom pays off, and we will say so. Many fixes are cheap. A reset, a cleared jam, a new switch or a resealed flange usually costs far less than a new unit and can keep a younger disposal going for years.
