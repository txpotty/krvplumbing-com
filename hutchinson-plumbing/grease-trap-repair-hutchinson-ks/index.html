    Grease Trap Repair vs Replacement Cost in Hutchinson, KS

    Age and material decide most repair versus replace calls. Once a steel unit has rust holes in the body, it is finished.     Commercial Grease Interceptor Repair in Hutchinson, KS

    In-ground gravity interceptors run from about 500 gallons to several thousand, usually precast concrete with two compartments.
