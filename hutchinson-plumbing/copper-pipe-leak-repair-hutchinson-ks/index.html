    Corrosion-Related Leak Repair in Hutchinson

    Corrosion is a natural enemy of copper pipes and is made worse by certain water chemistries. Water across much of Kansas runs hard, leaving mineral scale in water heaters, faucets, showerheads, and dishwashers. This can lead to thinning pipe walls and eventual leaks. Our team assesses the extent of corrosion, which may show up as blue-green stains on pipes or fixtures. For localized corrosion, we perform spot repairs, but if corrosion is widespread, we may recommend re-piping sections to prevent recurring issues.     Copper Pipe Joint Leak Repair in Hutchinson, KS

    Leaks at pipe joints are usually due to improper soldering, fatigued connections or shifting foundations.
