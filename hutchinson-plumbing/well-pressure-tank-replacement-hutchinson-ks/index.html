    The tank tee is the manifold that connects the tank, switch, gauge, relief valve and drain. Galvanized tees corrode and restrict flow, especially with sandy or mineral-heavy water. These small parts make a big difference in how well the system holds up.

    Well Pressure Tank Leak Repair vs. Replacement in Hutchinson

    When your well pressure tank shows signs of trouble, it is important to determine if a repair is feasible or if full replacement is necessary. Small air leaks or issues with the air valve might be repairable. However, if the tank itself is corroded, has a ruptured diaphragm, or is waterlogged, replacement is almost always the more cost-effective and reliable option. Our technicians in Hutchinson provide an honest assessment, explaining the pros and cons of repair versus a complete well pressure tank replacement, so you can make an informed decision.
