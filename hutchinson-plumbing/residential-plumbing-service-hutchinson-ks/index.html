    Slab Leak Detection & Repair in Hutchinson

    Slab leaks are a serious concern for homes built on concrete foundations. Much of Kansas has expansive clay soil that swells when wet and shrinks in drought, which can move foundations and stress buried pipes. Our technicians use modern, non-invasive acoustic and thermal imaging technology to accurately pinpoint the exact location of the leak under your foundation. Early detection is crucial to prevent significant structural damage and costly water waste. Once located, we provide efficient repair options, which may include direct access, rerouting or pipe bursting, always choosing the least disruptive method for your home.
