    Residential Plumbing Service in Hutchinson, KS: What We Fix

    Leak Repair and Slab Leak Service in Hutchinson

    On a slab foundation, a supply leak can run for weeks unseen. The usual clues are a warm patch of floor, a meter dial moving with everything off, or a sudden jump in the water bill. Nothing gets quoted until the leak is pinpointed. Repeat leaks usually point to a repipe instead of another patch.

    Sump Pumps and Basement Drainage in Hutchinson

    A sump pump only gets tested when it rains hard. We install and replace cast iron submersible pumps, check float switches and replace worn check valves, and we can add a battery backup so the pit keeps draining when the power goes out in a storm. Pour a bucket in to test it.

    Emergency Plumbing Repairs in Hutchinson

    Sewage in the tub at night counts as an emergency. The plumber shows up with a drain machine and a shop vac, clears the line, and checks whether the backup came from a simple clog or a main line that needs a camera and a real repair. We tell you which one it was before leaving.

    Slab Leak Location and Repair in Hutchinson

    Slab leaks hide under concrete until something gives them away. Much of Kansas has expansive clay soil that swells when wet and shrinks in drought, which can move foundations and stress buried pipes. Our techs find them with ground microphones and thermal imaging, so the only concrete we break is the small patch right over the leak, not half the kitchen floor. Then you pick the fix. Some leaks get a spot repair, others a reroute overhead that skips the slab entirely.

    Water Heater Repair and Installation in Hutchinson

    A water heater works hardest in winter, when incoming water is coldest. We work on gas, electric, tank and tankless heaters from Rheem and Bradford White, and the usual culprits are a heavy layer of sediment or a thermocouple that has quit reading the pilot. No hot water is often a quick part swap. New units get an expansion tank and pan when the code requires it.

    Gas Lines for New Appliances in Hutchinson, KS

    Switching from an electric range to gas means a new line and a shutoff. We size the pipe for the appliance's BTU load, run it in black iron or CSST, bond it where the code requires and pressure-test before the gas company or inspector signs off on the work. Then we light it and check the flame.

    Faucet and Fixture Repair in Hutchinson

    Most dripping faucets need a cartridge, not a new faucet. We carry cartridges for Moen, Delta, Kohler and Pfister, and when you do want new fixtures we install them, connect new braided supply lines, and check the angle stops under the sink while we are there. Old angle stops often break when turned.

    Pressure Regulators and Shutoff Valves in Hutchinson, KS

    Water pressure over 80 psi wears out hoses and valves early. A gauge on the hose bib tells us your static pressure in a minute, and if it reads high we install or replace a pressure reducing valve and set it in the 50 to 70 psi range most fixtures are rated for. Seized main shutoffs get replaced too.
