    Frequently Asked Questions About Hydro Jetting in Hutchinson

    Expect $300 to $800 in Hutchinson. Severity changes the price. A longer or wider pipe costs more to jet, and so does a line packed with roots or a long commercial run, with a written estimate before work.

    Spartan Tool and MyTana jetters. These are professional machines. We run these brands because they hold up day after day and give us the right pressure and flow for each job.

    A thorough jetting often keeps a line clear for a year or more, depending on use and roots. Lines with heavy root intrusion may need regular maintenance or a repair.

    Sound PVC, cast iron and concrete handle it fine. Damaged pipe does not. Severely corroded, cracked or fragile pipe can be damaged further, which is why a camera inspection before jetting is part of every job we do.
