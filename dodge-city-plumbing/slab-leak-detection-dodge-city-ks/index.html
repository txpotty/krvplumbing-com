    Slab Leak Reroutes in Dodge City

    Rerouting leaves the failed pipe in the slab. New pipe runs through the walls or attic instead, so there is no concrete work and the cost is often lower than going through tile. We usually use PEX because it handles heat and resists corrosion. It suits tile and hardwood homes.

    Repiping After Repeat Slab Leaks in Dodge City, KS

    A second slab leak is often a sign of more. The rest of the pipe has likely aged the same way, so repiping replaces every supply line with new PEX or copper through the walls and attic, which ends the pattern of new leaks and new floor openings. Most repipes wrap up in two to four days.

    Pressure Testing and Isolation in Dodge City

    Pressure isolation is always the first step. Our tech closes the main, caps or valves off the hot and cold sides, and puts a gauge on each, then watches which side loses pressure over a set time. The gauge points us to the right line. It also rules out a toilet or faucet leak masquerading as a slab leak in your Dodge City, KS home.

    Post-Tension Slab Leak Detection in Dodge City

    A lot of newer houses are built on post-tension slabs.     Slab Leaks in Older Homes in Dodge City, KS

    Older homes in Dodge City, KS often have soft copper or galvanized steel under the slab, and both wear out with time.
