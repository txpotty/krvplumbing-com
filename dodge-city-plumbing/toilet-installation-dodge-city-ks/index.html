    Frequently Asked Questions About Toilet Installation in Dodge City

    Labor to install a customer-supplied standard toilet typically runs $200 to $400. Including a mid-range toilet, most installs total $450 to $900, while wall-hung systems with carriers range from $1,500 to $3,500 or more. We give you a firm price up front.

    KRV Plumbing stands behind our workmanship. We offer a warranty on all our toilet installations, covering labor and making sure your new toilet works as expected. Specific warranty details will be provided at the time of service.

    Toilets carry manufacturer warranties, often 1 to 5 years on parts and longer on porcelain. We also guarantee our installation workmanship and will come back to fix any leak caused by the install.

    A comfort height toilet has a bowl that is taller than standard models, typically around 17 to 19 inches from the floor to the top of the seat. This height is closer to a standard chair, making it more comfortable for many adults, especially those with mobility issues.

    A one-piece toilet has the bowl and tank integrated into a single unit, offering a sleek look and simpler cleaning. A two-piece toilet has a separate bowl and tank, which are bolted together during installation. Both types are easy to find and perform well.

    We are skilled at installing all major toilet brands, including Kohler, American Standard, Toto, Delta, Gerber, and many others. Our plumbers are familiar with the specific requirements for each manufacturer to ensure a proper and efficient installation.

    The rough-in is the distance from the finished wall to the center of the drain, usually 12 inches but sometimes 10 or 14. Buying a toilet with the wrong rough-in means it will not fit or will sit too far from the wall.

    When choosing a new toilet, consider factors like bowl shape (round vs. elongated), height (standard vs. ADA-compliant), flushing system (gravity-fed vs. pressure-assisted), water efficiency (gallons per flush), and overall style to match your bathroom. We can help you navigate these choices.
