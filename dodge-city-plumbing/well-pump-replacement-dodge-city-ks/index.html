    Common signs include no water from your faucets, weak or inconsistent water pressure, strange noises from the well or pressure tank area, short cycling where the pump turns on and off frequently, or cloudy or muddy water.     The lifespan of a well pump varies widely depending on usage, water quality and pump type, but most last between 10 and 20 years. Regular maintenance and proper sizing can extend its life, but eventually wear and tear will call for replacement.
