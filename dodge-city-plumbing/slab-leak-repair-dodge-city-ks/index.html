    Slab Leak Reroute in Dodge City

    Older copper under a slab tends to leak again. A reroute stops the cycle. We leave the leaking run in the ground and pull new PEX through the attic and down the walls, cutting openings where they are easiest to patch, often behind a vanity or in a closet.

    Pipe Rerouting and Repiping in Dodge City

    Old galvanized or thin copper is hard to patch reliably. A reroute bypasses it. Dodge City homes with original plumbing see this often.
