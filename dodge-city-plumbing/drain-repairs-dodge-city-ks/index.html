    Broken Drain Pipe Repair in Dodge City, KS

    Broken drain pipe usually comes from settling, roots or age. Sometimes it is a bad glue joint. We find the break with a camera and a locator, then cut out the damaged length and install new PVC or cast iron with transition couplings sized to both pipes. We test it before closing up.

    Laundry and Floor Drain Repair in Dodge City

    A washer dumps a lot of water fast. Undersized drains spill over. We correct standpipe height, replace cracked laundry box drains and add venting where it is missing, then refill or repair floor drain traps so they hold water and block sewer gas. Trap primers get repaired too.

    Preventive Drain Maintenance in Dodge City

    Scheduled cleaning keeps buildup from turning into a repair. Once a year is common. For older homes in Dodge City with cast iron or a history of kitchen clogs, a yearly jetting and camera check catches small defects before they back up. Hard water lines benefit most.

    Storm Drain Repair in Dodge City, KS

    Yard and storm drains carry rain away from the foundation. Drought and watering limits also affect Kansas, and dry clay soil pulling away from pipes can lead to cracks and leaks. Leaves and silt clog them. We clear the line with a jetter, camera it to look for crushed or separated sections, and replace damaged corrugated or PVC pipe so water flows to the outlet instead of pooling against the house. A clean outlet matters too.

    Kitchen Branch Line Repair in Dodge City

    This branch takes more grease and food than any other line in most homes. The grease goes far down the pipe. We replace cracked tailpieces, fix slip nuts and correct trap height, and when grease has built up deep in the branch, we jet it clean and check the slope so it does not come right back. Dishwasher and disposal connections get checked.

    Drain Vent Repair in Dodge City, KS

    Gurgling and slow draining often mean the vent is blocked. Air cannot get in behind the water. Without air, draining water pulls the trap seal along with it and lets sewer gas into the room, so we check the roof vent for nests or debris and look for cracked or disconnected vent pipe in the walls. Clearing it is often quick.

    Vent Pipe Repair in Dodge City

    Sewer gas leaking into a room can point to a missing vent. Remodels cause a lot of these. Bird nests, debris and roofing work can block existing vents, while fixtures that never had a vent can sometimes get an air admittance valve if code allows. Good venting keeps all trap seals intact.

    Clogged Drain Repair in Dodge City

    Grease, hair, food scraps and mineral scale cause most clogs. Some come back fast. We clear the line with a drum auger or a jetter, then run a camera if the clog returns within a few weeks or sits more than a few feet past the trap, which shows whether you have buildup, a sag or a broken fitting. The footage tells us what to fix.
