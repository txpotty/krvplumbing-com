    Reduced Pressure Zone (RPZ) Assembly Installation in Dodge City

    Two independent checks and a relief valve make up an RPZ. When pressure between the checks gets too close to supply pressure, the relief opens, dumping water to the floor or a drain so nothing can pass upstream, which is why we never bury one in a pit in Dodge City. Brands include Watts and Febco.

    Residential Backflow Devices in Dodge City

    Homes with irrigation or a fire sprinkler line often need a device to keep that water out of the municipal supply. We pick the assembly based on the hazard, then mount it where the tester can reach it. Hard freezes happen every winter in Kansas, and pipes in exterior walls, garages, and crawlspaces are the most at risk. Exposed devices get freeze-rated parts and a cover.

    Reduced Pressure Zone (RPZ) Device Installation in Dodge City, KS

    High-hazard connections in Dodge City, KS call for a reduced pressure zone assembly. Think chemical injection or medical equipment, where a backflow event could put something toxic in the supply, so the RPZ adds a relief valve between two checks that dumps water if either check leaks. It has to drain somewhere.

    Double Check Valve Assembly Installation in Dodge City

    Double check assemblies work for low-hazard connections such as many fire lines and some irrigation. They have two spring-loaded checks and test cocks for annual certification, and since they do not discharge water, they can go in a vault or below grade where local rules allow, sized so the pressure loss stays small. Flow drives the size.

    Pool and Spa Fill Backflow Protection in Dodge City, KS

    Pool auto-fill lines and hose connections give chlorinated water a straight path back to the supply. We add the right device on the fill line, check that every hose bibb has a vacuum breaker, and can fold the work into a pool build or remodel so it is done before the deck goes in. A hose bibb vacuum breaker costs a few dollars.
