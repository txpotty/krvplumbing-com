    Backflow Preventer Testing in Dodge City

    Backflow preventers protect the drinking water supply from contamination, and many water providers call for annual testing for irrigation and commercial devices. Our certified testers check each valve and file results with your water utility as required. If a device fails, we repair or replace the internal parts. We also check that devices are protected from heat and freezing where necessary. Keeping up with testing avoids fines and water shutoffs.

    Water Softener Maintenance in Dodge City

    Water softeners combat hard water, protecting your pipes and appliances from mineral scale. Our maintenance service includes checking salt levels, cleaning the brine tank, confirming the regeneration cycle works correctly, and inspecting valves and resin beds. Proper softener maintenance extends its life and keeps soft water flowing to your home, preventing costly scale damage.

    Water Heater Maintenance in Dodge City

    Regular water heater maintenance is key to preventing sediment buildup. Water across much of Kansas runs hard, leaving mineral scale in water heaters, faucets, showerheads, and dishwashers. Our service includes flushing the tank, inspecting the anode rod for corrosion, checking the temperature and pressure relief valve, and examining all connections and heating elements. This extends the life of your unit, boosts efficiency, and helps avoid sudden cold showers or leaks.

    Drainage Services and Hydrojet Plumbing in Dodge City

    Preventive drain maintenance is key to avoiding clogs and sewer backups. We offer routine drain camera inspections to find grease buildup, roots, or other obstructions before they cause a problem. Our hydrojet plumbing service can clear out stubborn blockages, keeping your drainage system clear and free-flowing, which is especially important for homes with mature trees near the sewer line.

    Sewer Line and Drain Camera Inspection in Dodge City, KS

    A camera inspection shows the condition of your sewer line and main drains. We look for roots, bellies, cracks and buildup before they cause a blockage. Older homes with clay or cast iron lines benefit most from regular checks. We record the inspection and give you a copy. If cleaning or repair is needed, we explain your options.

    Sump Pump Maintenance in Dodge City, KS

    Severe thunderstorms are common in Kansas, and heavy downpours can overwhelm gutters, storm drains, and older sewer lines. A working sump pump is essential for homes with basements or crawl spaces when heavy rain overwhelms drainage. Our maintenance includes testing the float switch, checking for proper discharge, cleaning the pit of debris, and inspecting the check valve. That way your sump pump is ready to protect your home from flooding when sudden storms hit Dodge City.
