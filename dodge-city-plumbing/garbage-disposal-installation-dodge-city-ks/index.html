    Switching From Batch-Feed to Continuous-Feed in Dodge City, KS

    A continuous-feed unit needs a switch, which a batch-feed kitchen may not have. That can be a wall switch, an air switch button on the sink deck or a switched outlet under the sink, and the choice depends on how hard it is to run wire to the spot you want. Air switches avoid new wiring in the wall. The plumbing side is usually a straight swap.

    Air Switch Disposal Installation in Dodge City, KS

    Air switches start the disposal with a button on the sink or counter. The button pushes air through a tube to a control box in the cabinet, which keeps electricity away from the button and avoids a new wall switch. Islands use them often.
