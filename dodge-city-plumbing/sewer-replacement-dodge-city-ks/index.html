    Clay and Orangeburg Pipe Replacement in Dodge City, KS

    Clay pipe came in short sections with a joint every two or three feet. That is a lot of joints for roots to find. Orangeburg is worse, since the tar-paper wall softens and deforms under the weight of the soil, and both are good candidates for bursting because they break apart easily. Fast pulls.

    Emergency Sewer Line Replacement in Dodge City, KS

    Sewage coming up a floor drain or tub is an emergency. Stop running water in the house right away. We open the cleanout to relieve the backup, run a camera to find the break, and when the pipe has caved in, we dig to that spot immediately, putting in a section of new pipe to get you flowing before planning the full replacement.

    Sewer Cleanout Installation in Dodge City, KS

    A lot of older houses have no outside cleanout. Every clog then means pulling a toilet. When we replace a sewer line we add a two-way cleanout near the foundation, with a cap at ground level you can find, so future cleaning or camera work is quick and cheap.

    Commercial Sewer Replacement in Dodge City

    Bigger pipe, more flow, more rules. Commercial laterals usually start at 6 inches. We handle the permit, set up bypass pumping when the building cannot shut down, and arrange traffic control when the work reaches the road, keeping the outage as short as the job allows. Night shifts possible.

    Traditional Trench Sewer Replacement in Dodge City

    An open trench gives full access to the pipe from the house to the main. A small excavator does most of it, with hand digging near other lines. The old pipe comes out, the trench bottom gets a bed of gravel or sand, and new 4-inch PVC goes in at a steady quarter inch of fall per foot, confirmed with a level on every length. Then the camera.

    Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Dodge City

    Cast iron sewer pipe lasts a long time, but not forever. The inside rusts and scales until the bottom of the pipe thins out and cracks. We replace it with PVC, and where cast iron remains under the house we use a shielded no-hub coupling at the transition. Clean seal.

    Open-Trench Sewer Replacement in Dodge City, KS

    Trenching lets us fix grade. That is its big advantage. When an old line has a sag that holds water and solids, bursting would just follow the same sag, so we dig and reset the pipe on a proper bed at a proper fall. Bellies disappear.
