    Slab Leak Location and Repair in Dodge City

    Slab leaks hide under concrete until something gives them away. Much of Kansas has expansive clay soil that swells when wet and shrinks in drought, which can move foundations and stress buried pipes. Our techs find them with ground microphones and thermal imaging, so the only concrete we break is the small patch right over the leak, not half the kitchen floor. After that, you choose the repair. Some leaks get a spot repair, others a reroute overhead that skips the slab entirely.
