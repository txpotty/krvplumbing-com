    Frequently Asked Questions About Rooter Services in Dodge City

    Rooting a sink, tub or toilet usually costs $125 to $300. Main sewer rooting through a cleanout generally runs $200 to $500. With hydro jetting added, expect roughly $450 to $1,000, depending on how long the line is and how heavy the grease is. Nights and weekends run higher.

    Snaking runs a spinning cable with a cutting head through the clog. Hydro jetting sends water at several thousand psi through a nozzle that scrubs the whole pipe wall as it is pulled back toward the cleanout. Jetting cleans more. Snaking handles one-time clogs, while jetting is for grease lines and scaled mains that keep coming back.

    Yes. Evenings, weekends and holidays are covered for urgent backups. You hear the after-hours rate before anything starts.

    Rooting is safe with a trained tech and the correct cable and blade. Collapsed or badly cracked pipe can be hurt by anything run through it. The tech's touch matters. If the cable tells us the pipe is damaged, we stop and send in a camera before going further.

    Drain snaking, also called augering, uses a flexible cable and cutting head to break up or pull out the clog. It works well on hair, wipes and roots. Jetting scours the whole pipe with high-pressure water and strips the grease, scale and sludge a cable slides past. For recurring clogs, jetting is the better bet.

    Rental machines are easy to get, but they are dangerous in untrained hands. Cables can kink, whip and wrap around a wrist in a second. They also get stuck. Getting a rental cable stuck in clay pipe can mean digging it out, which costs far more than a rooter visit.

    Sewer pipes leak a little water vapor at the joints. Roots follow that moisture to the gap and push in. Once in, they grow quickly. In Dodge City, clay with joints every couple of feet and old cast iron with lead and oakum joints get hit the most.

    Most single-drain clogs take 1 to 3 hours from arrival to flow test. Main lines with heavy roots, or jobs that need a jetting pass, can stretch to half a day or more. Access makes the biggest difference. With no usable cleanout, we pull a toilet to get in, and that adds about an hour.
