    Drainage System Integration in Dodge City, KS

Our team designs and installs custom drainage solutions for Dodge City, KS properties, so water is managed well and moved away from your home's vulnerable areas.

    Crawl Space Vapor Barriers in Dodge City

    Many homes in Dodge City have crawl spaces, which are highly susceptible to moisture issues. Installing a heavy-duty vapor barrier on crawl space walls and floors is a key waterproofing strategy. This thick polyethylene sheeting stops ground moisture from evaporating into the crawl space, protecting subflooring and joists and improving indoor air quality. It's an effective way to control humidity and prevent wood rot.

    Waterproofing Costs vs. Repeat Water Damage in Dodge City, KS

    Shower Pan Leak Repair and Waterproofing in Dodge City, KS

    A cracked or poorly sloped shower pan often causes wet baseboards and stained ceilings below. We test the pan by plugging the drain and flooding it to verify the leak. Then we rebuild the pan with correct pre-slope, a new liner or bonded membrane and a properly clamped drain. A second flood test confirms the pan is watertight before tile.

    Sump Pump Installation and Repair in Dodge City, KS

    Sump pumps are a key part of many interior waterproofing systems, particularly in homes that see heavy rainfall. Our services include primary sump pump installation, battery backup sump pump systems for power outages, and routine maintenance or repair of existing units.
