    Copper to PEX Conversions in Dodge City

    Copper lines can develop pinhole leaks from water chemistry, high velocity and sometimes soil contact under slabs. Private wells serve many rural homes, and minerals, sediment, and nitrates make periodic well testing a good habit. Once a home has had several pinhole leaks, more usually follow. Converting to PEX stops the cycle and reduces the risk of hidden water damage. We use approved transition fittings wherever new PEX connects to existing copper stubs or valves.

    PEX-A vs. PEX-B Installation in Dodge City

    We offer installation for both PEX-A and PEX-B piping and understand the different advantages of each. PEX-A, often preferred for its flexibility and ability to recover from minor kinks, uses expansion fittings. PEX-B is more rigid but also very durable, typically using crimp or compression fittings. Our plumbers in Dodge City can help you decide which type of PEX suits your project, considering factors like budget and application.

    PEX-B Crimp and Clamp Systems in Dodge City

    PEX-B is stiffer and more affordable, and it is joined with copper crimp rings or stainless steel cinch clamps over brass or poly fittings. It is a good option for smaller projects and budget-conscious repipes. Brands like Viega, Zurn and SharkBite offer reliable PEX-B systems. We use a go/no-go gauge on every crimp to verify a proper seal.

    Leak Repair with PEX in Dodge City, KS

    For specific pipe leaks, PEX can be used to repair or replace sections of damaged piping. Our plumbers can isolate the compromised section and tie in PEX, providing a durable and flexible fix that holds up to future pressures. This targeted approach is cost-effective for localized issues and brings the benefits of PEX to problem areas of your existing plumbing in Dodge City, KS.

    PEX-A Expansion Fitting Systems in Dodge City, KS

    PEX-A, such as Uponor, uses an expansion tool to stretch the pipe and a ring over the fitting, which then shrinks back into a tight seal. The fittings have a larger inner diameter, so flow restriction is lower. PEX-A is also more flexible and can be repaired from kinks with a heat gun. It is our favored choice for many full repipes.
