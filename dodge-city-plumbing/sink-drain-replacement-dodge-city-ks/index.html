    Code Updates During Drain Replacement in Dodge City

    Old drains sometimes break current rules. S-traps and drum traps were common long ago, and replacement brings them up to a vented P-trap. That can mean an air admittance valve. Local rules decide.

    Pop-Up Drain Replacement in Dodge City

    Lift-rod pop-ups are the most common bath drain. The old body comes out, the seat gets cleaned, and the new flange goes in sealed with a clevis set for easy lifting. We match the faucet finish. Overflow-style drains are standard.

    Kitchen Drain and Disposal Tie-In in Dodge City

    Disposals change the kitchen drain. The outlet on the disposal has to sit above the trap inlet, the dishwasher hose needs a high loop or an air gap, and the trap has to line up without the slip joints pulling sideways. We set them all at once. Then the disposal runs with water while we watch.

    Utility Sink Drain Replacement in Dodge City

    Laundry tubs see lint and paint water. Lint and grit wear out thin utility tub drains and pack the trap so it never drains right. We fit a 1-1/2 inch trap with a cleanout. A lint trap on the washer hose helps.

    Corroded Drain Replacement in Dodge City, KS

    Drain cleaners speed up corrosion. Caustic products sit in the bottom of a metal trap between uses and eat at the chrome plating and the thin brass underneath, which is why so many old traps we pull have pinholes right at the bottom of the bend. PVC does not have that problem. We recommend it for most cabinets.

    Hard-Water Drain Damage Repair in Dodge City, KS

    Water across much of Kansas runs quite hard, leaving mineral scale in water heaters, faucets, showerheads, and dishwashers. Scale grows inside pop-up bodies and on stopper rods until the linkage seizes. Forcing a scaled lift rod usually snaps the pivot and leaves the stopper stuck, with a narrowed tailpiece slowing the drain. The scaled parts all come out. Smooth PVC sheds buildup better.

    Trap Arm and Wall Drain Repair in Dodge City, KS

    Leaks right at the wall point to the trap arm or stub. A cracked arm or rusted stub drips down the back panel, out of sight, until the wood swells or smells. The bad section gets cut back. Then new PVC gets tied in.

    Choosing Drain Material in Dodge City, KS

    PVC is the default choice under most sinks. It will not rust and costs less, while thick brass is worth it where the trap shows, like under a pedestal. Some houses use black ABS instead. We match what you have, with a proper transition where they meet.
