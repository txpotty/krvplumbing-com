    Mixing Valve Repair in Dodge City

    Mixing valves blend hot and cold inside the wall.     Weak Shower Spray Repair in Dodge City, KS

    Most weak showers come down to the head. Minerals fill the nozzles and the screen behind them, and the spray fades so gradually you barely notice until it is a mist. Cleaning often works. If the cartridge is clogged too, we replace it and test the flow again.

    Leaking Shower Cartridge Repair in Dodge City

    Cartridges wear from the inside. Mineral grit scores the sealing faces and flattens the O-rings until the cartridge can no longer stop the flow. We swap in a new one. The new cartridge goes in with silicone grease, which keeps the handle smooth.

    Showerhead and Arm Leak Repair in Dodge City

    Shower arms leak at the threads more than people realize. A leak at the arm threads follows the pipe into the wall, and it shows up as bubbling paint in the next room. We reseal the threads. Handheld hoses and slide bars can go in during the same visit.

    Leaky Shower Valve Repair in Dodge City

    One drip every few seconds adds up to hundreds of gallons a year. Single-handle showers drip from a worn cartridge or O-rings, while old two-handle valves drip from a washer and a pitted seat. We carry the common parts. Moen, Delta, Kohler and Pfister cartridges cover most of the valves we open, so the repair is usually done that same visit.

    Same-Day Shower Leak Repair in Dodge City, KS

    Shut off the main first if water is getting where it should not.     Shower Diverter Repair in Dodge City, KS

    Diverters send water up to the head instead of out the tub spout. When the gate in a lift-style spout wears or a three-handle diverter stem loses its washer, water keeps pouring from the spout and the shower gets weak. Usually a new spout fixes it. It is a short job.

    Shower Handle Leak Repair in Dodge City

    Leaks at the handle or behind the trim plate are easy to miss. Water running down the inside of the trim gets behind the tile at the valve opening, and over a few months it soaks the drywall around the valve without ever showing on the shower side. The trim comes off first. Worn O-rings or a loose bonnet nut are the usual cause.
