    Pressure-Assist and Dual-Flush Repair in Dodge City

    Pressure-assist toilets use a sealed vessel inside the china that compresses air with incoming water pressure, and the cartridge inside that vessel eventually wears out. You will not find most of these parts at a hardware store. We carry Flushmate cartridges and dual-flush seals for the common TOTO and American Standard models, and we check house pressure, since these units need steady line pressure to flush properly. Low pressure makes them sound weak.

    Running Toilet Repair in Dodge City

    A tank that keeps hissing and refilling every few minutes is almost always losing water past the flapper or overfilling into the overflow tube. It adds up fast. We check the chain, the float height, the flapper and the seat before touching a part, then replace only what failed. Most running toilets are quiet again in about thirty minutes.

    Leaking Toilet Repair in Dodge City

    Water on the floor can come from four places: the wax ring, the tank-to-bowl gasket, the supply line or sweat running off a cold tank. Each one needs a different fix. We dry everything and watch where the water shows up first, which takes about ten minutes and saves pulling a toilet that did not need it. A cracked bowl gets a replacement quote, not a patch.

    Flush Valve Seat Repair in Dodge City

    Scale on the flush valve seat is the reason a brand-new flapper sometimes keeps leaking, because the rubber cannot seal against a rough, crusted ring. Feel the seat with a finger. A scrub pad and some vinegar clean most seats, and if it is pitted we install a seat repair kit or a new flush valve. Then we set the float so the water stops about an inch below the overflow.

    Toilet Leaking Through the Ceiling in Dodge City, KS

    Water coming through the ceiling under an upstairs bathroom usually traces back to a failed wax ring or a cracked supply nut, and the drywall below holds the evidence. Leave that toilet alone until we look. The toilet comes up, the flange gets checked, and the bowl goes back on a new seal, then we show you how far the water traveled so you can decide about the ceiling.

    Weak Flush and Clog Repair in Dodge City, KS

    Needing two flushes on a regular basis usually comes down to scale in the rim jets, a clogged siphon jet, a partial blockage in the trapway, or a flush valve that does not open all the way. Gurgling in a nearby sink points to the vent. We clear the trap with an auger and treat the jets with a descaler. A few first-generation 1.6 gallon toilets never flushed well, and we will tell you plainly if yours is one of them.

    Toilet Gasket and Seal Replacement in Dodge City, KS

    The rubber parts in a toilet, the flapper, the tank-to-bowl gasket, the bolt washers and the wax ring at the floor, all break down with age and chlorine. They get stiff. A stiff gasket stops sealing and lets water seep out slowly where you will not see it until the floor stains. When one tank seal has failed, we replace the full set so the others do not follow next month.
