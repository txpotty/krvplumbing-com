    Dual Tank Softener Systems in Dodge City

    Large households and homes with very hard water can benefit from a dual tank system that provides soft water all day and night. While one tank regenerates, the other stays in service. Kinetico and similar non-electric twin tank systems are popular for this reason. We size dual tank systems to your peak demand.     Salt-Based Water Softener Replacement in Dodge City, KS

    Salt-based water softeners are a common and proven solution for hard water in Kansas. When replacing these units, we carefully disconnect the old brine tank and resin tank, then install the new components.     Salt-Free Conditioner Alternatives in Dodge City, KS

    Some homeowners prefer a salt-free scale reduction system that uses template-assisted crystallization media. These systems do not remove hardness minerals but change them so they are less likely to stick. They need no drain and no salt. We explain the differences honestly, since they work differently than true softeners. They can be a good fit when drain access is limited or sodium is a concern.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Softener Replacement in Dodge City

    The cost of water softener replacement in Dodge City, KS can vary widely, typically ranging from $1,500 to $4,000. This depends on the type and size of the new system, labor costs and any necessary plumbing changes. We provide transparent, upfront pricing after assessing your needs.

    Most residential water softener replacements can be finished within 3 to 6 hours, assuming no major plumbing changes are required. Our experienced plumbers work efficiently to minimize disruption, restoring soft water to your home on the same day.

    Most softener replacements take two to four hours. New loops or drain lines can take longer.

    KRV Plumbing installs a wide range of trusted water softener brands, including Fleck, Autotrol, Culligan, Pelican and Watts. We select brands known for their reliability, efficiency and long-term performance, so you get a quality system that meets your needs.

    Most softeners last 10 to 15 years. Resin and valves wear faster with very hard or chlorinated water.

    To extend the life of your new water softener, use the correct type of salt, if applicable, and keep the brine tank filled. Regular maintenance, such as cleaning the brine tank and periodic inspections by a professional, can also help ensure its longevity and efficiency.

    In many areas, a plumbing permit is not usually required for a like-for-like water softener replacement, especially if there are no major changes to the existing plumbing lines.
