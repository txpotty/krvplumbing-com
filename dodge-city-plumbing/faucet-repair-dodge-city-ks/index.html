    Cartridge Faucet Repair in Dodge City, KS

    Many modern single-handle faucets, often found in newer homes, use a cartridge system. When these fail, you might experience inconsistent water temperature, reduced flow, or difficulty turning the faucet on or off. Our technicians are experts at diagnosing cartridge issues and replacing them with the correct manufacturer-specific part, so your faucet works like new. We work with all major brands to provide precise repairs.

    Leaking Faucet Handle and Base Repair in Dodge City, KS

    Water seeping around the handle or pooling at the base points to failed O-rings or a loose mounting nut. If left alone, the leak can soak into the cabinet below and damage the sink base. We replace O-rings, tighten mounting hardware and reseal the base. We also check the deck for water stains.

    Bathroom Faucet Repair in Dodge City, KS

    Bathroom faucets, whether in sinks, showers, or tubs, require specialized attention.     Faucet Repair Versus Replacement in Dodge City, KS

    A cartridge repair often costs well under the price of a new faucet and can add years of life.     Outdoor Hose Bib Repair in Dodge City

    Dripping Faucet Repair in Dodge City

    A dripping spout typically means a worn cartridge, washer or valve seat. Hard water speeds up this wear by leaving scale that scratches seals and keeps them from closing tight. We disassemble the faucet, replace the worn parts and clean the valve body. Fixing a drip right away keeps a small leak from adding up on your water bill.

    Low Water Pressure Faucet Fix in Dodge City, KS

    Experiencing weak water flow from your faucet? This could be due to mineral buildup in the aerator, a faulty cartridge, or issues with your home's main water line.
