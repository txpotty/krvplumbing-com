    Maintenance for Every Tankless Brand in Dodge City, KS

    We service Bosch, Rheem, Takagi and Navien heaters around Dodge City, and each one has its own filter locations and code list in the manual. We follow the manufacturer's steps, not a generic checklist. Warranty claims later can hinge on it.

    Rinnai Air Filter and Flame Rod Care in Dodge City

    Rinnai cabinets are tight. We wash the air intake filter and check the flame rod for carbon. On recirculating models we also check the pump schedule, because a wrong setting burns gas for nothing.

    Service Valve Kit Installs in Dodge City, KS

    Plenty of tankless heaters went in without service valves, and without them there is no clean way to hook up a descaling pump. A pair of isolation valves with purge ports goes on the hot and cold lines. The kit usually has a relief valve port too. Future flushes then take minutes to set up.

    Rinnai Tankless Service in Dodge City

    Rinnai units tell you a lot through the controller. Code 11 means the burner never lit, and code 12 means the flame went out after it did. On a Rinnai visit we clean the air intake filter and check the flame rod for soot or cracks. The LC codes are scale warnings, and they usually clear once the exchanger has been flushed and the unit is reset.

    Inlet Screen and Air Filter Cleaning in Dodge City

    Grit gets in. The small screen on the cold inlet catches sand and loose scale after a water main repair. Units with an air intake filter collect dust and lint, which can push the burner into a combustion code. We clean both on every visit.

    Noritz Descaling in Dodge City, KS

    Noritz exchangers scale up fast on hard water. The descaler is one Noritz approves, and the cycle stays within the manual's time limit so the metal does not etch. The fan motor and exhaust path get checked while the pump runs.

    Heat Exchanger Descaling in Dodge City

    Picture a bundle of thin tubes with a gas flame under them and cold water racing through at three or four gallons a minute. That is the exchanger. Minerals settle on the hottest tube walls first, and the passages get narrower. We dissolve it with 45 to 60 minutes of pump-driven descaling solution.

    Tankless Error Code Diagnosis in Dodge City, KS

    An error code is the heater telling you what it saw. On a Rinnai an LC code means scale, on a Navien E003 means no ignition, and on a Noritz a 90 usually means an air or exhaust problem. First we read the code history. If a part has failed, you hear the repair and the price before we start.
