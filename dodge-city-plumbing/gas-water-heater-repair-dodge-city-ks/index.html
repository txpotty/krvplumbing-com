    Propane Water Heater Repair in Dodge City

    Rural properties often run water heaters on propane instead of natural gas. Propane units use different orifices and regulator pressures, and improper conversions cause sooting and poor performance. We confirm the correct parts are installed and repair pilots, valves and burners. We also check tank regulators for proper pressure.

    Pilot Light and Ignition System Repair in Dodge City

    A common culprit for no hot water in gas heaters is a faulty pilot light or ignition system. Our technicians are experienced in diagnosing issues like a dirty pilot tube, thermocouple failure or a bad igniter. We carry common replacement parts on our service vehicles to ensure a quick fix and restore your hot water flow promptly.

    Gas Control Valve Replacement in Dodge City, KS

    The gas control valve controls temperature and shuts off gas if the pilot goes out. Newer valves from Honeywell and White-Rodgers use status lights that blink codes when something goes wrong. If the valve itself has failed, we replace it with an exact match or approved universal model. We leak test and confirm temperature control afterward.

    A malfunctioning burner assembly can result in poor heating or unusual noises. We carefully inspect the burner for blockages, corrosion or wear.
