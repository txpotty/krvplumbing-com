    Softener Loop Installation in Manhattan, KS

    Many homes have a softener loop near the main water line that makes for easy installation. When a home lacks one, we can add a loop with shutoff valves and a bypass. We also route a drain line to a laundry box, floor drain or approved location. A good loop keeps outdoor hose bibs on hard water to save salt. This makes future service easier.

    Replacing Fleck Water Softeners in Manhattan, KS

    Fleck is a well-known brand for reliable water softener control valves, found in many residential and commercial systems across Kansas. If your Fleck-controlled softener is beyond repair or simply outdated, KRV Plumbing can properly replace the entire unit.     Salt-Based Water Softener Replacement in Manhattan, KS

    Salt-based water softeners are a common and proven solution for hard water in Kansas. When replacing these units, we carefully disconnect the old brine tank and resin tank, then install the new components. We make sure all connections are leak-free and the drain line is correctly routed. We will also provide tips on initial setup and salt refills for lasting soft water.

    Whole-Home Water Softener System Upgrades in Manhattan

    Upgrading your whole-home water softener system in Manhattan is a significant investment that pays off in the long run. Older systems may struggle with the mineral content in your water or simply be undersized for your household's needs. We assess your current water usage and hardness levels to recommend and install a properly matched system. Each faucet in your home then delivers soft, clean water.

    Brine Tank Replacement in Manhattan

    Cracked brine tanks, salt bridges and failing float assemblies cause softeners to stop regenerating properly. We replace brine tanks and safety floats to restore consistent brine draw. We also clean out mushy salt buildup that clogs the tank. A new brine tank is often part of a full system replacement. We recommend the best salt type for your system.

    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Replacement in Manhattan, KS

    For homeowners in Manhattan, KS seeking an alternative to traditional salt-based systems, salt-free water conditioners offer a different approach. These units, sometimes called descalers, condition water to prevent mineral scale without removing the minerals or adding salt. While they do not produce soft feeling water, they effectively protect pipes and appliances from scale. We can replace your existing salt-free system or help you switch from a salt-based unit.

    Residential Water Softener Replacement in Manhattan

    For homeowners in Manhattan, hard water can lead to scale buildup in pipes, shorter appliance lifespan and dry skin. Our residential water softener replacement service involves removing your old, inefficient unit and installing a new, high-efficiency system designed to meet your family's needs. We confirm proper sizing and setup for strong performance, protecting your home's plumbing.

    Metered Softener Replacement in Manhattan

    Older timer-based softeners regenerate on a set schedule whether you used water or not. Metered softeners track actual usage and regenerate only when the resin is nearly exhausted. This saves salt, water and wear on the system. We replace old timer valves with metered Fleck 5600SXT or Clack WS1 valves. Most homeowners notice lower salt use within the first few weeks.
