    Exterior Mount Tankless Units in Manhattan, KS

    Many homes have tankless heaters mounted on an exterior wall with no venting at all, since the unit exhausts directly outside. These units face sun, rain and temperature swings, which can wear out plastic covers and wiring over time. Hard freezes happen every winter in Kansas, and pipes in exterior walls, garages, and crawlspaces are the most at risk. When we replace them, we check the pipe cover, freeze protection settings and the electrical connection. We can add a protective pipe cover kit to guard the water and gas lines. Exterior models are often the simplest swap when the gas line is already sized correctly.

    Commercial Tankless Replacement in Manhattan

    Restaurants, salons and small offices often use multiple tankless units linked together on a rack system. When one or more units fail, we replace them and rebalance the cascade controls so they split the load. We check gas pressure under full demand, since commercial racks can draw more than 600,000 BTU combined. Scheduling is flexible, so we can work after hours and keep your business open. We also set up a descaling schedule suited to your water usage.

    Recirculation Tankless Upgrades in Manhattan, KS

    If you are tired of running the tap while you wait for hot water, a tankless with a built-in recirculation pump can bring heat to the fixture in seconds. Navien A series and Rinnai units with internal pumps can use a dedicated return line or a crossover valve under the farthest sink. We program schedules so the pump runs only during your morning and evening peaks. That keeps gas use reasonable while saving thousands of gallons of wasted water a year. This upgrade is a favorite in long single-story ranch homes.
