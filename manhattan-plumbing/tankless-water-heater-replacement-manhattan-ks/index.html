    Tankless vs. Tank Replacement Cost in Manhattan, KS

    Tankless costs more up front than a tank, often twice as much installed. Across 20 years the gap narrows, because one tankless may outlast two tanks, but your usage decides the payback. We price both.

    Commercial Tankless Replacement in Manhattan

    Cascade controls on a commercial rack decide which unit fires first, and after one unit is replaced they need rebalancing. Otherwise one unit does all the work. We also set a descaling schedule.

    Like-for-Like Tankless Swaps in Manhattan

    A cracked heat exchanger or a failed control board on an older tankless is usually not worth repairing past the ten-year mark. The replacement gets matched to the existing gas, vent and mount where possible. Most swaps take four to six hours.

    Point-of-Use Electric Tankless in Manhattan

    Point-of-use electric tankless heaters under a sink are a different animal from whole-house units. A small under-sink unit needs one 20 or 30 amp circuit and suits a distant bath or break room. Swaps take an hour or two.

    Exterior Mount Tankless Units in Manhattan, KS

    Exterior-mount tankless units hang on an outside wall and vent straight out the front, so there is no vent pipe to run. Covers and wiring take a beating outdoors. Hard freezes happen every winter in Kansas, and pipes in exterior walls, garages, and crawlspaces are the most at risk. We check the freeze protection settings and the pipe cover kit on every outdoor swap. Often the simplest job we do.

    Gas Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Manhattan

    A gas tankless unit at full fire can pull 199,000 BTU, roughly five times what a 40 gallon tank burner uses. The gas line usually has to be 3/4-inch or larger. We check meter capacity and pipe length against the manufacturer's sizing chart, then confirm pressure with the unit running. An undersized line causes ignition faults.

    Emergency Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Manhattan

    No hot water from a tankless usually shows up as an error code on the controller, and the code tells us half of what we need to know. Send us a photo of the display. If the heat exchanger is leaking, replacement is the fix, and we can often have a new unit running the same or next day.

    Navien Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Manhattan, KS

    Navien NPE units are condensing, and the A models have a built-in recirculation pump and buffer tank that cut down on cold-water sandwiches. Their combi boilers handle both space heat and domestic water. We set up both.
