    Sump Pump Maintenance in Manhattan, KS: Pits, Floats and Valves

    Pre-Storm Season Pump Testing in Manhattan

    Severe thunderstorms are common in Kansas, and heavy downpours can swamp gutters, storm drains, and older sewer lines. A few weeks ahead of the wet season, we run a hose into the pit until the float trips and the pump starts. We time the drawdown and listen for bearing noise. A struggling pump can then be replaced on a calm day instead of in the middle of a storm.

    Check Valve and Discharge Pipe Repair in Manhattan, KS

    A bad check valve can nearly double how often the pump starts. Every startup puts heat into the motor. We swap worn valves for quiet, spring-loaded models and inspect the discharge pipe for cracks and loose couplings all the way to the exit point. Discharge needs to land far enough from the slab or footings that it does not seep right back into the pit.

    Discharge Line and Check Valve Service in Manhattan

    The check valve sits on the discharge pipe a foot or so above the pump and keeps the water in the line from falling back into the pit. A failed valve sends that water back down for the pump to move again. Motors wear out faster. We test the valve, look for a weep hole between the pump and the valve, and check every joint up to the wall.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Maintenance in Manhattan, KS

    Backup pumps sit in the pit next to the main pump and run off a 12-volt battery when the power drops or the primary fails. We check the battery with a load tester, not just a voltmeter. Then we unplug the main pump and let the backup empty the pit on its own. Alarms get tested too.

    Emergency Sump Pump Diagnostics in Manhattan, KS

    Grinding, clicking and a hum with no water moving each point to a different failure. Our plumber works from the outlet and cord to the float and then the motor. A tripped GFCI is a quick fix. A seized motor means a new pump, and we keep replacements on the truck so the pit is never left unprotected overnight.

    Emergency Sump Pump Repair in Manhattan

    A pump that runs nonstop, hums without moving water or will not start at all needs a plumber the same day, before the pit overflows. Our plumber arrives with spare floats, check valves and a replacement pump in the van. A jammed impeller usually clears in a few minutes. Burned-out motors get a new pump installed before we leave, so the pit is protected again that day.

    Float Switch Checks in Manhattan, KS

    The float switch tells the pump when to start and stop, and a float with too little travel between on and off makes the pump short cycle dozens of times an hour. Our plumber watches the float rise and drop through a complete cycle. Then we set the on and off points so the pump runs longer, less often.

    Commercial Sump Pump Maintenance in Manhattan, KS

    Commercial pits are often bigger and deeper, and many have two pumps on an alternating controller. Each pump gets tested, along with the alternator and the high-water alarm. Property managers get a written log of every reading we take, which helps at insurance renewal and during code inspections. Quarterly visits are common.
