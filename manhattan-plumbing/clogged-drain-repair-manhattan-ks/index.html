    Floor Drain and Garage Drain Clearing in Manhattan

    Floor drains in garages, utility rooms and basements collect sand, dirt and debris. A dry trap lets sewer gas in.     Hydro-Jetting Services in Manhattan, KS

    A jetter pushes water at thousands of psi through a nozzle that sprays backward and pulls itself down the pipe. It scours the whole wall. Cables punch a hole through a clog, but a jetter washes away the grease, scale and soft roots that cause the next clog to form. It works best on lines that clog again and again.

    Bathroom Sink and Vanity Drain Clogs in Manhattan, KS

    Vanity drains clog at the pop-up stopper first. Hair wraps around the rod. We pull and clean the stopper, clear the trap and run a small cable through the wall line, and we can replace a worn pop-up assembly or corroded tailpiece during the same visit. Mineral crust can narrow the tailpiece too.

    Recurring Drain Clogs and Camera Diagnosis in Manhattan, KS

    The same drain clogging every few weeks is a pipe problem, not bad luck. There is a reason it keeps happening. We run a small inspection camera through the line, and you watch the screen with us as it passes sags, broken fittings, scale or roots coming through a joint. The choice is yours from there. A deeper cleaning, a spot repair or a new section are the usual options.

    Several drains backing up at once usually means the clog is in the main. It needs a bigger machine.
