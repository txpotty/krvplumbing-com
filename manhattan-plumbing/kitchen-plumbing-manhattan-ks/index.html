    Grease Trap Cleaning in Manhattan

    A grease trap that smells sour is normally overdue for a pump-out. We remove the lid, scrape the baffles, pull the floating grease and settled solids, and check the gasket so the lid seals again and odor stays inside. We log every visit.     Dishwasher and Ice Maker Lines in Manhattan

    A dishwasher needs a hot supply with its own shutoff, a drain to the disposal or a wye tailpiece, and either an air gap at the sink or a high loop under the counter, depending on local code. Ice makers need a cold line. We use copper or braided stainless with a quarter-turn valve, not the thin plastic tubing that cracks behind refrigerators.

    Recurring Kitchen Clogs in Manhattan, KS

    Grease, rice, pasta and coffee grounds stack up in the trap and the drain arm over months, not days. We clear the line with a cable sized for kitchen pipe and can camera it when the same clog returns. Sometimes the pipe itself is the problem. A sag in the branch line or a drain arm with too little slope will hold grease no matter how often it gets cleaned.

    Disposal Repair and Replacement in Manhattan, KS

    A disposal that hums but will not spin is almost always jammed. We cut the power, work the flywheel loose with a 1/4 inch hex key from underneath, pull out the bottle cap or bone, and press the red reset button before testing it again. Leaks are a different story. A drip from the bottom of the housing means the internal seal failed, and that unit has to be replaced.

    Disposal Jams and Hums in Manhattan, KS

    Humming with no grinding means the motor is getting power but the plate is stuck. Usually a ten minute fix. We free the plate from below with a hex key, clear the hopper with pliers, never a hand, and reset the overload before running water through it. Silence with no hum points to the switch, the outlet or the breaker instead.

    Emergency Kitchen Plumbing in Manhattan, KS

    First thing: turn both angle stops under the sink clockwise until they stop.     Disposal Repairs That Make Sense in Manhattan, KS

    Jams, tripped resets, loose mounts and leaking sink flanges are all fixable in under an hour. A failed motor or seal is another story.
