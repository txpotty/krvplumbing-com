    Electric Element Service in Manhattan, KS

    Two elements, two thermostats. Sediment coats the lower element first, it runs hot and burns out, and that is the most common reason an electric tank suddenly gives you only half a tank of hot water. We test with power off. Good parts stay in.

    Attic, Closet and Garage Units in Manhattan

    Location changes the risks. Attic heaters need pans and drain lines, and garage gas units often need elevation or protection. We check the pan and drain. We check venting and access.

    Flushing a Tank Water Heater in Manhattan

    Popping and rumbling mean sediment. Calcium and magnesium settle on the bottom of the tank and form a layer that traps heat under it, so water boils in pockets beneath the scale and pops every time the burner or lower element fires. We flush until it runs clear. Bad tanks need a few rounds.
