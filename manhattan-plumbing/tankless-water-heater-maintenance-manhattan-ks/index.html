    Why Choose KRV Plumbing for Tankless Water Heater Maintenance in Manhattan, KS?

    A tankless heater has more going on inside than a tank does, from a modulating gas valve to a flow sensor that decides when the burner fires. The job needs the right tools. We bring a manometer and a descaling pump, and we pull up the service manual for your model.

    • Scale Removal, A pump-driven flush breaks down scale and clears most scale-related codes on the first pass.
    • Lower Gas Bills, A clean exchanger transfers heat better, so the burner fires for less time per gallon.
    • Fewer Repair Calls, Worn igniters and weak flame rods get caught early, before they leave you without hot water.

    Cascade systems link several tankless units to one controller that fires them in turn. Heavy use means scale builds faster than in a single home unit.     Condensate and Neutralizer Service in Manhattan

    Condensing tankless heaters drip acidic condensate, often around pH 3 to 4, and it needs to pass through a neutralizer before it reaches a drain. We clean the trap and look at the neutralizer media. We replace spent media. An outdoor run of condensate line gets checked for support and protection.

    Yearly Tankless Inspection in Manhattan

    A yearly inspection costs less than one service call for a dead heater. Gas connections get a leak test, and we work the pressure relief valve. We pull the air filter and check the igniter. Anything that is wearing out goes on a written list with a plain price, and you decide what gets done now and what can wait until next year.

    Annual Tankless Checkup in Manhattan, KS

    The annual checkup is a top-to-bottom pass on the heater. We read gas pressure at the inlet with a manometer while the burner is at full fire and compare it to the rating plate. We check the flue for blockages. A written report with every reading goes in your file for next year's comparison.

    Tankless Error Code Diagnosis in Manhattan, KS

    An error code is the heater telling you what it saw. On a Rinnai an LC code means scale, on a Navien E003 means no ignition, and on a Noritz a 90 usually means an air or exhaust problem. First we read the code history. If a part has failed, you hear the repair and the price before we start.

    Commercial Tankless Maintenance in Manhattan, KS

    Commercial kitchens and salons burn through hot water all day, so their tankless units scale up much faster than a house unit. We test gas pressure under full fire, not at idle. We pull and clean the inlet screens and air filters. Downtime is the real cost. We can book it before you open or after you close.

    Rinnai Air Filter and Flame Rod Care in Manhattan

    Rinnai cabinets are tight. We wash the air intake filter and check the flame rod for carbon. Recirculating Rinnai models also have a pump setting worth checking, since a wrong schedule wastes gas every hour the pump runs for no reason.

    Noritz Tankless Tune-Ups in Manhattan, KS

    Noritz builds a solid heater, but solid does not mean maintenance-free. A Noritz tune-up starts at the exhaust, where we look for soot and blocked terminations. Then we check the fan and the combustion air path. Last, we check the temperature setting and run a few fixtures at once, which shows whether the unit holds temperature between back-to-back showers.
