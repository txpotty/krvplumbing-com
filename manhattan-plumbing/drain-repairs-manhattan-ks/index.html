    Broken Drain Pipe Repair in Manhattan, KS

    Broken drain pipe usually comes from settling, roots or age. Poor original work causes some. We find the break with a camera and a locator, then replace that section with new pipe joined by shielded couplings that fit the old and new outside diameters. We test it before closing up.

    Cast Iron Drain Stack Repair in Manhattan, KS

    Old cast iron stacks rot from the inside out. The bottom goes first. Rust scale catches debris and hairline cracks let water seep into walls and floors, so we cut out the failing section and replace it with ABS or PVC using shielded transition couplings. If the whole stack is failing, we price it separately.

    Main Line Drain Repair in Manhattan

    A problem in the main line shows up at every drain in the house. The lowest fixture backs up first. Roots, cracks from ground movement and collapsed sections are the common causes, and we confirm which one on camera, then repair it with a spot dig, a trenchless liner or a section replacement depending on what the footage shows. You see the footage first.

    Drain Vent Repair in Manhattan, KS

    Gurgling and slow draining often mean the vent is blocked. Air cannot get in behind the water. Without air, draining water pulls the trap seal along with it and lets sewer gas into the room, so we check the roof vent for nests or debris and look for cracked or disconnected vent pipe in the walls. Many vent fixes take under an hour.

    Emergency Drain Repairs in Manhattan, KS

    A drain that bursts under a sink can soak a cabinet in minutes. Stop using that fixture. We answer after hours, talk you through stopping the flow, and get a plumber there to isolate the damaged section and make a permanent repair whenever the parts allow it. We also flag any wet drywall.

    Kitchen Branch Line Repair in Manhattan

    This branch takes more grease and food than any other line in most homes. The grease goes far down the pipe.
