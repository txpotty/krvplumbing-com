    Cold Water Slab Leak Detection in Manhattan, KS

    Cold water slab leaks do not create warm spots, so they are harder to see. They often show up as a running water meter, damp carpet, a musty smell or water seeping at baseboards. We isolate the cold side and use acoustic equipment to listen for the leak through the slab. Line tracers map the pipe route so we know where to listen.

    Repiping After Multiple Slab Leaks in Manhattan, KS

    If your home has had two or more slab leaks, the remaining pipe is likely corroded too. Repiping replaces all supply lines with new PEX or copper run through the walls and attic. It is a bigger project, but it ends the cycle of repeat slab leaks. We can typically complete a full repipe in two to four days.

    Slab Leak Detection and Repair Cost Comparison in Manhattan, KS

    Slab leak detection typically costs $150 to $450. Direct access repairs usually run $1,000 to $3,000, including concrete patching but not flooring replacement.     Acoustic Slab Leak Detection in Manhattan

    Our technicians use highly sensitive acoustic listening devices to pinpoint the exact location of a leak under your slab. This method involves listening for the faint sound of escaping water, even through concrete. It's particularly effective for pressurized water lines, allowing us to find leaks without unnecessary excavation and minimizing disruption to your Manhattan home.

    Slab Leak Reroutes in Manhattan

    Rerouting abandons the leaking line under the slab and runs new pipe through the walls or attic. It avoids breaking concrete and often costs less than direct access under finished floors. We typically use PEX for reroutes because it handles heat and resists hard-water corrosion. This option is a good fit when the slab is covered in tile or hardwood.
