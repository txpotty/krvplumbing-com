    Prefab Shower Unit Installation in Manhattan, KS

    Fiberglass and acrylic shower units are budget-friendly and quick to install. We set the base, connect the drain and install the valve and trim. Prefab units are ideal for guest baths, rentals and guest houses. We make sure the base is fully supported so it does not flex or crack.

    New Construction Shower Installation in Manhattan, KS

    For new homes and additions, we rough in shower valves, supply lines and drains during framing. Valves are set to depth for the planned wall finish, and drains are located for the chosen pan. We pass rough-in inspections and return to install trim after tile. Our scheduling keeps your build on schedule.

    Curbless Shower Installation in Manhattan

    Curbless showers have no step, which makes them easier to use and gives a modern look. They require a recessed floor and careful drain placement, often with a linear drain. On a slab, we may need to lower the floor in the shower area. We work closely with your contractor to plan the slope and waterproof layers.

    Accessible and ADA Shower Installation in Manhattan, KS

    Accessible showers need low or no thresholds, handheld showers on slide bars and properly placed controls. We install grab bar blocking while walls are open and set valves within easy reach. These features help seniors and people with mobility needs bathe safely. We follow ADA guidelines when requested.

    Shower Valve & Fixture Installation in Manhattan, KS

    Beyond the main shower unit, we also install new shower valves, diverters, and various fixtures.     Custom Shower Enclosure Installation in Manhattan
