    Gas Line Replacement in Manhattan

    Well Pressure Tank Replacement in Manhattan

    A well pump that clicks on and off every few seconds usually points to a failed pressure tank. When the bladder inside the tank ruptures, the tank fills with water, loses its air cushion and forces the pump to start every time someone opens a tap, which burns out pump motors years early. A new tank fixes it. The pressure switch gets checked too.

    Sump Pump and Well Pump Replacement in Manhattan, KS

    A dead sump pump during a storm floods a basement fast, and a dead well pump means no water at all. In Manhattan, KS, we replace sump pumps with cast iron or thermoplastic models sized for your inflow, add a check valve, and recommend a battery backup, while well pumps get matched to depth, flow and the pressure switch setting. We run each one through cycles.

    Pipe Replacement and Repiping in Manhattan

    Corroded, leaking or galvanized pipe in Manhattan can stain water and cause costly damage. Failing sections or the whole house get new PEX or copper, routed to keep openings small and pressure tested long enough to catch any slow drip. Walls get patched.

    Water Line Replacement in Manhattan, KS

    A leaking water service line runs your meter all day and night and can soften the soil next to the foundation.     Water Softener Replacement in Manhattan, KS

    Softeners wear out as the resin breaks down and the control valve stops cycling properly.
