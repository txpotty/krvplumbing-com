    Sectional Point Repairs in Manhattan

    A point repair is a short liner, often 3 to 5 feet long, set over one bad spot. We push it in on a packer, guided by the camera footage, and the packer inflates to press the resin-soaked sleeve against the pipe wall until it cures. One crack, one patch. Sound lines with one bad joint are the right candidates.

    Trenchless Drain Pipe Repair in Manhattan, KS

    Smaller drain lines inside the house can be lined too, including 2-inch kitchen drains and the 3-inch cast iron stacks found in many older homes.     Pipe Bursting Sewer Repair in Manhattan

    Bursting can upsize the line. A 4-inch clay lateral can be replaced with 6-inch HDPE, which helps on lines that clog because they were undersized from the start or have been added onto with extra bathrooms. Each end only needs a pit around three by four feet. No trench in between.

    Sewer Camera Inspection for Trenchless Repair in Manhattan

    Every trenchless job starts with a push camera fed in from the cleanout or a pulled toilet. The screen shows the inside of the pipe live, and a sonde in the camera head lets us mark the exact spot on the ground above it. That tells us the depth. No guessing where to dig.

    Root Intrusion Fixes in Manhattan, KS

    Roots enter at the joints, where clay bells or cast iron hubs have a small gap or a failed gasket. Once in, they fill the pipe. We cut them out with a mechanical root cutter or a hydro jet, then line the pipe so those joints are sealed for good and the line stops needing a cleaning every few months. With no joints left, there is nowhere for them to return.

    Commercial Trenchless Sewer Repair in Manhattan, KS

    Office and retail buildings often have a cast iron building drain under the slab that has rotted along the bottom. Lining it avoids cutting the floor. We coordinate around tenant hours. A typical commercial lining job runs one to three days, depending on footage and how many branch connections need to be reinstated with a cutter.

    Perma-Liner and NuFlow Lining Systems in Manhattan

    Perma-Liner and NuFlow both make lining systems we use, each with its own resin chemistry and installation equipment. Both systems are made to meet the ASTM standards written for cured-in-place pipe. The brand matters less than the prep, because a liner bonded to a dirty, scaly pipe wall will not hold the way the manufacturer rates it. We clean to bare pipe first.

    Orangeburg and Clay Pipe Repair in Manhattan

    Orangeburg is bituminous fiber pipe that was laid in houses mostly from the late 1940s into the early 1970s. It goes oval and blisters, so a liner has nothing round to bond to and bursting is usually the better fix. Clay is different. Clay that still holds its round shape is a good candidate for a liner.
