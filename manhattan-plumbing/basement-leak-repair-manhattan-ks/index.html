    Sump Pump Installation & Repair in Manhattan

    A sump pump lives in a pit cut below floor level and kicks on when a float rises. Stuck floats, worn motors and failed check valves cause most of the service calls we run on sumps. We test yours with a bucket. Add a battery backup if storms knock out power.

    Wall and Slab Seepage Repair in Manhattan

    Seepage at the wall or the slab joint is usually surface water pooling against the foundation. Much of Kansas has expansive clay soil that swells when wet and shrinks in drought, which can move foundations and stress buried pipes. We look at gutters and grading first, since fixing them outside often stops the seepage for far less money. We always try the cheap fixes first.

    Outside Wall Waterproofing in Manhattan, KS

    Excavating the outside of a foundation puts the repair where the water actually gets in. Strong winds and dry spells can pull moisture out of the soil, while sudden cold fronts can drop temperatures sharply overnight. We pick a membrane rated for that exposure, lay new perforated drain pipe at the footing in clean stone, and backfill so the grade slopes away from the house at least six inches over the first ten feet. Downspouts get extended too.

    Water Damage Assessment in Manhattan, KS

    Before any repair, we figure out where the water came from and how far it spread. We map the wet zone with a moisture meter and use a thermal camera to see behind finished walls. Guessing costs money. We give you the readings and photos for your insurer.

    Interior Drainage Systems in Manhattan

    Where digging outside is not practical, an interior drain system does the same job from inside. We cut the slab along the perimeter, lay perforated pipe in washed stone, and slope it to a new or existing sump pit before patching the floor with fresh concrete. The wall gets a dimpled membrane. Water then runs behind it into the channel.

    Wall Crack Sealing for Basements in Manhattan

    Injection works on poured concrete walls, not block. Ports go along the crack, the face gets sealed, and resin goes in from the bottom up until it fills the full depth. Epoxy bonds the two sides. Polyurethane is better for active leaks because it foams on contact with water.

    Supply Line Leak Repair in Manhattan

    Water lines along a basement ceiling can leak at fittings, valves or pinholes. Water across much of Kansas runs quite hard, leaving mineral scale in water heaters, faucets, showerheads, and dishwashers. We cut out the bad section and replace it with copper or PEX, and if we find more than two or three pinholes on one run, we recommend repiping that run instead of patching it again. Repiping a basement ceiling is usually a one-day job.
