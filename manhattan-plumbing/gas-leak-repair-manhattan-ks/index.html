    Appliance Connector Replacement in Manhattan

    Old uncoated brass connectors, the kind made before the mid-1970s, are the subject of consumer safety warnings because the flare nut can separate from the tube. If your range or water heater still has one, it should come out. We see them weekly. The replacement is a coated stainless connector sized for the appliance, with a new ball valve and a sediment trap where one is required. Bubble testing follows before the pilot is lit.

    Commercial Gas Leak Services in Manhattan

    Restaurant kitchens and small shops run more appliances on bigger pipe, often 1-1/4 inch or larger, fed from a meter with its own regulator. When the leak is small enough, we plan the shutdown for after close. Fryers on quick-disconnect hoses fail often. We test every hose and manifold joint and leave tags on anything we had to shut off. New pipe still needs the commercial sign-off.

    Corrugated Stainless Steel Tubing Repair in Manhattan, KS

    CSST is the yellow-jacketed flexible tubing used in many newer homes, and it can be damaged by a nearby lightning strike if it was never bonded. Bonding means a 6 AWG copper wire from the gas piping to the electrical grounding system. Plenty of older installs lack it. We repair CSST only with the matching brand's fittings, because TracPipe and Gastite parts do not interchange. A splice that skips that rule fails inspection.

    Underground Gas Line Repair in Manhattan

    Yard lines run from the meter to the house, and often on to a detached garage, a pool heater, a grill or an outdoor kitchen. Steel pipe in the ground corrodes wherever the wrap was nicked during the original install. Roots and settling do damage too. We call 811 for locates, dig at the suspected spot and replace the bad run with yellow polyethylene pipe and tracer wire. Sand goes around the new pipe before backfill, per Manhattan trench rules.

    Permit and Inspection Coordination in Manhattan

    Replacing more than a connector usually means a permit, and in Manhattan the inspector will want to see a gauge holding pressure before the pipe is covered. The permit and the inspection booking are on us. Most inspections take under twenty minutes. The test is set to whatever the local fuel gas code requires, often a few psi held for fifteen minutes or longer. The signed permit card stays with your records.

    Outdoor Gas Line Repair in Manhattan

    Grills, fire pits, pool heaters and patio heaters often sit at the end of a long buried line that nobody has looked at since it went in. Fence posts and sprinkler trenches hit those lines more often than corrosion does. Dead grass is a tell. The outdoor branch gets isolated and tested by itself, then the bad run is replaced with polyethylene and a tracer. Above-ground stub-ups get a fresh riser and a shutoff valve.

    Commercial Gas Leak Repair in Manhattan

    Commercial gas piping often runs at 2 psi or higher with regulators at each appliance bank, so a leak test there is not the same as a house test. We check regulator lockup and vent lines along with the joints. Rooftop units leak too. If a shutdown would cost you a dinner rush, we valve off just the bad branch and leave the rest live. Where a length has rusted badly, usually near a damp crawlspace vent, we cut back to sound pipe and splice in new. Every remade joint gets a detector check and a pressure test.
