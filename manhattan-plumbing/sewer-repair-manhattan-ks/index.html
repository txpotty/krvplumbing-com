    Cracked and Broken Pipe Repair in Manhattan

    Clay and cast iron sewer pipe crack from soil movement, heavy vehicles and simple age. A cracked section lets sewage leak into the soil and lets dirt fall into the pipe, which leads to repeat clogs. We excavate at the marked spot, cut out the damaged length and install new Schedule 40 PVC with shielded couplings rated for the transition. The repair is tested before we backfill so you know it will hold.

    Bellied Sewer Line Correction in Manhattan, KS

    A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged and holds standing water and solids. Bellies often form where the original bedding washed out or where the soil settled after heavy rain. Slab leaks and broken sewer laterals are common results of shifting clay, especially after long dry spells followed by heavy rain. We confirm the depth of standing water on camera, then re-bed and re-grade that section to restore proper fall. Correct slope is typically a quarter inch per foot for a typical residential line.

    Video Camera Sewer Inspection in Manhattan

    Before any repair, we use advanced video camera inspection to accurately locate and assess sewer line damage. This involves inserting a flexible rod with a camera into your sewer line, allowing us to see blockages, cracks, root intrusion or pipe collapses in real time. This non-invasive method prevents unnecessary digging and helps us provide a precise diagnosis and repair plan. It's crucial for understanding issues in older homes in Manhattan and identifying problems like bellied pipes or collapsed sections.

    Orangeburg and Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Manhattan

    Some older homes still have Orangeburg pipe, a tar-paper material that flattens and blisters over time. Cast iron from the same era corrodes from the inside and scales up until little flow remains. When a camera shows widespread failure, patching is a waste of money. We replace the line in sections or end to end with PVC, and we can often use trenchless methods to avoid tearing up the whole yard.

    Sewer Pipe Relining in Manhattan, KS

    Sewer pipe relining is a trenchless repair technique where a new, durable pipe is created within the existing damaged one. A flexible, epoxy-impregnated liner is inserted into the old pipe and inflated, curing in place to form a seamless, strong and corrosion-resistant new pipe. This method is effective for addressing cracks, minor breaks and root intrusion without major digging. It extends the life of your sewer system and is an excellent option for preserving landscapes in Manhattan.
