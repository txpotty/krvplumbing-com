    Commercial Toilet Replacement in Manhattan, KS

    Office and retail restrooms wear out toilets faster than any house, and a replacement there usually means a floor-mount or wall-hung bowl with a flushometer instead of a tank. Work gets scheduled around your business hours. Bowls are matched to the flush valve's gallons per flush, since a mismatch leaves waste behind or wastes water.

    Wall-Hung Toilet Replacement in Manhattan

    Wall-hung toilets mount to a steel carrier inside the wall, so replacing one means checking the carrier bolts, the gasket on the outlet and the condition of the wall finish around it. Some carriers need new bolts. The new bowl gets matched to the carrier already in the wall whenever possible, which avoids opening the wall.

    High-Efficiency Toilet Installation in Manhattan, KS

    Toilets made before 1994 often use 3.5 gallons a flush, and some from the 1970s use five or more. Current WaterSense models are rated at 1.28 gallons per flush or less, and the better ones still clear the bowl in one try because the trapway and flush valve are larger than they used to be. Savings show up fast. Some water providers offer a rebate, so ask before you buy.

    Toilet Brands We Replace in Manhattan

    Brand matters less than model, because every major maker sells a great toilet and a mediocre one at nearly the same price. We look at the flush valve size and MaP score. Parts availability counts too. Proprietary flappers and fill valves mean that ten years from now, a simple fix becomes a special order.

    Cracked Bowl or Tank Replacement in Manhattan

    Sometimes only the tank is cracked, and if the model is still in production, a matching tank costs less than a whole new toilet. We check the model number first. A cracked bowl always means a full replacement, since the bowl carries the seal to the floor and cannot be patched.

    Standard Toilet Replacement in Manhattan

    Two-piece toilets are what most houses have, and after twenty or thirty years the tank bolts corrode, the porcelain gets crazed and the flush never quite clears the bowl. At that point a swap makes more sense than another repair. The old unit comes off, the flange gets scraped clean and checked for cracks, then set the new one on a fresh wax ring with new closet bolts.

    ADA and Commercial Toilet Replacement in Manhattan, KS

    Public restrooms need bowls rated for heavy use, and many need to meet ADA height and clearance rules when a fixture is replaced. We confirm the clearances before ordering. Sensor flush valves are an easy add during the swap, and they cut down on unflushed bowls.

    High-Efficiency Toilet (HET) Upgrades in Manhattan, KS

    High-efficiency toilets carry a WaterSense label and a MaP score, which tells you how many grams of waste the toilet clears in one flush. Look for a score of at least 800. A high score means fewer double flushes and fewer clogs, and that is where the real water savings come from.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Toilet Replacement in Manhattan

    Most replacements with a quality standard toilet, labor and disposal cost $450 to $950, and that covers the wax ring, bolts and a new supply line too. Premium one-piece or smart toilets can bring the total to $1,200 to $2,500. Flange repairs usually add $100 to $250.

    Rough-in is the distance from the finished wall behind the toilet to the center of the drain, which you can find by measuring to the closet bolts. Twelve inches is standard. Some older houses have a 10 or 14 inch rough-in instead, and a toilet made for the wrong one either sticks out or will not fit.

    Older toilets use 3.5 to 7 gallons per flush, and a WaterSense toilet uses 1.28 or less, so a household of four can save thousands of gallons a year. The savings start on the first bill.

    Two layers. The manufacturer covers defects in the toilet, often for years on the china, and our workmanship guarantee covers the installation itself, including the seal at the floor and the supply connection. Keep your invoice.

    Most swaps take 1.5 to 2.5 hours. Flange or subfloor repairs can add an hour.

    Manufacturers usually cover the china for years and the tank parts for a shorter period, often one to five years depending on the brand. Our labor guarantee sits on top of that. If the install leaks, we come back.

    A handle that needs jiggling, a tank that refills on its own, a hiss after the flush or a flush that stops halfway all point to worn tank parts. Those can be replaced one at a time. Once a toilet is 25 years old, a full replacement usually beats rebuilding it.

    TOTO, American Standard, Kohler and Gerber all make good toilets, and we steer people toward the models in each line with a 3 inch flush valve and a strong MaP score. Flush design matters more than the badge. Parts availability is the other thing we weigh.
