    Grease Line Jetting in Manhattan

    Grease that slips past a full trap coats the 4-inch line out to the main. It builds up in rings, thickest at the bottom of the pipe. A snake cuts a narrow path through and the line plugs again within weeks, while a jetter with a rotating nozzle peels the grease off the whole pipe wall. We run a camera after to confirm.

    Scheduled Grease Trap Maintenance Plans in Manhattan

    Most pretreatment programs use the 25 percent rule: pump before floating grease and settled solids together take up a quarter of the trap's liquid depth. Some cities set a fixed interval instead, often 90 days for interceptors. We work to whichever rule applies. Changes are easy.

    Grease Trap Hydro-Jetting in Manhattan, KS

    Some traps have years of grease baked onto the walls and inlet piping. Pumping alone leaves that crust in place. We jet the inside of the empty trap and the line feeding it, which takes the unit back to bare walls and restores the capacity the crust was eating up. It adds about an hour.

    Grease Trap Inspection and Repair in Manhattan

    An empty trap is the only time anyone can see what is wrong with it. We look for cracked baffles and missing outlet tees. Without an outlet tee, the grease cap flows right into the sewer, and older concrete interceptors lose that tee more often than you would think. We can often fix small problems on the spot.
