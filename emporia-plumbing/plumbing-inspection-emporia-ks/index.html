    Water Pressure and Supply Pipe Review in Emporia, KS

    We measure static water pressure and check the pressure reducing valve if one is installed. Too much pressure wears parts. Pressure above 80 psi shortens the life of fixtures, supply hoses and water heaters, and we also identify the supply pipe material, from copper and CPVC to polybutylene, Kitec and galvanized.

    Sewer Camera Inspection in Emporia, KS

    Many older homes have clay, cast iron or Orangeburg sewer lines that can crack, sag or fill with roots. We run a high-resolution camera from a cleanout to the city connection or septic tank. Every offset, belly, root intrusion and break shows up with its depth and location, and the recording is yours to keep.

    Fixture, Drain and Shutoff Valve Check in Emporia, KS

    We run every faucet, shower and toilet and look for slow drains, leaks and weak flow. Shutoffs get tested too. Stops and the main valve tend to seize with age, and gurgling drains can mean a vent problem, both of which are simple fixes once found.

    Commercial Plumbing Inspections in Emporia, KS

    Commercial buildings have equipment homes do not, so our commercial inspections cover more ground. Backflow devices, grease traps, large heaters, flushometers and the main sewer are all part of it. Records matter for inspectors. The written report helps facility managers plan and budget repairs before a health inspector or the water provider writes them up.

    Pre-Purchase Plumbing Inspections in Emporia

    A house can pass a general inspection and still have a sewer full of roots. Buyers use our inspection to learn the pipe type, the sewer condition and the heater's age before the option period ends. That leaves room to negotiate. Galvanized supply lines, a cracked clay sewer or a water heater past fifteen years are all things a seller can credit for or fix before closing.

    Pipe Material and Water Quality Review in Emporia, KS

    Pipe material says a lot about what to expect from a house. We identify copper, CPVC, PEX, galvanized, polybutylene and Kitec, and we note the known failure patterns for each one. Certain types need a plan. Polybutylene and Kitec are known for sudden failures, and galvanized slowly closes up with rust, so those get flagged with replacement options.

    Rental and Commercial Property Inspections in Emporia

    Landlords and property managers use our inspections between tenants or before buying an investment property. We document each unit. Water heaters, fixtures and sewer lines get recorded unit by unit, and commercial inspections can add backflow assemblies and grease trap lines, which helps owners budget for maintenance and larger repairs.

    Hard Water Damage Assessment in Emporia

    Private wells serve many rural homes, and minerals, sediment, and nitrates make periodic well testing a good habit. Hard water leaves scale in pipes, fixtures and water heaters. Signs include crusted aerators, clogged showerheads, slow toilet fill and heater sediment, and we can test hardness during the visit. You get options, not a pitch. Softeners, filters and scale reducers all help, and we suggest the one that matches the reading we took.
