    Shower Handle Leak Repair in Emporia

    Leaks at the handle or behind the trim plate are easy to miss. Water leaking behind the trim slips into the wall cavity at the valve opening and soaks the drywall while the tile face stays dry. We pull the trim and look. Worn O-rings or a loose bonnet nut are the usual cause.

    Shower Valve Replacement in Emporia

    Temperature that swings or a handle that fights you points to the valve. When the body is corroded or the brand stopped making cartridges for it, we replace the valve, usually through a small access panel behind the shower wall so the tile stays put. The new valve will have scald protection. Thermostatic valves are an option if you want exact temperature control.

    Showerhead Repair and Replacement in Emporia

    Showerheads clog from the inside out. Scale builds behind the face plate and in the flow restrictor, so the spray goes uneven, shoots sideways or drops to a trickle even when the valve is wide open. A vinegar soak can work. Otherwise we put on a new head with rubber nozzles that rub clean with your thumb.

    Dripping Showerhead Repair in Emporia

    Drips from the head itself almost always come from the valve, not the head. If the head drips with the valve closed, water is slipping past the cartridge, and a new head will not change that. The fix is in the valve. Drips from the arm threads just need fresh tape and a snug turn.

    Emergency Shower Leak Repair in Emporia

    Water coming through a ceiling below a bathroom needs fast action. Close the main, set a bucket under the drip and stay clear of any drywall that is bulging, because wet ceilings can drop. Then give us a call. Our plumber locates the leak, stops it and starts drying the area.

    Leaky Shower Valve Repair in Emporia

    One drip every few seconds adds up to hundreds of gallons a year. Single-handle showers drip from a worn cartridge or O-rings, while old two-handle valves drip from a washer and a pitted seat.
