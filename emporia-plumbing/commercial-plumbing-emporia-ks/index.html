    Commercial Restroom Plumbing in Emporia

    Slab Leak Detection for Businesses in Emporia

    A slab leak under a store or office floor often shows up as a warm spot, a running water meter with everything off, or a crack in floor tile. Slab leaks and broken sewer laterals are common results of shifting clay, especially after long dry spells followed by heavy rain. We confirm it with a meter test and pressure test, then use acoustic listening gear and thermal imaging to pinpoint the leak within a foot or two. Then we decide. Some leaks are best fixed with a small cut through the slab, while others are cheaper to abandon and reroute overhead. You get a price on each option.

    Commercial Water Heater Repair and Installation in Emporia, KS

    A commercial water heater is judged by recovery rate, the gallons it can reheat per hour, not by tank size alone. Undersized heaters run cold by mid-shift. Our techs work on AO Smith, Rheem, Bradford White and State commercial heaters and check flue draft, gas valves and the T and P valve each visit. New installs get sized from your peak hour demand. Proper sizing is what keeps a busy kitchen in hot water all night.

    Commercial Drain Cleaning and Hydro-Jetting in Emporia

    Commercial drains carry grease, paper towels and food waste in volumes no house ever sees. A cable just pokes a hole. We use high-pressure jetting with rotating nozzles that scrub grease off the whole circumference of 4-inch and 6-inch lines, not just the bottom. A jetted line stays open far longer than a cabled one. Restaurants, schools and apartment stacks are where we see the biggest difference.

    Commercial Water Heaters and Boilers in Emporia, KS

    Gyms, commercial kitchens and apartment buildings burn through hot water in short, heavy peaks. Our work covers high-input tanks, multi-unit tankless racks and boilers feeding indirect storage tanks. Private wells serve many rural homes, and minerals, sediment, and nitrates make periodic well testing a good habit. Scale cuts output fast. We put the heaters on a descaling schedule and install mixing valves so hand-wash sinks get tempered water at a safe temperature.

    Commercial Plumbing Costs and Maintenance Plans in Emporia, KS

    Backflow Prevention Assembly Service in Emporia

    A backflow assembly keeps water from your building, including irrigation lines, boiler water and chemical feeds, from flowing back into the public supply. Most providers want a yearly test. Our certified testers check reduced pressure zone assemblies, double check valves and pressure vacuum breakers with calibrated differential gauges, then file the report with your water provider. Failed devices usually need a rebuild kit, not a new valve. That is why the truck carries Watts, Wilkins and Febco kits.

    Grease Interceptor and Line Service in Emporia, KS

    Grease does not stay in the trap if the trap is full or the baffles are broken. It ends up in your line. Our crew checks the baffles and tees, replaces what is broken and jets the feed lines to strip hardened grease a cable cannot touch. A jetted line holds up much better on a busy night.
