    Septic Pipe Repair in Emporia, KS

    Old homes may still have clay tile or Orangeburg pipe running to the tank, and both fail badly with age. Slab leaks and cracked sewer lines are common results of shifting clay, especially after long dry spells followed by heavy rain. Shifting ground and roots crack the joints and flatten the pipe. We replace the bad run with 4-inch PVC, set on sand bedding with a quarter inch per foot of fall. Short sections take an afternoon.

    Camera Inspection of Septic Baffles in Emporia, KS

    Some tanks have no riser, so we look at the baffles through the small inspection port with a push camera. That saves digging up the whole lid. A cracked or missing outlet tee means we dig at the outlet end alone, then install a PVC tee with a filter housing. Concrete lids get a riser while they are open.

    Septic Maintenance and Small Repairs in Emporia

    A sludge judge, a long clear tube, tells us how full the tank really is. We measure sludge and scum on every maintenance visit, and pumping is due once solids fill roughly a third of the tank. Filters get rinsed. Small cracks around the riser get sealed before groundwater finds them.

    Septic Alarm Troubleshooting in Emporia

    A septic alarm sounding is the system's way of saying the water is too high in the pump chamber or the aerobic unit. Press the silence button and cut back on water. We check whether the pump runs and whether a float is hung up on its cord. A tripped breaker is the cheapest answer.

    Drain Field Line Jetting in Emporia
