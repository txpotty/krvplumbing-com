    Commercial Walls Waterproofing in Emporia

    Commercial basements, elevator pits and mechanical rooms in Emporia take on water the same way houses do, just with more at stake. One wet electrical room can close a business for days. We schedule around operating hours, work with property managers on access, and follow the waterproofing specs written into the building plans for below-grade walls. Downtime stays short.

    Drainage System Integration in Emporia, KS

    Water has to go somewhere once a wall is sealed. Exterior footing drains, interior drain tile and surface French drains all do the same job at different depths. We match the drain to where the water comes from, which might be a high water table, a yard that slopes toward the house, or a gutter dumping at the corner. Downspouts get extended at least four feet out.

    Crawl Space Vapor Barriers in Emporia

    Bare dirt in a crawl space releases gallons of water vapor a day into the air under your floor. A 10 mil or heavier polyethylene barrier, lapped 12 inches at the seams and taped, cuts that off and keeps the joists and subfloor dry. We run it up the foundation walls and fasten it with termination bar. Cheap fix, big difference.

    Shower Wall Waterproofing in Emporia

    Tile and grout are not waterproof. Water passes through grout every time you shower, so the layer behind the tile, cement board with a membrane or a foam board system, is what actually keeps the studs dry. We tear out failed tile, install new backer, and apply Schluter Kerdi, RedGard or Hydro Ban at the thickness the manufacturer calls for. Corners, niches and valve holes get extra fabric or preformed pieces.

    Shower Pan Leak Repair and Waterproofing in Emporia, KS

    Wet baseboards outside the shower or a stain on the ceiling below usually point to the pan. We plug the drain, fill the pan with an inch or two of water, and mark the level and wait 24 hours. A drop means a leak. Rebuilding means a mortar pre-slope of a quarter inch per foot, a new PVC liner or bonded membrane, and a clamping drain tightened onto the liner. The new pan gets its own flood test before tile.

    Exterior Block Wall Waterproofing in Emporia, KS

    White crust on block or stem walls is efflorescence, salt left behind as water moves through the masonry and dries. The usual cause is a sprinkler head aimed at the house instead of the bed, or soil graded toward the foundation. We fix the water first. Then we clean the wall, let it dry and apply a breathable masonry sealer or elastomeric coating.

    Below-Grade and Basement Wall Waterproofing in Emporia

    A lot of Kansas housing dates from the mid-century building boom, with galvanized steel supply lines and cast iron drains. Any wall below grade sits in soil that gets saturated after heavy rain or snowmelt, and that water pushes against the wall with steady hydrostatic pressure. We inject cracks, coat the wall inside or out, and add drain tile wherever water collects at the footing. A sump goes in if groundwater sits high. Not every basement needs one.

    Basement Wall Sealing in Emporia, KS

    Concrete block is porous, and its hollow cores fill with water like a sponge. Around Emporia, summer heat keeps irrigation systems and outdoor spigots working hard, so leaks and backflow issues are common. We drill weep holes at the bottom course to drain the cores, then coat the face with a masonry waterproofing product that lets vapor out but stops liquid water. The musty smell usually fades within weeks. Those weep holes empty into the interior drain if the house has one.
