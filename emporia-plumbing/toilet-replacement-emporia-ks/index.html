    Toilet Rough-In Adjustments in Emporia, KS

    Rough-in is the distance from the finished wall to the center of the drain, usually 12 inches, sometimes 10 or 14 in older homes. Take the measurement from the wall to the closet bolts, not from the baseboard trim. If the new toilet's rough-in does not match, it either sits too far out or will not fit. An offset flange can correct an inch or two without moving the drain.

    Commercial Toilet Replacement in Emporia, KS

    Office and retail restrooms wear out toilets faster than any house, and a replacement there usually means a floor-mount or wall-hung bowl with a flushometer instead of a tank. Work gets scheduled around your business hours. We match the bowl to the gallons per flush on the valve, since a mismatch leaves waste behind or wastes water.

    Comfort-Height Toilet Replacement in Emporia

    Going from a standard bowl to a comfort-height model raises the seat two or three inches, which matters a lot for seniors and anyone recovering from knee or hip surgery. No drain changes are needed. If there is room, a grab bar beside it helps too, and we can anchor that into a stud during the same visit.

    High-Efficiency Toilet (HET) Upgrades in Emporia, KS

    High-efficiency toilets carry a WaterSense label and a MaP score, which tells you how many grams of waste the toilet clears in one flush. Look for a score of at least 800. Higher numbers mean fewer double flushes and fewer plunger trips, and that is where the real water savings come from.

    Cracked Bowl or Tank Replacement in Emporia

    Sometimes only the tank is cracked, and if the model is still in production, a matching tank costs less than a whole new toilet. We check the model number first. If the bowl is the cracked part, the whole toilet gets replaced, since the bowl carries the seal to the floor and cannot be patched.

    Urgent Toilet Replacement in Emporia, KS

    Late-night failures happen, usually a tank that splits along a seam or a bowl that cracks at the base and keeps seeping no matter how many towels go down. Closing the angle stop stops the water. We can usually replace it the next morning, and we will tell you if the floor needs to dry before we set the new one.

    Standard Toilet Replacement in Emporia

    Two-piece toilets are what most houses have, and after twenty or thirty years the tank bolts corrode, the porcelain gets crazed and the flush never quite clears the bowl. That is when we swap them. The old unit comes off, the flange gets scraped clean and checked for cracks, then set the new one on a fresh wax ring with new closet bolts.

    Emergency Toilet Replacement in Emporia

Most of the time the new toilet goes in that same day, and we check the subfloor for soaked plywood before setting it.
