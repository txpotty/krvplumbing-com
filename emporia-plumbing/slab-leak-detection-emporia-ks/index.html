    Slab Leak Reroutes in Emporia

    A reroute abandons the leaking line under the floor. New pipe runs through the walls or attic instead, so there is no concrete work and the cost is often lower than going through tile. We usually use PEX because it handles heat and resists corrosion. It suits tile and hardwood homes.

    Acoustic Slab Leak Detection in Emporia

    Water escaping a pressurized pipe makes a hiss that carries up through concrete. Our ground microphones hear it. We move the pickup across the floor in a grid over the traced pipe route, listening for the spot where the sound peaks and then checking it again from a different angle, which works best on copper and less well on PEX.

    Cold Water Slab Leak Detection in Emporia, KS

    Cold-side leaks leave no warm spot. They are harder to see. They usually show up as a running meter, damp carpet, a musty smell or water seeping at baseboards, so we isolate the cold side, map the pipe with a line tracer and listen along the route with a ground microphone.

    Pressure Testing and Isolation in Emporia

    We always start with isolation. Our tech closes the main, caps or valves off the hot and cold sides, and puts a gauge on each, then watches which side loses pressure over a set time. The gauge points us to the right line. It also rules out a toilet or faucet leak masquerading as a slab leak in your Emporia, KS home.

    Post-Tension Slab Leak Detection in Emporia

    Many newer homes sit on post-tension slabs. These slabs have steel tendons stretched under tens of thousands of pounds of force, and cutting one by mistake is dangerous and can damage the foundation. We scan for tendons with ground-penetrating radar or a rebar locator before opening anything, or recommend a reroute that never touches the concrete.

    Why Locating First Saves Money in Emporia
