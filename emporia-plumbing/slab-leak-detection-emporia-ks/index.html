    Slab Leak Reroutes in Emporia

    Rerouting abandons the leaking line under the slab and runs new pipe through the walls or attic. It avoids breaking concrete and often costs less than direct access under finished floors. We typically use PEX for reroutes because it handles heat and resists hard-water corrosion. This option is a good fit when the slab is covered in tile or hardwood.

    Thermal Imaging for Slab Leaks in Emporia, KS

    Thermal imaging cameras allow us to see temperature differences on surfaces, which can indicate a hidden water leak. As water escapes a hot water line, it creates a distinct thermal signature on the concrete slab. This technique is particularly useful for hot water line leaks and helps us narrow down the search area quickly without any physical disruption to your property in Emporia.

    Slab Leak Repair Through Direct Access in Emporia, KS

    When a single leak is found on otherwise healthy pipe, direct access can be the most economical repair. We cut a small opening in the floor at the marked spot, repair the pipe and pressure test it. Then we patch the concrete and leave the area ready for flooring. Accurate detection keeps that opening as small as possible.

    Repiping After Multiple Slab Leaks in Emporia, KS

    If your home has had two or more slab leaks, the remaining pipe is likely corroded too. Repiping replaces all supply lines with new PEX or copper run through the walls and attic. It is a bigger project, but it ends the cycle of recurring slab leaks. We can typically complete a full repipe in two to four days.

    Emergency Slab Leak Response in Emporia

    A slab leak that is flooding floors or causing water to bubble up through tile needs fast attention. We respond quickly, help you shut off the water and isolate the leaking side if possible. That often lets you keep some water service until the repair. Then we locate and fix the leak.

    Electronic Leak Detection in Emporia

    For elusive leaks, our electronic leak detection equipment sends a signal through your pipes and measures changes that indicate a breach. This technology allows us to locate leaks in many pipe materials, including copper, PEX and PVC, commonly found in Emporia homes. It helps prevent exploratory digging, saving you time and money.

    Post-Tension Slab Leak Detection in Emporia

    Many newer homes are built on post-tension slabs that use steel cables under high tension. Cutting into one without care is dangerous and can damage the foundation. We locate the cables with scanning equipment before any repair access is opened. This lets us repair the leak safely or recommend a reroute that avoids the slab entirely.
