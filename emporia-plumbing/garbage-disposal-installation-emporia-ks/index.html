    Disposal Installation for Double Sinks in Emporia, KS

    Double bowl sinks put the disposal on one side and a continuous waste or end outlet tee on the other. The layout keeps both bowls draining and stops the disposal from pushing water into the second bowl, a problem when the tee is too close to the discharge. We use heavy-wall fittings.

    Commercial Disposal Installation in Emporia

    Commercial disposals run 1 to 5 horsepower and typically come with a control panel and solenoid valve and need their own circuit. We set the unit on the mounting collar, run the drain to the grease trap where the local rules require it, and wire the controls so the water runs whenever the motor does. Health codes vary.     Disposal Upgrades in Emporia, KS

    Upgrading from an old 1/3 HP unit to something quieter is a common request. We disconnect and remove the old disposal, check the trap and drain arm for corrosion, and install the new unit with a new mount, so the upgrade does not inherit the old unit's worn parts. Old drain arms often need replacing. Galvanized arms rust from the inside.

    Air Switch Disposal Installation in Emporia, KS

    Air switches start the disposal with a button on the sink or counter. The button pushes air through a tube to a control box in the cabinet, which keeps electricity away from the button and avoids a new wall switch. Islands use them often.

    Batch-Feed Disposal Installation in Emporia, KS

    A batch-feed disposal will not start until the lid is in, and the lid holds a magnet that closes a switch inside the unit. No wall switch is needed, which makes them handy in kitchens where running a new switch would mean opening finished walls. You grind one load at a time. Families with small kids often like the trade.

    Standard Disposal Installation in Emporia

    Most households in Emporia do well with a continuous-feed unit between 1/3 and 1/2 horsepower. We remove the old unit if there is one, set a new flange in fresh plumber's putty, hang the disposal on a new mount and connect it to the trap with a new discharge tube and gasket. Then we wire it and test it. We listen for a smooth spin and watch the drain clear.

    Batch-Feed Units for Kitchens Without a Switch in Emporia

    Batch-feed disposals only run when the stopper lid is in place, which adds safety for families with kids. They need no wall switch, so they are a good choice for kitchens where adding a switch would mean cutting into finished drywall or tile backsplash. The drain hookup is the usual setup.
