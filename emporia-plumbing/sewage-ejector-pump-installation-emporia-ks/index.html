    Ejector Pump Alarms and Backup Options in Emporia

    A high water alarm belongs on every ejector system, since a pump failure can lead to a sewage backup quickly. We install audible alarms with their own power source, or Wi-Fi alarms that text your phone, so you hear about a stuck float while the basin is still filling and not after waste has reached the basement floor. Severe thunderstorms are common in Kansas, and heavy downpours can swamp gutters, storm drains, and older sewer lines. Battery backup is an option.

    Upflush and Macerating Toilet Systems in Emporia

    Macerating systems like Saniflo let you add a toilet and sink without breaking the concrete floor. The unit sits behind the toilet, grinds the waste and pumps it through a small discharge line, often three-quarter inch or one inch, up to the nearest drain, which makes it a good fit for a small basement half bath.     Ejector Pump Installation Cost Comparison in Emporia, KS

    Replacing a pump in an existing basin is the least expensive option, often done in a few hours. A full new system with basin, floor excavation, roof vent, electrical and alarm costs more, but it is usually the only way to add a below-sewer bath besides a macerator. Ask for both numbers.

    Ejector Pump Replacement in Emporia

    Basement Bathroom Ejector Systems in Emporia
