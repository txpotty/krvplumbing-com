    Tree Root Sewer Clogs in Emporia

    Clay and older cast iron sewer pipe have a joint every few feet. Each joint is an opening.     Sewer Cleanout Installation in Emporia

    Some older homes have no cleanout at all. That makes every clog harder. Without one, a plumber has to pull a toilet or climb on the roof to reach the main, and a two-way cleanout by the foundation fixes that. It pays for itself on the next call.

    Main Line vs. Branch Line Clogs in Emporia, KS

    One slow drain is a branch clog. Several slow drains is the main. Water rising in the shower when you flush is a strong sign the clog is past the point where those two lines join, which is why we ask which fixtures are acting up before we decide where to open the line. The right entry point saves time.

    Residential Clogged Sewer Lines in Emporia

    Most home sewer clogs come from three things: roots, grease and wipes. Often all three together. Grease coats the pipe, wipes catch on it and roots lock everything into a plug. We clear it with a sewer machine, then check on camera to see the main cause.

    Hydro-Jetting Services in Emporia, KS

    Jetting sends water at thousands of psi through a nozzle that sprays backward and drives itself down the pipe. It scrubs the whole wall. A cable punches a hole through a grease plug, and a jetter clears the coating that starts the next clog. We camera the pipe first. Badly cracked pipe gets a gentler approach.

    Grease and Sludge Buildup Removal in Emporia, KS

    Kitchen grease cools in the sewer line and sticks to the walls. Layer after layer builds up. Soap scum and food bind to it until the opening shrinks to a fraction of the pipe, after which ordinary use causes backups. KRV Plumbing jets it out with a grease nozzle in Emporia, KS.
