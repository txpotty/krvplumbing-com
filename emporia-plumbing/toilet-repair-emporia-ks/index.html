    Running Toilet Fixes in Emporia

    A toilet that constantly runs or refills itself intermittently is not only annoying but also a major water waste. This issue in Emporia is usually caused by a faulty flapper valve, an improperly adjusted float, a worn-out fill valve, or sediment buildup. Our technicians diagnose the specific component causing the problem and replace it with durable, long-lasting parts. We ensure proper alignment and adjustment of all internal mechanisms to restore your toilet's efficient operation and stop unnecessary water consumption.

    Weak Flush and Clog Repair in Emporia, KS

    If your toilet needs two flushes or clogs often, the problem may be scale in the rim jets, a partial blockage in the trapway or a flush valve that does not open fully. We auger the trap, check the vent if gurgling is present and clean the jets with a descaling treatment. Some low-flow toilets from the 1990s simply flush poorly, and we will tell you honestly if replacement is the better fix.

    Leaking Toilet Base Repair in Emporia, KS

    Water around the base typically means the wax ring has failed or the toilet has been rocking on a loose flange. On slab foundations, the flange often sits on concrete and can crack when bolts are overtightened. We pull the toilet, inspect the flange, install a repair ring or new flange if needed and reset the bowl on a new seal. We also check for soft flooring so you know if any water damage needs attention.
