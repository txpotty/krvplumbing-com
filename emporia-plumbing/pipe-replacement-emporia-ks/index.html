    Repiping and Pipe Replacement Options in Emporia, KS

    PEX Pipe Replacement in Emporia

    PEX bends around corners and comes in long rolls, so a typical repipe needs fewer fittings than copper would on the same layout. Hard freezes happen every winter in Kansas, and pipes in exterior walls, garages, and crawlspaces are the first to burst. PEX can stretch a little in a freeze instead of splitting. We use PEX-a with expansion fittings where possible, which give a full-bore connection and hold up well over time. Faster install.

    PEX Repiping in Emporia

    PEX installs quicker. Flexible lines snake through walls with fewer openings, while manifold setups give you separate shutoffs. Popular choice.

    Galvanized Pipe Replacement in Emporia, KS

    Galvanized steel was common before the 1960s. Inside, rust builds once the coating fails. Over decades the opening shrinks, pressure drops and the water turns brown after sitting, and the threads where pipes join are often the first to leak. Replacing it is the only fix.

    Polybutylene Pipe Replacement in Emporia

    Polybutylene is gray or blue plastic installed from roughly the late 1970s to mid 1990s. It degrades from the inside. Failures tend to be sudden splits at fittings rather than slow drips, which can cause insurance headaches. Replace it.

    Slab Leak Pipe Replacement in Emporia

    Rerouting is often better than digging. Breaking a slab is messy and costly. We run new pipe through the attic or walls to bypass the leaking line under the concrete, which leaves the floors alone and prevents the same line from failing again. Fast too.

    Drain Pipe Replacement in Emporia, KS

    Drain lines fail too. Cast iron cracks and scales. We replace cracked cast iron, failing ABS or old galvanized drains with PVC, and making sure vents and fall are correct. Camera first.

    Copper Pinhole Leak Replacement in Emporia, KS

    Pinhole leaks start small. First a damp spot, then drywall damage. They usually mean the copper wall has thinned from corrosion, so finding one often means others are close. Repipe or patch, we explain.

    Burst Pipe Repair and Replacement in Emporia, KS

    Hard freezes happen every winter in Kansas, and pipes in exterior walls, garages, and crawlspaces are the first to burst. Frozen pipes split when water inside expands. Exposed pipe in crawlspaces, garages and outside walls is most at risk, so we fix it and protect it from the next freeze. Then we test it.
