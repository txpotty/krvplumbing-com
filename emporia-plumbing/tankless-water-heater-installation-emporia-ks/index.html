    What Goes Into a Tankless Conversion in Emporia, KS

    Rinnai Tankless Water Heater Installation in Emporia, KS

    Rinnai has been making tankless heaters for decades, and parts are easy to get. We install both condensing and non-condensing Rinnai models, and the right one depends on where the vent can go and how much you want to spend up front. The non-condensing ones need stainless vent pipe. Condensing models use PVC vent and a condensate drain.

    Navien Tankless Water Heater Installation in Emporia

    Navien builds condensing tankless units with stainless heat exchangers, and several models come with a recirculation pump built in. The built-in pump can move hot water toward a distant bath on a timer, cutting the wait at the tap without a separate pump. Navien vents in PVC. Condensing units also need a condensate drain.

    Commercial Tankless Water Heater Installation in Emporia

    Commercial hot water loads in Emporia run longer and harder than a house. A restaurant can run a dish machine, hand sinks and a mop sink together, and the rack must hold temperature through the rush. Units share a header manifold. Service one, keep the others going.
