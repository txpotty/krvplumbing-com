    Sectional Point Repair in Prairie Village

    When only one section of a line is damaged, a point repair can fix it without lining the whole length. We position a short resin liner at the damaged spot using the camera and inflate it until it cures. Point repairs are a cost-effective fix for a single crack, hole or root entry. They are common on otherwise healthy lines.

    Pipe Bursting Sewer Line Replacement in Prairie Village

    Pipe bursting is a trenchless method used for full sewer line replacement. It involves pulling a new pipe through the old one while breaking apart the existing pipe. This creates a seamless, durable replacement, often using high-density polyethylene pipe, which is very resistant to corrosion and root intrusion, common problems in older Prairie Village homes. This method is ideal when the existing pipe is severely damaged or collapsed.

    Perma-Liner Trenchless Solutions in Prairie Village

    We use proven trenchless products like those from Perma-Liner, a leader in CIPP technology. Their materials and systems deliver a durable, long-lasting repair that stands up to the demands of local soil and climate conditions. Using trusted brands like Perma-Liner protects the integrity and longevity of our work.

    Cured-In-Place Pipe Relining in Prairie Village, KS

    CIPP relining creates a new pipe within an existing damaged sewer line. A resin-saturated felt tube is inserted into the old pipe and then inflated, curing in place to form a smooth, durable and watertight liner. This technique is well suited for repairing cracks, leaks and minor offsets without needing to dig up the entire line. It is a quick and effective solution for many common sewer problems faced by Kansas homeowners.
