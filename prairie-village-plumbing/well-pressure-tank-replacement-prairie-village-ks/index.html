    Constant pressure systems use a variable speed drive that adjusts pump speed to demand. Pressure stays steady even with two showers running. These systems can run on a smaller tank, though the drive and wiring add to the price. We explain the trade-off.

    Tank Tee, Gauge and Relief Valve Replacement in Prairie Village

    The tank tee is the brass manifold that connects tank, switch, gauge, drain and relief valve. Old ones leak at threads. The relief valve should open before the system reaches a dangerous pressure, so we replace a crusted or corroded one rather than trust it. Whole set, one visit.

    Pressure Tanks for Well Houses and Barns in Prairie Village, KS

    Tanks in outbuildings face heat, cold and damp. Steel rusts fast in a damp well house. We often recommend a composite tank in those spaces, raise it off the floor on a stand, and insulate exposed piping and the switch when the building sees freezing weather. Simple protection.

    Cost-Effective Well Pressure Tank Upgrades in Prairie Village, KS

    Upgrading a size often costs less than people think. Going one size larger adds only a little. A larger tank means fewer pump starts per day, which is the cheapest way to extend the life of your pump, so we often recommend it during a replacement.

    Pressure Switch Replacement in Prairie Village, KS

    Switch contacts pit and burn over time. A pitted switch can stick on or chatter. When we swap a tank we look at the contacts and the sensing port underneath, and we usually recommend replacing a switch older than the tank it served. Cheap part.

    Pressure Tank Sizing and Upsizing in Prairie Village

    Drawdown is the usable water between cut-out and cut-in. Small tanks give surprisingly little. We divide pump flow by drawdown to see how many starts per hour the pump will face, aiming for at least one minute of run time per cycle. That often points to a bigger tank.
