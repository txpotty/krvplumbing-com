    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Expansion Tank Installation in Prairie Village

    While less common than with traditional tank water heaters, an expansion tank can still be required or beneficial for tankless water heaters, especially if your home has a closed plumbing system because of a backflow preventer or pressure reducing valve.     When water heats up, it expands. Without an expansion tank, this expanded water has nowhere to go, increasing pressure within your closed plumbing system. This constant pressure can prematurely wear out water heater components and relief valves and even cause leaks or ruptures. The expansion tank absorbs this excess volume, protecting your whole system.

    Most expansion tanks last 5 to 10 years. Hard water and high pressure can cut that life, so we check them during water heater service.

    If your home has a pressure reducing valve, check valve or backflow preventer on the main, you likely have a closed system that needs one. Many local codes require it whenever a water heater is installed on a closed system.

    Typically, installing a water heater expansion tank takes about 1 to 2 hours. This includes assessment, preparation, installation and testing. Our skilled plumbers work efficiently to minimize any disruption to your hot water supply.
