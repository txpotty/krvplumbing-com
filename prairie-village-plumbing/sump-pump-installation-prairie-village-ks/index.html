    Exterior Drainage and Yard Sump Pumps in Prairie Village

    Some homes collect water in sunken patios, below-grade driveways or window wells rather than a basement.     Submersible Sump Pump Installation in Prairie Village

    Submersible sump pumps sit directly inside the sump pit, running quietly and taking up less space. They are typically more powerful and durable, making them a popular choice for many homeowners. We handle the complete installation of submersible units, including proper sealing and electrical connections, tailored to the particular needs of your Prairie Village property.

    Primary Sump Pump Installation in Prairie Village

    A primary sump pump is your first defense against basement flooding. We install robust, electrically powered pumps designed to automatically activate when water levels rise in the sump pit. Our plumbers will assess your basement's water intrusion risks, considering local water tables and soil types in Prairie Village, to recommend the right pump size and horsepower for effective water removal.

    New Sump Pit Installation in Prairie Village

    If your basement or crawl space has no pit, we can create one. We cut the slab, excavate through soil, compacted clay or rock and set a durable basin. We add gravel around the basin to help water flow in. Then we patch the concrete and install the pump and cover. A new pit gives you a reliable place to collect and remove water.

    High Water Alarms and Smart Monitoring in Prairie Village

    A high water alarm lets you know when water rises above normal levels. Smart monitors can send alerts to your phone when the pump runs or fails. We install alarms and Wi-Fi monitors on new and existing pumps. These devices are particularly useful for vacation homes and rental properties. Early warnings can prevent major water damage.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Prairie Village, KS
