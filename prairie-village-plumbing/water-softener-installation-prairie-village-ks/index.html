    Salt-Based Water Softener Installation in Prairie Village

    Salt-based (ion exchange) water softeners are the most common and effective solution for truly softening water. These systems use a resin bed to exchange hard minerals like calcium and magnesium with sodium ions. KRV Plumbing carefully installs many models of salt-based softeners, ensuring proper connection to your main water line, correct drainage setup and accurate programming for regeneration cycles in your Prairie Village home.

    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Installation in Prairie Village

    For homeowners looking for an alternative to traditional softeners, we also offer salt-free water conditioner installation. These systems condition the water to prevent scale buildup without removing healthy minerals or adding salt. While not true softeners, they are an effective option for scale prevention and are popular in many Prairie Village households.

    Whole-House Water Softener Systems in Prairie Village

    A whole-house water softener system is installed directly on your main water line, treating all the water entering your Prairie Village home. This means every faucet, shower and appliance receives soft water. Our installation process involves carefully integrating the system into your existing plumbing, including needed bypass valves and pressure regulators, to provide comprehensive protection against hard water throughout your property.
