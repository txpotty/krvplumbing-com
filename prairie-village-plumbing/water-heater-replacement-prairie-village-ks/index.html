    Attic Water Heater Replacement in Prairie Village

    Newer construction mostly uses PEX and plastic drains, while older houses often mix copper, galvanized, and plastic from past repairs. Attic water heaters are harder to swap than ones in a garage or closet. Moving a 50-gallon tank through a pull-down ladder or small opening takes two people, and code wants a pan with a drain line, a platform and a light. Two-person crew, always.

    Electric Water Heater Replacement in Prairie Village

    Electric water heaters in Prairie Village usually fail from a burned-out heating element, a bad thermostat or a tank that has finally rusted through at the bottom. Elements are cheap to swap. A leaking tank cannot, so if water is coming from the tank body itself, we replace the heater and match the voltage, wattage and breaker size to what your panel already supplies. Hot water is back the same day.

    Conventional Tank Water Heater Replacement in Prairie Village

    Standard tank heaters remain the most common type, and they usually fail from age, rust or sediment. A leak at the base, loud popping or rusty hot water means it is time, and a like-for-like or resized swap usually takes two to four hours. You shower tonight.

    Hybrid Water Heater Installation in Prairie Village, KS

    Hybrid heat pump water heaters pull heat from the air around them, using a fraction of the electricity a standard electric tank uses. They need room and airflow, about 700 cubic feet or a louvered door, plus a condensate drain, and they do best in a garage or basement that stays above freezing. The space runs cooler.

    Bradford White Water Heater Replacement in Prairie Village

    Bradford White sells through professional plumbers only, so you will not find one at a big-box store. They hold up well, and warranty support runs through the installing plumber, which makes a future claim easy for us to handle. Supply houses stock them.

We price all three.
