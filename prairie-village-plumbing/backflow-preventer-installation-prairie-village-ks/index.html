    Reduced Pressure Zone (RPZ) Assembly Installation in Prairie Village

    RPZ assemblies offer the highest level of backflow protection and are commonly required for high-hazard applications in Prairie Village. These devices feature two independent check valves and a relief valve that opens to discharge water if pressure differentials indicate a backflow condition. KRV Plumbing specializes in precise RPZ installation, with proper sizing, placement and testing to meet all regulatory requirements for residential and commercial properties. We work with top brands like Watts and Febco.

    Backflow Preventer Installation Cost Comparison in Prairie Village, KS

    A residential pressure vacuum breaker is the most affordable option, while larger RPZ assemblies for commercial buildings cost significantly more in parts and labor. Size, brand, enclosure needs and how much existing pipe must be modified all affect the price. Replacing a failed old device with a new one is usually cheaper than repeated rebuilds. We give you an itemized quote that includes the initial certification test.

    Pressure Vacuum Breaker (PVB) Installation in Prairie Village, KS

    For low-hazard applications, such as lawn irrigation systems in Prairie Village, KS, a pressure vacuum breaker, or PVB, is a popular and cost-effective backflow prevention device. PVBs protect against back-siphonage by letting air into the system when pressure drops. Our certified plumbers carefully install PVBs above the highest point of downstream piping, as code requires, to maintain effective protection. We also check for proper clearance and drainage.

    Backflow Testing and Certification in Prairie Village

    Most water providers require testable backflow assemblies to be certified at installation and then once a year. Our certified testers check each valve's opening point, relief valve operation and differential pressure. If a device fails, we can repair or rebuild it on the spot with genuine parts. We submit passing results so you avoid compliance letters or service interruption.

    Double Check Valve Assembly Installation in Prairie Village

    Double check assemblies are used for low hazard connections such as many fire sprinkler systems and some irrigation lines. They have two spring-loaded check valves and test cocks for annual certification. Unlike an RPZ, they do not discharge water, so they can be installed in vaults or below grade where local rules allow. We size them to avoid too much pressure loss on your system.
