    Tank to Tankless Conversions in Prairie Village

    Conversions in Prairie Village tend to run a full workday.     Propane Tankless Water Heater Installation in Prairie Village

    Propane homes in Prairie Village are good candidates for tankless, using a model built or converted for propane.     Gas Tankless Water Heater Installation in Prairie Village

    Gas tankless units in Prairie Village are the common pick for whole-house hot water because a single 199,000 BTU burner can feed two showers and a sink at the same time without losing temperature. The catch is gas supply. Most homes need the line upsized from the meter, often from half-inch to three-quarter inch or larger. We size it and pressure test it before the unit is lit.

    Rinnai Tankless Water Heater Installation in Prairie Village, KS

    Rinnai has been making tankless heaters for decades, and replacement parts are widely stocked. Condensing models use PVC vent and a condensate drain.

    Upgrading from Tank to Tankless in Prairie Village, KS

    Switching from a tank takes more than unbolting one and hanging the other. A typical 40-gallon gas tank uses around 40,000 BTU, while a whole-home tankless unit may need 150,000 to 199,000, so the gas line from the meter almost always gets upsized. The vent changes as well. We plan both on the quote.

    Navien Tankless Water Heater Installation in Prairie Village

    Navien builds condensing tankless units with stainless heat exchangers, and several models come with a recirculation pump built in. The built-in pump can move hot water toward a distant bath on a timer, cutting the wait at the tap without a separate pump. Venting is usually two-inch or three-inch PVC. Condensing units also need a condensate drain.

    Electric Tankless Water Heater Installation in Prairie Village, KS

Some need two or three 40 to 60 amp double-pole breakers, and a 100-amp panel often cannot carry that on top of the air conditioner and range. We check the panel first.
