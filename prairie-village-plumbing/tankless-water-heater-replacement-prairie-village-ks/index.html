    Frequently Asked Questions About Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Prairie Village

    The cost of tankless water heater replacement in Prairie Village, KS usually ranges from $3,000 to $6,000, depending on the unit's capacity, brand, complexity of installation, such as converting from a tank model, and any necessary electrical or gas line upgrades. This price usually includes the unit itself and labor. We provide a detailed, upfront quote after a thorough assessment of your home.

    Repeated error codes, fluctuating temperature, water leaking from inside the cabinet and a unit older than 15 years are common signs. If the heat exchanger is leaking, replacement is typically the better value.

    We install Navien, Rinnai, Noritz, Takagi and Rheem tankless heaters. Each has strengths, and we recommend based on your flow needs, venting options and budget.

    A standard tankless water heater replacement usually takes between 4 and 8 hours. This timeframe can vary based on whether we are replacing an existing tankless unit with a similar model or upgrading from a traditional tank water heater. A tank conversion can call for more extensive changes to gas lines, electrical or venting.

    Most jurisdictions in Kansas require a permit for water heater replacement, especially when gas piping or venting changes. We handle permits where they are required.

    Well-maintained tankless units can last 15 to 20 years. In hard water areas without regular descaling, heat exchangers can fail far sooner. Annual flushing makes the largest difference.

    Most manufacturers offer 10 to 15 years on the heat exchanger and about 5 years on parts for residential use. We add our own labor warranty and register the unit for you.

    Tankless water heater replacement is a complex job that involves gas lines, electrical wiring, water connections and venting. It is highly recommended to hire a licensed professional like KRV Plumbing due to the safety risks and potential for improper installation, which can lead to leaks, gas hazards or inefficient operation. Professionals also ensure code compliance and warranty validity.
