    PEX-B Crimp and Clamp Systems in Prairie Village

    PEX-B is stiffer and more affordable, and it is joined with copper crimp rings or stainless steel cinch clamps over brass or poly fittings.     PEX Main Water Line Installation in Prairie Village

    The main water line brings water into your home, and a failing one can cause serious issues. We provide professional PEX main water line installation, replacing old, damaged lines with durable PEX.     Manifold and Home-Run PEX Layouts in Prairie Village, KS

    A manifold system sends a dedicated line from a central hub to each fixture, similar to a breaker panel for water. Each line gets its own shutoff, which makes repairs easier and keeps pressure steady when multiple fixtures run. Home-run layouts use more pipe but fewer fittings in walls. We often combine manifold and trunk-and-branch designs to balance cost and performance.

    Full Home PEX Repiping in Prairie Village

    If your home has old, corroded or leaking pipes, a full home PEX repipe can drastically improve your plumbing system. We remove outdated galvanized, copper or polybutylene pipes and replace them with durable PEX, upgrading your entire water delivery system. This prevents future leaks, improves water pressure and eliminates the rust-colored water often seen with older piping in Prairie Village.

    PEX-A vs. PEX-B Installation in Prairie Village

    We offer installation for both PEX-A and PEX-B piping and understand the different advantages of each. PEX-A, often preferred for its flexibility and ability to recover from minor kinks, uses expansion fittings. PEX-B is more rigid but also very durable, typically using crimp or compression fittings. Our plumbers in Prairie Village can help you decide which type of PEX suits your project, considering factors like budget and application.
