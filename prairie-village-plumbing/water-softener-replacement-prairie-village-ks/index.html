    Fleck control valves, the 5600SXT and 9100SXT in particular, are on a large share of the softeners we see in Kansas homes. If the valve is old enough that parts are hard to find, a new valve on a new tank is the smarter buy. Newer Fleck heads drop right in.

    Scale in the water heater is the expensive part of hard water. Scale settles on the bottom of a tank heater and insulates the water from the burner, and tankless heaters clog even faster. A working softener stops that.

    Emergency Softener Replacement in Prairie Village, KS

    A softener that sticks in regeneration can run water to the drain for hours, and a cracked bypass or tank can flood a garage floor. First step, turn the bypass handle. We can usually replace a leaking or stuck system within a day or two, and same day if we have the size on the truck.

    Softener Replacement on a Budget in Prairie Village, KS

    Capacity and valve brand set most of the price. For a small house on moderately hard water, a basic metered single-tank unit often does the job for much less than a twin-tank system. We show both options side by side.

    Long-Term Softener Costs in Prairie Village, KS

    Long-term cost on a softener is mostly salt and water. A metered valve with an efficient brine setting can cut salt use by a third or more compared with an old timer unit, and that adds up over fifteen years. That is how we compare models.

    Fleck Valve Programming in Prairie Village, KS

    Programming is where most Fleck replacements go wrong. A Fleck needs hardness, household size and regen time entered right, otherwise it cycles too often or runs out of capacity. It is programmed before we pack up.

    Resin Tank and Media Replacement in Prairie Village, KS

    Resin beads break down after 10 to 15 years, faster on chlorinated city water, and the softener keeps regenerating while the water slowly turns hard again. Rebedding with new resin can be cheaper than a new system. It depends on the valve. New resin under a tired valve rarely lasts.
