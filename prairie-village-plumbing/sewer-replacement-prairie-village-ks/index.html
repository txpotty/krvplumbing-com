    Residential Sewer Replacement in Prairie Village, KS

    Clay and Orangeburg Pipe Replacement in Prairie Village, KS

    Clay pipe came in short sections with a joint every two or three feet. Each of those joints is an opening for roots. Orangeburg is worse, since the tar-paper wall softens and deforms under the weight of the soil, and both are good candidates for bursting because they break apart easily. Fast pulls.

    Traditional Trench Sewer Replacement in Prairie Village

    An open trench gives full access to the pipe from the house to the main. A small excavator does most of it, with hand digging near other lines. The old pipe comes out, the trench bottom gets a bed of gravel or sand, and new 4-inch PVC goes in at a steady quarter inch of fall per foot, checked with a level before backfill. Then the camera.

    Sewer Cleanout Installation in Prairie Village, KS

    A lot of older houses have no outside cleanout. Every clog then means pulling a toilet. When we replace a sewer line we add a two-way cleanout near the foundation, bringing a capped riser up to grade where you can reach it, so future cleaning or camera work is quick and cheap.

    Emergency Sewer Line Replacement in Prairie Village, KS

    Sewage coming up a floor drain or tub is an emergency. Stop running water in the house right away. We open the cleanout to relieve the backup, run a camera to find the break, and when the pipe has caved in, we dig to that spot immediately, putting in a section of new pipe to get you flowing before planning the full replacement.

    Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Prairie Village

    Cast iron sewer pipe lasts a long time, though not indefinitely. Over decades it scales up inside and the bottom wears through. We replace it with PVC, and where cast iron remains under the house we use a shielded no-hub coupling at the transition. Clean seal.

    Trenchless Sewer Repair Options in Prairie Village, KS

    Pipe bursting needs two pits. One sits at the house, the other at the far end of the run. A cone-shaped head is pulled through the old line, breaking it outward, while fused HDPE pipe follows right behind it, so the new line takes the same path without a trench. Driveways stay put.

    Open-Trench Sewer Replacement in Prairie Village, KS

    Trenching lets us fix grade. Correcting slope is where it shines. When an old line has a sag that holds water and solids, bursting would just follow the same sag, while a trench lets us lay new pipe at the right pitch. Bellies disappear.
