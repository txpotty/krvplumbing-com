    Frequently Asked Questions About Rooter Services in Hays

    Rooting a sink, tub or toilet typically costs $125 to $300. Main sewer line rooting through a cleanout usually runs $200 to $500. Adding hydro jetting brings the total to about $450 to $1,000.

    Our technicians take every precaution to keep your home clean during rooter service. We use drop cloths and protective gear to contain any mess, so the work area is tidy after the job is done. We respect your property and aim for a clean, efficient service.

    Snaking uses an auger to break through and pull out blockages, while hydro-jetting uses high-pressure water to scour the entire inside of the pipe, removing buildup like grease and mineral deposits. Hydro-jetting provides a much more thorough cleaning, often recommended for recurring clogs or main sewer lines.

    For most standard drain cleaning or rooter services within your property's existing plumbing system, permits are generally not required in Hays, KS. However, if the rooter service reveals a need for significant repair, replacement, or excavation of the main sewer line, permits may be necessary. We will let you know if any permits are needed for your situation.

    Drain snaking, also known as augering, uses a long, flexible cable with a cutting head to physically break through or retrieve blockages like hair or tree roots. Hydro-jetting uses high-pressure water to clean the entire interior of the pipe, removing grease, mineral buildup, and sludge, leaving pipes much cleaner than snaking alone. Hydro-jetting is usually more effective for severe or recurring clogs.

    Slow drains in several fixtures, gurgling toilets and sewage odors are common signs. Water backing up into a tub when you flush is another clue. Frequent clogs in the same line also suggest a deeper blockage.

    While chemical drain cleaners are available, KRV Plumbing generally advises against them. They often only partially clear clogs, can damage pipes over time, and pose safety risks. Professional rooter services are much more effective, safer for your plumbing, and provide a lasting solution to blockages.

    Rooting is safe when done by trained technicians using the correct cable and blade. Badly cracked or collapsed pipe can be harmed by any cleaning method. We watch for warning signs and stop if the pipe shows damage.
