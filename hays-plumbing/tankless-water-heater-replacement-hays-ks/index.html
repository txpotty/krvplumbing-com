    A gas tankless unit at full fire can pull 199,000 BTU, roughly five times what a 40 gallon tank burner uses. The gas line usually has to be 3/4-inch or larger.     Like-for-Like Tankless Swaps in Hays

    A cracked heat exchanger or a failed control board on an older tankless is usually not worth repairing past the ten-year mark. The replacement gets matched to the existing gas, vent and mount where possible. Plan on four to six hours.

    Gas Line Testing on Tankless Installs in Hays, KS
