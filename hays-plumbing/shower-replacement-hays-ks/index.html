    Frequently Asked Questions About Shower Replacement in Hays

    Shower valve replacement usually costs $450 to $1,200. Shower pan replacement generally runs $1,500 to $4,000, and a full shower rebuild with tile can cost $8,000 to $20,000 or more.

    New valves and fixtures carry manufacturer warranties, often lifetime for the original owner. We guarantee our workmanship on every replacement.

    We work with a variety of reputable brands known for quality and durability, including Kohler, Moen, Delta, Sterling, and American Standard. Our recommendations will depend on your budget, style preferences, and specific functional needs, ensuring you get a reliable product.

    Common signs include persistent leaks, extensive cracking or staining on the shower pan or walls, outdated fixtures that constantly drip, or a desire for improved accessibility. If your shower is moldy beyond cleaning or has poor water pressure due to old plumbing, replacement might be the best option.

    A valve replacement takes about half a day. A pan replacement may take 2 to 4 days, and a full rebuild with tile can take 1 to 2 weeks or so.

    A standard shower unit replacement can often be completed within 1 to 3 days, depending on the complexity and any unexpected issues. More extensive projects, like custom tiled showers or conversions, may take 3 to 7 days due to material drying times and intricate work.

    KRV Plumbing stands behind our workmanship with a solid warranty on all shower replacement installations. Manufacturer warranties also cover the specific fixtures and components we install. We will provide full details on both at your consultation.

    Many Kansas homeowners use water softeners to protect their tank water heaters and fixtures against heavy mineral buildup. Hard water can lead to mineral buildup on showerheads, fixtures, and glass doors over time, possibly reducing water flow and dulling the look of your shower. We can recommend and install water softening systems to protect your new shower and extend its lifespan, keeping it looking cleaner for longer.
