    Full Home Repiping with PEX in Hays

    Most poly replacements we do use PEX-A, a flexible tubing that runs long distances with few fittings. For slab houses, new PEX-A runs through the attic and drops down interior walls, so none of it sits under the floor. It also tolerates freezing better. Expansion fittings keep the bore full size, which helps pressure at the last fixture on the line.

Expect a higher price and a slightly longer job with copper, in exchange for good heat and UV tolerance and a long history.

    Copper Replacement Option in Hays

    Type L copper appeals to some homeowners for its long history and rigid feel. We solder or press each joint. Copper costs more than PEX and takes a little longer to install, so we help you weigh the cost, your water conditions and how long you plan to stay in the house.

    Why Polybutylene Fails in Hays

    Polybutylene reacts with oxidizers like chlorine that water utilities use to disinfect tap water. The inner wall of the pipe and fittings slowly turns brittle, and small cracks grow until the pipe splits. Private wells serve many rural homes, and minerals, sediment, and nitrates make periodic well testing a good habit. Hot water speeds it up. Most of the failures we see are on hot lines and at fittings close to the heater.

    Fitting Failure and Emergency Repair in Hays

    Acetal plastic and copper crimp ring fittings are often the first part of a poly system to fail. A burst fitting in an attic or wall can cause major damage in minutes. After an emergency repair we schedule the full replacement, since removing the poly system is the only lasting fix.
