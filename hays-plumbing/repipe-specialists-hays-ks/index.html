    Repiping for Hard Water in Hays, KS

    Many Kansas homeowners use water softeners to protect their tank water heaters and fixtures against heavy mineral buildup. Hard water leaves calcium and magnesium scale inside pipe, and over years it narrows the bore and speeds up corrosion in metal lines. Pressure suffers. For homes with hard water, we usually recommend PEX-A, which resists scale and does not corrode, and we can add a softener loop or a whole-house filter during the repipe. Adding the loop now costs less than later.

    Repipe Costs Versus Leak Repairs in Hays, KS

    PEX Repiping in Hays, KS

    PEX is flexible plastic tubing that can run long distances without a fitting, which means fewer joints hidden in your walls. Fewer joints, fewer leaks. PEX shrugs off scale and chlorine, survives freezing better than copper and installs faster, so it is our most common repipe material. Our PEX-A installs use expansion fittings that do not shrink the flow path. Flow stays strong at the last shower.

    Urgent Repipes After Repeated Failures in Hays, KS

    A widespread pipe failure sometimes forces a repipe sooner than you planned. The first step is temporary repairs so you have water, and then we book the repipe as quickly as the permit and schedule allow. We move fast. Even urgent repipes usually take just two to four days after we begin.

    Copper Repiping in Hays, KS

    Some homeowners prefer copper for its long track record and rigidity. We install Type L copper, which has a thicker wall than the Type M used in many older homes, with lead-free solder or ProPress fittings. Copper takes a bit longer. Most copper jobs run a day longer than PEX, since each turn needs its own fitting.
