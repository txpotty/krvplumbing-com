    Gas Leak Repair at Fittings and Valves in Hays

    Most residential gas leaks are at a threaded joint, a valve stem or a flexible connector, not in the middle of a pipe. That keeps repairs small. Leaking joints get taken apart, cleaned, resealed with gas-rated compound and tightened, and damaged fittings or valves get replaced. We soap-test the repaired joint and pressure test the system before leaving.

    After-Hours Gas Line Repair in Hays

    Gas problems do not wait for business hours, and losing heat or hot water overnight is a real problem. We answer late calls.     Gas Line Relocation and Upgrades in Hays, KS

    A kitchen remodel or a new tankless water heater can push a gas system past what its original pipe can deliver. Tankless units need a lot of gas. A whole-house tankless heater can draw 150,000 BTU or more, and that often means upsizing the gas line all the way back toward the meter. We calculate the full load first. Then we size the new piping so every appliance in the house still gets full pressure at the same time.

    Appliance Connector and Shutoff Repair in Hays

    Old flexible connectors, especially uncoated brass ones, crack and leak with age. New coated stainless connectors go on, sized to the appliance, with a reachable shutoff valve if the old one is stuck or missing. Usually under an hour per appliance. Furnaces, water heaters, ranges and dryers each need a sound connection, and most need a drip leg before the appliance valve.

    Honest Gas Line Repair Pricing in Hays, KS
