    Frequently Asked Questions About Tankless Water Heater Maintenance in Hays

    Most tankless descaling visits cost $175 to $350, depending on brand and access. Adding an isolation valve kit usually runs $150 to $300 more.

    Once a year is standard, and homes with very hard water and no softener may need it every six to nine months. Heavy users and commercial units need it more often.

    With regular descaling, many tankless heaters last 15 to 20 years. Neglected units in hard water can fail in less than half that time.

    The descaling process involves shutting off the unit, isolating the water lines and connecting a circulating pump to flush a specialized acidic solution through the heat exchanger. This solution dissolves mineral deposits like calcium and magnesium, which are common in hard water, improving heat transfer efficiency.

    Usually, a permit is not required for routine tankless water heater maintenance, such as flushing and inspections. However, if major repairs or component replacements that alter the original installation are needed, a plumbing permit from Hays may be necessary to ensure compliance with local building codes.

    Most visits take one to two hours, including the descaling cycle. Installing isolation valves on the same trip adds about an hour.
