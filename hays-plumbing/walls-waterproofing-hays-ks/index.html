    Exterior Foundation Waterproofing in Hays

    Exterior foundation waterproofing involves excavating around the foundation to apply a waterproof membrane or coating directly to the wall. This is often the most effective method for preventing water from entering the basement or crawl space. We use high-quality asphalt-modified polyurethane membranes or polymer-based coatings that resist hydrostatic pressure and soil movement. Slab leaks and cracked sewer lines are common results of shifting clay, especially after long dry spells followed by heavy rain. Proper drainage solutions, such as French drains, are also added to divert water away.

    Interior Walls Waterproofing in Hays, KS

    Interior walls waterproofing focuses on managing water that has already penetrated the foundation walls. This typically involves installing an interior drainage system, often called a French drain, along the perimeter of the basement floor. Water is collected and channeled to a sump pump, which then sends it safely away from the foundation. While not preventing water from entering the wall, it effectively stops it from pooling inside your living space. This solution is less disruptive than exterior excavation and is suitable where exterior access is limited.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Walls Waterproofing in Hays

    Shower wall waterproofing with a membrane system typically runs $1,200 to $3,500 including demolition and backer board, not counting new tile. Exterior block wall sealing often costs $800 to $3,000 depending on size. Below-grade foundation waterproofing usually ranges from $2,500 to $8,000.

    The duration of a walls waterproofing project varies depending on its scope. Minor crack repairs might take a few hours, while an interior drainage system could take 1 to 3 days. A full exterior foundation waterproofing project, involving excavation around the entire home, could take 3 to 7 days or more. We provide a clear timeline during our initial consultation.

    Skilled DIYers can roll on liquid membranes, but mistakes at seams, corners and drains often lead to leaks. Shower pans also require correct slope and flood testing. Professional installation reduces the risk of hidden water damage.

    Key signs you need walls waterproofing include damp or wet basement walls, visible mold or mildew growth, a musty odor in your basement or crawl space, efflorescence on concrete walls, cracks in the foundation, or standing water after heavy rains. Don't ignore these indicators, as they often point to ongoing water intrusion.

    When professionally installed using high-quality materials, walls waterproofing can last for decades, often 20 to 50 years or even longer. The lifespan depends on the materials used, the quality of installation, and environmental factors like soil movement and water exposure. Modern membranes and sealants are designed for lasting durability.

    For extensive walls waterproofing projects involving excavation, structural changes, or major changes to drainage systems, a permit from the city of Hays may be required. Simple crack repairs typically do not need a permit. KRV Plumbing will help you determine if a permit is necessary for your project and assist with the application process if needed.

    Walls waterproofing is generally not recommended as a DIY project due to its complexity and the high importance of a sound foundation. It often involves heavy excavation, specialized equipment, precise material application, and knowledge of drainage principles and local building codes. Improper waterproofing can lead to more severe and costly damage down the line, so professional help is the smart choice.

    Waterproofing prevents the moisture mold needs to grow. Existing mold should be removed and the area dried before sealing. We remove affected materials when needed and recommend remediation for larger growth.
