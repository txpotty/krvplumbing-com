    Frequently Asked Questions About Storm Drain Cleaning in Hays

    Residential storm drain cleaning in Hays typically runs $200 to $500 depending on the number of inlets and pipe length. Dry well cleaning and commercial systems typically cost more and are quoted after a site visit.

    Our technicians are trained to service all types and brands of storm drain systems commonly found in residential and commercial properties. This includes standard PVC, corrugated HDPE, concrete, and cast iron drainpipes, as well as many types of catch basins and grates from manufacturers like NDS, ADS, and Zurn.

    Hydro jetting is a powerful drain cleaning method that uses high-pressure water streams to blast away stubborn blockages and thoroughly clean the interior of drain pipes. For storm drains, it is highly effective at removing compacted soil, heavy silt, grease, and roots, restoring the drain to its full flow capacity much better than snaking.

    In Hays, storm drains are often clogged by natural debris such as leaves, twigs, dirt, and silt, especially after heavy winds or storms. Other causes include grass clippings, trash, sediment from construction, and sometimes even tree roots getting into older pipes. Basements are common in the eastern part of Kansas, while slab and crawlspace homes are more typical in many other areas.

    Cleaning does not need a permit. New dry wells or major drainage changes may require permits or registration, and we can help you.

    To prevent storm drain clogs, regularly clear leaves and debris from grates and catch basins, avoid planting trees directly over drain lines, and keep landscaping in good shape to prevent soil erosion. Consider installing grates with smaller openings in high-debris areas and schedule annual professional cleanings.

    Water pooling around grates, overflowing downspouts, standing water against walls and mud stains near inlets are typical signs.

    If our video inspection shows that your storm drain is damaged, such as collapsed, cracked, or severely invaded by roots, cleaning alone will not solve the problem. We will provide a detailed assessment and discuss repair or replacement options, including trenchless methods where appropriate, for a long-term solution for your Hays property.
