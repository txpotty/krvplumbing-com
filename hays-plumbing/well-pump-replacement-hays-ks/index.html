    Frequently Asked Questions About Well Pump Replacement in Hays

    Well pump replacement typically costs $1,800 to $4,500 depending on well depth and pump size. Deep wells or constant pressure systems can cost $4,000 to $8,000.

    Jet pumps sit above ground and pull water up from shallower wells, typically less than 25 feet. Submersible pumps are placed directly inside the well casing, submerged in water, and are used for deeper wells, pushing water up to the surface. Each has specific applications and benefits.

    Most standard well pump replacements can be completed within a single day, typically 4 to 8 hours. However, complex installations involving very deep wells, difficult access or additional system repairs may take longer. Our team works efficiently to restore your water as soon as possible.

    In many areas, permits may be required for significant plumbing work like well pump replacement, especially if it involves changing the well system's electrical or structural components. KRV Plumbing will help you work through any permitting in Hays, KS so your installation is fully compliant with local regulations.

    It is often a good idea to consider replacing your pressure tank at the same time as your well pump, especially if both are of similar age. A new pressure tank can improve the efficiency and lifespan of your new pump by reducing cycling. It also saves on labor costs to do both jobs at once.

    Common signs of a failing well pump include no water, sputtering faucets, reduced water pressure, discolored water or surprisingly high electricity bills. If your pump runs constantly or cycles on and off too frequently, these are also signs that replacement might be necessary.

    Most new well pumps come with a manufacturer's warranty, usually ranging from 1 to 5 years, covering defects in materials and workmanship. KRV Plumbing also stands behind our installation work with our own labor warranty for complete peace of mind.
