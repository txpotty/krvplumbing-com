    For high-hazard applications in Hays, KS where toxic substances could contaminate the water supply, a reduced pressure zone, or RPZ, device is often required. RPZ backflow preventers offer the highest level of protection, using a relief valve to discharge water if the pressure drops. KRV Plumbing specializes in RPZ installation, making sure all components are correctly sized, positioned and set up according to state and local plumbing codes. Proper installation is essential for effective operation.

    Double Check Valve Assembly (DCVA) Installation in Hays

    The double check valve assembly, or DCVA, is a common backflow preventer for low-hazard applications where the consequences of backflow are not severe. In Hays, DCVAs are frequently installed on fire sprinkler systems or irrigation lines. Our certified plumbers precisely install these devices, making sure both check valves operate correctly and are properly sealed to prevent backpressure or back-siphonage into the main water supply.
