    Mixing Valve Repair in Hays

    Mixing valves blend hot and cold inside the wall. A sticking cartridge or spool shows up as temperature that drifts or a handle that has to go much farther than it used to. We replace the cartridge. If the valve is too old for parts, we talk through a replacement valve and what it means for the tile.

    Tub and Shower Combo Repair in Hays, KS

    Tub and shower combos share one valve, one drain and a diverter between them. That means a worn diverter steals pressure from the head, a clogged tub drain slows the shower and a leaky valve shows up at both the spout and the head. We check all of it. We also check the overflow gasket, a frequent hidden leak.

    Commercial Shower Service in Hays

    Hotels and dorms cannot wait days on a shower. We carry commercial cartridges and thermostatic parts, and we can stage repairs room by room so only one unit is down at a time. We can work nights. Each repaired valve gets its limit stop set and tested before we move on.

    Dripping Showerhead Repair in Hays

    Drips from the head itself almost always come from the valve, not the head. If the head drips with the valve closed, water is slipping past the cartridge, and a new head will not change that. The fix is in the valve. Leaks at the arm threads are different, and those get new thread tape and a snug turn.

    Slow Shower Drain Repair in Hays, KS

    Standing water at your ankles is the first sign. Hard water makes it worse, because the scale binds with soap scum and hair into a mat that sticks to the pipe wall and builds out over time. We cut it out with an auger. For stubborn buildup in the branch line, we jet the line with water instead of using acid cleaners.

    Low Shower Pressure Repair in Hays, KS

    Low pressure at the shower with good pressure at the sink narrows it down fast. That points to the head, the flow restrictor or the cartridge, not the main or the water heater, and it is usually something we can fix within an hour. The head comes off first. If every fixture is weak, we check the regulator and the main.

    Shower Handle Leak Repair in Hays

    Leaks at the handle or behind the trim plate are easy to miss. Water running down the inside of the trim gets behind the tile at the valve opening, and over a few months it soaks the drywall around the valve without ever showing on the shower side. The trim comes off first. It is usually a loose bonnet nut or a worn O-ring.

    Showerhead and Arm Leak Repair in Hays

    Shower arms leak at the threads more than people realize. A leak at the arm threads follows the pipe into the wall, and it shows up as bubbling paint in the next room. We reseal the threads. Handheld hoses and slide bars can go in during the same visit.
