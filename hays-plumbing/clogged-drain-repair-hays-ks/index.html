    Frequently Asked Questions About Clogged Drain Repair in Hays

    Clearing a single sink, tub or shower drain typically costs $125 to $300. Harder clogs that need a larger machine or access through a roof vent run $250 to $450. Hydro jetting a heavily scaled or greasy line typically costs $350 to $800.

    Many Kansas homeowners use water softeners to protect their tank water heaters and fixtures against heavy mineral buildup. Hard water leaves mineral scale that narrows pipe walls and gives grease and hair something to cling to. Older homes may also have cast iron pipes that rust and roughen inside. Both conditions make clogs form faster.

    We use professional-grade equipment from reputable manufacturers known for durability and effectiveness. This includes drain snakes and augers from brands like RIDGID, high-definition sewer cameras for inspection, and powerful hydro-jetting machines designed for different pipe sizes and clog types.

    Drain snaking uses a flexible auger to physically break through or pull out blockages. Hydro-jetting uses high-pressure water to scour the inside of pipes, thoroughly removing scale, grease, and roots, providing a much more complete cleaning. Hydro-jetting is often used for tough clogs or routine maintenance.

    Hydro-jetting uses high-pressure water to clear stubborn blockages and completely clean the inside of your pipes. It's often necessary for severe clogs caused by tree roots, heavy grease buildup, or heavy mineral deposits from hard water, which traditional snaking might not fully clear.

    Common signs of a clogged drain include slow drainage, gurgling sounds from drains or toilets, unpleasant odors coming from your pipes, and water backing up into sinks, tubs, or showers. If multiple drains are affected, it often indicates a main line issue.

    Most single fixture clogs are cleared in 30 to 90 minutes. Lines with heavy buildup or limited access can take longer. We tell you if the job will run beyond the usual time.

    Enzyme or bacterial cleaners can help reduce organic buildup in kitchen and bathroom drains over time. They are gentler than caustic products and safe for most pipes. They will not clear a full clog but can help prevent one.
