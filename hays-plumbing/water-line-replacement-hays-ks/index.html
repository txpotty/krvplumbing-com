    Galvanized Main Line Replacement in Hays

    Galvanized steel was common in homes built before the 1960s. The zinc coating wears off and the steel rusts from the inside.     Main Water Line Replacement in Hays

    Your service line runs from the meter or curb stop to the main shutoff inside the house, usually as 3/4-inch or 1-inch pipe.     Copper Water Line Installation in Hays

    Type K copper is the thick-wall grade made for burial. Street pressure does not bother it. We bed it in sand, keep it away from sharp rock, and sleeve it where it passes through a foundation wall, because concrete and copper rubbing together for twenty years eventually wear a hole. It costs more than PEX.

    Water Line Materials We Install in Hays, KS
