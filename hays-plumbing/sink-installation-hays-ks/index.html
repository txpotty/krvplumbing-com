    Frequently Asked Questions About Sink Installation in Hays

    Many vessel sinks have no overflow, so the drain must be a non-overflow type, or air will not flow freely. We match the drain to the bowl to prevent this.

    We guarantee our installation labor, and the sink and drain carry the manufacturer's warranty. Details are listed on your invoice.

    You can either purchase the sink yourself, or we can help you with selecting and sourcing one from our trusted suppliers. Many customers prefer to choose their own sink to match their style, but we are happy to recommend durable, high-quality options suitable for Hays homes.

    Common signs you might need a new sink include persistent leaks around the basin or drain, cracks or chips in the material, severe staining that won't clean, rust, or if you're simply upgrading for style or function. An old, damaged sink can also lead to water damage in your cabinets.
