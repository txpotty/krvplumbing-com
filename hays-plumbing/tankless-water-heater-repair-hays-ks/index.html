    Gas Tankless Water Heater Repair in Hays

    Noritz Tankless Water Heater Repair in Hays, KS

    Noritz heaters hold up well, though flow sensors and vent joints still fail with age. A code 90 points to combustion trouble, often a dirty fan or a restricted intake.     Igniter and Flame Rod Repair in Hays, KS

    Gas tankless heaters light with a hot surface or spark igniter, then prove flame with a flame rod. We clean or replace the rod, look for a cracked igniter or a lazy spark, and confirm the gas valve opens on call. Five-minute fix sometimes.
