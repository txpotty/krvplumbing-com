    Pilot Repair in Hays

    A pilot that lights and then dies when you let go of the knob almost always means a weak thermocouple. Sometimes the cause is a clogged flame arrestor screen starving the burner of air. The thermocouple gets a millivolt test. If it reads fine, the gas control valve is next.

    Leaking Water Heater Repair in Hays, KS

    Not every leak means a dead tank. Drips from the relief valve, the drain valve or a supply connection are repairable for a modest cost. Water from the tank body or bottom seam usually means the lining has failed, and replacement is the only real fix. We protect your floors either way.

    Water Heater Maintenance and Tune-Ups in Hays, KS

    A yearly tune-up adds years to a tank. We flush the sediment, check the anode rod, test the relief valve and look over the gas or electrical connections. Water across much of Kansas runs quite hard, leaving mineral scale in water heaters, faucets, showerheads, and dishwashers. Left alone, the sediment hardens until a hose flush cannot break it loose.

    Relief Valve and Fitting Leak Repair in Hays, KS

    T&P valves drip when the tank is overheating or the house pressure spikes, so replacing the valve alone may not stop it. We check the thermostat setting and the house pressure first. On homes with a pressure regulator, a missing expansion tank is often the real problem. Leaking fittings get redone.

    Tankless Water Heater Repair in Hays

    Scale is the main enemy of a tankless heater. It coats the heat exchanger and trips the unit into an error code. A descaling flush, a clean inlet screen and a check of the igniter and flow sensor cover most tankless calls. All major brands.

    Gas Burner and Venting Repair in Hays, KS

    Combustion is the part of a gas heater that matters most for safety. Yellow flame tips or soot at the draft hood mean the burner is not getting enough air to burn cleanly. The burner gets cleaned, and draft gets tested at the hood. Backdrafting is dangerous.
