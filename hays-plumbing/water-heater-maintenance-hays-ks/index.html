    Tank Water Heater Maintenance in Hays

    Flushing is the heart of tank maintenance. Private wells serve many rural homes, and minerals, sediment, and nitrates make periodic well testing a good habit. The tank gets drained until it runs clear, then refilled, and the anode, relief valve and connections get checked. Plan on an hour. A clean tank heats faster and lasts longer.

    Flushing a Tank Water Heater in Hays

    Popping and rumbling mean sediment. Calcium and magnesium settle on the bottom of the tank and form a layer that traps heat under it, so water boils in pockets beneath the scale and pops every time the burner or lower element fires. We flush until it runs clear. Bad tanks need a few rounds.

    Tank Flush and Inspection in Hays

    Sediment forms from minerals in the water. On a standard tank visit we flush that sediment out, test the temperature and pressure relief valve, check the anode for corrosion and inspect every fitting for leaks, which protects one of the more expensive appliances in the house. Efficiency comes back. Recovery speeds up.

    Anode Rod Replacement in Hays

    Some water eats anode rods fast. Water chemistry can wear a rod out in a few years, so we check it every time. Spent rods get replaced. It keeps rust off the tank lining.

    Electric Water Heater Tune-Up in Hays

    Electric tanks fail at the lower element. Sediment piles up around the lower element, it runs hotter than it should, and eventually it burns out, which is why you suddenly get only a few minutes of hot water. We test both elements. Thermostats and wiring get checked with the power off.
