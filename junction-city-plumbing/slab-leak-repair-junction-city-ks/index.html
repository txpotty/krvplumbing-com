    Slab Leak Repair for PEX and Copper Lines in Junction City, KS

    Copper and PEX fail in different ways under a slab. Copper tends to pinhole. Copper wears through at rub points or from water chemistry, and PEX usually leaks at a poorly crimped fitting or where a rodent got to it. Trucks in Junction City, KS carry parts for both.

    Hot Water Slab Leak Repair in Junction City, KS
