    Low Water Pressure Diagnosis in Junction City

    Weak pressure on a well is not always the pump. A clogged sediment filter, a pressure switch set too low or a leak in the yard line can all cause it. In some cases the pump is fine and the well just cannot keep up with demand. We test each one.

    Submersible Pump Pulling and Repair in Junction City

    Sand wears impellers down over time, and a pump that once filled the tank in a minute starts taking three. Other common failures are the motor, the splice and the check valve. We test the motor and wire from the surface before deciding whether to pull. Replacement pumps get sized to the yield and depth of the well.

    Pressure Tank Repair in Junction City, KS

    A waterlogged pressure tank makes the pump short cycle, turning on and off every few seconds. That rapid cycling burns out pumps early. The air charge gets checked and the bladder inspected, then the tank is recharged or replaced so the pump runs a full minute per cycle. Steadier pressure follows.

    Well Pump Diagnostic Services in Junction City, KS

    Voltage, amperage, pressure and flow rate tell us most of what is happening down the well without pulling a single length of pipe. A weak motor draws high amps. Low flow with normal amp draw points to a plugged intake or a falling water level, which is a different repair. Testing first saves a pull.

    Jet Pump System Repair in Junction City, KS

    Jet pumps sit above ground and pull water up through a suction line with an ejector. Air leaks on the suction side cause most failures. KRV Plumbing checks the foot valve, the ejector nozzle and every suction fitting, then reprimes the pump and confirms it builds pressure. Nonstop running without pressure usually means lost prime or an air leak.

    Above-Ground Jet Pump Repair in Junction City

    Lost prime is the number one jet pump complaint. Shaft seal leaks and worn impellers come next. The pump gets reprimed and resealed, and the foot valve and suction line get checked, because a single air leak will undo the work. Everything is above ground.
