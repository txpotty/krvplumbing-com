    Anode Rod Replacement in Junction City, KS

    The anode rod sacrifices itself so the steel tank does not rust. Hard water can eat through one in a few years. After pulling the old rod, we fit a magnesium, aluminum or powered anode to suit your water and any smell. Rotten-egg odor often clears up.

    Gas Control and Thermocouple Repair in Junction City

    Thermocouples, flame sensors, igniters and gas control valves cause most gas heater failures. Each part gets tested, the burner gets cleaned and the vent gets a draft check. A pilot that lights and then goes out a few seconds later usually points to a weak thermocouple or a clogged flame arrestor screen. Most are done in under two hours.

    Sediment Flush and Noise Repair in Junction City

    Popping, rumbling or crackling usually means a thick layer of sediment is trapping water and overheating the steel at the tank bottom. We drain the tank, break up scale with repeated fills and check the drain valve for blockage. Flushing restores capacity. Homes on hard water benefit from a yearly flush.

    Relief Valve and Fitting Leak Repair in Junction City, KS

    T&P valves drip when the tank is overheating or the house pressure spikes, so replacing the valve alone may not stop it. First we check the thermostat and the water pressure. A missing expansion tank is a common cause on homes with a check valve or pressure regulator. Loose fittings get tightened or replaced.

    Pilot Repair in Junction City

    A pilot that lights and then dies when you let go of the knob almost always means a weak thermocouple. Sometimes the cause is a clogged flame arrestor screen starving the burner of air. We test the thermocouple output in millivolts. If it reads fine, the gas control valve is next.
