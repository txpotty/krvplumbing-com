    Pressure Vacuum Breaker (PVB) Installation in Junction City, KS

    For low-hazard applications, such as lawn irrigation systems in Junction City, KS, a pressure vacuum breaker, or PVB, is a popular and cost-effective backflow prevention device. PVBs protect against back-siphonage by letting air into the system when pressure drops. Our certified plumbers carefully install PVBs above the highest point of downstream piping, as code requires, to maintain effective protection. We also make sure of proper clearance and drainage.

    Irrigation Backflow Preventer Installation in Junction City

    Landscape irrigation is the most common reason homeowners need a backflow device. Fertilizer, herbicides and stagnant water in sprinkler and drip lines can be siphoned into your drinking water if pressure drops. We install pressure vacuum breakers or double check assemblies based on local requirements and the design of your system. Above-ground installs are set at least 12 inches above the highest sprinkler head when a PVB is used.

    Double Check Valve Assembly Installation in Junction City

    Double check assemblies are used for low hazard connections such as many fire sprinkler systems and some irrigation lines. They have two spring-loaded check valves and test cocks for annual certification. Unlike an RPZ, they do not discharge water, so they can be installed in vaults or below grade where local rules allow. We size them to avoid too much pressure loss on your system.

    Double Check Valve Assembly (DCVA) Installation in Junction City

    The double check valve assembly, or DCVA, is a common backflow preventer for low-hazard applications where the consequences of backflow are not severe. In Junction City, DCVAs are frequently installed on fire sprinkler systems or irrigation lines. Our certified plumbers carefully install these devices, making sure both check valves operate correctly and are properly sealed to prevent backpressure or back-siphonage into the main water supply.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Backflow Preventer Installation in Junction City

    The cost for backflow preventer installation in Junction City, KS typically ranges from $400 to $1,500, depending on the type of device, its size and the complexity of the installation. A basic residential PVB for an irrigation system is generally on the lower end, while a commercial-grade RPZ assembly costs more because of material and labor requirements.

    In Junction City, most backflow preventers must be tested once a year by a certified backflow tester to confirm they are working correctly and providing adequate protection. This is a separate service from installation but is essential for ongoing compliance and safety.

    An RPZ, or reduced pressure zone device, provides the highest level of protection and is used for high-hazard applications where contaminants could be toxic. A DCVA, or double check valve assembly, is used for low-hazard applications where contaminants are non-toxic. The choice depends on the risk of contamination at the cross-connection point.

    We install all common types of backflow preventers, including reduced pressure zone assemblies, pressure vacuum breakers and double check valve assemblies. Our team will recommend the right device based on your property's hazard level and local plumbing codes in Junction City.

    You likely need a backflow preventer if you have an irrigation system, a fire sprinkler system, a swimming pool or any auxiliary water source that connects to the main water supply. Local regulations in Junction City often mandate these devices for certain applications to protect public health.
