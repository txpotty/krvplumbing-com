    Faucet repair involves fixing a specific component, like a washer or cartridge, to stop a leak or improve function. Replacement means installing an entirely new faucet fixture.     Common signs include constant dripping even when turned off, visible corrosion or rust, difficulty turning handles, inconsistent water temperature, or a noticeable decrease in water pressure. If you notice any of these issues, it's likely time for a replacement.
