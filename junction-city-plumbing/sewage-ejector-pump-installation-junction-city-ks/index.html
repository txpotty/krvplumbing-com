    Signs that you might need a new sewage ejector pump include unusual noises from the pump or pit, frequent cycling on and off, slow drainage from basement fixtures or a sewage odor coming from the area. If you notice any of these issues, it is best to have a professional check your system to prevent a complete failure and costly backups.

    We install Zoeller, Liberty Pumps and Wayne ejector pumps, plus Saniflo macerating systems. We choose the brand and model based on your fixtures and lift height.

    With proper installation and regular maintenance, a high-quality sewage ejector pump can last 7 to 15 years. Factors such as usage frequency, the type of waste it handles and water quality can affect its lifespan. Annual inspections can help maximize its longevity.
