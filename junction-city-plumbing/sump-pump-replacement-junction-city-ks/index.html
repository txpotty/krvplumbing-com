    Float Switch and Check Valve Replacement in Junction City

    Many sump pump failures start with a stuck float switch or a leaking check valve. A failed check valve lets water flow back into the pit and causes the pump to short cycle. We install new check valves with every pump replacement and verify the switch moves freely. Vertical float switches work well in tight pits. Tethered floats work best in larger pits.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Sump Pump Replacement in Junction City

    The cost of a sump pump replacement in Junction City, KS can range from $500 to $1,500, depending on the type of pump, its horsepower and the complexity of the installation.     A primary sump pump is your main defense against basement flooding, powered by your home's electricity. A backup sump pump, usually battery-powered or water-powered, is a secondary system that kicks on if the primary pump fails, loses power or cannot keep up with heavy water inflow. Many Junction City homes benefit from a backup system due to storm risks.

    A battery backup sump pump system is a secondary pump powered by a battery, designed to turn on automatically if your primary sump pump loses power during an outage or fails. This is critical for maintaining flood protection during power outages caused by severe weather.

    Most standard sump pump replacements can be finished within 2 to 4 hours. More complex installations, such as those requiring new pit excavation or extensive discharge line work, might take longer, up to a full day.
