    Check Valve and Discharge Line Service in Junction City, KS

    The check valve stops pumped water from falling back into the pit when the pump shuts off. A failed valve makes the pump run twice as often and wears out the motor. We test and replace check valves and inspect the discharge pipe for cracks and loose joints. We also make sure water exits away from the house so it does not soak into the soil around the slab or foundation.

    Discharge Line and Check Valve Service in Junction City

    The discharge line carries water away from your basement, and the check valve prevents pumped water from flowing back into the pit. We inspect these components for blockages, leaks or damage. A faulty check valve can cause the pump to short-cycle, leading to premature wear. We make sure these vital parts are functioning correctly to maintain an effective water removal system for your Junction City residence.

    Submersible Sump Pump Service in Junction City

    Our team is skilled in maintaining submersible sump pumps, a popular choice in Junction City due to their quiet operation and compact design.     Float Switch Repair and Adjustment in Junction City, KS

    The float switch is the most common reason a sump pump fails to start. Tethered floats can tangle on the pump body, and vertical floats can stick from mineral crust or debris. We clean or replace the switch and set the on and off levels so the pump does not short cycle. Electronic sensor switches are an option for tight pits where floats get stuck.

    Battery Backup System Testing in Junction City, KS

    A power outage during a heavy storm can leave your primary sump pump useless. Our maintenance service includes thorough testing of your battery backup sump pump system. We check the battery charge, verify the charger's function and ensure the backup pump activates when the main power is interrupted. This dual protection is vital for homes in Junction City, KS where power interruptions can coincide with severe weather.
