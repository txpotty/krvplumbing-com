    Emergency Polybutylene Burst Repair in Junction City

    Poly fittings tend to let go during sharp temperature swings, when the pipe expands and contracts more than usual. Hard freezes happen every winter in Kansas, and pipes in exterior walls, garages, and crawlspaces are the first to burst. If a line bursts in your house, close the main shutoff, then call us so we can stop the leak, make a temporary repair and restore water. Then we plan the full fix. Living with poly until the next failure costs the most in the end.

    Polybutylene Identification and Inspection in Junction City

    Polybutylene is usually gray, though it can be white, blue or black, and is often stamped PB2110. Start at the water heater. It also shows up under sinks, at the main and through garage walls, and we follow it into the attic to see how much of the house it feeds.

    Polybutylene Leak Repair in Junction City

    Repairs on polybutylene are temporary by nature, because the pipe next to the repair is the same age. A repair buys time. When a poly line leaks, we cut out the bad section and splice in PEX with approved transition fittings, which stops the leak and gives you time to schedule a full replacement on your own terms.

    Whole-Home PEX Replacement in Junction City, KS

    PEX-A is the most common replacement for polybutylene because it is flexible, scale resistant and easy to route. Our crews use Uponor PEX-A and full-bore fittings.     Copper Pipe Replacement in Junction City

    Copper is a solid alternative to PEX for poly replacement. Copper is stiff, heat tolerant and long proven. Plan on a higher price. Some water can pinhole copper over time, so we look at your water and any past leak history before recommending it over PEX.
