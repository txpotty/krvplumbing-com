    Why Choose KRV Plumbing for Hydrojet Plumbing in Junction City, KS?

    Hydrojetting cleans more thoroughly than any cable can. A cable leaves most of the buildup behind. Jetting removes grease, scale, sludge and soft roots from the full circumference of the pipe, restoring close to its original diameter, which is why a jetted line stays clear so much longer than a snaked one.

    • Full Pipe Cleaning, Jetting cleans the entire pipe wall, unlike a snake that only opens a hole.
    • Longer Results, A jetted line stays open much longer than one that was only cabled.
    • Pipe-Safe, Done by a trained tech, jetting is safe for most sound pipe.

    Whole-House Drain Jetting in Junction City

    Slow drains in several rooms point to buildup in the main or a branch line. The main gets jetted from the cleanout, and branches from vents or fixtures. Most homes take two to three hours.

    Preventive Hydrojet Maintenance in Junction City

    Preventive jetting pays off for lines that clog more than once a year. Camera findings set the interval. Grease-heavy lines may need six-month service, while root-prone sewers can often go 18 to 24 months.

    Commercial Hydrojet Plumbing in Junction City, KS

    Businesses put more through their drains than any house. Multifamily and office buildings carry heavy daily loads. We jet stacks, laterals and mains for commercial buildings, often before opening hours, and we leave a written report with camera notes for the property manager's records.

    Sewer Camera Inspection Before Hydrojetting in Junction City

    Jetting a broken pipe can make the break worse. We camera every line before we set up the jetter. Footage shows the material and joint condition, which sets our nozzle and pressure.

    Hydrojetting vs. Snaking in Junction City, KS

    Snaking pokes a hole through a clog. Jetting cleans the whole pipe. Snaking suits a one-time clog, while jetting fixes a line that clogs repeatedly.

    Hydrojetting for Cast Iron Pipes in Junction City, KS

    Older cast iron drains rust and scale from the inside, leaving a rough surface that grabs debris. A jet clears that crust. Badly corroded cast iron with thin walls or cracks needs gentler settings or no jetting at all, and the camera tells us which before we start, along with whether the pipe is a candidate for lining.

    After-Hours Hydrojet Service in Junction City

    Backups at night or on weekends get the same equipment as weekday calls. Our on-call trucks carry jetters. We can clear and jet a line in one visit instead of returning Monday.

    Tree Root Removal with Hydrojetting in Junction City

    Roots find their way into sewer lines through cracked joints, and older clay pipe has a joint every few feet. We use a spinning root-cutter nozzle. The jet then flushes the debris to the main, and the camera marks the joints that let the roots in.
