    Submersible and Jet Pump Work in Junction City, KS

    Franklin Electric Well Pump Replacement in Junction City

    Franklin Electric builds a large share of the submersible motors sold in the US, including ones sitting under other brands' pump ends. Replacement control boxes are easy to source. We match the motor and the wire gauge to the depth of your well.

    Well Pump Sizing in Junction City

    Sizing starts with the well log. We get depth and static level from the well log or a sounder, then a draw-down test shows what the well can really produce in gallons per minute. Pump more than the well yields and it runs dry.

    Jet Pump Replacement in Junction City, KS

    Jet pumps sit above ground, in a well house or basement, and pull water up by suction from wells where the water level is within about 25 feet. Deep-well jets with a two-pipe setup can reach further, but they work harder. Lost prime is the classic complaint. Most of the time a bad foot valve or a suction leak is letting water drain back, and we check those before condemning the pump.

    Well Pump Efficiency Upgrades in Junction City, KS

    Older 1 HP pumps running against a 60 psi switch use more power than most people expect, especially on a well with a long horizontal run to the house. A correctly sized pump, or a variable-speed drive, can cut the run time. Not always worth it. We run the numbers with you.

    Jet Pump Seal and Motor Replacement in Junction City, KS

    Single-stage and two-stage jet pumps wear out at the seal and impeller, and a leaking shaft seal shows up as a puddle under the motor. We replace the pump and prime it properly. Then we watch the gauge through a full cycle.

    Solar Well Pump Systems in Junction City, KS

    Solar well pumps make sense for livestock tanks and cabins where running power from the meter would cost more than the pump itself. A DC pump and a controller run off a few panels during daylight. The storage tank does the job a battery would. Panel count comes from how many gallons you need a day.

    Well Pressure Tank Replacement in Junction City

    A waterlogged pressure tank makes a healthy pump look sick. Short cycling, where the pump starts every few seconds of water use, usually means the tank bladder has failed and the pump is paying for it. We check the air charge with a gauge. Precharge should sit about 2 psi below the cut-in pressure.

    Well Pump Electrical Troubleshooting in Junction City, KS

    No-water calls often end at the control box, not the pump. On a three-wire submersible, the start capacitor or the relay in that box fails far more often than the motor down the well does. Those get tested first. Swapping a capacitor costs a fraction of pulling a pump.
