    Undermount Sink Installation in Junction City, KS

    Undermounts hang from clips anchored into the underside of the stone, with silicone run around the rim. We support the bowl from below with a brace while the silicone cures, typically for a full day, before loading it with water. Rushing this causes the classic sag. Just an eighth of an inch of sag leaves a gap that catches water and food along the edge.

    Undermount Sinks on Stone in Junction City, KS

    Every fabricator handles undermount clips a bit differently. Some epoxy threaded studs into the stone, others use a rail system, and we work with whichever one your countertop shop prefers. The bowl is braced regardless. We do not connect the trap until the adhesive has cured.

    Sink and Disposal Installed Together in Junction City, KS

    A new sink is the cheapest time to add a disposal. We mount the disposal on the new flange, set the trap height for its outlet, and knock out the dishwasher plug so the hose has somewhere to go. You also need power. We check for a switched outlet in the cabinet.

    Top-mount Sink Installation in Junction City

    Drop-in sinks are common in Kansas homes with laminate or tile counters. The rim carries the weight. After checking the cutout, we lay silicone under the lip and draw the clips down evenly so the whole rim sits flat. Then the drain and faucet get connected and tested.

    Apron-Front Sinks in Junction City, KS

    Apron sinks need a cabinet cut to fit the front panel, and the sink has to be in place before the counter is templated.
