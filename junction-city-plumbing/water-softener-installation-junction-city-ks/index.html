    Softener Loop Installation in Junction City

    A softener loop is the plumbing connection that routes your main water line through a softener. Many newer homes include a loop, but older homes often do not. We cut into the main line, install the loop and add a bypass valve. We keep outdoor hose bibs and irrigation on hard water when possible to save salt. A proper loop makes future service easy.

    High-Efficiency Water Softener Installation in Junction City

    Modern water softeners offer improved efficiency, using less salt and water during the regeneration process.     Combination Softener and Carbon Filter Systems in Junction City, KS

    Municipal water often contains chlorine or chloramine that affects taste and smell. A carbon filter paired with a softener removes those chemicals and protects resin.
