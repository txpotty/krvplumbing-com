    Pipe Material and Water Quality Review in Junction City, KS

    Pipe material says a lot about what to expect from a house. Our plumber identifies the supply pipe, whether copper, CPVC, PEX, galvanized, poly or Kitec, and explains how each usually fails. Some need replacing soon. Polybutylene and Kitec are known for sudden failures, and galvanized slowly closes up with rust, so those get flagged with replacement options.

    Plumbing Inspection Costs in Junction City, KS

    A basic plumbing inspection usually costs $150 to $300, and adding a sewer camera usually brings it to $250 to $500. Bigger properties cost more.     Commercial Plumbing Inspections in Junction City, KS

    Commercial buildings have equipment homes do not, so our commercial inspections cover more ground. We look at backflow assemblies, grease interceptors, commercial water heaters, flush valves and the building sewer. Records matter for inspectors. The written report helps facility managers plan and budget repairs before a health inspector or the water provider writes them up.

    Sewer Line Camera Inspection in Junction City, KS

    Older homes in Junction City often have cast iron or clay sewer pipe that cracks, scales up or lets roots in at the joints. The camera records every defect along with its distance from the cleanout. Then we locate it. Knowing the depth and location means a repair estimate can be accurate, and it helps decide between a spot repair, a liner or a full replacement.

    Seasonal Plumbing Checks in Junction City, KS

    Strong winds and dry spells can draw moisture out of the soil, while sudden cold fronts can drop temperatures sharply overnight. A seasonal check looks at the parts of the system that weather affects most. We look at hose bibbs, exposed pipe in garages and crawlspaces, irrigation backflow devices and the water heater's relief valve and drain. Insulation gets checked too. Swapping a cracked outdoor faucet or insulating a pipe costs little next to a burst line in the wall.

    Leak Detection and Assessment in Junction City, KS

    Hidden leaks waste water and quietly damage floors and walls. Start at the meter. With all fixtures off, we watch the meter's leak indicator, then use acoustic listening gear and a thermal camera to trace the leak behind walls, under floors or in the slab. Catching it early keeps it small. Slab leaks and cracked sewer lines are common results of shifting clay, especially after long dry spells followed by heavy rain.

    Sewer Camera Inspection in Junction City, KS

    Many older homes have clay, cast iron or Orangeburg sewer lines that can crack, sag or fill with roots. The camera starts at a cleanout. The video shows offsets, bellies, roots and breaks along with their depth and location, and you keep a copy of the recording for your records or for a contractor.
