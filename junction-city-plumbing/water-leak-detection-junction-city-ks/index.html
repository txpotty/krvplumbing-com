    Water Meter Leak Testing in Junction City

    The water meter is the first and simplest leak test. With every fixture and appliance off, a moving leak indicator means water is escaping somewhere. We read the flow rate to estimate how many gallons are being lost. Then we shut off zones one at a time to see which part of the property is losing water.

    Hidden Wall & Ceiling Leak Detection in Junction City, KS

    Leaks behind walls or in ceilings can cause serious damage before they become visible, leading to stained drywall, peeling paint or a musty odor. These often stem from faulty pipe connections, old galvanized pipes or issues with shower pans and drains. Our technicians use moisture meters and infrared cameras to scan these areas, identifying elevated moisture levels and temperature differences that indicate a hidden leak, minimizing the need for extensive wall demolition.

    Irrigation Leak Detection in Junction City

    Drip and sprinkler systems run underground across yards and often account for hidden water loss. Cracked tubing, broken emitters and stuck valves can waste huge amounts of water. We isolate the irrigation system, test each zone and trace leaking lines. Repairs restore efficient watering for your landscape.

    Pool Water Leak Detection in Junction City, KS

    Pools lose a lot of water to evaporation in summer heat, so a leak can hide behind normal loss. We use a bucket test to compare evaporation with actual water loss. Then we pressure test pool plumbing and dye test fittings, lights and skimmers. Finding the leak safeguards the pool and the soil around it.

    Smart Leak Detection Device Installation in Junction City

    Smart shutoff valves like Moen Flo and Phyn monitor water flow and can shut off water automatically when they detect a leak. Small sensors placed near water heaters, sinks and washers send alerts to your phone. We install and set up these systems so you get warnings before small leaks become big ones. Some insurers offer premium discounts for these devices.

    Toilet and Fixture Leak Detection in Junction City, KS

    Toilets are one of the most common sources of wasted water. A worn flapper or a fill valve that never shuts off can leak quietly around the clock. We use dye tests to find leaking toilets and check faucets, showers and supply lines for drips. These fixes are typically quick and save water right away.
