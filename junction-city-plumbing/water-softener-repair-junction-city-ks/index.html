    Water Softener Problems We Fix in Junction City, KS

    Injector and Brine Line Cleaning in Junction City, KS

    The injector, sometimes called a venturi, creates the suction that draws brine into the resin tank. Sediment and salt residue clog its tiny ports. Once it clogs, the brine tank fills with water instead of emptying, and the softener regenerates with plain water that does nothing to the resin. We clean or replace it and check the brine line for air leaks.

    Water Softener Repair vs. Replacement in Junction City, KS

    Age and parts availability decide whether a softener is worth repairing. KRV Plumbing gives honest advice based on both. A sound tank with a rebuildable valve is worth saving, but an obsolete head without parts usually is not. You get a clear breakdown.

    Bypass and Fitting Leak Repair in Junction City, KS

    Bypass valves, the O-rings where the valve meets the tank, cracked fittings and the drain line are the usual leak points. Even a small drip wastes water and can stain a garage floor or rot a cabinet. We replace O-rings or fittings and test under pressure. Mineral tank cracks cannot be fixed.

    Softener Repair vs Replacement Cost in Junction City, KS

    Most softener repairs cost $150 to $450, while a resin replacement often runs $400 to $900 depending on tank size. Installing a new quality softener usually runs $1,500 to $3,500. With a good tank and valve, repair is the better value. An obsolete valve with scarce parts tips the decision toward a new head or a new unit.

    Salt Usage and Settings Repair in Junction City

    Too much salt or water use usually means settings that do not match your hardness or household. Older timer units regenerate on a schedule no matter how much water you used. We retest hardness and reprogram the valve, or upgrade the head to a metered control that only regenerates when the resin is actually close to exhausted. Salt bills drop.

    Resin Bed Problems in Junction City

    Fouled resin is the usual reason a softener regenerates on schedule but still sends hard water to every faucet in the house. Iron and sediment coat the beads so they cannot grab hardness. Depending on the test, the resin gets cleaned or replaced. New resin restores full capacity.

    Brine Tank and Salt Bridge Repair in Junction City, KS

    A crust of salt across the brine tank stops regeneration, and hard water shows up at every tap within a day or two. The bridge gets broken up and the tank cleaned out. Cracked brine tanks on older units get replaced. Fill the tank halfway.

    Popular Water Softener Brand Repair in Junction City

    GE, Whirlpool, Culligan, Kenmore, EcoPure and Morton make the popular softeners we repair most often, along with the private-label units from hardware stores. Every brand has its own valve layout and weak points. KRV Plumbing knows those designs, so diagnosis is quick. Make or model, the repair in your Junction City garage is done right.
