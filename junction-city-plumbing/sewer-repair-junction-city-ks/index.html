    Emergency Sewer Repair in Junction City

    Raw sewage on a basement floor is not a next-week problem. Our phone is answered after hours.     Video Camera Sewer Inspection in Junction City

    A self-leveling camera goes in at the cleanout or through a pulled toilet. The full length gets recorded. Cracks, root intrusion, offset joints and standing water all show up on the screen, and the sonde in the camera head lets us mark the exact spot and depth on the ground above, so any excavation stays small and targeted. Older homes in Junction City often hide two or three problems in one line, and the footage shows every one.

    Sewer Line Backups & Blockages in Junction City

    Water coming up in the tub when the washer drains is a classic main line symptom. The clog is past the branches. We clear it from the nearest cleanout, then run the camera to see what caused it, because wipes, grease and roots all call for different follow-up. Older Kansas homes with clay lines often show roots right at the joints. We record what we find.

    Sewer Repair for Commercial Properties in Junction City, KS

    Commercial laterals run six inches or larger and carry far more flow than a house. Downtime costs money. For restaurants, strip centers and apartment buildings in Junction City we schedule repairs after hours where we can, set up temporary bypass pumping when the line has to stay in service, and coordinate with property managers on tenant notices.     Sewer Vent Pipe Repair in Junction City, KS

    A plugged vent makes drains gurgle and traps run dry. Then the sewer smell shows up. Bird nests, leaves and ice can block a roof vent, and older galvanized or cast iron vent stacks crack inside the walls where nobody sees them, so we check the vent any time the drains act up but the main line looks clean. Fixes range from a quick clearing to a new section of stack.

    Bellied Sewer Line Correction in Junction City, KS

    A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged and holds standing water. Paper and solids settle there. Basements are common in the eastern part of Kansas, while slab and crawlspace homes are more typical in many other areas. We measure the depth of standing water on camera, dig out that section, re-bed the pipe on compacted gravel and set it back to a quarter inch of fall per foot, which is the typical code minimum for a four-inch line. Not every sag needs digging, and we will tell you which yours is.

    Root Intrusion Repair in Junction City, KS

    Cutting roots treats the symptom. Fixing the joint treats the cause. After clearing the line, we look at each rooted joint on camera and decide between a short sectional liner, a full liner or cutting in new pipe, depending on how many joints are affected and how far apart they sit. One or two joints is a small job. Ten or twelve usually justifies lining the run.
