    Bellied Sewer Line Correction in Junction City, KS

    A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged and holds standing water and solids. Bellies often form where the original bedding washed out or where the soil settled after heavy rain. Slab leaks and cracked sewer lines are common results of shifting clay, especially after long dry spells followed by heavy rain. We confirm the depth of standing water on camera, then re-bed and re-grade that section to restore proper fall. Correct slope is typically a quarter inch per foot for a typical residential line.

    Trenchless Sewer Line Repair in Junction City, KS

    For many sewer line issues, especially those caused by tree roots or pipe corrosion, trenchless repair offers a less invasive solution. This method, which includes pipe bursting or pipe lining, requires minimal excavation, saving your yard and landscaping from extensive disruption. We use specialized equipment to either pull a new pipe through the old one or insert a resin-coated liner that cures in place, creating a new, durable pipe inside the old one. It's an efficient and popular choice for preserving property appearance in Junction City.
