    PEX-A vs. PEX-B Installation in Junction City

    We offer installation for both PEX-A and PEX-B piping and understand the distinct advantages of each. PEX-A, often preferred for its flexibility and ability to recover from minor kinks, uses expansion fittings. PEX-B is more rigid but also very durable, typically using crimp or compression fittings. Our plumbers in Junction City can help you decide which type of PEX suits your project, considering factors like budget and application.

    Polybutylene Replacement With PEX in Junction City, KS

    Gray polybutylene pipe was common in homes built from the late 1970s through the mid 1990s and is known for sudden failures. Chlorine in municipal water breaks down the plastic and its fittings over time. Plenty of insurers now ask about polybutylene when writing a policy. Replacing it with PEX removes that risk and can make your home easier to insure and sell.

    Signs you might need PEX repiping include consistently low water pressure, discolored or rusty water, frequent leaks, or old galvanized steel or polybutylene pipes. These issues are typical in older Junction City homes and point to a failing plumbing system.

    PEX offers several advantages over copper, including lower material and installation costs, greater flexibility and resistance to corrosion and freezing.     PEX is well suited for a wide range of climates because of its flexibility and resistance to freezing.
