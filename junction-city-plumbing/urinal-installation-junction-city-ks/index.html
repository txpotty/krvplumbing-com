    Sensor Urinal Installation in Junction City, KS

    Sensor valves stop missed flushes and double flushes.     Waterless Urinal Upgrades in Junction City

    Switching to waterless is mostly a fixture swap. We remove the flush valve, cap the supply and set the waterless bowl on the same carrier. Some codes want the supply left in place. That allows a future change back.

    Urinal Installation Cost Factors in Junction City, KS

    Carrier work is the biggest cost swing.     Urinal Replacement and Upgrades in Junction City

    Cracked or stained urinals are worth replacing. A hairline crack in vitreous china will eventually leak, and a bowl stained by years of hard scale often cannot be cleaned back to white, so a new fixture on the old carrier is usually the fastest fix. We check the carrier bolts first. Rusted bolts get replaced.

    Sloan Royal valves are on most commercial urinals we see.     Flush Valve Urinal Installation in Junction City, KS
