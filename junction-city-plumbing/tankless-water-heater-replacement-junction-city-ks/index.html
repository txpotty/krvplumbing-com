    Picking a Tankless for Your Junction City, KS Home

    Leaking Tankless Replacement in Junction City

    A leaking tankless can drip inside the cabinet for weeks before anyone notices the puddle. Shut the gas and cold isolation valve now. Then we look at whether the leak is a fitting or the heat exchanger itself, because one is a $20 fix and the other means a new unit.

    Navien Venting and Install in Junction City

    Navien allows 2-inch or 3-inch PVC venting on its condensing units, with maximum lengths that drop as you add elbows. We stay inside those limits. Go past them and the warranty suffers.

    Residential Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Junction City

    Sizing a tankless starts with how many fixtures run at once and how cold the incoming water is, not the square footage of the house. Two showers and a dishwasher at the same time can need 7 or 8 gallons per minute. The unit is chosen from that figure. Any gas, water or vent changes the new unit needs get done to current code.

    Recirculation Tankless Upgrades in Junction City, KS

    Waiting a minute for hot water at the far bathroom wastes thousands of gallons a year in a long single-story house. Built-in pumps on Navien A and some Rinnai models push water through a crossover valve or a dedicated return. We set a schedule. Mornings and evenings, usually.

    Condensing Tankless Replacement in Junction City

    Condensing tankless units like the Navien NPE or Rinnai Sensei pass 90 percent efficiency by pulling extra heat out of the exhaust. They vent through PVC. Acidic condensate goes through a neutralizer and then to an approved drain. Strong winds and dry spells can draw moisture out of the soil, while sudden cold fronts can drop temperatures sharply overnight. The savings carry through every season because hot water use does.

    Rinnai Condensing Upgrades in Junction City, KS

    Rinnai Sensei condensing models replace older non-condensing Rinnai units in a lot of homes, but the venting changes from stainless to PVC. We handle that conversion. Gas and water connections usually line up.

    Point-of-Use Electric Tankless in Junction City

    Point-of-use electric tankless heaters under a sink are a different animal from whole-house units. They run on a single 20 or 30 amp circuit and work well for a far bathroom or an office sink. Most take under two hours.

    Electric Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Junction City, KS

    Electric tankless water heaters skip the gas line entirely, but a whole-house model can need 120 to 160 amps on two to four double-pole breakers. Many panels cannot spare that. We check panel capacity and wire gauge before quoting.
