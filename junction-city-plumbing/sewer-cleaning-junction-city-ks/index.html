    Cast Iron Sewer Cleaning in Junction City, KS

    Many older homes have cast iron drains under the slab or floor. Homes from the late seventies through the mid nineties may have gray polybutylene supply pipe, which is prone to sudden failure. Over time the inside of cast iron corrodes, scales and develops a channel along the bottom. Aggressive cutters can break thin spots, so we use descaling chains and careful jetting made for older iron. This restores flow and gives a clear view on camera of the remaining wall. It also helps you decide if lining is a smart option.

    Hydro Jetting for Sewer Lines in Junction City

    For the most stubborn sewer line clogs, hydro jetting is the top choice. This powerful technique uses high-pressure water streams to blast away grease, scale, mineral buildup, and even tree roots from the interior walls of your sewer pipes. KRV Plumbing employs professional hydro jetting equipment for sewer cleaning in Junction City, delivering a thorough cleaning that leaves your pipes nearly spotless, greatly reducing the chance of future blockages and extending the life of your sewer system.

    Grease and Scale Buildup Cleaning in Junction City, KS

    Grease from kitchens cools and coats the inside of sewer pipes, and minerals in the water bond with it to form a tough crust. Many Kansas homeowners use water softeners to extend the life of their tank water heaters and fixtures against heavy mineral buildup. Over years the pipe opening shrinks until flow slows to a trickle. A cable can punch a hole through, but jetting cleans the walls back to full size. We choose the method based on pipe material and condition. Cleaning before the line fully closes spares you money and stress.

    Clay and Orangeburg Sewer Lines in Junction City

    Older properties may still have vitrified clay or even Orangeburg fiber pipe running to the street. Clay joints are open invitations for roots, and Orangeburg can flatten under soil weight. We clean these lines carefully and use the camera to map joints and soft spots. If the pipe is failing, we will tell you plainly and show the footage. Cleaning keeps you running while you plan a replacement.
