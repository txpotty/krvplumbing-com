    Hydro Jetting Cost Compared to Snaking in Gardner, KS

    Jetting costs more on the first visit. The results hold up better. If you are paying for snaking several times a year, jetting often costs less over time because it removes the coating that causes the repeat clogs in the first place, though a simple soft clog may only need a cable.

    Scale Removal in Cast Iron Pipes in Gardner

    Cast iron grows rust tubercles. Scale adds to them. Jetting with the right nozzle can strip much of this buildup and restore flow, while we keep the pressure in a safe range for aged iron and watch the camera for thin spots, which is also the standard prep before pipe lining.

    Kitchen Line Grease Jetting in Gardner, KS

    Grease clings and hardens. Lines under a slab get it bad. Jetting melts and scrubs that buildup away instead of poking a hole through it, using smaller hose and nozzles sized for 1-1/2 and 2 inch kitchen lines, so your sink and dishwasher drain properly for much longer.

    Restaurant and Hotel Jetting in Gardner, KS

    Restaurants and hotels build grease fast. Kitchens are not the only problem. We jet commercial kitchen and restroom lines on a schedule that keeps the building open and the drains moving.

    Main Sewer Line Hydro Jetting in Gardner

    Every drain in the house empties into the main. One blockage, every fixture. Roots from nearby trees are the usual cause, and a jetter cuts them out and flushes the line clean to the street.

    Preventive Maintenance Jetting in Gardner
