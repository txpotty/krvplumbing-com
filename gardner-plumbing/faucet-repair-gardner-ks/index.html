    What We Fix on Faucets in Gardner, KS

    Compression Faucet Repair in Gardner

    Two-handle faucets with separate hot and cold handles are often compression faucets, which seal by pressing a rubber washer down onto a brass seat inside the valve. Washers harden and seats get pitted. New washers go in and the seat gets dressed or replaced with a seat wrench, and the packing gets redone if the stem leaks.

    Disc Faucet Repair in Gardner

    Ceramic disc faucets last a long time, but grit from a water main repair or scale from hard water can scratch the discs or wear out the rubber inlet seals underneath. The seals are cheap. A scratched disc means a new cartridge, since the two discs are lapped to fit each other and cannot be resurfaced.

    Faucet Repair Versus Replacement in Gardner, KS

    A new cartridge usually costs well under the price of a new faucet and can add years of life, as long as the faucet body is sound and parts are still made. A cracked body or discontinued parts change the answer. We give you both prices so the choice is yours.

    Tub Spout and Diverter Repair in Gardner, KS

    Water still running from the tub spout while the shower is on means the diverter is worn, either the gate inside the spout or a diverter stem in the valve. Spouts install two ways. The pipe stub gets measured so we bring the right spout, and the fix gives you full pressure at the showerhead again.

    Bathroom Pop-Up Drain Repair in Gardner

    Pop-up drains on bathroom sinks fail at the pivot ball, the clevis strap, the lift rod or the stopper itself, and a slow drain often turns out to be hair wrapped around the stopper. Try lifting out the stopper first. A new pop-up assembly goes in, matched to your faucet finish when the old one is corroded or the pivot leaks.

    Cartridge Faucet Repair in Gardner, KS

    Single-handle faucets from Moen, Delta, Kohler and Pfister mostly use replaceable cartridges, and a worn one shows up as a drip, a stiff handle or water that will not hold a steady temperature. Each brand uses its own part number. We match it. Swapping a common cartridge like the Moen 1225 takes about half an hour, cleanup included.

    Bathroom Faucet Repair in Gardner, KS

    Bathroom sinks see less wear than kitchen sinks, but scale still builds up in cartridges, and pop-up drains break at the pivot rod under the sink. Both are quick fixes. Cartridges get matched for widespread, centerset and single-hole faucets, and pop-up assemblies are replaced to fit the finish.

    Commercial Faucet Repair in Gardner, KS

    Restaurant and office faucets cycle hundreds of times a day, so cartridges, handle springs, metering valves and sensor solenoids wear out far faster than they do in a house. We carry commercial cartridges. Metering faucets get timed so they run long enough to rinse without wasting water, and we can work after hours.
