    Ball Faucet Repair in Gardner, KS

    Ball faucets, recognizable by their single lever that moves over a rounded cap, are common in many kitchens. They use a rotating ball joint to control water flow and temperature. If your ball faucet is leaking from the spout or base, or if the handle feels loose, it's likely time for a repair. We can replace the spring-loaded rubber seats and O-rings, which are typically the culprits, restoring smooth operation and preventing drips.

    Faucet Repair Versus Replacement in Gardner, KS

    A cartridge repair often costs well under the price of a new faucet and can add years of life. However, if the faucet body is cracked, parts are discontinued or it has needed several repairs, replacement might be better value. We give you both prices so you can choose. Many brands like Moen and Delta offer lifetime warranties on cartridges, which can reduce your repair cost.

    Cartridge Faucet Repair in Gardner, KS

    Many modern single-handle faucets, commonly found in newer homes, use a cartridge system. When these fail, you might experience inconsistent water temperature, reduced flow, or difficulty turning the faucet on or off. Our technicians are skilled at diagnosing cartridge issues and replacing them with the correct manufacturer-specific part, so your faucet works like new. We work with all major brands to provide precise repairs.

    Dripping Faucet Solutions in Gardner

    That constant drip, drip, drip isn't just annoying, it's a sign of a deeper problem. Most dripping faucets are caused by internal component wear, like a deteriorated rubber washer or a cracked cartridge. Our team provides precise solutions, replacing the specific parts that are failing. We work with all types of faucets, including compression, cartridge, disc, and ball varieties, restoring a quiet and drip-free operation once again.

    Low Water Flow Faucet Repair in Gardner

    Weak or sputtering flow is usually caused by mineral buildup in the aerator or cartridge. We remove and clean or replace the aerator, flush the lines and check the cartridge for scale blockage. On pull-down faucets, the spray head and check valves can also clog. If flow is low at every fixture, we test whole-house water pressure.

    Disc Faucet Repair in Gardner

    Ceramic disc faucets are known for their durability and are a popular choice in many contemporary Gardner bathrooms. While generally reliable, they can develop leaks if the ceramic discs become scratched or misaligned, or if the inlet and outlet seals wear out. Our plumbers have the expertise to carefully disassemble these precise mechanisms, clean or replace the ceramic discs, and reseal the faucet to eliminate leaks and restore full performance.

    Faucet Handle Repair & Replacement in Gardner, KS

    A loose, broken, or stripped faucet handle makes it difficult to control water flow and temperature. Over time, daily use can wear down the internal mechanisms or cause the handle itself to break. We offer skilled repair for various handle types, from single-handle mixers to separate hot and cold handles. If a repair isn't feasible, we can recommend and install a suitable replacement handle that matches your existing fixture and style, adding to its lifespan.

    Compression Faucet Repair in Gardner

    Compression faucets, often found in older Gardner homes, are known for their separate hot and cold handles. When these faucets leak, it's usually due to worn-out rubber washers or packing. Our team is skilled in disassembling these classic fixtures, replacing the necessary seals and washers, and reassembling them to stop leaks effectively. We respect the character of older homes and make sure these repairs are done carefully.
