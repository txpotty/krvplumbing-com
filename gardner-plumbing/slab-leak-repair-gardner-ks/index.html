    Direct Slab Leak Repair in Gardner, KS

    Direct repair is often the most practical fix for a single leak. A small section of slab gets cut.     Electronic Slab Leak Detection in Gardner

    Locating comes before cutting. We use a ground mic and a thermal camera. The mic picks up the hiss of water escaping through concrete, and the camera shows warm water spreading from a hot line, so together they narrow the leak to a spot about a foot across. We test hot lines, cold lines and drains in your Gardner home.
