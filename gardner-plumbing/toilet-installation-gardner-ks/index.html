    Why Choose KRV Plumbing for Toilet Installation in Gardner, KS?

    Installing a toilet looks simple until the bolts are overtightened and the porcelain cracks. We snug closet bolts by hand and finish with a quarter turn. China does not flex. A wrench used the way you would on a pipe fitting will split the foot of the bowl, and that crack can take weeks to start weeping.

    • Correct Rough-In, We measure 10, 12 or 14 inch rough-ins from the finished wall before you buy.
    • Right Tools, A level, composite shims and a light hand on the bolts keep the porcelain intact.

    Types of Toilet Installation in Gardner, KS

    Smart Toilet Installation in Gardner, KS

    Smart toilets with heated seats and built-in bidets need two things the old toilet did not: power and a dedicated water connection. Most require a GFCI outlet within reach of the cord. We run the water side and mount the unit, and if a new outlet is needed we bring in a licensed electrician rather than splicing anything ourselves. Then we test every button.

    Toilet Flange and Wax Ring Replacement in Gardner, KS

    The closet flange is the ring that ties the toilet to the drain, and it is the weak point on most old installs. On cast iron, the bolt slots rust out first. Plastic flanges crack from overtightened bolts, and a stainless repair ring screwed over the old one usually gives a solid anchor again. Seal goes on last.

    Commercial Restroom Toilets in Gardner, KS

    Restaurants and offices get heavy-duty fixtures and accessible stalls built to code. Downtime costs a business money. We schedule commercial installs early in the morning or after hours, and we stage the fixtures and valves on site so each stall is down for as short a time as possible. We work with your GC on inspections.

    New Construction Toilet Installation in Gardner

    On new builds we set toilets after the finish floor is down, never before. The flange should sit on top of the finished floor. An early set means the tile goes around the flange, leaving it low, and that calls for an extender ring later. Each bowl gets a fresh seal.

    Bathroom Remodel Toilet Installation in Gardner, KS

    New tile over old vinyl can raise a bathroom floor by half an inch or more. Suddenly the old flange is below the floor. Either an extender ring brings the flange up or a new flange goes in, and relocating a toilet on a slab means saw-cutting concrete to move the drain. Moving it on a wood floor is easier.

    Standard Toilet Installation in Gardner

    A standard floor-mount toilet is a two-hour job when the flange is sound. We shut off the stop, drain the tank and pull the old bowl. After scraping the old wax off, we check the flange for cracks, set new stainless bolts, and lower the new toilet straight down onto a fresh seal so it compresses evenly. The old one leaves with us.

    Wall-Mounted Toilet Installation in Gardner

    Wall-mounted toilets hang off a steel carrier bolted to the floor inside the wall. An access plate hides the tank. Because the carrier carries all the weight, we anchor its feet to the slab or framing first and check it for plumb before the drywall or tile goes up. Plan on a remodel, not a swap.

    Low-Flow WaterSense Toilet Installation in Gardner, KS

    WaterSense toilets are capped at 1.28 gallons per flush, and some dual-flush models drop to around 0.8 for liquid waste. It adds up quickly. A family of four swapping one old 3.5 gallon toilet can save thousands of gallons a year, and some water providers offer rebates worth checking. Pick a strong MaP rating.
