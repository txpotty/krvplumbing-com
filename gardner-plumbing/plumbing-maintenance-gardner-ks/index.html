    Routine Plumbing Maintenance in Gardner, KS

    Softener Upkeep in Gardner, KS

    Softeners fail quietly. A unit that stopped regenerating or ran out of salt keeps sending water to the house, it just is not soft anymore, and you will not notice until scale shows up on the faucets and the water heater starts popping. We check the bypass and drain line. Then we test the treated water.

    Drain Cleaning as Maintenance in Gardner

    Slow drains rarely fix themselves. Camera checks and cleaning on problem lines keep grease and roots from causing a holiday backup. We jet greasy lines. Rooted lines get regular cutting.

    Hot Water Tank Service in Gardner, KS

    Gas and electric tanks need different checks. On gas units we look at the burner flame, the flue draft and the gas connection for leaks, and on electric units we test the elements and the thermostats and check the wiring for heat damage. Sediment comes out either way. Mineral buildup on the bottom wastes fuel and can lead to early tank failure.

    Tankless Water Heater Descaling in Gardner

    Tankless units need descaling. Scale builds up inside the heat exchanger, especially with hard water, and the unit starts throwing error codes, running lukewarm or cutting out mid-shower long before it actually wears out. We pump descaler through the service valves. The inlet filter, venting and gas line get checked too.

    Water Heater Maintenance in Gardner

    Sediment is the tank's slow killer. Water across much of Kansas runs hard, leaving mineral scale in water heaters, faucets, showerheads, and dishwashers. We drain and flush the tank, pull and check the anode rod, test the temperature and pressure relief valve, and look over the gas or electric connections for corrosion and wear. Plan on an hour. A clean tank heats faster and lasts years longer.

    Backflow Preventer Testing in Gardner

    Backflow devices protect the drinking water. Yearly tests are often required, and our certified testers check each valve and file results. Failed devices get rebuilt. We also check that devices are protected from heat and freezing where needed. Late tests can mean fines.

    Gas Connector and Shutoff Checks in Gardner

    Gas feeds water heaters, furnaces, ranges and dryers. We inspect visible gas piping, test connections with leak detector solution, make sure each appliance shutoff valve turns and replace any old or kinked flexible connectors. Repairs follow local code. If you smell gas, get outside before calling the gas company.

    Slab Leak and Hidden Leak Checks in Gardner

    A lot of Kansas housing dates from the mid-century building boom, with galvanized supply lines and cast iron drains. Buried and under-slab water lines can wear from soil movement and hard water until they start leaking where nobody can see it, sometimes for months. Warm floors are a clue. So is a meter that spins with everything off. We use acoustic and thermal tools to find the spot.
