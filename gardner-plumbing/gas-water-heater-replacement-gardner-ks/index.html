    Picking a Gas Water Heater for Your Gardner, KS House

    Power Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Gardner, KS

    Power vent heaters use a blower to push exhaust through 2 or 3-inch PVC, so they can sit far from a chimney. A standard outlet has to be within reach of the cord. Vent length and elbows stay inside the manufacturer's limits.

    40 and 50 Gallon Tank Replacement in Gardner

    Forty and fifty gallon gas tanks are the workhorse in most Kansas homes, and a leak at the bottom seam means the tank is finished. Newer tanks can run taller than the old one. We measure the closet before ordering. Plan on about two to three hours.

    Large Capacity 75 Gallon Gas Heaters in Gardner, KS

    Big families with soaking tubs often outgrow a 50 gallon tank and need a 75 gallon heater or a high-recovery 50 at around 65,000 to 75,000 BTU. A bigger burner needs more gas. We check the meter and pipe size first, because a starved burner never reaches its rated recovery.

    Tankless Gas Conversion in Gardner

    Going tankless usually means a bigger gas line, since a whole-house tankless unit can draw 199,000 BTU against the 40,000 a tank burner uses. The 1/2-inch line feeding a tank is rarely enough. The venting changes too. We check meter capacity and pipe length before quoting so there are no surprises halfway through the job.

    Propane Gas Water Heater Replacement in Gardner, KS

    Propane heaters run at about 11 inches of water column instead of the 7 inches typical for natural gas, so the gas valve and orifice have to match the fuel. We never swap a natural gas tank onto propane without the right conversion. We confirm pressure with a manometer.

    Atmospheric Vent Upgrades in Gardner, KS

    Flue pipes on older atmospheric heaters rust at the joints and lose their upward slope, and a loose connector can spill exhaust into the closet. We replace damaged sections and the draft hood if needed. Then we test draft with the burner on.

    Bradford White Tank Replacement in Gardner

    Most of the tanks on our trucks are Bradford White, partly because their Defender combustion system and ICON gas valve are simple to service. We help you match model to demand. The install meets current code.

    Choosing Between Gas Tank Brands in Gardner, KS

    Warranty length on a tank mostly tracks anode size and burner design, so a 12-year tank is a different build from a 6-year one. Some have self-cleaning dip tubes for sediment. Powered anodes outlast standard rods. Your plans for the house help decide it.
