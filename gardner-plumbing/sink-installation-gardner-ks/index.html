    Undermount Clips and Cure Time in Gardner

    Undermount bowls need overnight to cure. The silicone bead under the rim reaches handling strength in a few hours but needs about a day before it carries the weight of a full basin of water and a pot leaning on the edge. We brace it and come back. Plumbing goes in on the second visit.

    Vessel Sink Installation in Gardner, KS

    Vessel sinks sit on top of the counter. That raises the rim several inches, so we either pick a taller vessel faucet or mount a wall faucet, and we use a vessel drain that matches the bowl's overflow or lack of one. No overflow means no slotted drain. Getting that wrong leaves water under the bowl.

    Undermount Bowl Setting on Stone in Gardner

    Stone shops cut the hole, but we set the bowl. An even reveal around a polished cutout takes shims and patience before the clips are tightened. A crooked reveal never goes away. We check it from both ends of the counter.

    Bathroom Sink Installation in Gardner, KS

    Bathroom sinks are lighter but fussier. The faucet holes have to match the faucet you picked, whether that is a single hole, a 4 inch centerset or an 8 inch widespread, and the overflow has to line up with the pop-up drain. We check the spec sheet first. Mismatches get caught before the counter is cut.

    Emergency Sink Replacement in Gardner

    A cracked bowl cannot be patched for long. When a bowl cracks or a drain rusts through, we cap the lines first so the house keeps water, then set a new sink. Common sizes can go in the same day. Odd cutouts take longer.

    Pedestal Sink Installation in Gardner, KS

    Pedestal sinks hide almost nothing. The supply stops and the trap sit right behind the column, so we center the drain stub, use chrome or polished brass trap parts, and set the stops tight to the wall where the pedestal can hide them. The wall needs solid blocking. The basin hangs from it, not the pedestal.
