    Battery Backup System Testing in Gardner, KS

    Storms knock out power when the pit fills fastest. That is the moment a backup pump earns its keep. Our plumber reads the battery date, cleans corrosion off the terminals and tests the charger, because a failed charger can leave the battery flat within a season.

    Commercial Sump Pump Maintenance in Gardner, KS

    Commercial pits are often bigger and deeper, and many have two pumps on an alternating controller. We test both pumps, the alternator and the high-water alarm. You get a written record of each reading, which comes in handy for insurance renewals and inspections. Quarterly visits are common.

    Float Switch Repair and Adjustment in Gardner, KS

    Float trouble causes more no-start calls than anything else. A tethered float can tangle on the pump body or catch on the side of the basin. Vertical floats hang up once minerals coat the rod. In tight pits where nothing has room to swing, we can switch you to an electronic sensor switch with no moving float at all.

    Sump Pit Cleaning and Debris Removal in Gardner

    Silt and gravel wash into a pit through the drain tile every time it rains, and over a few years that layer can bury the intake screen. Our plumber drains the basin and digs the sediment out by hand. With the pit clean, the float swings without catching. The pump runs cooler too.

    Pit Cleaning and Liner Checks in Gardner

    Long dry stretches let dust, sand, leaves and gravel settle at the bottom of the basin. Sooner or later the pump sucks it into the screen and locks up the impeller. We pump the basin dry, rinse the screen and check the plastic liner for cracks, since a cracked liner lets soil wash in from the sides. Takes about twenty minutes.

    Check Valve and Discharge Pipe Repair in Gardner, KS

    A bad check valve can nearly double how often the pump starts. Each start heats the motor windings. We swap worn valves for quiet, spring-loaded models and inspect the discharge pipe for cracks and loose couplings all the way to the exit point. Discharge needs to land far enough from the slab or footings that it does not seep right back into the pit.

    Discharge Line and Check Valve Service in Gardner

    The check valve sits on the discharge pipe a foot or so above the pump and keeps the water in the line from falling back into the pit. A failed valve sends that water back down for the pump to move again. Motors wear out faster. Our plumber tests the valve, confirms there is a weep hole below it to prevent air lock, and checks each joint to the wall.

    Commercial Building Sump Pumps in Gardner, KS

    Parking garages, mechanical rooms and loading docks often drain to large pits with pumps rated at a horsepower or more. Those units need more frequent checks than a house pump. Our crew tests the controls, the alarm panel and each pump in a duplex station, and records amp draw every visit so a slow decline gets caught. Downtime costs money.
