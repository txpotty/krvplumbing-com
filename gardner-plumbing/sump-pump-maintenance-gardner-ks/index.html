    Storm-Ready Sump Pump Maintenance Solutions in Gardner, KS

    Pre-Storm Season Pump Testing in Gardner

    Flash flooding can push water into basements and crawlspaces, so sump pumps and good grading matter for many homes. We test your pump ahead of the rainy season by filling the pit until the float trips. We watch the pump empty the basin, listen for grinding bearings and measure how fast it cycles. If the pump struggles, you still have time to fix it before the first big storm.

    Check Valve and Discharge Line Service in Gardner, KS

    The check valve stops pumped water from falling back into the pit when the pump shuts off. A failed valve makes the pump run twice as often and wears out the motor. We test and replace check valves and inspect the discharge pipe for cracks and loose joints. We also make sure water exits away from the house so it does not soak into the soil around the slab or foundation.

    Zoeller and Wayne Sump Pump Maintenance in Gardner

    KRV Plumbing is proficient in maintaining all major sump pump brands, including popular models from Zoeller and Wayne. Our technicians understand the specific components and maintenance requirements for these trusted brands, so your unit receives expert care. Whether it's a submersible Zoeller pump or a pedestal Wayne pump, we provide thorough service to keep it running well for your Gardner home.

    Pit Cleaning and Debris Removal in Gardner

    Long dry stretches let dust, sand, gravel and leaves settle in the pit. That debris can get sucked into the intake screen and jam the impeller. We pump down and scoop out the basin, rinse the intake and inspect the pit liner for cracks. A clean basin helps the pump run cooler and last years longer.

    Battery Backup System Testing in Gardner, KS

    A power outage during a heavy storm can render your primary sump pump useless. Our maintenance service includes thorough testing of your battery backup sump pump system. We check the battery charge, verify the charger's function and ensure the backup pump activates when the main power is interrupted. This dual protection is vital for homes in Gardner, KS where power interruptions can coincide with severe weather.

    Float Switch Repair and Adjustment in Gardner, KS

    The float switch is the most common reason a sump pump fails to start. Tethered floats can tangle on the pump body, and vertical floats can stick from mineral crust or debris. We clean or replace the switch and set the on and off levels so the pump does not short cycle. Electronic sensor switches are an option for tight pits where floats get hung up.

    Float Switch Inspection and Adjustment in Gardner, KS

    The float switch is a key component that tells your sump pump when to turn on and off. A stuck or improperly adjusted float switch can lead to the pump running continuously or failing to activate at all. During maintenance, we inspect the float switch for freedom of movement and proper calibration, making adjustments as needed to support reliable operation for your home in Gardner, KS.

    Battery Backup Pump Maintenance in Gardner

    Severe storms often bring power outages along with the rain. A battery backup pump keeps water moving when the main pump loses power or fails. We load test the battery, check the charger and alarm and run the backup pump on its own. Most backup batteries need replacement every three to five years.
