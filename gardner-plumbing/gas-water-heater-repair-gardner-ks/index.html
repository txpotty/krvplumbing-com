    Frequently Asked Questions About Gas Water Heater Repair in Gardner

    The cost of gas water heater repair in Gardner, KS can vary widely depending on the issue. Minor repairs like a thermocouple replacement might range from $150 to $350, while more complex issues such as a gas valve replacement could be $400 to $800 or more. We always provide a detailed, upfront estimate once we diagnose the problem.

    A thermocouple is a safety device in a gas water heater that senses if the pilot light is lit. If it doesn't sense heat, it shuts off the gas supply to prevent gas leaks. A faulty thermocouple is a leading reason for a pilot light not staying lit and is a critical safety component.

    KRV Plumbing stands behind our work. All gas water heater repairs performed by our technicians come with a warranty covering both parts and labor. We will provide full warranty details at the time of service, giving you peace of mind.

    Because of the risks that come with natural gas and possible carbon monoxide poisoning, gas water heater repairs should always be handled by a licensed professional. DIY attempts can be dangerous and may void warranties or break local Gardner, KS plumbing codes.

    Most repairs are completed the same day in one to two hours. Special order gas valves may take one to three business days.

    We repair Rheem, Ruud, A.O. Smith, State, Bradford White, American Standard and Whirlpool gas tanks, among others. We carry common pilot and valve parts.

    On average, a well-maintained gas water heater can last 10 to 12 years. Regular maintenance, including flushing the tank, can help extend its lifespan. If your unit is nearing this age and having frequent issues, replacement might be more sensible than continuous repairs.

    Watch for a lack of hot water, inconsistent water temperature, a pilot light that won't stay lit, unusual noises like rumbling or popping, a rotten egg smell, which can point to a gas leak, or visible leaks around the tank. Any of these warrant a professional inspection.
