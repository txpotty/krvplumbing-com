    Gas Tank Water Heater Installation in Gardner

    Gas tank water heaters provide strong recovery and are common in many local homes. We install 40, 50 and 75 gallon models with proper venting and combustion air. Each install includes a new gas connector, sediment trap and shutoff valve. We check draft and test for gas leaks. Gas units are a good choice for families with high hot water demand.

    Water Heater Expansion Tank Installation in Gardner

    Many plumbing codes require the installation of an expansion tank with a new water heater, particularly in closed-loop systems. Plumber licensing in Kansas is handled locally, so requirements differ by city and county and are worth checking before work starts. An expansion tank protects your water heater and plumbing system from excessive pressure buildup, which can be caused by the heating process. Our installation service includes correctly sizing and installing the expansion tank to ensure proper system operation and prevent premature wear on your new water heater and other plumbing fixtures in Gardner. This small component offers real long-term protection.

    Tankless Water Heater Installation in Gardner, KS

    Tankless water heaters provide on-demand hot water, saving energy and space, and they are more and more popular in Kansas homes.     Electric Tank Water Heater Installation in Gardner, KS

    Electric tank water heaters are common in homes without natural gas service. We install models with the correct voltage and breaker size for your electrical panel. These units do not need venting, which allows more flexible placement. We also install a drain pan when the heater is in a closet or attic. Electric models are simple, dependable and quiet.
