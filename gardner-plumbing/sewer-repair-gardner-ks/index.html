    A single spot repair is the most affordable fix when the rest of the line is sound. We lay out all three options with real numbers so you can weigh cost against how long the repair will last.

    Bellied Sewer Line Correction in Gardner, KS

    A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged and holds standing water and solids. Bellies usually form where the original bedding washed out or where the soil settled after years of wet weather. Basements are common in the eastern part of Kansas, while slab and crawlspace homes are more typical in many other areas. We confirm the depth of standing water on camera, then re-bed and re-grade that section to restore proper fall. Correct slope is typically a quarter inch per foot for a typical residential line.

    Root Intrusion Repair in Gardner, KS

    Tree and shrub roots go straight for the moisture and nutrients leaking from old sewer joints. Once inside, they grow into dense mats that catch paper and waste. We cut the roots with a mechanical cutter or hydro-jetter, then repair or seal the joint so they cannot come right back. For lines with many rooted joints, we will show you when lining or replacement makes more sense than repeated cleaning.

    Orangeburg and Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Gardner

    Some older homes still have Orangeburg pipe, a tar-paper material that flattens and blisters over time. Cast iron from the same era corrodes from the inside and scales up until little flow remains. When a camera shows widespread failure, patching is a waste of money. We replace the line in sections or end to end with PVC, and we can often use trenchless methods to avoid tearing up the whole yard.

    Tree Root Invasion Solutions in Gardner

    Tree roots are a common culprit for sewer line damage in Kansas, attracted to the moisture and nutrients inside pipes. They can work into tiny cracks, expand and eventually crush or clog the line. Our technicians are adept at diagnosing root intrusion using camera technology. We can remove roots with specialized cutting tools and recommend preventive measures or durable pipe relining options to prevent future invasions, protecting your home's foundation.

    Sewer Line Backups and Blockages in Gardner

    Sewer backups are usually caused by severe blockages from grease buildup, foreign objects or heavy tree root intrusion. Our team uses HD sewer camera inspections to identify the exact cause and location of the blockage. We then use hydro-jetting or mechanical snaking to clear the obstruction, restoring proper flow to your system and preventing further damage. This approach is highly effective for the stubborn clogs often found in older Kansas homes.
