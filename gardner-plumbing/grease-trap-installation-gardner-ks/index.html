    Hydromechanical Grease Trap Installation in Gardner

    Hydromechanical grease traps sit under or near sinks and use baffles and flow control to separate grease from water. They are common in small cafes, coffee shops and quick-service restaurants with limited space. We install units from Zurn, Watts, Schier and Endura sized by gallons per minute. Each install includes a flow control fitting and proper venting to keep the trap working as designed. These units need frequent cleaning, so we place them where staff can reach the lid without trouble.

    Hydro-Mechanical Grease Trap Installation in Gardner, KS

    Hydro-mechanical grease traps, also known as automatic grease removal units, offer an active solution for FOG management in Gardner, KS. These units often include internal baffles and flow control mechanisms to improve separation efficiency. We provide professional installation of various hydro-mechanical models, ensuring all electrical and plumbing connections are correctly made for optimal performance and automated FOG removal.
