    Composite Sink Installation in Gardner

    Granite and quartz composite sinks resist scratches on the surface but can chip at the drain hole if a strainer is overtightened. Hand tight comes first. Composite makers usually call for silicone instead of plumber's putty under the strainer, since some putties stain the material, and we follow that sheet.

    New Construction Sink Rough-In in Gardner, KS

    Rough-in sets the drain height and the stops long before the cabinets arrive. We usually set the kitchen drain stub around 16 to 20 inches off the floor, then adjust for deep bowls or a disposal so the trap does not end up below the wall outlet. Vents go in now. We work from your sink and faucet specs to avoid moving pipe later.

    Double Bowl Sink and Continuous Waste in Gardner

    Both bowls of a double sink share one trap, using either a center outlet continuous waste or an end outlet tee. We pick the layout around your disposal and dishwasher, since a disposal on one side changes where the tee can go and how much fall each side gets. Heavy-wall fittings resist leaks.

    Emergency Sink Replacement in Gardner

    A cracked bowl or a rusted-through strainer can soak a cabinet in an afternoon. First, close both angle stops and stop using the sink. We carry common sizes. For a standard 33 by 22 inch double bowl drop-in, we can often pull the old sink and set a new one the same day.

    Undermount Sinks on Stone in Gardner, KS

    Every fabricator handles undermount clips a bit differently. Some epoxy threaded studs into the stone, others use a rail system, and we work with whichever one your countertop shop prefers. The bowl is braced regardless. We do not connect the trap until the adhesive has cured.

    Sink and Disposal Installed Together in Gardner, KS

    A new sink is the cheapest time to add a disposal. The disposal mounts to the new flange, the trap is set to its outlet height and the dishwasher plug gets knocked out for the hose. Power is a separate question. There needs to be a switched outlet under the sink.
