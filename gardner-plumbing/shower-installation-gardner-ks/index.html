    Curbless showers need the floor lowered in the shower area. A wood floor gets dropped joists or a recessed pan, while a slab gets cut and lowered so the floor flows straight in. Linear drains help a lot. We plan the slope with your contractor before demo.

    Digital Shower Systems in Gardner, KS

    Digital shower systems like U by Moen and Kohler DTV need a little more planning.     Tub-to-Shower Conversion in Gardner, KS

    Tub-to-shower conversions are one of our most common jobs. The tub comes out, the drain shifts to the pan location and the valve moves up to about 48 inches. Slab homes need concrete work. No more stepping over a tub wall.

    Tiled Walk-In Shower Installation in Gardner

    Frameless glass walk-ins are popular, and the plumbing behind them has to be dead on.     Custom Shower System Installation in Gardner, KS

    Multi-head showers depend on valve capacity. One head works fine off a standard valve, while multiple outlets need a thermostatic valve, volume controls and larger 3/4-inch supply lines. Layout comes first. Then we check whether the water heater can keep up.

    Shower Valve and Fixture Installation in Gardner, KS

    Pressure-balancing valves are the baseline for any new shower. Thermostatic valves hold a set temperature and add volume control, which works well with rain heads and handhelds. Either one protects against scalding. We help you choose based on how many outlets you plan.

    Accessible and ADA Shower Installation in Gardner, KS

    Accessible showers start with blocking in the wall. Blocking goes in for grab bars, the valve goes where a seated person can reach it and a handheld goes on a slide bar. No high curbs. We follow ADA guidelines when you ask.
