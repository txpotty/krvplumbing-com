    Well Pressure Tank Replacement in Gardner

    A well pump that clicks on and off constantly usually points to a failed pressure tank. A ruptured bladder lets the tank waterlog, the air cushion disappears, and the pump starts with every tap, wearing the motor out years early. A new tank fixes it. We also check the switch.

    Whole-Home Pipe Replacement in Gardner

    Several pinhole leaks, galvanized pipe or polybutylene in a Gardner home usually mean it is better off with a complete repipe. New PEX or copper often runs through the attic and down interior walls, letting us abandon leaking under-slab pipe instead of breaking concrete. No jackhammer needed.

    Sump Pump and Well Pump Replacement in Gardner, KS

    A dead sump pump during a storm floods a basement fast, and a dead well pump means no water at all. In Gardner, KS, sump pumps get sized for inflow with a check valve and an optional battery backup, while well pumps get matched to depth, flow and switch settings. We run each one through cycles.

    Well Pump & Pressure Tank Replacement in Gardner

    Rural homes around Gardner that run on a well have no water at all when the pump quits. We pull the old submersible pump, check the wire and drop pipe for damage, set the new pump at the right depth and test flow and pressure, often replacing the pressure tank and switch at the same visit. Water is usually back that day.
