    Slab Leak Reroutes in Gardner

    A reroute abandons the leaking line under the floor. New pipe runs through the walls or attic instead, so there is no concrete work and the cost is often lower than going through tile. We usually use PEX because it handles heat and resists corrosion. Tile and hardwood floors make this the favorite.

    Residential Slab Leak Detection in Gardner

    Single-family homes and townhouses across Gardner all go through the same testing. Meter test first. We isolate, trace and listen, then confirm with a thermal scan or tracer gas, then explain what we found and give you written repair prices.

    Slab Leaks in Older Homes in Gardner, KS

    Older homes in Gardner, KS often have soft copper or galvanized steel under the slab, and both wear out with time. Copper fails at bends and where it rubs concrete, while galvanized rusts from the inside until it splits. Our techs know where these fail. That knowledge points the search at the likeliest spots first.

    Pressure Testing and Isolation in Gardner

    We always start with isolation. Our tech closes the main, caps or valves off the hot and cold sides, and puts a gauge on each, then watches which side loses pressure over a set time. That tells us where to look. It also rules out a toilet or faucet leak masquerading as a slab leak in your Gardner, KS home.

    Thermal Imaging for Slab Leaks in Gardner, KS

    A leaking hot line heats the concrete over it. A thermal camera shows that warm bloom through tile, vinyl and even thin carpet, often spreading outward from the leak point in a recognizable shape. Cold-side leaks will not show. It shrinks the search area before we listen.

    Repiping After Repeat Slab Leaks in Gardner, KS

    Two slab leaks usually means more are coming. The rest of the pipe has likely aged the same way, so repiping replaces every supply line with new PEX or copper through the walls and attic, which ends the pattern of new leaks and new floor openings. Most repipes wrap up in two to four days.

    Cold Water Slab Leak Detection in Gardner, KS

    Cold-side leaks leave no warm spot. They are harder to see. They usually show up as a running meter, damp carpet, a musty smell or water seeping at baseboards, which is why we isolate the cold line, trace its route and listen along it with acoustic gear.

    Acoustic Slab Leak Detection in Gardner

    Water escaping a pressurized pipe makes a hiss that carries up through concrete. A ground microphone picks it up. We move the pickup across the floor in a grid over the traced pipe route, finding where the sound is loudest and confirming it from another position, which works best on copper and less well on PEX.
