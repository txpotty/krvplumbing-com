    Frequently Asked Questions About Tankless Water Heater Maintenance in Gardner

    The cost for tankless water heater maintenance in Gardner, KS generally ranges from $150 to $300, depending on the unit's age, condition and the extent of descaling required. This includes a full flush and inspection of critical components. More complex issues or additional repairs would be quoted on their own.

    The descaling process involves shutting off the unit, isolating the water lines and connecting a circulating pump to flush a mild acidic solution through the heat exchanger. This solution dissolves mineral deposits like calcium and magnesium, which are common in hard water, restoring heat transfer efficiency.

    Tankless water heater maintenance focuses mainly on descaling the heat exchanger and checking electronic components for efficiency. Hot water tank maintenance typically involves flushing sediment from the bottom of the tank, checking the anode rod for corrosion and inspecting the pressure relief valve. Both aim to prolong life but address different system specifics.

    With regular descaling, many tankless heaters last 15 to 20 years. Neglected units in hard water can fail in less than half that time.

    While some basic tasks like cleaning external filters might be possible for a homeowner, complete maintenance, especially descaling the heat exchanger and inspecting gas lines, should be left to a licensed professional. Improper servicing can damage the unit, void warranties and pose safety risks related to gas and carbon monoxide.

    Once a year is standard, and homes with very hard water and no softener may need it every six to nine months. Heavy users and commercial units need it more frequently.

    Most visits take one to two hours, including the descaling cycle. Installing isolation valves on the same trip adds about an hour.
