    Service Valve Kit Installs in Gardner, KS

    Plenty of tankless heaters went in without service valves, and without them there is no clean way to hook up a descaling pump. A pair of isolation valves with purge ports goes on the hot and cold lines. Most kits include a relief valve port as well. Future flushes then take minutes to set up.

    Home Tankless Flushing in Gardner

    Many Kansas homeowners use water softeners to extend the life of their tank water heaters and fixtures against heavy mineral buildup. Inside a tankless heater, that water runs through a small copper or stainless coil while the burner fires, and every minute of hot water leaves a little calcium behind. We hook a pump to the service valves and circulate descaling solution for about an hour. The flow returns. The temperature holds steady and the high-limit shutdowns stop.

    Emergency Tankless Repair in Gardner, KS

    No hot water before work is a rough start. Most no-heat calls come down to a failed igniter or an exchanger so scaled that the high-limit switch keeps tripping. We usually fix it on the first trip, since igniters and descaling gear ride along.

    Annual Tankless Checkup in Gardner, KS

    The annual checkup is a top-to-bottom pass on the heater. Gas pressure at the inlet gets read with a manometer at full fire and checked against the rating plate. We check the flue for blockages. A written report with every reading goes in your file for next year's comparison.

    Yearly Tankless Inspection in Gardner

    A yearly inspection costs less than one service call for a dead heater. Gas connections get a leak test, and we work the pressure relief valve. We pull the air filter and check the igniter.     Rinnai Air Filter and Flame Rod Care in Gardner

    Rinnai cabinets are tight. We wash the air intake filter and check the flame rod for carbon. On recirculating models we also check the pump schedule, because a wrong setting burns gas for nothing.
