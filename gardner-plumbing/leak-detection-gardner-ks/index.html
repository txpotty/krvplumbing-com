    Leak Testing for Multifamily and Offices in Gardner

    Multifamily buildings and office parks have long supply systems where leaks hide for a long time.     Our Leak Detection Equipment in Gardner

    Our trucks carry acoustic correlators and ground microphones, thermal cameras, line tracers, moisture meters and tracer gas kits. Each tool has a job. Acoustic gear hears pressurized water escaping, thermal cameras show temperature differences from hot water lines and wet drywall, and tracer gas finds leaks that make no sound at all on plastic pipe.

    Gas Leak Detection in Gardner

    A faint rotten-egg odor by the furnace or stove needs a professional check. Get out first if it is strong. Our licensed plumbers use a combustible gas detector and soap solution on fittings, appliance connectors and the meter riser, then pressure test the line to find a leak too small to smell, with repairs made to the fuel gas code before the gas comes back on.

    Slab Leak Detection in Gardner

    Concrete slab homes have supply lines buried in sand below the floor, and a pinhole there can run for months before anyone notices. A warm tile is the classic sign. We pressure test the hot and cold sides separately, then use a ground microphone to hear the leak through the concrete and confirm with a thermal camera on hot water lines.

    Emergency Leak Detection in Gardner, KS

    Water coming through a ceiling at night cannot wait until morning. Shut the main. We respond to Gardner, KS emergencies with full detection gear, isolate the failed line and restore water to the rest of the house while the permanent repair gets scheduled.

    Slab Leaks From Ground Movement in Gardner

    Pipe set in concrete cannot flex when the ground moves, so it cracks at elbows and where it rises through the slab. Slab leaks and broken sewer laterals are common results of shifting clay, especially after long dry spells followed by heavy rain. We isolate the line first. Then acoustic listening and thermal imaging find the leak without breaking up the floor to look, and you get a marked spot plus repair options before the saw comes out.

    Water Line Leak Detection in Gardner, KS

    Supply leaks hide inside walls, above ceilings and under yards. Your water bill is usually the first warning. We read the meter, close fixture stops one at a time and use a moisture meter to find the wet framing, then confirm with acoustic gear on pressurized lines, so the repair opening stays small in your Gardner home.

    Pool Leak Detection in Gardner

    A pool losing more than a quarter inch a day beyond evaporation likely has a leak somewhere. We start with a bucket test. Then we pressure test the suction and return lines, use an underwater hydrophone to listen, and dye test around the skimmer, lights and main drain to find the exact crack or fitting that is losing water.
