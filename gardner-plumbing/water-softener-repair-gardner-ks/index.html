    Softener Repair vs Replacement Cost in Gardner, KS

    Most softener repairs cost $150 to $450, while a resin replacement often runs $400 to $900 depending on tank size. A new quality softener installed typically costs $1,500 to $3,500. With a good tank and valve, repair is the better value. An obsolete valve with scarce parts tips the decision toward a new head or a new unit.

    Resin Testing and Replacement in Gardner

    Resin beads trap hardness minerals and release them during regeneration. Chlorine and chloramine in city water slowly break resin down until it turns to mush. We test resin capacity, then clean it with resin cleaner or replace it, and a solid tank and valve with fresh resin works like new for years. Mushy resin can clog the valve.

    Resin Bed Problems in Gardner

    Fouled resin is the usual reason a softener regenerates on schedule but still sends hard water to every faucet in the house. Iron and sediment coat the beads and block the exchange. Depending on the test, the resin gets cleaned or replaced. Fresh resin brings capacity back.

    Control Valve Repair in Gardner

    Water that runs to the drain all day usually means the control valve is stuck between cycles. KRV Plumbing technicians rebuild these valves with the correct seal and spacer kit for your model. Mechanical or electronic, we fix the timing. Common parts are on the truck, so most valve repairs are finished the same day without a second trip to the supply house.

    Injector and Brine Line Cleaning in Gardner, KS

    The injector, sometimes called a venturi, creates the suction that draws brine into the resin tank. Sediment and salt residue clog its tiny ports. A clogged injector leaves the brine tank full of water and the resin never gets recharged. The injector and screen get cleaned or replaced, and the brine line gets checked.

    Urgent Water Softener Repair in Gardner, KS

    Major leaks and total system failure call for a fast response. KRV Plumbing handles urgent softener calls in Gardner, KS ahead of scheduled maintenance. We bypass the unit so the house keeps water, then repair the valve, the tank or the fitting that failed and caused the leak. Bypass first.

    Water Softener Leak Detection and Repair in Gardner, KS

    Cracked tanks, loose fittings and failed O-rings are behind most softener leaks. Even a slow drip wastes water and soaks the floor. We find the source, whether it is the bypass, the valve-to-tank seal or the drain connection, and replace the seal or fitting that failed. We test it under pressure.

    Salt Bridge and Brine Tank Service in Gardner, KS

    The tank looks full but makes no brine. A hard crust has formed and is keeping the salt from dissolving. Bridges get broken up, mushy salt gets cleaned out and the float, brine well and safety valve get checked. In a hot or damp garage, switching to pellets cuts down on bridging.
