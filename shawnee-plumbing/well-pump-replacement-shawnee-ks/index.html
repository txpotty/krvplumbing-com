    Jet Pump Replacement in Shawnee, KS

    Jet pumps sit above ground, in a well house or basement, and pull water up by suction from wells where the water level is within about 25 feet. A two-pipe deep-well jet can reach further down at the cost of efficiency. The common failure is losing prime.     Solar Well Pump Systems in Shawnee, KS

    Solar well pumps make sense for livestock tanks and cabins where running power from the meter would cost more than the pump itself. A few panels feed a DC pump through a controller while the sun is up. You store water, not electricity. Panel count comes from how many gallons you need a day.

    Jet Pump Seal and Motor Replacement in Shawnee, KS
