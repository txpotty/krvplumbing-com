    High-Efficiency Water Softener Installation in Shawnee

    High-efficiency softeners use upflow brining and metered valves to cut salt and water use. Compared with an older timer-based unit, a modern metered softener can use roughly half the salt and noticeably less regeneration water each month. That pays back over time. We install several models.

    Whole-Home Water Softener Installation in Shawnee

    Whole-home softeners treat every gallon that enters the house, right after the main shutoff. Placing it on the main means the water heater, the dishwasher and every fixture get soft water, not just the kitchen. We size before we sell. Your test result and water use decide the tank size.

    Softener Loop Installation in Shawnee

    No loop? A loop is the plumbing that routes the main line through a softener, and many older homes were built without one. We add the loop. Outdoor taps can stay on hard water, which saves salt. Future service gets easier too.

    Reverse Osmosis Drinking Water Systems in Shawnee

    Reverse osmosis pushes water through a fine membrane that removes most dissolved solids, including the sodium a softener adds. Our RO installs go under the sink with their own faucet, and we can tie in the fridge or ice maker too. Pairs well with a softener. Filters get changed on schedule.

    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Installation in Shawnee

    Salt-free conditioners do not soften water, and we say so up front. They change the minerals into crystals that do not stick to pipe walls and heating elements, so scale drops off without adding sodium or using a drain line. Little upkeep is needed. They work best on moderate water.

    Water Hardness Testing and Programming in Shawnee

    Testing is what makes the settings right. Our crew checks hardness, iron and chlorine, then sets the regeneration and salt dosage to match. Iron adds to the setting. We review the settings with you before we leave.

    Water Softener Replacement in Shawnee, KS

    Older softeners lose ground as resin wears out, valve seals leak or injectors clog. While the old system is out, we inspect the loop and the drain, then install a newer metered unit. Metered valves save salt. You will notice soft water the same day.

    Fleck Water Softener Installation in Shawnee, KS

    Fleck valves, especially the 5600SXT and 7000SXT, are on a lot of the softeners we install. Their parts are easy to find and the valves can be rebuilt instead of replaced, so the system lasts. We program yours to your water. We confirm the setup before leaving.
