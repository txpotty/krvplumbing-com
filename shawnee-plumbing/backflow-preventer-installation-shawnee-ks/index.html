    Reduced Pressure Zone (RPZ) Assembly Installation in Shawnee

    Two independent checks and a relief valve make up an RPZ. If the zone between the checks loses its pressure margin, the relief valve opens, dumping water to the floor or a drain so nothing can pass upstream, which is why we never bury one in a pit in Shawnee. We install Watts and Febco units.

    Double Check Valve Assembly Installation in Shawnee

    Double check assemblies work for low-hazard connections such as many fire lines and some irrigation. They have two spring-loaded checks and test cocks for annual certification, and since they do not discharge water, they can go in a vault or below grade where local rules allow, sized so the pressure loss stays small. Flow drives the size.
