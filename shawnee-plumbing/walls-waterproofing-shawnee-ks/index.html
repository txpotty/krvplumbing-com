    Exterior Foundation Waterproofing in Shawnee

    Exterior foundation waterproofing involves excavating around the foundation to apply a waterproof membrane or coating directly to the wall. Basements are common in the eastern part of Kansas, while slab and crawlspace homes are more typical in many other areas.
