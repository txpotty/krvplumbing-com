    Flushometer Urinal Installation in Shawnee

    Manual flushometers are still the most common setup. Manual valves go on a 3/4 inch line with the control stop tuned and the vacuum breaker at the correct height. Handles can go left or right. ADA units need the handle within reach.

    Residential Urinal Installation in Shawnee, KS

    Basement and garage bathrooms are where most home urinals go. A 2 inch drain, a vent tie-in and a supply sized to the valve go in, with blocking or a compact carrier. Many use 0.125 gallons per flush. That barely touches the water bill.

    ADA-Compliant Urinal Installation in Shawnee

    At least one urinal in a public restroom with more than one usually has to be accessible. That means a rim no higher than 17 inches above the floor, an elongated bowl projecting at least 13-1/2 inches from the wall, and a 30 by 48 inch clear floor space in front. The flush handle has to be easy to reach. We mark it all during rough-in.

    Rough-In Plumbing for Urinals in Shawnee, KS

    Rough-in decides whether the finish goes smoothly. We set the carrier, run a 2 inch waste line to the outlet height on the spec sheet, stub the 3/4 inch supply where the flush valve will land, and tie the vent in before anyone hangs drywall. Inspectors see it open. Then the walls close.

    Urinal Brands We Install in Shawnee, KS

    Brand choice is mostly about parts support. American Standard, Kohler, Zurn and Sloan all make reliable urinals, and we steer customers toward models whose flush valves and cartridges are stocked at local supply houses so a repair never waits on shipping. Each of those brands is routine for us. Spec sheets come first.

    Waterless Urinal Installation in Shawnee

    Waterless urinals save the most water and need the most discipline. A cartridge or sealing liquid blocks sewer gas while letting urine through, and it must be replaced on schedule. Your custodial team needs training. We show them how.

    Urinal Permits and Compliance in Shawnee, KS

    New urinals usually need a permit. New fixtures mean rough-in and final inspections, and commercial jobs often need plans. We pull the permit. Like-for-like swaps may not need one.

    Sensor Urinal Installation in Shawnee, KS

    Sensor valves stop missed flushes and double flushes. Battery or hardwired valves get a tuned sensing range and an optional sanitary flush for idle periods. Batteries last a few years. Hardwired units need an electrician first.
