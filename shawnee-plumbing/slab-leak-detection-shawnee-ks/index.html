    Precise Slab Leak Detection Services in Shawnee, KS

    Hot Water Slab Leak Detection in Shawnee

    Hot water lines leak under slabs more often than cold lines because heat speeds up corrosion and causes expansion and contraction. Signs include warm spots on the floor, a water heater that runs constantly and higher gas or electric bills. Thermal imaging shows the heat pattern spreading through tile or concrete. We confirm with a pressure test and acoustic listening before flagging the spot.

    Electronic Acoustic Leak Detection in Shawnee

    Our team uses electronic listening devices to pinpoint the precise location of a slab leak. These highly sensitive microphones can detect the faint sound of water escaping from a pressurized pipe under your foundation. This non-invasive method helps us avoid unnecessary concrete breaking, saving you time and money on repairs. It's especially effective for copper and PEX lines commonly found in Shawnee homes.

    Slab Leak Repair Through Direct Access in Shawnee, KS

    When a single leak is found on otherwise healthy pipe, direct access can be the most economical repair. We cut a small opening in the floor at the marked spot, repair the pipe and pressure test it. Then we patch the concrete and leave the area ready for flooring. Accurate detection keeps that opening as small as possible.

    Post-Tension Slab Leak Detection in Shawnee

    Many newer homes are built on post-tension slabs that use steel cables under high tension. Cutting into one without care is dangerous and can damage the foundation. We locate the cables with scanning equipment before any repair access is opened. This lets us repair the leak without risk or recommend a reroute that avoids the slab entirely.

    Thermal Imaging for Slab Leaks in Shawnee, KS

    Thermal imaging cameras allow us to visualize temperature differences on surfaces, which can indicate a hidden water leak. As water escapes a hot water line, it creates a distinct thermal signature on the concrete slab. This technique is particularly useful for hot water line leaks and helps us narrow down the search area significantly without any physical disruption to your property in Shawnee.

    Cold Water Slab Leak Detection in Shawnee, KS

    Cold water slab leaks do not create warm spots, so they are harder to see. They often show up as a running water meter, damp carpet, a musty smell or water seeping at baseboards. We isolate the cold side and use acoustic equipment to listen for the leak through the slab. Line tracers map the pipe route so we know where to listen.

    Emergency Slab Leak Response in Shawnee

    A slab leak that is flooding floors or causing water to bubble up through tile needs fast attention. We respond quickly, help you shut off the water and isolate the leaking side if possible. That often lets you keep some water service until the repair. Then we locate and fix the leak.

    Pressure Testing & Isolation in Shawnee

    When a leak is suspected, we can perform pressure tests on individual water lines. By isolating sections of your plumbing system and monitoring pressure gauges, we can confirm the presence of a leak and often determine which particular line is compromised. This step-by-step approach is crucial for homes with complex plumbing layouts, ensuring an accurate diagnosis before any excavation in Shawnee, KS.
