    Large Outdoor Grease Interceptor Installation in Shawnee

    For high-volume commercial kitchens in Shawnee, large outdoor grease interceptors are often required by code. These substantial units are installed underground and designed to manage significant amounts of FOG before wastewater enters the municipal sewer system. We handle the entire process, including excavation, precise placement, connection, and final inspection, ensuring a robust and compliant installation.

    Hydromechanical Grease Trap Installation in Shawnee

    Hydromechanical grease traps sit under or near sinks and use baffles and flow control to separate grease from water. They are common in small cafes, coffee shops and quick-service restaurants with limited space. We install units from Zurn, Watts, Schier and Endura sized by gallons per minute. Each install includes a flow control fitting and proper venting to keep the trap working as designed. These units need frequent cleaning, so we place them where staff can reach the lid without trouble.

    Grease Trap Installation for Schools and Institutions in Shawnee

    School cafeterias, hospitals, senior living facilities and churches with kitchens often need commercial interceptors. These sites usually have large dish machines and steam kettles that push high flow. We size interceptors to handle peak lunch or dinner loads without bypassing grease. Our crews schedule work around school breaks or off hours. We also provide documentation for facility managers and inspectors.

    Food Truck Commissary and Ghost Kitchen Traps in Shawnee, KS

    Commissary kitchens and ghost kitchens often have several prep stations and heavy dishwashing loads. We design grease trap installs that handle high flow from multiple sinks and dish machines.     Under-Sink Grease Trap Setup in Shawnee, KS

    Smaller establishments or specific kitchen stations in Shawnee, KS may benefit from compact under-sink grease traps.     Grease Trap Sizing and Placement in Shawnee, KS

    Correct sizing and smart placement are critical for a grease trap's effectiveness. Installing a unit that is too small will lead to frequent clogs and overflow, while an oversized one can be an unnecessary expense. Our plumbers carefully calculate the required capacity based on your kitchen's water flow, fixture count, and operating hours, ensuring optimal performance for your Shawnee, KS business.

    Gravity Grease Interceptor Installation in Shawnee, KS

    Gravity grease interceptors, or GGI units, are larger, typically located outdoors, and rely on the natural difference in specific gravity between FOG and water. These are common for high-volume commercial kitchens. Our team handles the involved excavation, concrete work, and plumbing connections required for proper GGI installation, maintaining compliance with all local Kansas ordinances for waste management.
