    Manifold and Home-Run PEX Layouts in Shawnee, KS

    Think of a manifold as a breaker panel for water. Each fixture gets its own line from a central header, and each line gets its own valve, so you can shut off one toilet without killing water to the kitchen. It uses more pipe. The trade-off is almost no fittings hidden in the walls and steadier pressure when two showers run at once.

    PEX-A, PEX-B, and PEX-C Systems in Shawnee, KS

    There are three grades of PEX, named for how the plastic is cross-linked at the factory. PEX-A is the most flexible and joins with expansion rings, PEX-B is stiffer and joins with crimp rings or cinch clamps, and PEX-C sits closer to B in feel. All three are code-listed for drinking water. We match the pipe to the fittings and tools from one maker so the warranty stays intact.

    PEX-B Crimp and Clamp Systems in Shawnee

    PEX-B is stiffer, is cheaper by the roll, and joins with copper crimp rings or stainless cinch clamps over brass or poly-alloy fittings. A crimp joint is only as good as the tool, so we check every ring with a go/no-go gauge, and a ring that does not pass gets cut out and redone. It is a five-second check. Skipping it causes most crimp leaks.

    PEX Repipe Cost Comparison in Shawnee, KS

    Price on a repipe comes down to a few things: bathroom count, square footage, how many stories, and how easy the attic and walls are to work in. PEX usually beats copper on price because the pipe costs less and a crew finishes in two to four days, not a full week. Patching is sometimes quoted on its own line. Ask and we will price all three, PEX-A, PEX-B and copper, side by side.

    PEX-A vs. PEX-B Installation in Shawnee

    Homeowners ask us which one is better, and it really depends on the house. PEX-A bends tighter, recovers from a kink with a heat gun, and its expansion fittings flow more water, while PEX-B costs less and its crimp joints are just as reliable when they are gauged properly. Both last decades. In a cramped attic, we usually pick PEX-A.

    Copper to PEX Conversions in Shawnee

    Pinhole leaks in copper rarely show up alone. Water chemistry, high flow velocity and contact with soil under a slab can all pit the pipe wall from the inside, and once a few pinholes appear, more are usually on the way. Water across much of Kansas runs hard, leaving mineral scale in water heaters, faucets, showerheads, and dishwashers. A switch to PEX ends it. Where new PEX meets existing copper stubs or valves, we use listed transition fittings.

    Hydronic Heating with PEX in Shawnee

    Radiant floor loops are PEX too, but a different kind you drink from. Heating loops need oxygen-barrier PEX, which has a coating that keeps oxygen from migrating through the wall and rusting the boiler, the pump and any iron in the system. Loop length matters as well. Too long and the far end runs cold.
