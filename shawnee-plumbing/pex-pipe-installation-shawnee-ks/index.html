    PEX-A Expansion Fitting Systems in Shawnee, KS

    PEX-A, such as Uponor, uses an expansion tool to stretch the pipe and a ring over the fitting, which then shrinks back into a secure seal. The fittings have a larger inner diameter, so flow restriction is lower. PEX-A is also more flexible and can be repaired from kinks with a heat gun. It is our favored choice for many full repipes.

    Copper to PEX Conversions in Shawnee

    Copper lines can develop pinhole leaks from water chemistry, high velocity and sometimes soil contact under slabs. Many Kansas homeowners use water softeners to extend the life of their tank water heaters and fixtures against heavy mineral buildup. Once a home has had several pinhole leaks, more usually follow. Converting to PEX stops the cycle and lowers the risk of hidden water damage. We use approved transition fittings wherever new PEX connects to existing copper stubs or valves.

    PEX-B Crimp and Clamp Systems in Shawnee

    PEX-B is stiffer and more affordable, and it is joined with copper crimp rings or stainless steel cinch clamps over brass or poly fittings. It is a good option for smaller projects and budget-conscious repipes. Brands like Viega, Zurn and SharkBite offer reliable PEX-B systems. We use a go/no-go gauge on every crimp to confirm a proper seal.
