    Well Pressure Tank Replacement in Shawnee, KS

    KRV Plumbing replaces well pressure tanks, the steel tank that holds water under air pressure between the pump and your faucets. Inside is a rubber bladder or diaphragm that separates air from water. Once that bladder tears, the tank fills with water, the air cushion disappears, and the pump starts and stops every few seconds. Pumps hate that.

    Preventative Well Pressure Tank Maintenance in Shawnee, KS

    Once a year, test the pre-charge. Shut off the pump, drain the system to zero, and put a tire gauge on the valve at the top of the tank, topping it up to two psi below the cut-in if it reads low. Water out of the valve means a torn bladder.

    Galvanized Steel Pressure Tank Upgrades in Shawnee

    Old galvanized tanks have no bladder at all. Air and water share the same space. Over time the water absorbs the air, the tank waterlogs, and someone has to drain and recharge it every few months. A sealed bladder tank ends that chore.

    Well-Rite Pressure Tank Installation in Shawnee, KS

    Well-Rite tanks use a replaceable diaphragm design in many models. Common sizes are easy for us to get. Like any tank, the key is setting pre-charge before it fills, and checking that drawdown matches the pump. A good tank installed badly fails early.

    Well-X-Trol Pressure Tank Installation in Shawnee, KS

    Amtrol's Well-X-Trol line uses a diaphragm design. It is one of the most common tanks we install. Its stainless or polypropylene water connection keeps water off the steel shell, limiting internal corrosion, and the factory pre-charge still gets checked and adjusted to your switch on install.

    Constant Pressure System Conversion in Shawnee, KS

    Constant pressure systems use a variable speed drive that adjusts pump speed to demand. You get even pressure across fixtures. These systems can run on a smaller tank, but they cost more up front and need a compatible pump and drive. We explain the trade-off.

    Pressure Switch and Gauge Integration in Shawnee, KS

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop. It works with the tank, not apart from it. A switch set at 40/60 needs a tank pre-charged to 38 psi, and a gauge that reads correctly so you can see what the system is doing, and a cheap new gauge beats guessing. Small parts, big role.

    Flex-Lite Pressure Tank Replacement in Shawnee

    Flex-Lite tanks have a composite shell. Rust is not a concern. That makes them a good pick for damp basements, crawlspaces and well houses where steel tanks tend to corrode from the bottom up, and they are lighter to carry into tight spaces. Same plumbing as steel.
