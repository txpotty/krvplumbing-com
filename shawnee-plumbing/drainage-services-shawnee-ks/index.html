    Hydro-Jetting Services in Shawnee

    A hydro jetter sends water at several thousand psi through a nozzle that scours the full pipe wall. Unlike a cable, the jet cleans the whole pipe wall, not just a hole through the clog. Grease lines benefit most. We camera the line first to make sure the pipe is sound enough to jet.

    Sump Pump Services in Shawnee, KS

    A sump pump keeps groundwater out of basements and crawlspaces. Our test covers the float, the check valve and the discharge line. Discharge must go outside. A frozen or buried discharge line is a common reason a working pump still floods the pit.

    Downspout and Roof Drain Solutions in Shawnee

    Gutters concentrate a lot of roof water into a few points, often right next to the foundation. Much of Kansas has expansive clay soil that swells when wet and shrinks in drought, which can move foundations and stress buried pipes. We pipe downspouts underground in solid PVC to a pop-up emitter or dry well well away from the house, with a cleanout at the base so the line can be flushed. One inch of rain on 1,000 square feet of roof is about 600 gallons. That is a lot of water.

    Sewer Line Repair and Replacement in Shawnee, KS

    Sewer laterals fail from roots, settling, or plain age. Older clay and cast iron sewers crack at the joints, which lets roots in. We camera the line, locate the damage from the surface, and price a spot repair, a full replacement, or trenchless lining where the pipe is a good candidate. You pick.

    Jetting for Scale and Grease in Shawnee

    Jetting uses water at high pressure to strip a pipe back to its original diameter. Scale and grease on the pipe wall catch debris, and stripping them off keeps the line clear far longer. Most jetting jobs take two hours.
