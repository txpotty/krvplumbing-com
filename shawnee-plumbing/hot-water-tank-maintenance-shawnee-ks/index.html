    Pressure Relief Valve Testing in Shawnee

    A properly functioning temperature and pressure relief (T&P) valve is a crucial safety device for any hot water tank. Our technicians will test this valve to confirm it's not stuck or leaking, preventing dangerous pressure buildup within the tank. We verify it opens and closes correctly, and if it fails, we will recommend immediate replacement to keep your Shawnee home safe and compliant with plumbing codes.

    Water Heater Expansion Tank Service in Shawnee, KS

    Many modern plumbing systems include an expansion tank connected to the water heater. This tank absorbs excess pressure created during the heating cycle, protecting your hot water tank and other plumbing fixtures. Our maintenance includes checking the expansion tank's air pressure and overall condition. Proper expansion tank function is key to preventing premature wear on your hot water tank and protecting system integrity.

    Gas Burner and Venting Tune-Up in Shawnee, KS

    On gas tanks we clean the burner, inspect the thermocouple or flame sensor and look for soot, rust flakes or scorching around the combustion chamber. We check the draft hood and vent connector for proper slope and secure joints. Dust and lint from garages can clog the flame arrestor screen on newer FVIR models, which causes the pilot to drop out. A clean burner gives a stable blue flame and lower gas bills.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Hot Water Tank Maintenance in Shawnee

    The cost of hot water tank maintenance in Shawnee, KS typically ranges from $100 to $250, depending on the tank type, age and specific services required. This normally includes flushing, anode rod inspection and safety valve checks. We provide upfront pricing before any work begins.

    Private wells serve many rural homes, and minerals, sediment, and nitrates make periodic well testing a good habit. Mineral content adds to sediment buildup in hot water tanks, which reduces efficiency and can damage heating elements or tank linings over time. Regular flushing is particularly important in hard water areas.

    Neglecting maintenance can lead to inefficient operation, higher energy bills, premature tank failure from corrosion or sediment buildup, and potential safety hazards from a malfunctioning pressure relief valve. Regular service prevents these costly and dangerous issues.

    The anode rod, also known as a sacrificial rod, is a metal rod inside your hot water tank designed to corrode before the tank itself. It protects the tank lining from rust and corrosion, which counts most when water carries a lot of minerals, and it lengthens the lifespan of your unit.

    Most manufacturers and plumbing experts recommend annual hot water tank maintenance. This is particularly true where water is hard, since minerals speed up sediment buildup and anode rod depletion.

    KRV Plumbing stands behind our work. While maintenance services usually don't have long-term warranties like new installations, we guarantee our workmanship for a specific period. Any parts replaced during maintenance will carry their manufacturer's warranty.

    Regular, professional maintenance can often be a condition for keeping your manufacturer's warranty valid. Check your warranty documentation, but generally, proof of annual service can help keep your warranty intact.

    Once a year is the baseline for most homes, and more often where water is hard. Homes without a softener or with heavy use may do better with a flush every six months.
