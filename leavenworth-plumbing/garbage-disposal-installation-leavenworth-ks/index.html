    Batch-Feed Disposal Installation in Leavenworth, KS

    A batch-feed disposal will not start until the lid is in, and the lid holds a magnet that closes a switch inside the unit.     Disposal Upgrades in Leavenworth, KS

    Upgrading from an old 1/3 HP unit to something quieter is a common request.     Dishwasher Connection With Disposal Installation in Leavenworth

    New disposals come with a knockout plug in the dishwasher inlet. We knock it out with a screwdriver and hammer, fish the plug out of the chamber and connect the hose with a new clamp.
