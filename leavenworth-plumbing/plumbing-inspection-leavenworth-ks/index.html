    Commercial Plumbing Inspections in Leavenworth, KS

    Commercial buildings have equipment homes do not, so our commercial inspections cover more ground.     Sewer Line Camera Inspections in Leavenworth, KS

    A sewer camera shows the inside of the main drain without digging. The camera goes in at a cleanout and travels to the main or the tank, recording roots, cracks, offsets and bellies along the way. A locator marks the depth. Slab leaks and cracked sewer lines are common results of shifting clay, especially after long dry spells followed by heavy rain.

    Water Heater and Softener Inspection in Leavenworth

    We check the water heater's age by serial number, look for rust, leaks and sediment and test the relief valve. Venting and gas connections get checked. We also look at the expansion tank, drain pan and softener, and a hardness test shows whether the softener is working or just eating salt.

    Water Pressure and Supply Pipe Review in Leavenworth, KS

    We measure static water pressure and check the pressure reducing valve if one is installed. High pressure ages fixtures fast. Anything over 80 psi cuts the life of hoses, fixtures and heaters, and we note the supply pipe type, from copper and CPVC to poly, Kitec and galvanized.

    Residential Plumbing Inspections in Leavenworth

    A full home inspection covers the system from the water meter to the sewer connection. We check every visible pipe for corrosion and leaks, run each fixture for flow and drainage and test the water heater, the main shutoff and the angle stops. Pressure gets measured too. High pressure gets flagged.

    Fixture, Drain and Shutoff Valve Check in Leavenworth, KS

    We run every faucet, shower and toilet and look for slow drains, leaks and weak flow. Shutoffs get tested too. Stops and the main valve tend to seize with age, and gurgling drains can mean a vent problem, both of which are simple fixes once found.

    Water Heater Safety Inspection in Leavenworth

    A water heater stores a lot of hot water under pressure, so safety items matter. We test the T and P valve, confirm its discharge line runs to a safe place, look for an expansion tank where one is needed and check the drain pan. We check gas units for draft. Backdrafting heaters can push combustion gases indoors, which is why we test the vent and report problems before we leave.
