    Frequently Asked Questions About Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Leavenworth

    The cost for sewage ejector pump installation in Leavenworth usually ranges from $2,500 to $5,000. This price can vary depending on the pump type, basin size, complexity of the plumbing and electrical work and whether an existing system needs to be removed. We provide a detailed quote after assessing your project.

    A standard sewage ejector pump installation usually takes one to two days. This includes excavating the pit, setting the basin and pump, connecting the plumbing and completing the electrical hookups. More complex installations, like those requiring extensive trenching or electrical panel upgrades, may take longer.

    A sump pump removes groundwater or seepage, while an ejector pump handles sewage and wastewater from fixtures. Ejector pumps are designed to pass solids and require a sealed, vented basin.

    A sewage ejector pump is designed to handle solid waste and wastewater from plumbing fixtures below the main sewer line, like toilets and sinks, discharging it into the sewer system. A sump pump, in contrast, removes groundwater from a basement or crawl space, only handling clear water to prevent flooding. They serve distinct purposes for different types of water.

    We recommend having your sewage ejector pump inspected annually, especially if it is in a high-use area. Regular inspections can identify potential issues early, extend the life of your pump and prevent unexpected failures and costly backups. This is especially important for older units.

    KRV Plumbing offers a comprehensive warranty on all sewage ejector pump installations, covering both parts and labor. The warranty period can vary based on the pump manufacturer's warranty and our service guarantee. We will provide full details on your coverage before starting any work.

    Most ejector pumps last 7 to 10 years with normal use. Flushing wipes or other non-flushable items can shorten that life significantly.
