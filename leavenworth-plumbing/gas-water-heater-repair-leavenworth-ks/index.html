    What Our Gas Water Heater Repairs Cover in Leavenworth, KS

    Pilot Light and Ignition Service in Leavenworth

    No hot water and a dark pilot window usually point to the ignition side of the heater. The tech pulls the pilot assembly and checks the thermocouple tip, the orifice and the igniter wire. A thermocouple that tests weak on the millivolt meter will not hold the gas valve's magnet open, so the pilot drops out a minute after you relight it by the label. Common pilot kits ride on most of our trucks.

    Gas Control Valve Replacement in Leavenworth, KS

    Honeywell and White-Rodgers valves report faults with a blinking LED, and the flash count matches a chart on the label. We read the code, then test the thermopile and sensors before blaming the valve. Valves do fail. When the control itself is the problem, we replace it with an exact-match valve or a listed universal model, set the temperature and leak test every joint on the gas train before relighting. You get the old valve back if you want it.

    Bradford White and Rheem Gas Water Heater Repair in Leavenworth, KS

    Bradford White and Rheem tanks make up a big share of what we repair in Leavenworth, KS. Rheem gas models mostly use White-Rodgers or Honeywell controls with a status light. Bradford White uses its own sealed combustion design with a lint screen at the base and a thermal switch, and it needs a specific kit when the pilot assembly goes bad. Both kits ride on the truck. Using factory parts keeps your tank warranty valid.

    Tank Leaks & Anode Rods in Leavenworth

    Corrosion starts at the anode rod and works its way into the steel once the rod is spent. Many Kansas homeowners use water softeners to extend the life of their tank water heaters and fixtures against heavy mineral buildup. We pull the anode, check how much magnesium or aluminum is left and replace it if the core wire is showing. A fresh anode, a drained tank and a relief valve that seats properly can add years to a heater in a Leavenworth home that has not started leaking from the shell. Water from the shell seam is another story. At that point the tank has to go.

    Repairing Older Gas Water Heaters in Leavenworth

    Older tanks with standing pilots and no vapor sensor are still out there in Leavenworth, mostly in basements and utility closets. Parts for them are simpler, and a universal thermocouple fits most. We can keep a pre-2003 heater running when the tank is sound, but we also check the draft hood, the flue connector and the gas cock, since those fittings on an old install are often original and corroded. Some old setups lack a sediment trap. One goes in while we are there.

    Burner Cleaning and Repair in Leavenworth, KS

    Yellow flame tips, soot on the burner door or a roaring sound at startup all mean the burner is not getting clean air. We pull the burner, brush the ports and blow out the orifice. We also confirm the orifice is drilled for the fuel you have, because a natural gas orifice on a propane heater, or the reverse, will soot up the flue and push carbon monoxide levels well past normal. A good flame is blue and steady. Rust flakes on the burner tell us to look up the flue.

    Pilot Light Repair in Leavenworth

    The thermocouple is the first thing we test when a pilot will not stay lit. With a millivolt meter, it takes about a minute to see if it is putting out enough. If it reads fine, the next suspects are a clogged pilot orifice, a cracked piezo wire or a thermal cutoff that tripped because the combustion chamber overheated from blocked air. Most of these jobs run about an hour.

    Burner Assembly and Venting Repair in Leavenworth

    Rust scale on the burner tray is a sign the flue baffle or the tank top is shedding. The burner comes out, the ports get cleared and the flame arrestor gets a look. Then the vent gets checked from the draft hood to the roof, because a flue that is loose, sagging or capped by a bird nest can push carbon monoxide back into your Leavenworth home. A smoke pencil shows the draft.
