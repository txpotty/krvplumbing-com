    Which Sump Pump Fits Your Leavenworth, KS Home

    Light Commercial Sump Pump Replacement in Leavenworth, KS

    Light commercial work, think retail basements and small office buildings, usually means a 1/2 HP or larger cast iron pump with a 2-inch discharge and a panel alarm. We replace like for like or upsize. Sewage ejectors and grinder pumps we quote separately.

    Float Switch and Check Valve Replacement in Leavenworth

    Stuck floats and leaking check valves cause more sump failures than dead motors do. A check valve that leaks lets the discharge column fall back into the pit, so the pump runs again for the same water and wears itself out early. New valve every time. Vertical switches suit tight pits, and tethered floats suit wide ones.

    Sewage Ejector Pump Replacement in Leavenworth

    Basement bathrooms below the sewer line drain into a sealed, vented basin, and the ejector pump in that basin has to pass 2-inch solids. It is a different pump entirely. We replace the ejector and its discharge check valve, reseal the lid gasket, and confirm the vent ties in. A bad seal shows up as sewer odor.

    Wayne Sump Pump Replacement in Leavenworth

    Wayne makes everything from a thermoplastic quarter-horse pump at the hardware store to cast iron models with vertical switches, and the cheap ones are what we replace most. The usual failure is a burned-out motor or a float that hangs up on the cord. You get a matching Wayne or a sturdier cast iron model, your call. Figure on a couple of hours.

    High-Capacity Sump Pump Upgrades in Leavenworth

    If your pump runs every 30 seconds during a storm and still loses ground, it is undersized for the water coming in. First we time the pump cycles and measure the pit refill rate. The rest is simple arithmetic. Often a 1/2 or 3/4 HP pump with more GPM at your lift, plus a 2-inch discharge line, solves it.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Replacement in Leavenworth, KS

    Pedestal pumps keep the motor up on a column above the pit, out of the water, which is why a good one can run fifteen years in a dry basement. They are louder, no question. The new column gets set plumb and braced, and we set the float rod stops so the pump turns on and off at the right water levels. Narrow pits, under about 12 inches across, are where they make the most sense.

    Pedestal Pump Service in Leavenworth, KS

    Louder, yes, but a pedestal motor sits high and dry, so in a narrow pit it often outlasts a submersible by several years. Narrow pits favor pedestals. We replace worn units and set the float rod stops for a proper cycle. If your pit is wide enough, a submersible may serve you better.

    Choosing a Backup Battery in Leavenworth

    Battery choice decides how long the backup actually runs. A sealed AGM or deep-cycle marine battery in the 75 to 100 amp-hour range will typically handle several hours of intermittent pumping during an outage. We size it to how often your pump runs on a wet day. Batteries need replacing every three to five years.
