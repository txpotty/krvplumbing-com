    PEX Service Lines for Long Runs in Leavenworth

    We buy PEX in long coils. That makes it the easy pick when the meter sits out at the road and the house is set far back, since a single coil reaches all the way without a splice. Fewer joints underground means fewer places a leak can start, and it is the joints that usually fail first.

    Copper Water Line Installation in Leavenworth

    Type K copper is the thick-wall grade made for burial. It handles high street pressure without complaint. We bed it in sand, keep it away from sharp rock, and sleeve it where it passes through a foundation wall, because concrete and copper rubbing together for twenty years will wear through the pipe. PEX is cheaper.

    Trenchless Water Line Replacement in Leavenworth, KS

    Type L vs. Type K Copper in Leavenworth, KS

    The thickest wall belongs to Type K, the usual choice underground. Type L is thinner and mostly used inside the house. Some codes allow L for burial, but we stick with K for service lines because a thicker wall buys more years against corrosive soil and stray rocks. Same fittings, different pipe.
