    The 'rough-in' measurement is the distance from the finished wall behind the toilet to the center of the drainpipe. Common rough-ins are 10, 12, or 14 inches. It is essential for selecting a new toilet that fits correctly without extensive changes to your bathroom plumbing.

    A like-for-like toilet replacement in the same location generally does not require a permit. If drains are moved or a new bathroom is added, a permit is usually required.
