    Well Pump Efficiency Upgrades in Leavenworth, KS

    Older 1 HP pumps running against a 60 psi switch use more power than most people expect, especially on a well with a long horizontal run to the house. A correctly sized pump, or a variable-speed drive, can cut the run time. The math does not always work. We run the numbers with you.

    Jet Pump Suction Line Repair in Leavenworth, KS

    Foot valves and suction lines cause most jet pump trouble, not the pump. Air sneaks in through a pinhole in the suction line, the pump loses prime and the seal burns up running dry. We pressure test the line. If the well is too deep for a jet pump, we will say so and quote a submersible.

    Grundfos Well Pump Replacement in Leavenworth, KS

    Grundfos SQ and SQE pumps have a built-in soft start and dry-run protection, and they run on two wires with no control box at the surface. They are pricier than a standard pump. Add the CU 301 controller to an SQE and you get constant pressure.

    Sandy Wells and Submersible Pumps in Leavenworth

    Sand kills submersibles early. Grit in a well chews up impellers fast, so we look for it on the old pump and may set the new one higher or add a sand separator. Pump setting depth matters.

    Pulling a Submersible Pump in Leavenworth

    Pulling a submersible means lifting the pump and every length of drop pipe out through the pitless adapter, sometimes hundreds of feet of it. On the way up, we look at the torque arrestor and the splices. Most jobs are done in a day.

    Control Box and Pressure Switch Replacement in Leavenworth, KS

    Not every dry faucet means a dead pump. A pressure switch with pitted contacts or a plugged sensing tube can stop the pump even though nothing is wrong down the well. A new switch runs about $30 in parts. We show you the burned contacts.

    Emergency Well Pump Service in Leavenworth, KS

    Lost water at the house? Call (316) 229-8459 and tell us what the pressure gauge reads. The gauge reading, with the pump running or silent, tells us whether to bring a pump or just a control box. We try to restore water the same day.

    Well Pump Sizing in Leavenworth

    Sizing starts with the well log. We get depth and static level from the well log or a sounder, then a draw-down test shows what the well can really produce in gallons per minute. Pump more than the well yields and it runs dry.
