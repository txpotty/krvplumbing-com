    KRV Plumbing stands behind our work. We offer a warranty on our clogged drain repair services for your peace of mind. Specific warranty details will be provided at the time of service, confirming our commitment to quality and lasting solutions.

    Gurgling sounds, water backing up into a different fixture and frequent clogs in several drains point to a deeper issue. Sewage odors or water around a floor drain are also warning signs. These usually mean the main line needs attention.

    Private wells serve many rural homes, and minerals, sediment, and nitrates make periodic well testing a good habit. Hard water leaves mineral scale that narrows pipe walls and gives grease and hair something to grab. Older homes may also have cast iron pipes that rust and roughen inside.     While chemical drain cleaners are readily available, we generally advise against them. They often contain harsh chemicals that can corrode pipes, especially older ones, and can be harmful to your health and the environment. Professional plumbers use safer, more effective methods.

    To prevent future clogs, avoid pouring grease down kitchen drains, use drain screens to catch hair and food particles, and flush only toilet paper. Regular professional drain maintenance, especially in areas with hard water, can also help prevent mineral buildup.

    Most single fixture clogs are cleared in 30 to 90 minutes. Lines with heavy buildup or limited access can take longer. We tell you if the job will run beyond the usual time.
