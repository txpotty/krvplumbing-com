    Sump Pump and Well Pump Replacement in Leavenworth, KS

    For homes with basements or relying on well water, a working pump is critical. KRV Plumbing replaces faulty sump pumps and well pumps with high-quality, reliable models. We work with brands like Zoeller and Grundfos.

    Water Heater Replacement in Leavenworth

    When your water heater is leaking, not heating or providing too little hot water, it is often more cost-effective to replace it than to repair it, especially if it is over 10 years old. We install standard tank water heaters, sized properly for your home's needs and compliant with current energy efficiency standards. Our team handles the safe removal of your old unit and the precise installation of your new one, connecting gas or electric lines and confirming correct venting and pressure relief valve operation for performance and safety.

    Water Line Replacement in Leavenworth, KS

    A leaking main water line can lead to major water loss, high bills and foundation damage, which is a serious concern for homes on slab foundations. Our water line replacement services address issues like corroded pipes, leaks or low water pressure. We locate the leak, excavate the area if necessary and replace the damaged section or the entire line with durable PEX or copper piping. We make sure of a secure, leak-free connection from the meter to your home, restoring reliable water flow.

    Whole-Home Pipe Replacement in Leavenworth

    Homes with multiple pinhole leaks, galvanized pipe or polybutylene may benefit from a complete repipe. We replace supply lines with PEX or copper, often rerouting through the attic to avoid future slab leaks. Most repipes take two to four days with water restored each evening whenever we can. Drywall openings are patched when the work is complete.

    Pipe Replacement & Whole-Home Repiping in Leavenworth, KS

    Homes in Leavenworth, KS with older galvanized steel or polybutylene pipes often deal with low water pressure, discolored water or frequent leaks. A whole-home repipe with new PEX or copper piping can greatly improve water quality and pressure and eliminate leak concerns. We carefully plan and complete repiping projects, minimizing disruption and making sure all new pipes are properly routed, insulated and connected, providing a durable and efficient plumbing system for decades to come.

    Repair vs Replacement Cost Comparison in Leavenworth, KS

    A common rule of thumb is to replace when a repair costs more than half the price of new equipment or when the item is near the end of its useful life.     Water Softener Replacement in Leavenworth, KS

    Hard water leads to mineral buildup in pipes and appliances. Private wells serve many rural homes, and minerals, sediment, and nitrate levels make periodic well testing a good habit. If your old water softener is no longer effectively removing minerals, or if it is over 15 years old, a replacement will protect your plumbing and improve water quality. We replace all types of water softeners, including salt-based and salt-free systems, sized for your household's water usage and installed correctly on your main water line to provide truly soft water throughout your Leavenworth home.

    Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Leavenworth, KS

    Switch to endless hot water with a tankless water heater replacement. These units heat water on demand, saving energy and space, which is a great benefit for Leavenworth homes. We focus on replacing older tankless units or converting from traditional tank heaters to high-efficiency tankless models from brands like Rinnai and Noritz.
