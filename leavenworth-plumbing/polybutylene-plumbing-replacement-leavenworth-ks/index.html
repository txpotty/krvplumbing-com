    Understanding Polybutylene Failure in Leavenworth

    Polybutylene's inherent weakness stems from its chemical reaction with oxidants like chlorine, commonly used in municipal water treatment. Over time, this reaction causes the pipes and their plastic fittings to become brittle, leading to hairline cracks and eventual rupture. Many Kansas homeowners use water softeners to extend the life of their tank water heaters and fixtures against heavy mineral buildup. Hard water can also add stress to pipe that is already breaking down. Understanding this failure mechanism is important for diagnosing issues and recommending the most effective and permanent replacement strategy for your home.

    Fitting Failure and Emergency Repair in Leavenworth

    Acetal plastic and copper crimp ring fittings are often the first part of a poly system to fail. A burst fitting in an attic or wall can cause major damage in minutes. We respond to emergency leaks, make temporary repairs and then schedule a full replacement. Stopping the problem for good is the only permanent fix.
