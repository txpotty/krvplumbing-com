    Condensing Tankless Repair in Leavenworth, KS

    Condensing tankless heaters, such as many Navien models, extract extra heat from exhaust and produce condensate. Clogged condensate traps, failed neutralizers and blocked drains can shut the unit down. We clean the trap, replace the neutralizer media and verify proper drainage. PVC venting is also checked for slope and sagging.

    Pilot Light and Ignition Repair in Leavenworth, KS

    For gas-fired tankless water heaters, a malfunctioning ignition system can completely stop hot water production. Our team in Leavenworth, KS diagnoses issues with igniters, flame sensors and gas valves. We ensure proper gas pressure and ventilation are maintained for safe and efficient operation. Timely repair of these components prevents dangerous gas problems and restores your unit's ability to heat water on demand.

    Tankless Descaling and Flushing in Leavenworth

    Hard water leaves calcium and magnesium scale inside the heat exchanger, limiting flow and causing overheating. Many units warn of this with scale codes, such as the LC code on Rinnai models. We connect a circulation pump to the service valves and run a descaling solution through the exchanger. Annual descaling is wise for most homes on hard water.

    Recirculation Pump Repair in Leavenworth

    Many tankless systems use internal or external recirculation to deliver hot water faster. Pump failures, check valve problems or wrong schedule settings can cause long waits or lukewarm water. We test the pump, check valves and controls and set the schedule to match your daily routine. A properly tuned system saves both water and gas.
