    Routine Sewer Line Cleaning in Leavenworth

    Routine cleaning clears slow buildup. It narrows the line over time. Our tech runs a cable from the cleanout with a blade sized for the line, then jets the walls if needed, and most older lines do well with cleaning every one to two years.

    Roots in Clay and Cast Iron in Leavenworth

    Older clay and cast iron invite roots. Cracks let them in. We clear root growth with cutters and jetting and talk about lining or repair to stop the next round.

    Residential Sewer Line Cleaning in Leavenworth

    Household laterals collect grease and hair. Both build up slowly. Over time, grease and hair coat the pipe and catch more debris, especially in older cast iron with a rough inner wall, and we clear it with a cable or a jetter sized for the pipe.

    Hydro Jetting for Sewer Lines in Leavenworth, KS

    Jetting cleans the pipe wall. Cables only open a path. High-pressure water strips grease, scale and fine roots off the inside of the line and flushes them toward the street, and with the right settings it is safe for PVC and older clay alike.

    Root-Infested Sewer Line Cleaning in Leavenworth, KS

    Roots are the top cause of blockages in established neighborhoods. Waste catches on them. They enter at clay joints and small cracks, so we cut them out with root saws and finish with jetting to remove the fine hairs left behind, then show each entry point on camera.

    Sewer Camera Inspections in Leavenworth

    Repeat problems call for a camera. Video ends the guessing. We look inside the line to see what is causing the trouble, then choose the cheapest fix that will hold.

    Urgent Sewer Backups in Leavenworth

    Raw sewage in the house is an emergency. We treat it that way. Our technicians respond quickly with the equipment to clear severe blockages that pose a health hazard and threaten property damage, then help with cleanup.

    Jetting Heavy Buildup in Leavenworth

    Some lines need more than a cable. Jetting handles those. Water at up to 4,000 PSI scours grease, scale, mineral deposits and roots off the pipe wall, cleaning the full diameter instead of punching a hole through the blockage.
