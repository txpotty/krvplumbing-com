    If your toilet isn't flushing properly, it could be due to issues with the flush chain, handle, flapper, or fill valve. Our plumbers are adept at diagnosing these internal tank problems, replacing worn or broken components like flush valves, fill valves, and toilet handles to restore full and effective flushing power.

    Toilet Gasket and Seal Replacement in Leavenworth, KS

    The gaskets and seals within your toilet tank and at its base are essential for preventing leaks. Over time, these rubber components can degrade, crack, or become brittle due to age and exposure to water, especially where the water is hard. We specialize in replacing worn-out flapper seals, tank-to-bowl gaskets, and the wax ring at the toilet's base, which is essential for preventing leaks onto your bathroom floor and maintaining a watertight seal.

    Weak Flush and Clog Repair in Leavenworth, KS

Some low-flow toilets from the 1990s simply flush poorly, and we will tell you honestly if replacement is the better fix.
