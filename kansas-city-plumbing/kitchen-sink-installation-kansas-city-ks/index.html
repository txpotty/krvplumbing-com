    Top-mount Sink Installation in Kansas City

    Top-mount, or drop-in, sinks are a common and straightforward option for many Kansas homes. While simpler to install, proper sealing and alignment are still crucial to prevent water damage and ensure stability. Our plumbers in Kansas City efficiently install top-mount sinks, applying durable sealant around the rim to create a watertight barrier. We make sure the sink is securely fastened and all plumbing connections, including the faucet and drain, are properly tightened and tested for leaks.

    Drop-In Kitchen Sink Installation in Kansas City

    Drop-in sinks rest on the countertop with a rim, making them a good choice for laminate and tile. We check the cutout size, seal the rim, and tighten mounting clips evenly. This is often the fastest and most affordable kitchen sink upgrade. It also works well in rentals and older homes.

    Farmhouse Sink Installation in Kansas City, KS
