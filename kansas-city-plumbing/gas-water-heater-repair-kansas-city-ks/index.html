    Power Vent Water Heater Repair in Kansas City

    Power vent heaters use a blower to push exhaust out through PVC pipe. Failures often involve the blower motor, pressure switch, hot surface igniter or condensate in the vent. We read the error codes, test each part and repair the failure. Correct vent slope and termination are verified before we leave.

    Restoring Hot Water for Older Homes in Kansas City

    Many homes in Kansas City feature older gas water heater models that require specific knowledge for repair. Our technicians are familiar with the common issues and unique parts associated with these systems. We can troubleshoot and repair older units, often extending their lifespan, rather than pushing for instant replacement. We focus on cost-effective repairs that respect the character of your home.

    Pilot Light Repair in Kansas City

    A pilot that will not light or will not stay lit is the most frequent gas water heater call. Causes include a worn thermocouple, a clogged pilot orifice, a bad piezo igniter or a tripped thermal cutoff. We test the thermocouple output with a meter and clean or replace the pilot assembly. Most pilot repairs are finished in about an hour.

    Burner Assembly and Venting System Repair in Kansas City

    A worn burner assembly can result in insufficient heating or unusual noises. We closely inspect the burner for blockages, corrosion or wear. We also check the venting system for proper draft and obstructions, which is essential for safe operation and preventing carbon monoxide buildup in your Kansas City home. Proper venting is a critical safety consideration.

    Water Heater Tank Leaks and Pressure Issues in Kansas City, KS

    While gas water heaters are sturdy, tank leaks or pressure problems can occur. We can often fix small fitting leaks or replace relief valves if pressure is too high. If the tank itself is failing due to rust or old age, a replacement might be the more sensible long-term solution. We will provide an honest assessment of the repair options for your Kansas City, KS home.
