    Main Water Line Replacement in Kansas City

    Over time, main water lines can corrode, crack or leak because of shifting soil, tree root intrusion or age. KRV Plumbing focuses on replacing old, failing main water lines, often upgrading from galvanized steel or polybutylene to more durable PEX or copper.     PEX and HDPE Water Line Installation in Kansas City, KS

    PEX and HDPE are flexible, which means fewer fittings in the ground and better tolerance for soil movement. HDPE is especially durable for long runs and holds up against cracking in expansive clay. We use approved compression or fusion fittings and add tracer wire so the line can be found with a locator later. PEX must stay out of sunlight before burial, so we store and lay it carefully on every job site.

    Commercial Water Service Installation in Kansas City

    Restaurants, retail suites and small commercial buildings need service lines sized for higher demand and often require backflow protection at the meter. We install larger diameter copper or HDPE lines and coordinate with your water utility on tap and meter requirements. Our plumbers schedule work to minimize downtime for tenants and customers. Inspection and backflow test reports are handled before the line goes into service.

    Upsizing Water Lines for Better Pressure in Kansas City, KS

    Many homes built decades ago have a 3/4 inch line that cannot keep up with modern fixture loads, irrigation and pools. If your pressure drops when two showers run or the sprinklers are on, an undersized line may be the cause.
