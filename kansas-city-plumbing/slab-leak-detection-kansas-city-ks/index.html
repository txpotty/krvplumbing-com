    Accurate Slab Leak Detection Services in Kansas City, KS

    Slab Leak Repair Through Direct Access in Kansas City, KS

    When a single leak is found on otherwise healthy pipe, direct access can be the most affordable repair. We cut a small opening in the floor at the marked spot, repair the pipe and pressure test it. Then we patch the concrete and leave the area ready for flooring. Accurate detection keeps that opening as small as possible.

    Repiping After Multiple Slab Leaks in Kansas City, KS

    If your home has had two or more slab leaks, the remaining pipe is likely corroded too. Repiping replaces all supply lines with new PEX or copper run through the walls and attic. It is a bigger project, but it ends the cycle of recurring slab leaks. We can usually complete a full repipe in two to four days.

    Cold Water Slab Leak Detection in Kansas City, KS

    Cold water slab leaks do not create warm spots, so they are harder to see. They often show up as a running water meter, damp carpet, a musty smell or water seeping at baseboards. We isolate the cold side and use acoustic equipment to listen for the leak through the slab. Line tracers map the pipe route so we know exactly where to listen.

    Emergency Slab Leak Detection in Kansas City

    A sudden drop in water pressure, visible dampness or the sound of running water can indicate an emergency slab leak. KRV Plumbing offers emergency detection services in Kansas City to address urgent situations quickly. We prioritize rapid response to limit water damage and prevent further structural compromise to your property.

    Post-Tension Slab Leak Detection in Kansas City

    Many newer homes are built on post-tension slabs that use steel cables under high tension. Cutting into one without care is dangerous and can damage the foundation. We locate the cables with scanning equipment before any repair access is opened. This lets us repair the leak without risk or recommend a reroute that avoids the slab entirely.

    Residential Slab Leak Detection in Kansas City

    We provide complete slab leak detection services tailored for residential properties throughout Kansas City.     Video Pipe Inspection for Sewer Leaks in Kansas City, KS

    Sewer line issues under a slab can also look like slab leak symptoms. We use high-resolution video cameras to inspect the interior of your drain lines, identifying cracks, blockages or separations. This is important if the issue isn't a pressurized water line, helping us determine if a sewer line repair is needed instead for your Kansas City home.

    Hot Water Slab Leak Detection in Kansas City

    Hot water lines leak under slabs more often than cold lines because heat speeds up corrosion and causes expansion and contraction. Signs include warm spots on the floor, a water heater that runs constantly and higher gas or electric bills. Thermal imaging shows the heat pattern spreading through tile or concrete. We confirm with a pressure test and acoustic listening before marking the spot.

    We stand by the accuracy of our slab leak detection services. While detection itself is a diagnostic service, we make sure our findings are precise. Any subsequent repairs performed by KRV Plumbing come with our standard labor and parts warranties, which will be clearly outlined for you.

    A water line slab leak involves pressurized fresh water escaping from supply pipes, often resulting in higher water bills, warm spots or the sound of running water. A sewer line slab leak involves wastewater leaking from drain pipes, which typically causes foul odors, slow drains or visible sewage seepage, and is not usually pressurized.

    KRV Plumbing relies on industry-leading brands for our slab leak detection equipment, including but not limited to LeakTronics, FLIR thermal cameras and specialized acoustic sensors. These tools are chosen for their accuracy and reliability in various leak detection scenarios, delivering the best results for your home.

    Common signs of a slab leak in Kansas homes include unexplained increases in water bills, warm spots on your floor, the sound of running water when no fixtures are in use, damp or mildewy smells and visible cracks in your foundation or walls. Pooling water outside your home near the foundation can also be a clear indicator.

    Once the slab leak is precisely detected, our technician will provide you with a detailed report and discuss the best repair options. These options may include trenchless repair, re-routing the affected pipe or traditional slab excavation and repair. We will then provide a clear estimate for the repair work.

    Direct repair works well for a single leak on otherwise healthy pipe in an accessible spot. Rerouting is often better under tile or hardwood and avoids future leaks on that line. We help you weigh both.
