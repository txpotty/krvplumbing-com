    What Our Commercial Plumbers Do in Kansas City, KS

    Commercial Leak Detection in Kansas City, KS

    Water bills that jump 30 percent with no change in business usually mean a hidden leak or a stuck fixture. Start with the meter. We shut fixtures off zone by zone and watch the meter, then use acoustic sensors, thermal cameras and tracer gas to find leaks in walls, ceilings and buried service lines. Running toilets count too. One stuck flush valve can waste several thousand gallons a week.

    Commercial Restroom Fixture Repair in Kansas City, KS

    Restroom calls are the most common commercial work we do. A flushometer that runs, a urinal that will not stop, or a sensor faucet that drips are usually fixed with a diaphragm kit, a solenoid or a new battery pack. We stock those. Clogged wall-hung toilets sometimes need the carrier checked, because a loose bowl on a carrier can leak behind the wall. Weak flow at one sink often comes down to a plugged stop valve.

    Grease Trap Installation in Kansas City

    A new restaurant or a kitchen expansion usually needs a grease interceptor sized from the fixtures that drain to it. Under-sink traps work for small kitchens, while larger operations need in-ground interceptors of 500 gallons or more. Sizing is set by code. We size the unit from the equipment schedule, set it at the right grade and vent it properly so it passes inspection the first time.

    Multi-Family and Property Management Plumbing in Kansas City

    Apartment complexes need a plumber who can handle many units and quick turns between tenants. Our work in multi-family buildings includes leak repair, heater swaps, stack clearing and full repipes, with posted notices before any shutoff. Pricing stays consistent by unit. Each invoice shows the unit number so it can be charged to the right ledger.

    Backflow Prevention Assembly Service in Kansas City

    A backflow assembly keeps water from your building, including irrigation lines, boiler water and chemical feeds, from flowing back into the public supply. Most providers want a yearly test. We test RPZ, double check and PVB assemblies with calibrated gauges and send the results straight to the water provider. A failed test usually means new rubber and springs, not a whole new assembly. We carry Watts, Wilkins and Febco kits for that reason.

    Commercial Drain Cleaning and Hydro-Jetting in Kansas City

    Commercial drains carry grease, paper towels and food waste in volumes no house ever sees. A cable just pokes a hole. We use high-pressure jetting with rotating nozzles that scrub grease off the whole circumference of 4-inch and 6-inch lines, not just the bottom. Jetting keeps the line open for months instead of days. Restaurants, schools and apartment stacks are where we see the biggest difference.

    Backflow Testing and Certification in Kansas City

    Each backflow test takes about 30 minutes per assembly and requires a short water shutoff to that line. Plan for a brief outage. Our tester opens each test cock in order, records the check valve and relief valve readings on a calibrated gauge and compares them against the pass limits printed on the test form. We tag every passing device. The provider gets the report from us, and we keep a copy for your next test.

    Emergency Commercial Plumbing in Kansas City

    A burst supply line above a drop ceiling can soak inventory and ceiling tile in under an hour. Shut the main first. Our dispatcher will walk your staff to the shutoff over the phone if needed, then send a plumber who can isolate the branch, restore water to the rest of the building and make the permanent repair. Sewage backups get the same response. After clearing the line we run a camera so you know whether it is a one-time clog or damaged pipe.
