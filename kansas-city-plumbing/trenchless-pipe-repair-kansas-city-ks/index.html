    Trenchless vs Traditional Costs in Kansas City, KS

    On paper, the two methods can price out close. Then add restoration. A trench under a driveway or through an established garden means paying to replace concrete, plants and irrigation after the plumbing is done, and trenchless avoids most of those costs. We price both side by side.

    Cured-in-Place Pipe (CIPP) Lining in Kansas City

    A CIPP liner is a felt tube soaked in resin. It hardens inside the old pipe. We pull or invert the tube into the cleaned line, inflate it against the pipe wall and cure it with hot water, steam or UV light, which leaves a jointless pipe inside the old one. It fixes cracks, breaks and roots without digging up your Kansas City yard.

    Pipe Bursting in Kansas City, KS

    Bursting is used when the old pipe is too damaged to line or needs a larger diameter. You'll see an entry and exit pit. The head fractures the old pipe as it travels, with new HDPE trailing behind, fused into one length with no joints.

    Pipe Bursting Trenchless Repair in Kansas City, KS

    Bursting works when the pipe has collapsed or is too broken to line. It also upsizes lines. Our crew sets an entry pit and an exit pit, then pulls a bursting head and new HDPE through on a hydraulic winch, which replaces the full run in a day or two for most homes. KRV Plumbing joins the new pipe by heat fusion.
