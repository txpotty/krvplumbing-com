    Gas Appliance Line Installation in Kansas City, KS

    A new range, dryer or water heater needs a gas line sized for its BTU rating and the length of the run. Undersized lines starve burners. We install the branch line, add an accessible shutoff valve and a sediment trap where the code calls for one, then connect the appliance with a new coated flexible connector. If the existing appliance line is leaking or damaged, we fix it on the same visit.

    Buried Yard Line Repair and Replacement in Kansas City, KS

    Many older homes have steel gas lines running from the meter to the house or out to a pool heater. The wrap fails eventually. Once the coating cracks, the pipe starts to corrode, and wet soil from sprinklers or a low spot in the yard speeds that up a great deal. Utilities get marked first. The new line is polyethylene with tracer wire, or coated steel where it has to be exposed, and we backfill and clean up the yard when the test passes.

    Black Iron Pipe Repair in Kansas City, KS

    Black iron is the standard interior gas pipe in a lot of homes, and it lasts a long time indoors. Joints are the weak point. Vibration and settling loosen threaded joints over time, and pipe in damp garages or outside walls can rust through from the surface. Bad sections get replaced with new pipe. Rusted pipe that runs outside gets painted or replaced with coated steel so it does not come back as the same problem.

    Gas Shut Off by the Utility in Kansas City

    When your utility shuts off gas for a leak, you may be without hot water, heat or cooking until a licensed plumber fixes it. Our plumber finds and fixes the leak, then runs the test your gas company requires before unlocking the meter. The truck carries what we need. We stay until the system holds its test pressure and the meter is ready to be unlocked.

    Gas Line Extensions and Relocations in Kansas City

    Adding a gas fireplace, moving a laundry room or switching a range from electric to gas usually means extending the gas line. Sizing comes first. Our plumbers total the BTU load, measure the run and size the pipe so the last appliance on the line still gets full pressure at peak use. After that comes routing, the tie-in and the test.

    Interior Black Iron Pipe Repair in Kansas City

    Threaded black iron pipe can leak at joints after years of vibration or settling. Our plumbers reseal or replace the leaking fittings and sections using new pipe and approved sealant. Rusted pipe goes too. Heavily rusted pipe in garages, crawlspaces or outside walls is replaced before it fails, and we pressure test the full system afterward.

    Corroded Gas Pipe Replacement in Kansas City

    Buried steel gas line corrodes once its coating cracks, and moisture, roots and ground movement speed it up. Slab leaks and broken sewer laterals are common results of shifting clay, especially after long dry spells followed by heavy rain. Rusted buried steel gets replaced with polyethylene and tracer wire, and we use a listed transition riser where it rises to the house. Exposed sections get coated steel. We test before backfilling so any problem joint is fixed while the trench is still open.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Gas Line Repair in Kansas City

    A small fitting repair in Kansas City can be around $250. Big jobs cost more. Trenching a long yard line or cutting through concrete can push the price to $5,000 or more, depending on length and access. You get the number in writing first.

    Gas piping should be repaired by a licensed professional. A bad repair risks fire or CO exposure and may fail inspection.

    Utilities lock the meter when they find a leak or a failed test. Service stays off until a licensed plumber fixes it and the system passes a test.

    We work on black iron, coated steel, CSST such as Gastite and TracPipe, and polyethylene for buried lines. Each material has its place. Polyethylene can only run underground and needs a listed riser where it comes up, while CSST needs bonding and black iron needs proper support and gas-rated thread sealant.

    CSST is approved and widely used when installed and bonded correctly. Bonding gets checked, and damaged runs get the maker's fittings.

    There is no required yearly inspection for most homes. Older homes benefit from a check every few years. It is worth a check if your home has old uncoated brass connectors, buried steel yard lines from decades ago or appliances that are burning with yellow or lazy flames, since those are common warning signs.

    Tightening a union or swapping a connector takes an hour or two. Buried lines take longer. Replacing a long section of buried line usually takes most of a day for digging, laying pipe, testing and backfill, plus whatever time the inspection needs.

    Our gas line repairs come with a written warranty on parts and labor, usually one year. Coverage varies by repair. The exact terms are listed on your estimate before work starts, and any new connectors, valves or CSST fittings also carry their manufacturer warranties.
