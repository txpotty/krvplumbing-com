    Scale Removal in Cast Iron Pipes in Kansas City

    Cast iron grows rust tubercles. Scale adds to them. Jetting with the right nozzle can strip much of this buildup and restore flow, while we keep the pressure in a safe range for aged iron and watch the camera for thin spots, which is also the standard prep before pipe lining.

    Hydro Jetting vs. Snaking Cost Comparison in Kansas City, KS

    Snaking costs less up front. Jetting usually holds up better. A cable punches through the blockage and leaves residue on the walls, which brings the clog back, while jetting cleans the whole pipe and cuts down the number of service calls over time.

    Residential Hydro Jetting in Kansas City

    Household lines collect a mix of buildup. Kitchen grease and bathroom soap scum both pile up. Adjustable pressure and the right nozzle let us jet small kitchen branches and full mains alike, safely and without spraying back into the house.

    Emergency Hydro Jetting Services in Kansas City, KS

    A full sewer backup needs fast help. We bring the jetter to you. The emergency crew arrives with jetting equipment and a camera, clears the blockage and restores flow, so water stops coming up through the lowest drain and the damage stops spreading.

    Kitchen Line Grease Jetting in Kansas City, KS

    Grease clings and hardens. Flat runs are the worst. We use smaller hose and nozzles for kitchen branches, which scrub the grease off the walls rather than tunneling through it, so your sink and dishwasher drain properly for much longer.

    Tree Root Removal with Hydro Jetting in Kansas City

    Mature trees near old sewers cause trouble. They enter at joints and cracks. A root-cutting nozzle uses a powerful spinning stream to cut and flush out the roots, removing more of the fine growth than a cable and slowing how fast it grows back into the line.
