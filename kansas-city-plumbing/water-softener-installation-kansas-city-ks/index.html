    Salt-Based Water Softener Installation in Kansas City

    Salt-based (ion exchange) water softeners are the most common and effective solution for fully softening water. These systems use a resin bed to exchange hard minerals like calcium and magnesium with sodium ions. KRV Plumbing carefully installs many models of salt-based softeners, ensuring proper connection to your main water line, correct drainage setup and accurate programming for regeneration cycles in your Kansas City home.
