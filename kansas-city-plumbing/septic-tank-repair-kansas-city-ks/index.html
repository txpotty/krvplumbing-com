    Inlet and Outlet Baffle Replacement in Kansas City, KS

    Baffles hold back the scum layer on top and the sludge on the bottom so only clear effluent leaves the tank.     Effluent pumps in a dosing chamber last roughly 8 to 15 years, and they usually quit without much warning at all. A pump that hums but moves no water has a stuck impeller or a bad capacitor. We pull it and test the discharge line before setting a new one. Floats get replaced at the same time.
