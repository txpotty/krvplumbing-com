    Septic Pump and Alarm System Repair in Kansas City

    Many modern septic systems, especially those on sloped properties or with advanced treatment units, rely on a working septic pump and alarm. When your pump fails or the alarm sounds, it signals a serious issue. Our team can repair or replace faulty pumps, floats and electrical components so your system reliably moves wastewater to the drain field. We service all major pump brands for homes in Kansas City.

    Commercial Septic System Repair in Kansas City, KS

    Commercial properties often have larger, more complex septic systems that demand specific repair knowledge. KRV Plumbing provides complete septic tank repair for businesses throughout Kansas City, KS, understanding the challenges of high-volume usage and diverse waste streams. We ensure your commercial septic system operates efficiently, preventing costly downtime and maintaining compliance with health regulations.

    Septic Baffle and Tee Repair in Kansas City

    Baffles and tees keep scum and solids in the tank so they do not flow out into the leach field. Concrete baffles crumble from hydrogen sulfide gas, and plastic tees can break off. When they fail, solids clog the field fast. We replace damaged baffles with durable PVC tees and add an effluent filter where appropriate.

    Emergency Septic Repair in Kansas City

    Sewage backing up into tubs or floor drains is a health hazard that needs prompt attention. We respond to emergency septic calls, find the blockage and restore flow. That might mean clearing a line, replacing a failed pump or arranging pumping if the tank is full. We then explain what caused the backup and how to avoid it.

    Alternative Septic System Repair in Kansas City, KS

    Some properties use aerobic treatment units or other alternative systems because of soil or lot size limits. These systems have air pumps, timers and alarms that need regular service. We repair aerators, replace diffusers and troubleshoot control panels. We also help keep maintenance records required by your permit.

    Drain Field Clog Remediation in Kansas City

    A clogged drain field is a common cause of septic system backups and can be a significant issue for homeowners in Kansas City. This can be caused by excessive solids, tree root intrusion or soil compaction. We offer professional drain field remediation, which may include hydro-jetting the distribution lines, addressing root infiltration or assessing the soil's percolation capacity to restore proper effluent absorption and prevent future saturation.

    Septic Repair Cost Comparison in Kansas City, KS

    Baffle or tee replacement often costs $300 to $900, and effluent pump replacement usually runs $900 to $2,000. Inlet line repairs range from $800 to $3,000 depending on depth and length. Leach field restoration or replacement can cost $5,000 to $20,000 or more. We help you choose the option that fixes the problem at the lowest long-term cost.

    Septic Tank Leak Repair in Kansas City

    Septic tank leaks can lead to foul odors, soggy spots in your yard and potential groundwater contamination. Our technicians use dye testing and camera inspections to locate the source of the leak, whether it is a crack in the tank or a failed pipe connection. We provide durable repair solutions using specialized sealants and patching materials designed for the harsh environment of a septic tank in Kansas City, creating a watertight seal and preventing future issues.
