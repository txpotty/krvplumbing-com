    Root Removal Jetting in Kansas City

    Shade trees and shrubs send roots toward small cracks in clay and cast iron pipe. Root-cutting nozzles clear them. Jetting does not stop roots from returning, because the crack is still there, but it clears the pipe completely and lets us see on camera whether a spot repair or a liner would stop the regrowth.

    Scheduled Jetting Maintenance in Kansas City

    Properties with chronic drain issues benefit from scheduled jetting. Commercial kitchens often jet quarterly. Homes with root-prone sewers usually jet every year or two, and we keep a camera record from each visit so you can see whether the roots are coming back slower, faster or the same.

    Sewer Lateral Jetting in Kansas City, KS

    The lateral from your house to the street carries everything, so buildup there causes backups everywhere at once. The whole lateral gets jetted to the tap. Settled low spots get extra passes, and we mark them on the video for future reference.

    Hard Water Scale Removal in Kansas City, KS

    Scale builds slowly inside drain pipe, narrowing it a little more each year. Many Kansas homeowners use water softeners to protect their tank water heaters and fixtures against heavy mineral buildup. A jetter with a scouring nozzle strips that scale from the walls, and on heavily scaled cast iron we may follow up with a descaling chain head to finish the job. The difference shows on camera.

    After-Hours Hydrojet Service in Kansas City

    Backups at night or on weekends get the same equipment as weekday calls. Our on-call trucks carry jetters. We can clear and jet a line in one visit instead of returning Monday.

    Emergency Hydrojet Services in Kansas City, KS

    A full sewer backup cannot wait for a weekday slot. We clear it first. A cable opens it, then the jetter removes the grease, roots or wipes that caused it.

    Hydrojetting vs. Snaking in Kansas City, KS

    Snaking pokes a hole through a clog. Jetting cleans the whole pipe. Snaking suits a one-time clog, while jetting fixes a line that clogs repeatedly.

    Same-Day Jetting for Backups in Kansas City
