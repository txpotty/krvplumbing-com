    Gas Line Installation in Kansas City, KS

    PEX Pipe Installation and Repiping in Kansas City

    Pinhole leaks in copper keep coming back once the pipe walls start to thin. A PEX repipe ends that cycle. We run either a manifold with home runs to each fixture, which balances pressure, or a trunk-and-branch layout when the walls make home runs impractical.     PEX Repiping in Kansas City

    Copper repipes cost more and take longer, which is why most whole-house repipes today use PEX-A or PEX-B tubing with brass or poly fittings. It runs quieter than copper, with less water hammer banging in the walls. Freeze damage is less likely too. Hard freezes happen every winter in Kansas, and pipes in exterior walls, garages, and crawlspaces are the most at risk. Fittings are matched to the tubing manufacturer.

    Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Kansas City, KS

    A basement bathroom sits below the sewer line, so gravity cannot carry the waste out. The fix is a sealed basin with an ejector pump that lifts waste to the main. We size the pump and basin to the fixtures it serves, vent the basin to code, and put a check valve and a full-port shutoff on the discharge so the pump can be pulled for service. We usually install Liberty or Zoeller pumps.
