    Well Pump & Pressure Tank Replacement in Kansas City

    For rural properties in and around Kansas City that rely on well water, a failing well pump or pressure tank can leave you with no water at all. We diagnose issues with your current well system and provide professional replacement for submersible or jet pumps. We also replace faulty pressure tanks, so your well system maintains consistent water pressure and your pump lasts longer. Proper sizing and installation are crucial for efficient operation and a reliable water supply.

    Sump Pump and Well Pump Replacement in Kansas City, KS

    For homes with basements or relying on well water, a working pump is essential. KRV Plumbing replaces faulty sump pumps and well pumps with high-quality, dependable models. We make sure of correct sizing and installation for effective water removal and consistent water pressure, protecting your Kansas City home from flooding and keeping a steady water supply.     Water Softener Replacement in Kansas City, KS

    Hard water leads to mineral buildup in pipes and appliances. Water across much of Kansas runs quite hard, leaving mineral scale in water heaters, faucets, showerheads, and dishwashers. If your old water softener is no longer properly removing minerals, or if it is over 15 years old, a replacement will protect your plumbing and improve water quality. We replace all types of water softeners, including salt-based and salt-free systems, sized for your household's water usage and installed correctly on your main water line to provide consistently soft water throughout your Kansas City home.

    Water Line Replacement in Kansas City, KS

    A leaking main water line can lead to significant water loss, high bills and foundation damage, which is a serious concern for homes on slab foundations. Our water line replacement services address issues like corroded pipes, leaks or low water pressure. We locate the leak, excavate the area if necessary and replace the damaged section or the entire line with durable PEX or copper piping. We make sure of a secure, leak-free connection from the meter to your home, restoring reliable water flow.

    Well Pressure Tank Replacement in Kansas City

    If your well pump is cycling too frequently or you have inconsistent water pressure, your pressure tank might need replacing. KRV Plumbing installs new well pressure tanks, so your well system operates efficiently and your well pump lasts longer. We correctly size the tank to your system in Kansas City, improving water delivery and energy usage.

    Whole-Home Pipe Replacement in Kansas City

    Homes with multiple pinhole leaks, galvanized pipe or polybutylene may benefit from a complete repipe. We replace supply lines with PEX or copper, often rerouting through the attic to avoid future slab leaks. Most repipes take two to four days with water restored each evening whenever we can. Drywall openings are patched when the work is finished.

    Sewer Line Replacement in Kansas City

    Persistent sewer backups, foul odors or lush spots in your yard can signal a failing sewer line. Much of Kansas has expansive clay soil that swells when wet and shrinks in drought, which can shift foundations and stress buried pipes. We offer comprehensive sewer line replacement using trenchless technology whenever possible, limiting disruption to your landscaping. For more severe damage, traditional excavation is performed with careful attention. We make sure new lines are properly graded, connected and compliant with local Kansas City plumbing codes, preventing future blockages and costly repairs.

    Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Kansas City, KS

    Switch to endless hot water with a tankless water heater replacement. These units heat water on demand, saving energy and space, which is a great benefit for Kansas City homes. We focus on replacing older tankless units or converting from traditional tank heaters to high-efficiency tankless models from brands like Rinnai and Noritz. Our service includes assessing your home's gas line capacity or electrical service, ensuring proper venting and precise installation to maximize efficiency and longevity.
