    Fill Valve Replacement in Kansas City

    A noisy, slow or hissing tank often points to a worn fill valve. Mineral scale clogs the small ports inside, which causes long refill times and phantom refills in the night. We replace old ballcock valves with modern adjustable fill valves from Fluidmaster or Korky and set the water level to the manufacturer mark. The job takes about 20 minutes and makes an older toilet noticeably quieter.

    Clogged Toilet Clearing in Kansas City, KS

    Tough toilet clogs are a frequent problem that can bring your bathroom to a halt. Our technicians use professional-grade augers and sometimes even camera inspections to locate and remove obstructions effectively. We handle everything from foreign objects to excessive toilet paper, ensuring your toilet drains freely again.

    Leaking Toilet Base Repair in Kansas City, KS

    Water around the base typically means the wax ring has failed or the toilet has been rocking on a loose flange.     Leaking Toilet Repair in Kansas City

    Toilet leaks can cause serious water damage to your bathroom floor, subfloor, and even the ceiling below. Leaks can originate from the base of the toilet, the tank, or the supply line. Common culprits include a worn-out wax ring, loose bolts, or cracked porcelain. We thoroughly inspect all potential leak points, replacing seals, tightening connections, or recommending bowl replacement if the porcelain is damaged, preventing further water intrusion.
