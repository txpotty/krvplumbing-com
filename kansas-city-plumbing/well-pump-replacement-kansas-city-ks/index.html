    Well Pump Sizing in Kansas City

    Sizing starts with the well log. The log, or a sounder down the casing, gives us static water level and well depth, and a draw-down test tells us roughly how many gallons per minute the well can actually give. Pumps bigger than the well can supply run dry.

    Constant Pressure Well Pump Installation in Kansas City, KS

    Constant pressure systems hold the house at one pressure, say 50 psi, whether one faucet is open or the shower and the irrigation are running together. A variable frequency drive speeds the pump up and slows it down to match demand. No more pressure dip mid-shower.

    Jet Pump Seal and Motor Replacement in Kansas City, KS

    Single-stage and two-stage jet pumps wear out at the seal and impeller, and a leaking shaft seal shows up as a puddle under the motor. We replace the pump and prime it properly. Then we watch the gauge through a full cycle.

    Sandy Wells and Submersible Pumps in Kansas City

    Sand kills submersibles early. Grit in a well chews up impellers fast, so we look for it on the old pump and may set the new one higher or add a sand separator. Where the pump hangs matters.

    Agricultural and Ranch Well Pumps in Kansas City

    Irrigation and stock wells often run 3 to 10 HP pumps, sometimes on three-phase power with a motor starter and overload protection at the panel. We replace those pumps and the starters that protect them. We schedule around watering times.
