    Tunneling Slab Leak Repair in Kansas City, KS

    In some cases, especially when the leak is under a load-bearing wall or in a difficult-to-access area, tunneling beneath the foundation is the preferred method. This allows our plumbers to reach the damaged pipe from outside your home, avoiding disruption to your interior floors and finishes. Tunneling spares the interior but requires skilled excavation and shoring to protect structural integrity throughout the repair process in Kansas City, KS.

    Pipe Rerouting and Repiping in Kansas City

    For more extensive damage, multiple leaks, or pipes that are too old and corroded to repair reliably, pipe rerouting is a long-term solution. This involves abandoning the leaking line under the slab and installing a new pipe through the walls or attic. This method skips the problem area entirely, providing a fresh, durable plumbing line. It is a common solution for aging homes in Kansas City with original copper or galvanized plumbing.

    Cold Water Slab Leak Repair in Kansas City

    Cold water slab leaks are harder to feel but still waste water and wet the soil. They often show up as a high water bill or a meter that moves with all fixtures off. We isolate the cold line, find the leak and repair or reroute it. Fixing cold leaks quickly helps protect your foundation.

    Slab Leak Reroute in Kansas City

    Rerouting is often the best long-term choice, especially with older copper lines. We abandon the leaking line under the slab and run a new PEX line through the attic and walls. Openings are small and placed where patching is simplest. Reroutes end future leaks in that section of pipe.
