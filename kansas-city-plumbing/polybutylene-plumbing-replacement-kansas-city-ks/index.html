    Polybutylene Identification and Inspection in Kansas City

    Polybutylene is usually gray, though it can be white, blue or black, and is often stamped PB2110. You may see it at the water heater, under sinks or near the main shutoff. We inspect visible lines, check the stamps and trace runs through the attic. You get a clear answer and a written replacement estimate.

    Full Home Repiping with PEX in Kansas City

    When a home with polybutylene pipes sits on a slab foundation, repiping becomes a more complex process. Our team specializes in full home repiping using PEX (cross-linked polyethylene), a flexible, durable, and corrosion-resistant material. PEX is an excellent choice for Kansas homes, as it resists freezing better than rigid pipes and expands to handle temperature changes. We carefully plan the repipe to limit disruption, often running new lines through attics or existing wall cavities, delivering a complete and long-lasting upgrade for your entire plumbing system.

    Copper Replacement Option in Kansas City

    Some homeowners choose Type L copper for its long history and rigid feel. We solder with lead-free solder or use pressed fittings for faster installation. Copper costs more than PEX and takes a little longer to install. We help you weigh the options for your budget and local water.

    Polybutylene to PEX Transition in Kansas City, KS

    Transitioning from polybutylene to PEX is a popular and cost-effective solution for many homeowners in Kansas City, KS. We use specialized transition fittings and techniques to seamlessly connect your existing main water line to the new PEX system. This ensures a watertight and code-compliant connection, providing a complete upgrade to a modern, durable plumbing material throughout your home's interior. This transition offers the benefits of PEX without the need for extensive excavation or disruption to your main water service line.
