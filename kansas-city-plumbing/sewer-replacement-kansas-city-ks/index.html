    Trenchless Sewer Repair Options in Kansas City, KS

    Where applicable, we offer trenchless sewer repair methods like pipe bursting. This technique involves pulling a new pipe through the old one, at the same time breaking the existing pipe apart. It's a less disruptive option that requires only two small access points, saving your landscaping and driveway from extensive excavation. Trenchless methods are well suited for certain types of pipe damage and property layouts in Kansas City.
