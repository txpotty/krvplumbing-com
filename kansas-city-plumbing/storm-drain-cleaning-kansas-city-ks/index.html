    Dry Well Cleaning and Inspection in Kansas City, KS

    Dry wells let storm water soak into the ground instead of flowing to a street. They are found on commercial sites, HOA common areas and some residential lots. Silt slowly seals the settling chamber and the drainage rock, slowing how fast water drains. We vacuum the chamber, inspect the inlet and outlet, and check drain-down time. If the dry well no longer drains properly, we explain options for repair or replacement.

    Downspout and Roof Drain Lines in Kansas City, KS

    Many homes connect downspouts to buried pipe that carries water away from the foundation. These lines clog with roof granules, leaves and nests, then back up and dump water at the wall. We jet them clear and test with a hose to verify flow at the outlet. For flat roofs with scuppers, we check that the discharge path is open too. Keeping water away from the slab helps protect against soil movement and settling.

    Preventative Storm Drain Cleaning in Kansas City, KS

    Proactive cleaning is key to avoiding major issues with storm drains in Kansas City, KS. We offer scheduled preventative maintenance to keep your drains clear of leaves, debris, and sediment throughout the year. Regular cleaning can prevent the buildup that leads to severe clogs and flooding during unpredictable rainy seasons, extending the lifespan of your drainage system and protecting your property investment.

    Yard and Area Drain Cleaning in Kansas City

    Yard drains and area inlets collect runoff from lawns, rock landscaping and patios. Over time the grates fill with leaves and the pipes fill with silt and small gravel. We pull the grates, vacuum the inlet box and jet the pipe toward its outlet. This restores the line so it can carry a full storm. We also check that grates are secure and level.

    Channel and Trench Drain Cleaning in Kansas City

    Channel drains across driveways and garage entries catch water before it reaches the house. Their narrow channels fill quickly with sand, leaves and debris. We remove the grates, clean the channel and jet the outlet pipe. Cracked or loose grates can be replaced while we are there. A clean trench drain keeps water out of your garage during a downpour.

    Root Removal from Storm Drains in Kansas City

    Tree roots are a common cause of severe storm drain blockages in Kansas City, especially in older neighborhoods with mature trees. Roots seek out moisture and can infiltrate drain pipes through tiny cracks or loose joints, growing and eventually completely blocking water flow. KRV Plumbing uses dedicated root-cutting attachments on our drain snakes and hydro jetting equipment to cut through and remove root intrusions. After removal, we can suggest further steps to prevent future root problems.

    Catch Basin Cleaning in Kansas City, KS

    Catch basins are critical parts of any storm drainage system in Kansas City, KS, designed to collect runoff and trap debris before it enters the main drain lines. Regular cleaning of catch basins is important to prevent them from becoming overloaded and causing blockages further down the system. Our service includes removing built-up leaves, sediment, trash, and other debris from catch basins, so they work correctly and protect your entire drainage system. This preventative maintenance is key to heading off larger issues.

    Hydro Jetting for Storm Drains in Kansas City, KS

    Hydro jetting is a powerful solution for deeply clogged storm drains in Kansas City, KS. This method uses high-pressure water streams to wash out stubborn obstructions like compacted soil, heavy silt, roots, and grease buildup. It is very effective for thoroughly cleaning the inside of the pipe walls, restoring the drain to its full capacity and preventing a quick return of blockages, a common issue with older drainage systems.
