    Metered Softener Replacement in Kansas City

    Older timer-based softeners regenerate on a set schedule whether you used water or not. Metered softeners track actual usage and regenerate only when the resin is nearly used up. This saves salt, water and wear on the system. We replace old timer valves with metered Fleck 5600SXT or Clack WS1 valves. Most homeowners notice lower salt use within the first few weeks.

    Dual Tank Softener Systems in Kansas City

    Large households and homes with very hard water can benefit from a dual tank system that provides soft water around the clock. While one tank regenerates, the other stays in service. Kinetico and similar non-electric twin tank systems are popular for this reason. We size dual tank systems to your peak demand. They cost more upfront but prevent hard water bleed-through during regeneration.
