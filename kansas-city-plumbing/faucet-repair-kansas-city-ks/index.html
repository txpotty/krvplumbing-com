    Bathroom Pop-Up Drain Repair in Kansas City

    Pop-up drains on bathroom sinks fail at the pivot ball, the clevis strap, the lift rod or the stopper itself, and a slow drain often turns out to be hair wrapped around the stopper. Try lifting out the stopper first. We replace the pop-up assembly in a matching finish when the old one is corroded or the pivot leaks.

    Specialty Faucet Repair in Kansas City, KS

    Pot fillers, laundry tub faucets and wall-mount utility faucets each have their own valve styles, and many use compression stems or quarter-turn cartridges that hardware stores do not stock. We bring the common ones. For filtered water faucets, we replace the cartridge or the check valve as needed.

    Dripping Faucet Repair in Kansas City

    One drip a second adds up to more than 3,000 gallons a year, roughly the water used in 180 showers. That waste lands on your water bill. We find the worn washer, O-ring or cartridge and replace it, then check the cabinet below for swelling or stains from the slow leak.

    Tub Spout and Diverter Repair in Kansas City, KS

    Water still running from the tub spout while the shower is on means the diverter is worn, either the gate inside the spout or a diverter stem in the valve. Spouts install two ways. We measure the pipe stub and bring the right style, and the fix gives you full pressure at the showerhead again.

    Bathroom Faucet Repair in Kansas City, KS

    Bathroom sinks see less wear than kitchen sinks, but scale still builds up in cartridges, and pop-up drains break at the pivot rod under the sink. Both are quick fixes. We match cartridges for widespread, centerset and single-hole faucets, and pop-up assemblies are replaced to fit the finish.

    Cracked Faucet Body Repair in Kansas City

    Cracked faucet bodies, usually from freezing or an old brass casting that finally gave out, cannot be repaired and need a replacement faucet. Shut the stops first. A new faucet can usually go in on the same visit, and we will check the supply lines and stops while the water is off.

    Pull-Down Kitchen Faucet Repair in Kansas City

    Pull-down kitchen faucets wear at the hose, the spray head and the docking magnet or counterweight that pulls the hose back into the spout. Hoses split near the quick-connect. Brand-matched hoses, heads and weights go in, then check for drips under the sink where the hose connects.

    Leaking Faucet Handle and Base Repair in Kansas City, KS

    Water seeping around the handle or pooling at the base usually means failed O-rings on the spout or cartridge, or a mounting nut that has worked loose under the sink. Look at the floor of the sink cabinet. New O-rings go on and the mounting nut gets tightened, then reseal the base so water on the counter cannot get under it.
