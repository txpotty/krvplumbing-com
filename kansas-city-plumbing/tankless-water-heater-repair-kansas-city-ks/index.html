    Condensing Tankless Repair in Kansas City, KS

    Condensing tankless heaters, such as many Navien models, extract extra heat from exhaust and produce condensate. Clogged condensate traps, failed neutralizers and blocked drains can shut the unit down. We clean the trap, replace the neutralizer media and confirm proper drainage. PVC venting is also checked for slope and sagging.

    Rinnai is a popular tankless brand, and its units can face challenges such as scaling from hard water or issues with their flame rod. We make sure your Rinnai system continues to provide reliable, on-demand hot water, following manufacturer specifications.

    Tankless Repair vs Replacement Cost in Kansas City, KS

    Descaling service typically costs $175 to $350, and sensor or igniter repairs run $250 to $600. Heat exchanger replacement can cost $800 to $1,500 or more unless covered by warranty.     Tankless Descaling and Flushing in Kansas City

    Hard water leaves calcium and magnesium scale inside the heat exchanger, limiting flow and causing overheating. Many units warn of this with scale codes, such as the LC code on Rinnai models. We connect a circulation pump to the service valves and run a descaling solution through the exchanger. Annual descaling is a smart move for most homes on hard water.

    Flow Sensor and Thermistor Repair in Kansas City

    Tankless heaters rely on flow sensors to start and thermistors to regulate temperature. A dirty inlet filter or failed sensor causes no hot water, fluctuating temperature or the unit turning off mid-shower. We clean filters, test sensor readings and replace failed parts. Temperature becomes steady again once readings are accurate.
