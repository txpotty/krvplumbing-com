    Code Updates During Drain Replacement in Kansas City

    Old drains sometimes break current rules. S-traps and drum traps were common long ago, and replacement brings them up to a vented P-trap. That can mean an air admittance valve. Local rules decide.

    P-Trap Replacement in Kansas City

    Thin chrome traps are the ones we replace most. They rust inside and their nuts seize, so we cut them out and fit heavy-wall PVC or polished brass. Then it gets a full flow test. Total time is often under an hour.

    Bathroom Sink Drain Replacement in Kansas City, KS

    Bathroom drains wear out at the pop-up. Pivot nuts leak and drain bodies corrode at the overflow holes, so a sink that will not hold water needs a new pop-up. Patching one piece rarely works. The new drain matches your faucet finish.

    Hard-Water Drain Damage Repair in Kansas City, KS
