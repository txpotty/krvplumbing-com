    Brands We Install in Kansas City

    Rheem, Bradford White, AO Smith, Navien and Rinnai make up most of the gas heaters we install in Kansas City homes and businesses. Each brand has different warranty lengths, recovery rates and vent options, and we lay those out side by side so you can pick based on facts. Budget matters too. We install all of them.

    Atmospheric Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Kansas City

    Atmospheric vent heaters rely on hot exhaust rising through a metal flue, with no fan. They cost the least, but the vent has to be the right size, slope upward and keep its clearances. We inspect the existing vent. Corroded or undersized pipe gets replaced.

    Bradford White Gas Water Heater Installation in Kansas City

    Bradford White sells only through plumbers, and we install a lot of them. We install their Defender models to the manual, keeping the bottom air intake clear and the clearances right. They are built heavy. Code-approved installs every time.

    Propane Water Heater Installation in Kansas City

    Propane homes need heaters set up for propane, not natural gas. Propane runs at a different pressure and needs a smaller burner orifice, so we install propane-specific models or convert units with the manufacturer kit where the manual allows it. We work with your supplier. Every joint is leak tested.

    Traditional Tank Gas Water Heater Installation in Kansas City

    Tank gas heaters are still the workhorse in most garages and utility rooms, usually 40 or 50 gallons. Each install gets a new gas connector and drip leg, a vent connector pitched upward and a relief line piped where code allows. We strap it if code calls for it. Your household sets the size.

    Gas Tankless Water Heater Installation in Kansas City, KS

    Gas tankless units fire at up to 199,000 BTU, far more than a 40,000 BTU tank burner. The higher input means a bigger gas line and a dedicated vent, often PVC on condensing models. Endless hot water follows. Private wells serve many rural homes, and minerals, sediment, and nitrates make periodic well testing a good habit. A scale filter may be worth adding.

    High-Efficiency Gas Water Heater Installation in Kansas City, KS

    High-efficiency gas heaters use condensing technology to pull extra heat out of the exhaust. Condensing units vent through PVC and need a condensate line, so placement and install work differ from a standard tank. Rebates may apply. We install Rheem, Bradford White and AO Smith models.

    Rheem Gas Water Heater Installation in Kansas City, KS

    Rheem gas heaters show up in a lot of local homes, from basic atmospheric tanks to power vent models. KRV Plumbing installs the full Rheem line. Our install covers gas, flue and the Guardian safety system, including the air intake that keeps it working. Then we test draft.
