    Burst and Leaking Pipe Repair in Kansas City

    Pipes burst from freezing, high water pressure, corrosion or a failed fitting. Hard or aggressive water can cause pinhole leaks in copper that quickly widen into a spray.     Emergency Water Heater Repair in Kansas City

    Sewer Line Backups in Kansas City, KS

    Sewer line backups are serious plumbing emergencies that can lead to hazardous wastewater entering your home. These are often caused by tree root intrusion, pipe corrosion or major blockages. Our team uses camera inspection technology to pinpoint the exact problem in your Kansas City sewer line and offers immediate solutions, from snaking to trenchless repair, to protect your family's health and property.

    Overflowing Toilet Solutions in Kansas City, KS

    An overflowing toilet is a common and distressing emergency. It's often caused by severe clogs in the trap or drain line, or a faulty fill valve. Our technicians use professional-grade augers and hydro-jetting equipment to clear stubborn blockages without damaging your porcelain or pipes.     Overflowing Toilets and Fixture Emergencies in Kansas City

    A toilet that overflows again and again or a faucet that will not shut off can flood a bathroom fast.
