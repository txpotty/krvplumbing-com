    Emergency Water Heater Replacement in Kansas City, KS

    Water on the garage floor and no hot water usually means the tank has rusted through. Shut the cold supply valve on top of the tank and turn the gas knob to pilot or flip the breaker, then call us. We carry common sizes. Same-day replacement is normal.

    Gas Tank Water Heater Installation in Kansas City

    A gas tank is the most common water heater we install, in 40, 50 and 75 gallon sizes. Every job gets a fresh connector, a drip leg, a shutoff and a vent connector with the right rise toward the chimney or flue. Then a draft test. Gas suits busy households.

    Commercial Water Heater Installation in Kansas City

    Restaurants, salons and offices burn through hot water at rates no household tank can keep up with. We install commercial tanks over 100,000 BTU and racked tankless systems, with the venting and gas supply those loads require. Downtime costs you money. Installs can happen after close.

    Water Heater Expansion Tank Installation in Kansas City

    Closed plumbing systems trap the extra volume water gains when it heats. Kansas leaves plumbing code adoption to cities and counties, and most local jurisdictions use a national model plumbing code. An expansion tank gives that water somewhere to go, so the relief valve stops dripping and pipe joints stop taking pressure spikes every time the burner runs. We set the air charge to match your water pressure. It mounts on the cold inlet.

    Electric Water Heater Installation in Kansas City, KS

    Electric tanks need a 240-volt circuit, usually a 30-amp double-pole breaker with 10-gauge wire. Before hooking up the new tank we check that the breaker and wire size match the elements, since a mismatch is a common cause of tripped breakers and scorched terminals. No venting needed. That makes placement easier.

    Major Brand Water Heater Installation in Kansas City, KS

    Rheem, Bradford White, A.O. Smith and Noritz are the brands we install most. Every manufacturer sets its own clearance, venting and relief valve rules, and following them is what keeps the warranty good. Our crews know each one. We help you compare models on warranty length and recovery rate.

    Attic and Closet Water Heater Installs in Kansas City

    Attic and closet installs take more planning than a garage swap. An attic heater needs a pan with its own drain line, a properly routed relief line and enough access to get the old tank out and the new one in. Ceilings are at stake. Relocation to a safer spot is an option.

    Electric Tank Water Heater Installation in Kansas City, KS

    Without gas service, an electric tank is the usual pick. We match voltage and breaker size to the panel, and since there is no vent, an electric tank can sit in spots a gas unit cannot. Closets and attics get a pan. Quiet and simple.
