    Rheem Gas Water Heater Replacement in Kansas City, KS

    Rheem Performance and Performance Platinum tanks cover most of the range we install, from basic 6-year models to 12-year tanks with better anodes. More warranty years generally buy a heavier anode rod. We help you pick.

    Rheem Gas Water Heater Service in Kansas City, KS

    Rheem tanks and their Ruud twins share most parts, which helps when a gas valve fails a few years in. We install both. Setup follows the factory manual, including the draft test.

    Power Vent Water Heater Replacement in Kansas City

    Blower motors and pressure switches are the weak points on power vent tanks. A second blower failure on an older tank is usually the point where a new heater makes more sense. We confirm a dedicated outlet first.

    Large Capacity 75 Gallon Gas Heaters in Kansas City, KS

    Big families with soaking tubs often outgrow a 50 gallon tank and need a 75 gallon heater or a high-recovery 50 at around 65,000 to 75,000 BTU. A bigger burner needs more gas. We size the gas line before installing, since an undersized line cuts recovery.

    Propane Gas Water Heater Replacement in Kansas City, KS

    Propane heaters run at about 11 inches of water column instead of the 7 inches typical for natural gas, so the gas valve and orifice have to match the fuel. We never swap a natural gas tank onto propane without the right conversion. We confirm pressure with a manometer.

    Atmospheric Vent Gas Water Heater Replacement in Kansas City

    Atmospheric vent heaters last 10 to 12 years on average, and the end usually shows up as rusty hot water or a slow drip from the bottom. The new tank ties into your existing gas line and flue. Combustion air matters. We confirm a closet install has louvers or ducted air before we light the burner.

    Commercial Gas Water Heater Service in Kansas City, KS

    Higher BTU inputs on commercial units mean bigger gas lines and larger vent sizes, plus a permit and inspection in most jurisdictions. That is all part of the job. We keep your downtime short.

    Gas Water Heater Efficiency Ratings in Kansas City

    Efficiency ratings on tank heaters are listed as UEF, uniform energy factor, and a higher number means less gas per gallon heated. A standard tank sits around 0.60 to 0.65. Condensing models top 0.80, and some qualify for utility rebates where those are offered.
