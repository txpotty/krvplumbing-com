    Frequently Asked Questions About Gas Water Heater Replacement in Kansas City

    The cost for gas water heater replacement in Kansas City, KS usually ranges from $1,200 to $3,500, depending on the unit size, brand, efficiency and any necessary plumbing changes. Factors like gas line upgrades or ventilation improvements can impact the final price.

    Most standard gas water heater replacements can be completed within 3 to 5 hours. More complex installations, such as converting to a power vent unit or relocating the water heater, may take longer, usually a full day.

    Most new gas water heaters come with a manufacturer's warranty, usually covering the tank for 6 to 10 years and parts for 1 to 2 years. KRV Plumbing also provides a labor warranty on our installation for added peace of mind.

    Atmospheric vent water heaters use natural convection to vent combustion gases up a chimney or flue. Power vent models use an electric fan to force gases out, allowing for more flexible venting options and often higher efficiency due to more complete combustion.

    A typical gas water heater has a lifespan of 10 to 12 years with proper maintenance. Factors like water quality in Kansas City, such as hard water causing mineral buildup, and usage patterns can affect its longevity.
