    Pipe Bursting Services in Kansas City, KS

    Pipe bursting is a trenchless technique where a new pipe is pulled through the path of the old, broken pipe, fracturing the old pipe outward at the same time. This method is highly effective for complete pipe replacement, creating a new, durable pipe of the same or larger diameter.
