    Foundation Crack Repair in Kansas City

    Foundation cracks are a common entry point for basement water leaks, especially in homes built on soil that swells and shrinks. Basements are common in the eastern part of Kansas, while slab and crawlspace homes are more typical in many other areas. We use epoxy and polyurethane injection techniques to seal these cracks, preventing water infiltration and reinforcing your foundation. Our methods are matched to the specific type and size of the crack, ensuring a durable repair.

    Floor Drain and Backwater Valve Repair in Kansas City

    A basement floor drain that backs up during heavy use or a storm usually points to a blockage or a failed trap. We clear the drain, check the line to the main sewer and replace cracked traps and covers. Installing a backwater valve stops sewage from pushing back into the basement if the main line surcharges. We set the valve where it can be serviced without trouble.

    Emergency Basement Flooding Response in Kansas City

    A burst pipe or a failed sump during a storm can put inches of water on the floor in minutes. Call us and shut off the main water valve if you can do so without risk.
