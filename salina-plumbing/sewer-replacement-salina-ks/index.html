    Sewer Pipe Material Upgrades in Salina, KS

    Original sewer pipe in older homes is usually clay, cast iron or Orangeburg. Clay joints open up and let roots in, cast iron scales and cracks from the inside, and Orangeburg, a tar-paper pipe from the mid 1900s, slowly flattens until almost nothing passes. Replacement means PVC or HDPE.

    Sewer Line Material Upgrades in Salina

    Clay, cast iron and Orangeburg all failed for different reasons. PVC avoids all three of those problems. Schedule 40 or SDR 35 PVC is what most codes call for on a lateral, and we match the grade to the depth and load over the pipe. Glued joints stay sealed.

    Code-Compliant Sewer Replacement in Salina

    Code sets the minimum slope, the cleanout spacing and the pipe type. The inspector looks at fall, cleanouts and material before backfill. We set fall at a quarter inch per foot unless the run requires otherwise, place cleanouts at the house and at major direction changes, and use pipe and fittings listed for underground drainage. Paperwork stays on file.

    Addressing Tree Root Intrusions in Salina, KS

    Roots find sewer lines by following moisture. They need only a tiny gap. Once inside, they grow into a mat that catches paper and grease, and a root cutter clears them for a season at most, because the opening is still there. New PVC with glued joints gives them no way in.
