    Gas Water Heater Replacement in Salina, KS

    Gas water heaters recover faster than electric, and in Salina, KS homes they fail most often from a corroded tank, a bad gas valve or a burner clogged with rust flakes. Gas gets shut off and capped, the old tank drained and removed, and the new one set with a fresh connector, sediment trap and properly sloped vent. Every joint gets checked.

    Attic Water Heater Replacement in Salina

    A lot of Kansas housing dates from the mid-century building boom, with galvanized steel supply lines and cast iron drains. Attic water heaters take more work to replace than ones in a garage or closet. Moving a 50-gallon tank through a pull-down ladder or small opening takes two people, and code wants a pan with a drain line, a platform and a light. Two-person crew, always.

    Bradford White, Rheem and AO Smith in Salina, KS

    We install Bradford White, Rheem and AO Smith on most tank jobs. Bradford White is trade-only and durable, Rheem offers range and parts, and AO Smith brings plenty of sizes, so the pick comes down to fit, warranty and stock. Any of the three works.

    Bradford White Water Heater Replacement in Salina

    Bradford White sells only through the trade, so you will not find one at a big-box store. Their tanks have a good reputation for durability, and parts and warranty support run through the plumber who installed it, which makes it simple for us to handle a warranty claim for a homeowner down the road. Supply houses stock them.

    Bradford White Water Heater Services in Salina

    We use Bradford White a lot on our trucks because the tanks hold up and the parts are easy to find. When replacing one, we match fuel, size and venting, check whether the old warranty still applies, and register the new heater right away. Registration matters.

    Rheem and Ruud Water Heater Replacement in Salina, KS

    Ruud and Rheem come out of the same company and share many parts. Our technicians install both, and since the parts overlap, a Ruud thermostat or element is easy to source when a repair makes more sense than a full replacement on a newer tank. We will say when repair works.

    Tankless Water Heater Upgrades in Salina

    Going tankless when the old tank fails is a popular move, but it is a bigger job than a tank swap. Since tankless can pull four times the gas of a tank, the meter line usually gets bigger, and the old vent is swapped for stainless or PVC. You see both numbers.

    Electric Water Heater Replacement in Salina

    Electric water heaters in Salina usually fail from a burned-out heating element, a bad thermostat or a tank that has finally rusted through at the bottom. Elements are cheap to swap. Tank leaks are the end, so we replace the heater and match the voltage, wattage and breaker size to your existing circuit. Most swaps take a few hours.
