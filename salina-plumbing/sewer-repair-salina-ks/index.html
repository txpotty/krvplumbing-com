    Bellied Sewer Line Correction in Salina, KS

    A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged and holds standing water. Paper and solids settle there. Slab leaks and broken sewer laterals are common results of shifting clay, especially after long dry spells followed by heavy rain. We measure the depth of standing water on camera, dig out that section, re-bed the pipe on compacted gravel and set it back to a quarter inch of fall per foot, which is the typical code minimum for a four-inch line. A shallow belly sometimes just needs regular cleaning.

    Sewer Line Backups & Blockages in Salina

    Water coming up in the tub when the washer drains is a classic main line symptom. The blockage sits downstream of every fixture. We clear it from the nearest cleanout, then run the camera to see what caused it, since a grease clog and a root mass need very different fixes.     Tree Root Invasion Solutions in Salina

    Roots in a sewer line are not the tree's fault. A leaking joint invited them in. Warm, wet, nutrient-rich air escaping from a loose joint draws roots toward the line, and once they get in they keep growing until the joint splits or the pipe cracks open. We cut them out, then talk about the long-term fix. Lining or a joint replacement keeps them from coming back.
