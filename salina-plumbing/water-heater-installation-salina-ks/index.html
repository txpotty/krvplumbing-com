    Electric Tank Water Heater Installation in Salina, KS

    Without gas service, an electric tank is the usual pick. We match the voltage and breaker size to your panel, and because electric tanks need no vent, they can go in a closet, an attic or a laundry room where a gas unit could not. A drain pan goes under closet installs. Quiet and simple.

    Gas Water Heater Replacement in Salina

    Swapping a gas water heater comes down to three connections done right, the gas, the vent and the water. We install a new flex connector with a sediment trap, check vent pitch and draft and test every joint with a gas leak detector. Venting is what keeps CO out. We follow code on all of it.

    Hybrid Heat Pump Water Heater Installation in Salina, KS

    Heat pump water heaters pull warmth from the surrounding air and can use about a third of the power of a standard electric tank. They need roughly 700 cubic feet of air around them and a condensate drain, so a warm garage or a large utility room usually works better than a closet. Some qualify for tax credits. We will tell you if your space works.

    Attic and Closet Water Heater Installs in Salina

    Attic and closet installs take more planning than a garage swap. Every attic heater needs a drain pan with a line to the outside or a drain, a relief line routed properly and a clear path for the next tank to come in and out. Ceilings are at stake. Relocation to a safer spot is an option.

    Commercial Water Heater Installation in Salina

    Restaurants, salons and offices burn through hot water at rates no household tank can keep up with. Installs can happen after close.

    Brand-Specific Water Heater Installation in Salina

    Each manufacturer publishes its own install rules, and they are not identical. Rheem, AO Smith, Bradford White and Navien each spell out clearances, vent lengths and relief valve placement, and a warranty claim can be denied if those were ignored. Our crews install to spec.     Gas Water Heater Installation in Salina, KS

    Gas heaters recover faster than electric, often refilling a 50 gallon tank in under an hour. Our crews set the gas line, the drip leg and the vent connector, then do a draft test at the hood to prove the exhaust is leaving the house. Leak checks follow. Expansion tanks go in where code requires.

    Electric Water Heater Installation in Salina, KS

    Electric tanks need a 240-volt circuit, usually a 30-amp double-pole breaker with 10-gauge wire. We confirm the breaker and wire size match the new unit before connecting it, since many older homes have the wrong gauge. No venting needed. That makes placement easier.
