    Picking a Tankless for Your Salina, KS House

    Electric Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Salina, KS

    Electric tankless water heaters skip the gas line entirely, but a whole-house model can need 120 to 160 amps on two to four double-pole breakers. Many panels cannot spare that. Panel size and wire gauge get checked first.

    Residential Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Salina

    Sizing a tankless starts with how many fixtures run at once and how cold the incoming water is, not the square footage of the house. Run two showers and the dishwasher together and you are near 8 GPM. The unit is chosen from that figure. Any gas, water or vent changes the new unit needs get done to current code.

    Leaking Tankless Replacement in Salina

    A leaking tankless can drip inside the cabinet for weeks before anyone notices the puddle. Shut the gas and cold isolation valve now. We then find out whether it is a fitting or the heat exchanger, since one is cheap and the other is replacement.

    Tank to Tankless Conversions in Salina, KS

    Pulling a 50 gallon tank out of a garage frees up floor space and ends standby heat loss. Gas is the catch, since tankless units usually need 3/4-inch pipe or larger and a new vent. Before quoting, we check the meter and pipe run, and afterward we cap the old flue and seal the roof. Expect a full day.

    Tankless-to-Tankless Replacement in Salina

    Replacing an existing tankless with a new one is usually the simpler job, since the gas line, vent path and water connections are already there. We confirm they are sized right for the new unit. Corroded service valves get swapped while we are there.

    Whole-House Electric Tankless Replacement in Salina

    Whole-house electric tankless performance drops when the incoming water is cold, since the unit has to raise the temperature further at the same amperage. Stiebel Eltron and EcoSmart are the common brands. A heat pump water heater is sometimes the better fit, and we will tell you.

    Rinnai Condensing Upgrades in Salina, KS

    Rinnai Sensei condensing models replace older non-condensing Rinnai units in a lot of homes, but the venting changes from stainless to PVC. We handle that conversion. Most of the plumbing lines up already.

    Tank to Tankless Conversion in Salina, KS

    Converting from a tank to tankless takes a full day in most homes. The work usually includes a larger gas line, new venting through a wall or roof, and a new spot on the wall for the unit. The old flue gets capped.
