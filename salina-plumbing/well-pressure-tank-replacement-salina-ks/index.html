    Bladder-Style Pressure Tank Replacement in Salina, KS

    A bladder tank holds water inside a rubber balloon surrounded by compressed air. Air pressure forces water to the fixtures. Bladders crack with age, especially if the tank ran with low pre-charge, letting water fill the whole shell and kill the air cushion. Replacement is the fix.

    Well-Rite Pressure Tank Installation in Salina, KS

    Cost-Effective Well Pressure Tank Upgrades in Salina, KS

    Upgrading a size often costs less than people think. Going one size larger adds only a little. A larger tank means fewer pump starts per day, and fewer starts is the biggest single thing you can do for pump life, so we often recommend it during a replacement.

    Rapid clicking at the switch is the classic sign. That fast on-off pattern means the tank has no usable air, and the pump is now taking every hit of demand directly, so each glass of water starts the motor. We swap the tank and the cycling stops.

    Tank Tee, Gauge and Relief Valve Replacement in Salina

    The tank tee is the brass manifold that connects tank, switch, gauge, drain and relief valve. Threads on old tees weep. The relief valve should open before the system reaches a dangerous pressure, and a corroded valve may not open when needed. Whole set, one visit.
