    Common Faucet Repairs in Salina, KS

    Compression Faucet Repair in Salina

    Two-handle faucets with separate hot and cold handles are often compression faucets, which seal by pressing a rubber washer down onto a brass seat inside the valve. Over time the washer hardens and the seat wears rough. We replace the washer and dress or replace the seat with a seat wrench, and the packing gets redone if the stem leaks.

    Disc Faucet Repair in Salina

    Ceramic disc faucets last a long time, but grit from a water main repair or scale from hard water can scratch the discs or wear out the rubber inlet seals underneath. Inlet seals cost very little. A scratched disc means a new cartridge, since the two discs are lapped to fit each other and cannot be resurfaced.

    Leaky Faucet Repair in Salina

    Most drips come from the part that seals the water inside the faucet, which is a rubber washer on older two-handle faucets and a cartridge or ceramic disc on newer ones. First we identify which type of valve you have. It matters. With the right part on the truck, most drips are fixed in one visit.

    Leaking Faucet Handle and Base Repair in Salina, KS

    Water seeping around the handle or pooling at the base usually means failed O-rings on the spout or cartridge, or a mounting nut that has worked loose under the sink. Check the cabinet floor. New O-rings go on and the mounting nut gets tightened, then reseal the base so water on the counter cannot get under it.

    Low Water Flow Faucet Repair in Salina

    Sputtering flow, where the water spits and surges, often means air or debris in the line after a water main repair, or a partly clogged cartridge screen. A flush of the lines usually fixes it. Pull-down faucets also have check valves in the hose that can stick, and we clean or replace those.

    Bathroom Pop-Up Drain Repair in Salina

    Pop-up drains on bathroom sinks fail at the pivot ball, the clevis strap, the lift rod or the stopper itself, and a slow drain often turns out to be hair wrapped around the stopper. Try lifting out the stopper first. We replace the pop-up assembly in a matching finish when the old one is corroded or the pivot leaks.

    Faucet Handle Repair and Replacement in Salina, KS

    Stripped handle splines, cracked handle bodies, worn adapters and loose set screws make a faucet hard to turn or impossible to shut tight. Handles are often sold separately. The replacement handle gets matched to both the stem and the finish, and if the stem itself is stripped we replace it at the same time.

    Bathroom Faucet Repair in Salina, KS

    Bathroom sinks see less wear than kitchen sinks, but scale still builds up in cartridges, and pop-up drains break at the pivot rod under the sink. Both are quick fixes. Cartridges get matched for widespread, centerset and single-hole faucets, and pop-up assemblies are replaced to fit the finish.
