    Repiping and Pipe Replacement Choices in Salina, KS

    Copper Pinhole Leak Replacement in Salina, KS

    Pinhole leaks start small. A drip, then a stain. They usually mean the copper wall has thinned from corrosion, and when one shows up, more are often on the way along the same lines. Repipe or patch, we explain.

    Drain Pipe Replacement in Salina, KS

    Drain lines fail too. Cast iron cracks and scales. We replace cracked cast iron, failing ABS or old galvanized drains with PVC, checking slope and venting so the new drains flow freely. Camera first.

    PEX Pipe Replacement in Salina

    PEX bends around corners and comes in long rolls, so a typical repipe needs fewer fittings than copper would on the same layout. Hard freezes happen every winter in Kansas, and pipes in exterior walls, garages, and crawlspaces are the most at risk. It often survives freezing where copper would crack. We use PEX-a with expansion fittings where possible, keeping flow strong at each joint. Faster install.

    Copper Pipe Replacement for Older Homes in Salina, KS

    Older homes sometimes have copper that is thin from decades of use. Pinholes start showing up. We replace it with new Type L copper or PEX, and fixing awkward runs from past remodels. Better flow.

    Whole-Home Re-Piping in Salina

    A whole-home repipe replaces every supply line from the main shutoff to each fixture, including the water heater connections. Plan on a few days for a typical house. We run new trunk lines through the attic or crawlspace, branch down to fixtures through small wall openings, and keep water on at night whenever we can. Then patching.

    Water Line Replacement vs. Repair in Salina, KS

    One leak on otherwise good pipe is a repair. Several leaks in different rooms, rusty water throughout, or pipe that crumbles when we cut it tells us the system is failing everywhere, and patching just moves the next leak down the line. We show you what we find.

    Galvanized Pipe Replacement in Salina, KS

    Galvanized steel was common before the 1960s. The zinc wears off and the steel rusts inside. Over decades the opening shrinks, pressure drops and the water turns brown after sitting, while threaded joints corrode through first. Replacing it is the only fix.

    Burst Pipe Repair and Replacement in Salina, KS

    Hard freezes happen every winter in Kansas, and pipes in exterior walls, garages, and crawlspaces are the first to burst. Ice inside a pipe cracks it open. Exposed pipe in crawlspaces, garages and outside walls is most at risk, and when it bursts we replace the damaged section with PEX or copper and add insulation or reroute it away from the cold. Then we test it.
