    Repiping for Hard Water in Salina, KS

    Private wells serve many rural homes, and minerals, sediment, and nitrate levels make periodic well testing a good habit. For homes with hard water, we usually recommend PEX-A, which resists scale and does not corrode, and we can add a softener loop or a whole-house filter during the repipe.
