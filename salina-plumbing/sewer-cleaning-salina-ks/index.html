    Frequently Asked Questions About Sewer Cleaning in Salina

    Main sewer cleaning in Salina usually costs $200 to $500 with a cable machine from an accessible cleanout. Hydro jetting a sewer line typically runs $400 to $900, and a camera inspection adds about $150 to $350.

    We strongly advise against chemical drain cleaners for sewer lines. These products are often ineffective against severe sewer clogs and can corrode pipes, especially older ones, causing more damage. They can also pose a health risk to you and our plumbers during service.

    For most homes in Salina, an annual or twice-yearly sewer cleaning is enough for preventative maintenance, especially if you have older pipes or mature trees nearby. If you have frequent clogs, more regular cleaning might be necessary. Your plumber can recommend a schedule based on your situation.

    Multiple slow drains, toilets that gurgle when a sink drains, and water coming up in the tub or shower are common signs. Sewer odors in the yard or a soggy spot near the cleanout can also point to a problem.

    Usually, standard home insurance policies do not cover routine sewer cleaning or damage from gradual wear and tear. However, if a sudden, unexpected event causes sewer damage, it might be covered. It is best to check with your insurance provider first.

    We use professional equipment from trusted brands like Spartan Tool, RIDGID, and Gorlitz for our sewer cleaning work. These brands are known for their reliability and effectiveness in tackling tough blockages.

    In most cases the homeowner owns the lateral from the house to the city main or property line. Your city utility can confirm where its responsibility begins.
