    What Our Repipe Crews Do in Leawood, KS

    PEX Repiping in Leawood, KS

    PEX is flexible plastic tubing that can run long distances without a fitting, which means fewer joints hidden in your walls. Fewer joints, fewer leaks. It resists scale and chlorine, handles a freeze better than copper and costs less to install, which is why most of our repipes use it. We use PEX-A with cold expansion fittings that keep the full inside diameter of the pipe open. That keeps pressure up at the far fixtures.

    Residential Repiping Plans in Leawood

    Every house is piped a little differently, so a repipe starts with a walk-through. We count fixtures, check attic and crawlspace access, look at where the main comes in and ask about plans for future remodels before we draw a route. Then we size the lines. A three-bath house with a tankless heater may need 1-inch main trunk lines to keep showers steady when the washer kicks on.

    Urgent Repipes After Repeated Failures in Leawood, KS

    A widespread pipe failure sometimes forces a repipe sooner than you planned. We make the house safe and usable first, with temporary repairs that get you water, then schedule the full repipe as soon as the crew and permit allow. We move fast. Most emergency repipes still finish within the normal two to four day window once work starts.

    Whole-Home Copper Repiping in Leawood

    Copper is the traditional choice, and Type L copper has a thicker wall than the Type M that went into many older homes. It holds up well, does not mind sunlight in an exposed run and has a long track record behind it. It costs more. We sweat joints with lead-free solder or press them, hang the pipe at proper support intervals and test the whole system before patching.

    Slab Leak Reroutes in Leawood

    When one line under the slab leaks, a full repipe is not always the first step. Rerouting one branch overhead and leaving the bad pipe in the slab is often cheaper than both a repipe and a jackhammer repair. It suits a first-time leak. After multiple leaks, we will show you why a full repipe may cost less over the next ten years.

    Slab Leak Repiping in Leawood

    A slab leak shows up as a warm spot on the floor, a running meter or a wet patch of carpet with no visible cause. Slab leaks and cracked sewer lines are common results of shifting clay, especially after long dry spells followed by heavy rain. Instead of breaking concrete to patch one pipe, we reroute new supply lines overhead through the attic and down interior walls, then abandon the old lines in the slab. No jackhammer needed. It ends this leak and takes the rest of the slab plumbing out of the picture.

    Drywall Patching and Restoration in Leawood, KS

    Repiping needs small openings in walls and ceilings to reach fixtures and pull pipe. We cut clean, square openings, protect the area around them and patch every hole once the plumbing passes its test. Textures get matched. Knockdown, orange peel and smooth finishes are all routine, and your walls are ready for primer and paint when we leave.

    Galvanized Pipe Replacement in Leawood, KS

    Galvanized steel rusts from the inside, and over decades the inside diameter closes down to a fraction of what it was. Pressure drops slowly. Rust-colored water in the morning, poor pressure upstairs and dripping threaded joints all point to failing galvanized. New PEX or copper takes its place. Owners usually notice stronger flow the first day.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Repipe Specialists in Leawood

    Most PEX repipes for Kansas homes cost $5,000 to $12,000, based on size, bathroom count and access. Copper repipes usually run 20 to 40 percent more.

    Copper is rigid metal pipe with a long track record. PEX is flexible plastic tubing. PEX is cheaper, quicker and quieter and handles freezes better, while copper tolerates sun and heat and suits owners who want metal, so it depends on your house.

    A documented repipe removes a known risk that buyers and inspectors look for. It helps at sale time. Swapping out polybutylene, Kitec or galvanized makes a home easier to insure and sell, especially with a permit record and transferable warranty.

    Our repipes come with a written workmanship warranty on parts and labor, usually covering several years. Manufacturer coverage adds more. Certified PEX-A installs carry the manufacturer's long-term warranty on the pipe and fittings, and the exact terms of both warranties are on your estimate.

    A whole-home repipe requires permits, pressure testing and code knowledge. Most homeowners are better served by a licensed crew that can finish fast and pass inspection.

    Yes, a whole-home repipe in Leawood, KS usually needs a plumbing permit. The permit is on us. Our office files the permit, schedules the rough-in inspection while the walls are open and handles the final, so you get a repipe that is on record with the building department.

    You get a written workmanship warranty covering labor and materials. Details are spelled out up front. Our repipes use Uponor PEX-A or domestic Type L copper, and certified PEX-A jobs also get the manufacturer's long-term warranty.

    Water across much of Kansas runs hard, leaving mineral scale in water heaters, faucets, showerheads, and dishwashers. Hard water deposits calcium and magnesium scale inside pipe over time. In metal pipe, scale also speeds up corrosion, so repiping with PEX-A and adding a softener or filter during the job can protect the new plumbing and your fixtures for years.
