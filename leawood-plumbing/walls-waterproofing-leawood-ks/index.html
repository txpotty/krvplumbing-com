    Below-Grade and Basement Wall Waterproofing in Leawood

    A lot of Kansas housing dates from the mid-century building boom, with galvanized steel supply lines and cast iron drains. Any wall below grade sits in soil that gets saturated after heavy rain or snowmelt, and that water pushes against the wall with steady hydrostatic pressure. We inject cracks, coat the wall inside or out, and install drain tile where water pools along the footing. A sump goes in if groundwater sits high. Some basements never need a sump.

    Exterior Foundation Waterproofing in Leawood

    Exterior waterproofing means digging down to the footing and coating the outside of the wall. It is the most reliable fix for a leaking basement, because the water never touches the block or concrete. We apply a rubberized asphalt or polymer membrane, add a dimple board to protect it, and set perforated drain pipe in gravel along the footing to carry the water away from the wall. Basements are common in the eastern part of Kansas, while slab and crawlspace homes are more typical in many other areas. We backfill after the drain is tested.

    Cost-Effective Waterproofing Solutions in Leawood, KS

    Not every wet wall needs a $15,000 excavation. Sometimes the fix is a downspout extension and regrading the soil so it slopes away from the house, six inches of drop over the first ten feet, and that costs a few hundred dollars. We start with the cheapest fix that will actually work. Every option comes written out with its price.

    Exterior Block Wall Waterproofing in Leawood, KS

    White crust on block or stem walls is efflorescence, salt left behind as water moves through the masonry and dries.
