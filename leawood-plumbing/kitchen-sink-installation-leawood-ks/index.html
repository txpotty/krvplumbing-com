    How We Install Kitchen Sinks in Leawood, KS: The Details

    New Construction Sink Rough-In in Leawood, KS

    Rough-in sets the drain height and the stops long before the cabinets arrive. We usually set the kitchen drain stub around 16 to 20 inches off the floor, then adjust for deep bowls or a disposal so the trap does not end up below the wall outlet. Venting happens at this stage. Having the sink and faucet specs early saves moving pipe later.

    Undermount Sink Installation in Leawood, KS

    Undermounts hang from clips anchored into the underside of the stone, plus a bead of silicone along the rim. We support the bowl from below with a brace while the silicone cures, usually overnight, before loading it with water. Skipping the cure time leads to sagging. Just an eighth of an inch of sag leaves a gap that catches water and food along the edge.

    Top-mount Sink Installation in Leawood

    Drop-in sinks are common in Kansas homes with laminate or tile counters. The rim holds the sink up. After checking the cutout, we lay silicone under the lip and draw the clips down evenly so the whole rim sits flat. Then the drain and faucet get connected and tested.

    Restaurant Sink Installation in Leawood

    Restaurants, cafes and commissary kitchens in Leawood get their commercial sinks installed by KRV Plumbing. Hand sinks need hot and cold. We install three-compartment sinks, prep sinks and hand sinks with the indirect drains, floor sinks and grease trap connections the health inspector will look for.

    Farmhouse Sink Plumbing in Leawood, KS

    Fireclay and cast iron farmhouse sinks can weigh well over 100 pounds empty, before a single drop of water goes in. We build a support frame inside the cabinet and set the sink level before the counter is installed. Deep bowls need a lower trap. The drain gets planned for that depth from day one.

    Undermount Kitchen Sink Installation in Leawood

    Undermount sinks rely on clips and adhesive to stay attached to granite or quartz. The bowl is mounted, the rim sealed with silicone and the sink braced while the adhesive sets, normally overnight before it can take a full bowl of water. Then come the strainers and trap.

    Workstation Sink Installation in Leawood, KS

    Workstation sinks have a ledge for cutting boards, colanders and drying racks, and most are single deep bowls with an offset drain. The trap layout follows the offset drain, which lets the disposal fit and leaves storage room on one side. Check the faucet reach as well.

    Composite Sink Installation in Leawood

    Granite and quartz composite sinks are tough on top but can chip at the drain opening if a strainer is overtightened. Hand tight comes first. Most composite brands want silicone rather than putty under the strainer because putty can stain them, so we go by the maker's instructions.
