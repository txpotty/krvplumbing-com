    Grease and Scale Buildup Cleaning in Leawood, KS

    Grease from kitchens cools and coats the inside of sewer pipes, and minerals in the water bond with it to form a stubborn crust. Water across much of Kansas runs quite hard, leaving mineral scale in water heaters, faucets, showerheads, and dishwashers. Over years the pipe opening shrinks until flow slows to a trickle. A cable can punch a hole through, but jetting cleans the walls back to full size. We choose the method based on pipe material and condition. Cleaning before the line fully closes spares you money and stress.
