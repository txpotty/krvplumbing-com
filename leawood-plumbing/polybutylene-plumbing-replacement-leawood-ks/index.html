    Chlorine and chloramine in treated water break down the plastic from the inside, making it brittle. Fittings and hot water lines often fail before the rest.

    Both PEX and copper are excellent choices. PEX is flexible, less expensive, more freeze-resistant, and handles ground movement better than rigid pipe.     Common signs include frequent leaks, discolored water, low water pressure, or visible grey or blue plastic pipes often marked 'PB2110'. If your home was built between 1978 and 1995 and has these pipes, replacement is strongly advised.
