    Many homes see high incoming pressure, so pressure reducing valves are common. The PRV protects your fixtures but also turns your plumbing into a closed system, which makes an expansion tank necessary. We test the PRV output and replace it if it is creeping or failing. Pairing a healthy PRV with a correctly charged expansion tank gives you steady pressure throughout the house.

    New Expansion Tank Installation in Leawood

    If your home has a pressure reducing valve and no expansion tank, the water heater is likely seeing a pressure spike every heating cycle. We install a new tank on the cold water supply near the heater using a tee and short nipple. The air side is precharged to match your static pressure, usually between 50 and 70 psi. We then run the heater and watch the gauge to confirm pressure stays steady.

    Standard Water Heater Expansion Tank Installation in Leawood

    Our standard installation service covers traditional tank-style water heaters, with the expansion tank correctly tied into your cold water supply line.
