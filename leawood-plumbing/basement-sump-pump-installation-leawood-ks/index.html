    Discharge Line and Check Valve Installation in Leawood

    A great pump is useless if the water it moves runs right back toward the foundation. We route rigid PVC discharge pipe out of the basement and extend it at least 10 feet from the house or into a pop-up emitter or dry well. A spring or swing check valve keeps the column of water from dropping back into the pit after every cycle, which protects the motor. Many Kansas homeowners let faucets drip and open cabinet doors on the coldest nights to keep water moving. Where lines can freeze, we add a freeze-resistant outlet so ice cannot block the line.

    Pedestal Sump Pumps in Leawood

    Pedestal pumps keep the motor above the pit, which makes them easier to service and a solid fit for narrow pits in older homes. They typically last a long time because the motor never sits in water. The tradeoff is more noise and a less finished look, so we generally recommend them for utility rooms rather than living spaces. We install them with a rigid discharge, a quality check valve and a float rod set to your pit depth.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Leawood

    Pedestal sump pumps are an excellent choice for narrow or shallow sump pits, as their motor sits above the water level. This design makes them easier to maintain and can sometimes offer a longer lifespan because the motor stays out of the water. KRV Plumbing can properly install pedestal sump pumps in your Leawood home, with the right sizing and setup for effective water removal without crowding your pit.
