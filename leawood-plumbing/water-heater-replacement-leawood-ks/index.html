    Gas Water Heater Replacement in Leawood, KS

    Gas water heaters reheat quicker than electric tanks, and in Leawood, KS homes they fail most often from a corroded tank, a bad gas valve or a burner clogged with rust flakes.     Hybrid Water Heater Installation in Leawood, KS

    Hybrid heat pump water heaters move heat from the surrounding air into the tank, using a fraction of the electricity a standard electric tank uses. They need room and airflow, about 700 cubic feet or a louvered door, plus a condensate drain, and they do best in a garage or basement that stays above freezing. The space runs cooler.
