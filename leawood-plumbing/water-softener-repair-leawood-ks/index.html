    Softener Repair vs Replacement Cost in Leawood, KS

    Most softener repairs cost $150 to $450, while a resin replacement often runs $400 to $900 depending on tank size. A new quality softener installed typically costs $1,500 to $3,500. If the tank and valve are sound, repair generally wins. If the valve is obsolete and parts are scarce, replacing the head or the whole unit may make more sense.

    Softener Not Regenerating in Leawood

    If hard water symptoms return, the softener may not be regenerating. Causes include a failed timer motor, a bad circuit board, a stuck meter turbine or incorrect settings after a power outage. We test each component, replace the failed part and program the valve for your household size and hardness. You get soft water again by the next cycle.

    Injector and Brine Line Cleaning in Leawood, KS

    The injector, sometimes called a venturi, creates suction to draw brine into the resin tank. Sediment and salt residue clog its tiny ports. When it clogs, the brine tank fills with water instead of emptying. We clean or replace the injector and screen and check the brine line for leaks.

    Hard Water Symptoms & Diagnostics in Leawood

    If you're noticing scale buildup on faucets, cloudy dishes, dry skin and hair, or clothes that feel stiff after washing, your water softener probably needs attention. KRV Plumbing offers comprehensive diagnostic services to identify the root cause of these hard water symptoms. We check water hardness levels, inspect all system components, and provide an accurate assessment and repair plan suited to your home in Leawood.

    Salt Bridge and Brine Tank Repair in Leawood, KS

    A salt bridge is a hard crust that forms in the brine tank and keeps salt from dissolving. The tank looks full, but the softener cannot make brine. We break up bridges, clean out mushy salt, inspect the float and brine well and repair the safety valve. We also recommend salt types that bridge less in warm or humid spaces.

    Water Softener Leak Detection & Repair in Leawood, KS

    Leaks around your water softener can be caused by cracked tanks, loose fittings, faulty O-rings, or pressure issues. Even a small leak can lead to water damage and wasted water. Our team uses specialized tools to quickly locate the source of any leak, whether it's a slow drip or a significant pool. We then perform precise repairs, from tightening connections and replacing seals to patching cracks or replacing damaged components, protecting your home from water intrusion.

    Salt Usage and Settings Repair in Leawood

    A softener using far too much salt or water usually has settings that do not match your hardness or household. Older timer units may regenerate on a schedule regardless of use. We retest hardness, reprogram the valve or upgrade the head to a metered control. Proper settings can significantly cut salt and water use.
