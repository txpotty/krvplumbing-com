    Battery Backup Sump Pump System Installation in Leawood

    Flash flooding can push water into basements and crawlspaces, so sump pumps and proper grading matter for many homes. Power outages during severe storms can leave standard sump pumps useless. Our battery backup sump pump system installation in Leawood provides an essential layer of protection.     Float Switch and Check Valve Replacement in Leawood

    Many sump pump failures start with a stuck float switch or a leaking check valve. A failed check valve lets water flow back into the pit and causes the pump to short cycle. We install new check valves with every pump replacement and verify the switch moves freely. Vertical float switches work well in tight pits. Tethered floats work best in larger pits.

    High-Capacity Sump Pump Upgrades in Leawood

    If your current sump pump struggles to keep up with heavy water flow, especially during intense thunderstorms, it may be undersized. We offer high-capacity sump pump replacements and upgrades designed to handle large water volumes. Our plumbers will assess your water intrusion patterns and recommend a pump with greater horsepower and a higher GPM (gallons per minute) rating. This prevents the pump from cycling too frequently or failing under stress, common issues in some Leawood properties.
