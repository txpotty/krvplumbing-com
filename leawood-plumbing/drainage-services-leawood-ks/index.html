    Frequently Asked Questions About Drainage Services in Leawood

    Cost depends on whether it is a clog or a construction job. Clearing a fixture or main line is a few hundred dollars at most. Jetting and sewer repairs cost more, and yard drains are priced by length and digging. You get a written quote first.

    We install and service Zoeller, Little Giant, Wayne and Liberty pumps. Cast iron models last longest. For homes that lose power in storms, we add a battery backup on its own float and a high-water alarm, so a dead primary pump or a power outage does not turn into a flooded floor.

    Installations like French drains, dry wells and sewer repairs carry a workmanship warranty. Clearing has its own guarantee. The terms are written on your invoice, and if a yard drain we installed does not move water the way we said it would, we come back and correct it at no charge for labor.

    Store-bought cleaners rarely clear a real clog. They often make it worse. The chemicals sit in the trap, heat up and can damage older pipe or soften glue joints, and if the clog does not clear, the plumber now has to open a trap full of caustic liquid. A hand auger is the better tool.

    Roots usually enter through an existing crack or joint, then widen it as they grow. Over years, that can split clay pipe. A root mass can also push a joint out of line, creating an offset that catches waste, and in bad cases the pipe collapses and has to be dug up or lined from the inside.

    We recommend skipping them. Harsh chemicals can damage older pipes. They rarely remove scale or roots, so the clog comes back, and if the cleaner sits in the trap when a plumber opens it, it can splash and burn, which is why we always ask before we start.

    Drain clearing carries a service guarantee for a set period. Repeat clogs get a return visit. If the camera shows broken or root-filled pipe, we tell you the clearing is temporary.

    A typical French drain or downspout system takes one to three days. Larger projects run longer. Projects with dry wells, long trench runs, or concrete that has to be saw-cut for a channel drain can take most of a week, especially if the ground is too wet to dig cleanly the first day.
