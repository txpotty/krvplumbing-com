    Copper to PEX Conversions in Leawood

    Pinhole leaks in copper rarely show up alone. Water chemistry, high flow velocity and contact with soil under a slab can all pit the pipe wall from the inside, and once a few pinholes appear, more are usually on the way. We use listed transition fittings anywhere PEX ties into copper that stays.

    Full Home PEX Repiping in Leawood

    PEX Main Water Line Installation in Leawood

    PEX-A vs. PEX-B Installation in Leawood

    Homeowners ask us which one is better, and the honest answer depends on the job. PEX-A makes tighter bends, can be heat-fixed if it kinks, and its fittings flow better, while PEX-B is cheaper and its crimps hold just as well when each one is gauged. Either one lasts for decades. In a cramped attic, we usually pick PEX-A.

    PEX-A Expansion Fitting Systems in Leawood, KS

    With PEX-A, the tech slips a ring over the pipe end and stretches both with a battery tool. The fitting goes in while the plastic is still expanded, and the material's memory pulls it back down around the barb, sealing harder over the first minute. In the cold, it takes longer to shrink back. We let joints set before testing.
