    Gas Tankless Water Heater Installation in Leawood, KS

    Gas tankless units fire at up to 199,000 BTU, far more than a 40,000 BTU tank burner. The higher input means a bigger gas line and a dedicated vent, often PVC on condensing models. Endless hot water follows. Private wells serve many rural homes, and minerals, sediment, and nitrate levels make periodic well testing a good habit. We recommend pre-filtration based on your water.

    Direct Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Leawood

    Direct vent heaters pull combustion air from outside and send exhaust out through a sealed, concentric vent. Because the combustion is sealed, they work well in small closets where room air is limited. Most run without electricity. The vent goes through a wall with proper clearances.

    Atmospheric Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Leawood

    Atmospheric vent heaters rely on hot exhaust rising through a metal flue, with no fan. They are common in older homes and usually the least expensive gas option, but they only work if the vent has the right size, slope and clearance to combustibles. We inspect the existing vent. Corroded or undersized pipe gets replaced.

    Power Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Leawood, KS

    Power vent heaters use a blower to push exhaust through PVC or other approved pipe. Without needing a chimney, the vent can exit through a side wall within the allowed length. A 120-volt outlet is required. We terminate the vent to spec.
