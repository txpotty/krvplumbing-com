    Commercial Restroom Plumbing in Lawrence

    Multi-Family and Property Management Plumbing in Lawrence

    Apartment complexes need a plumber who can handle many units and quick turns between tenants.     Commercial Water Heater Repair and Installation in Lawrence, KS

    A commercial water heater is judged by recovery rate, the gallons it can reheat per hour, not by tank size alone. Undersized heaters run cold by mid-shift. Our techs work on AO Smith, Rheem, Bradford White and State commercial heaters and check flue draft, gas valves and the T and P valve each visit. New installs get sized from your peak hour demand. That is how a busy kitchen stops running out of hot water during the dinner push.

    Commercial Drain Cleaning and Hydro-Jetting in Lawrence

    Commercial drains carry grease, paper towels and food waste in volumes no house ever sees. A cable just pokes a hole. We use high-pressure jetting with rotating nozzles that scrub grease off the whole circumference of 4-inch and 6-inch lines, not just the bottom. A jetted line stays open far longer than a cabled one. Restaurants, schools and apartment stacks are where we see the biggest difference.

    Commercial Water Heaters and Boilers in Lawrence, KS

    Gyms, commercial kitchens and apartment buildings burn through hot water in short, heavy peaks. Our work covers high-input tanks, multi-unit tankless racks and boilers feeding indirect storage tanks. Water across much of Kansas runs hard, leaving mineral scale in water heaters, faucets, showerheads, and dishwashers. Scale cuts output fast. We put the heaters on a descaling schedule and install mixing valves so hand-wash sinks get tempered water at a safe temperature.

    Backflow Assembly Testing and Rebuilds in Lawrence

    Most water providers require annual tests on commercial backflow assemblies, and many send late notices that come with fines. Test early. Our testers handle RPZ, double check and PVB devices with annually calibrated gauges and submit results the way your provider wants them. If a device fails, we usually rebuild it the same day.

    Slab Leak Detection for Businesses in Lawrence

    A slab leak under a store or office floor often shows up as a warm spot, a running water meter with everything off, or a crack in floor tile. Slab leaks and broken sewer laterals are common results of shifting clay, especially after long dry spells followed by heavy rain. Meter and pressure tests confirm it, and acoustic gear plus thermal imaging narrow the spot down to a foot or so. Then we decide. Some leaks are best fixed with a small cut through the slab, while others are cheaper to abandon and reroute overhead. You get a price on each option.

    Backflow Prevention Assembly Service in Lawrence

    A backflow assembly keeps water from your building, including irrigation lines, boiler water and chemical feeds, from flowing back into the public supply. Most providers want a yearly test. Our certified testers check reduced pressure zone assemblies, double check valves and pressure vacuum breakers with calibrated differential gauges, then file the report with your water provider. A failed test usually means new rubber and springs, not a whole new assembly. We carry Watts, Wilkins and Febco kits for that reason.
