    Direct Foundation Leak Repair in Lawrence, KS

    Direct repair means cutting into the floor right over the leak. It's usually the lowest bid. We saw a small opening, dig down to the pipe, replace the damaged section and pressure test it, then backfill in layers and patch the concrete flush with the floor around it. Your flooring contractor can finish the surface afterward.

    Water Line Reroute in Lawrence

    A reroute leaves the old pipe under the slab and runs new line through the attic or walls. No concrete gets broken. We usually run PEX because it bends around framing and does not corrode, and the drywall openings are cut small, often just a few squares at fixture walls, so patching goes fast.

    Leak Detection Tools We Use in Lawrence, KS

    Infrared cameras, acoustic listening gear and tracer gas cover most leaks we chase in Lawrence, KS. Gas helps when the leak is too quiet to hear. We push a hydrogen and nitrogen mix into the drained line and sniff for it at the surface, which finds pinholes that make almost no sound under thick concrete or carpet pad.

    PEX and PVC Foundation Leak Repair in Lawrence

    Newer houses use PEX or PVC under the slab. Plastic pipe fails too. Usually the culprit is a fitting that was not crimped all the way or a glued joint that separated when the ground shifted. KRV Plumbing makes new crimp, expansion or solvent-weld joints so the line under your Lawrence foundation is sound again.

    Tunneling for Foundation Leaks in Lawrence, KS

    Tunneling means digging under the slab from outside the house. No floor gets cut. From a pit next to the foundation, the crew tunnels by hand to the pipe, a good choice when the leak is under cabinets or expensive tile. KRV Plumbing backfills and compacts the tunnel when the Lawrence, KS repair is done.

    Advanced Leak Detection Technology in Lawrence, KS

    A ground microphone picks up the hiss of water leaving a pipe under four inches of concrete. That's the main tool. KRV Plumbing pairs it with a line tracer that follows the pipe route and a thermal camera that shows hot water spreading, so in most homes we can mark the leak within a foot or two, in Lawrence, KS or anywhere else.

    Foundation Leak Repair Cost Comparison in Lawrence, KS

    Direct repairs usually cost least up front. But flooring repair adds cost. Reroutes run a bit more but skip the concrete and remove the chance of another leak in the old line, while a full repipe makes sense when the house has had two or three leaks already. We line up the full cost of each, flooring and drywall included.
