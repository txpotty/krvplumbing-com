    High-Power Hydro Jetting in Lawrence

    For the toughest clogs and stubborn buildup, hydro jetting is an extremely effective solution. This method uses high-pressure water streams to scour the inside of your pipes, removing grease, mineral deposits, scale, and even small tree roots. It's an excellent preventative measure for older homes in Lawrence with cast iron pipes, providing a comprehensive cleaning that traditional snaking cannot match. This leaves your pipes clean from wall to wall.

    Hydro Jetting in Lawrence

    Hydro jetting sends water at up to 4,000 psi through a specialized nozzle that scours the full inside wall of the pipe. Unlike a cable, which punches a hole through the clog, jetting removes grease, scale and fine roots completely. It is the go-to method for restaurant drains and lines that clog again within months. We camera the line first to make sure the pipe is sound enough for jetting. Most residential jetting jobs take about two to three hours.
