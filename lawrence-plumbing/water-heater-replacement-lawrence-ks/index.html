    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Replacement in Lawrence

    Most standard gas or electric tank replacements cost $1,400 to $3,000 including the heater, labor and common code upgrades. Attic installs, larger tanks or relocations can raise the cost to $3,000 to $4,500.

    Most standard tank-style water heater replacements can be completed in 3 to 5 hours, assuming no major complications or plumbing reconfigurations. Tankless or hybrid installations might take a bit longer, often 5 to 8 hours, due to more complex venting or gas line changes.

    Most replacements take two to four hours. Attic installs or relocations can take more time.

    Tank water heaters often last about 8 to 12 years, and hard water and sediment buildup can shorten that. Private wells serve many rural homes, and minerals, sediment, and nitrate levels make periodic well testing a good habit. Annual flushing and anode rod checks can help extend that life.

    We install Bradford White, Rheem and A.O. Smith water heaters. Each offers reliable performance and a range of warranties.

    Choosing the right size depends on the number of people in your household, their hot water usage habits and peak demand times. Our technicians will calculate the right GPM (gallons per minute) for tankless or gallon capacity for tank models so you never run out of hot water, especially in multi-bathroom Lawrence homes.

    Leaks at the base, rusty water, rumbling noises, inconsistent hot water and an age over 10 years are common signs. A leaking tank should be replaced right away.

    New water heaters typically come with a manufacturer's warranty on the tank and parts, usually ranging from 6 to 12 years. KRV Plumbing also provides a labor warranty on our installation, giving you added peace of mind. Specific warranty details vary by brand and model.
