    Frozen and Burst Line Repair in Lawrence

    Pipes in exterior walls, attics and crawlspaces freeze first. Ice splits the pipe. The split usually does not leak until the ice thaws, so a pipe that froze overnight may start spraying the next afternoon once the house warms up. We repair the split and add insulation. We can add heat cable where needed.

    PEX Pipe Repair and Installation in Lawrence

    PEX is flexible and handles freezing better than rigid pipe. It still fails. Most PEX leaks happen at a crimp ring that was not fully seated, an early brass fitting that dezincified or a section damaged by sunlight in an attic, so we replace the failed joint with the right fitting for that brand of PEX. Most take under an hour.

    Leaking Pipe Repair in Lawrence, KS

    Even a slow drip can rot a subfloor in a year. They hide inside walls. We find leaks with moisture meters, thermal imaging and acoustic gear, then open the smallest section of drywall or ceiling that lets us cut out the bad pipe and install the repair. Older Lawrence homes see this a lot.

    Leaky Pipe Repair in Lawrence, KS

    Small leaks raise water bills and grow mold in hidden spaces. The meter tells you. If the dial moves with every fixture off, there is a leak somewhere, so we shut off branches one at a time to find which part of the system is losing water. One opening, one repair.

    Pipe Repair vs Repipe Cost in Lawrence, KS

    A single pipe repair costs far less than a whole-house repipe. Spot fixes suit healthy systems. If you are seeing repeated leaks, low pressure or rusty water, the total cost of patching can pass the cost of repiping within a few years, which is why we price both and give you our honest take. You make the call.

    Underground Pipe Repair in Lawrence

    Buried pipe takes stress from settling soil, roots and vehicles. Leaks hide well. We locate underground leaks with acoustic equipment and tracer gas, and either spot repair at the leak or replace the line by pulling new pipe through. We test before backfill.

    Galvanized Pipe Repair in Lawrence

    Threaded steel pipe rusts from the inside until flow drops and threaded joints start to leak. It can be patched. The trouble is that every fitting you disturb on old galvanized can start a new leak, which is why we use dielectric transitions and lay out a replacement plan for the rest. The schedule is yours.

    Pinhole Leak Repair in Lawrence

    Pinhole leaks form when the copper wall thins from the inside. Water quality is often behind it. High velocity, poor grounding and aggressive water all speed it up, and the leak starts as a tiny drip that stains drywall or a ceiling, so we cut out the affected section and replace it with new copper or a PEX transition. More pinholes often follow.
