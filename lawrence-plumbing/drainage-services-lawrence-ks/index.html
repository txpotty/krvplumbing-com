    Jetting for Scale and Grease in Lawrence

    Jetting uses water at high pressure to strip a pipe back to its original diameter. Water across much of Kansas runs hard, leaving mineral scale in water heaters, faucets, showerheads, and dishwashers. Scale and grease on the pipe wall catch debris, and stripping them off keeps the line clear far longer. Plan on about two hours.

    Sump Pump Services in Lawrence, KS

    A sump pump protects a basement or crawlspace from groundwater. Our test covers the float, the check valve and the discharge line. Never pipe it to the sewer. A frozen or buried discharge line is a common reason a working pump still floods the pit.

    Outdoor and Storm Drain Cleaning in Lawrence

    Area drains, catch basins and driveway trench drains fill with leaves and silt. A buried grate sends water across the yard, and the pipe under it fills with mud. We vacuum the basins and jet the lines. Fall cleaning helps most.

    Channel and Trench Drains in Lawrence, KS

    Driveways and patios that slope toward the house can send runoff straight into a garage or a door threshold. A trench drain cut into the concrete stops it. We saw-cut the slab, set a polymer or concrete channel with a grate rated for the traffic it will see, and pipe it in solid PVC to a safe discharge point away from the foundation.
