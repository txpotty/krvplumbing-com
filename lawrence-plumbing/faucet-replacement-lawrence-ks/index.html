    Bathroom Faucet Replacement in Lawrence, KS

    Old lav faucets rarely come off clean. Lav faucet nuts hide behind the bowl in a tight gap, and after a couple of decades they tend to fuse to the threaded shank.     Touchless Faucet Upgrade in Lawrence
