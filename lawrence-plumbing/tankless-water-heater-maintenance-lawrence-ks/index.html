    Isolation Valve Kit Installation in Lawrence, KS

    Some tankless heaters were installed without service valves, which makes proper flushing impossible. We install a set of isolation valves with purge ports on the hot and cold lines so future maintenance takes minutes. The kit also lets you shut off water to the heater without closing the main. It is a one-time upgrade that pays for itself in simpler service.

    Tankless Water Heater Venting System Check in Lawrence

    Proper ventilation is essential for the safe operation of gas-fired tankless water heaters. Our maintenance service in Lawrence includes a thorough inspection of the venting system for blockages, proper slope and secure connections. We confirm that exhaust gases are safely directed outside and that no carbon monoxide leaks are present, which is vital for the health and safety of your household.

    Rinnai Tankless Heater Servicing in Lawrence

    Rinnai units are popular for their efficiency and compact design. Our Rinnai-specific maintenance includes cleaning the air intake filter, checking the flame sensor and verifying the operation of the bypass valve. We also inspect the combustion chamber and confirm proper gas combustion. Our technicians are familiar with Rinnai's diagnostic codes and can address any issues proactively, improving performance for your Lawrence home.

    Condensing Unit and Neutralizer Service in Lawrence

    High-efficiency condensing tankless heaters produce acidic condensate that must be neutralized before it goes down a drain. We inspect the condensate trap, line and neutralizer cartridge and replace the media when it is spent. A clogged trap can cause shutdowns or water leaking inside the cabinet. We also check that the line is shielded where it runs outdoors.

    Inlet Filter and Air Intake Cleaning in Lawrence

    Every tankless unit has an inlet water screen that catches grit and scale flakes. Those screens can clog with sand and debris, especially after municipal line work. We pull and clean the screen and inspect the air intake filter, which can load up with dust and dryer lint. Clean filters keep the flow sensor reading correctly and the burner breathing properly.

    Heat Exchanger Descaling in Lawrence

    The heat exchanger in a tankless heater is a coil of small tubes that water races through while the burner fires. Hard water drops calcium and magnesium on those hot surfaces, narrowing the passages over time. We connect a submersible pump and hoses to the service valves and circulate descaling solution for 45 to 60 minutes. Then we flush with fresh water to rinse the system thoroughly. Restored flow means steadier temperatures and fewer shutdowns.
