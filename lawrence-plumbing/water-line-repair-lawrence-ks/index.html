    Main Water Line Repair in Lawrence

    Your main line is the pipe from the meter to the house, usually 3/4 inch or 1 inch, buried anywhere from a foot to several feet down depending on frost depth. When it fails you tend to see wet ground, a spinning meter and weak pressure at every tap. We start by closing the house shutoff and watching the dial. If it still moves, the leak is buried. Then a ground microphone narrows the spot, so the crew opens one small pit rather than trenching the whole run.

    Water Line Repair at the Meter in Lawrence, KS

    The meter box is where a lot of leaks start. Fittings in the box loosen with ground movement and traffic.     Pipe Bursting and Pulling in Lawrence

    Pipe bursting means splitting the old line in place while the new one follows behind it. It works best on straight runs. A cone-shaped head breaks the old pipe outward, and a length of HDPE trails behind it in the same path. You end up with a fresh line and two small pits, and the lawn, sidewalk and driveway in between stay where they are, which is the whole reason people pick this method over a trench.

    Underground Leak Detection in Lawrence, KS

    A ground microphone picks up the hiss of water escaping a pressurized pipe, even through a couple of feet of dirt. Some leaks barely make a sound. For those we push a hydrogen and nitrogen tracer gas into the drained line and sniff for it at the surface, which finds leaks the microphone cannot hear. Basements are common in the eastern part of Kansas, while slab and crawlspace homes are more typical in many other areas. Once the spot is marked, you get a written repair price before we dig.
