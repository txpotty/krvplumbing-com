    Dual Tank Softener Systems in Lawrence

    Large households and homes with very hard water can benefit from a dual tank system that provides soft water all day and night. While one tank regenerates, the other stays in service. Kinetico and similar non-electric twin tank systems are popular for this reason. We size dual tank systems to your peak demand. They cost more upfront but prevent hard water bleed-through during regeneration.

    Emergency Water Softener Failure Replacement in Lawrence, KS

    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Softener Replacement in Lawrence

    A standard single tank softener replacement typically costs $1,500 to $3,000 installed. Dual tank or premium systems usually run $3,000 to $5,000. Adding a new loop or drain line can raise the cost.

    With proper maintenance, most quality water softeners can last between 10 and 15 years, and sometimes even longer. Factors like water hardness, usage volume and the quality of the original installation can influence its lifespan. Regular servicing helps lengthen the life of your unit.

    The right size depends on your household's daily water usage and the hardness of your water in grains per gallon. Our technicians will perform a water test and calculate the appropriate softening capacity in grains per day for your Lawrence home, so the system performs well.

    Most residential water softener replacements can be finished within 3 to 6 hours, assuming no major plumbing changes are required. Our experienced plumbers work efficiently to minimize disruption, restoring soft water to your home on the same day.

    Most softeners last 10 to 15 years. Resin and valves wear faster with very hard or chlorinated water.

    Common signs include a return of hard water symptoms like scale buildup on fixtures, dry skin and hair, dingy laundry or heavy soap use. Other signs are a clear drop in water pressure, leaks from the unit, or a softener over 10 to 15 years old that needs frequent repairs.

    A new, properly sized water softener can sometimes improve water pressure if your old unit was undersized or had significant mineral buildup restricting flow. However, a water softener's main job is to remove hardness minerals, not to boost pressure. If low water pressure is your main concern, we can investigate other causes.

    We test your hardness in grains per gallon and estimate daily water usage. That tells us the resin capacity needed.
