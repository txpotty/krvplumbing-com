    Dual Tank Softener Systems in Lawrence

    Twin-tank systems keep one tank in service while the other regenerates, so you never get hard water during a middle-of-the-night regeneration. Kinetico's version uses water pressure, not power, to switch tanks. The price is higher.

    Long-Term Softener Costs in Lawrence, KS

    Long-term cost on a softener is mostly salt and water. Compared with a timer unit, a metered valve set efficiently often uses a third less salt, which matters over a fifteen-year life. That is how we compare models.

    Commercial Water Softener Replacement in Lawrence

    Restaurants and laundromats run softeners hard, and a commercial unit often needs a 1.5-inch or 2-inch valve and twin tanks to keep up with flow. Peak flow drives the sizing. We work around your business hours.

    Fleck Valve Programming in Lawrence, KS

    Programming is where most Fleck replacements go wrong. A Fleck needs hardness, household size and regen time entered right, otherwise it cycles too often or runs out of capacity. We set it before we leave.

    Softener Failure Diagnosis in Lawrence, KS

    A clogged injector, a dead motor or a wrong setting can make a good softener look finished. Hardness gets tested on both sides of the unit, and the cheap fixes get tried before we talk replacement. Discontinued valves usually mean replacement.

    Softener Loop Installation in Lawrence, KS

    Softener loops are the pair of stubbed-out lines near the garage wall or water heater where a softener ties into the main. Homes without one get a loop cut in, with shutoffs and a bypass. Hose bibs stay off it. We run the drain to a standpipe or floor drain through an air gap.

    Replacing Pelican Water Systems in Lawrence

    Pelican systems usually combine a carbon filter with either a salt-based softener or a salt-free conditioner in one setup on the main line. We replace the media or the whole system, depending on age. Following the install specs keeps the warranty intact.

    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Replacement in Lawrence, KS

    Salt-free conditioners, sometimes sold as descalers, change how hardness minerals behave without removing them, so the water still tests hard. What they do well is keep scale off pipe walls and heater elements. No salt, no drain. We can replace one or switch you to a true softener.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Softener Replacement in Lawrence

    A single-tank softener usually runs $1,500 to $3,000 installed. Twin-tank and premium units cost $3,000 to $5,000. A new loop or drain line adds to that, sometimes by several hundred dollars depending on where the pipes run.

    Two to four hours, usually. New loops or drain lines add time.

    Ten to fifteen years with decent care, sometimes longer. Very hard water, heavy daily usage and chlorine in a city supply all wear down the resin faster than average. Annual checks help.

    Scale on shower heads and spots on glasses coming back are the first clues. Dull laundry, extra soap and salt that never goes down all point to a failing unit, especially one older than 10 to 15 years. Water around the base counts too.

    Spots and crust returning after a few years of clean fixtures usually means the softener stopped working. Check the salt level first, because if the salt has not gone down in weeks the unit is not regenerating at all. Age matters too.

    Salt-based softeners swap calcium and magnesium for sodium through ion exchange, so the water actually tests soft. Salt-free conditioners leave the minerals in the water but change their structure so they are less likely to stick to pipes and heating elements. Only the salt unit softens.

    Use clean pellet salt and keep the brine tank at least a quarter full. Every year or two, clean the brine tank and break up any salt bridge before it blocks regeneration. Wells with iron need a prefilter.

    Fleck, Autotrol, Pelican and Watts systems are all on our list, along with Clack valves on many of the systems we build. We pick based on parts availability. A valve you can rebuild is worth more.
