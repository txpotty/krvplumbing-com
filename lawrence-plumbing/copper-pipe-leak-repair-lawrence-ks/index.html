    The cost of copper pipe leak repair in Lawrence can vary significantly depending on the leak's location, severity and accessibility.     Type K, L and M copper pipes differ primarily in their wall thickness and thus their pressure ratings. Type K has the thickest walls and is used for underground service and main water lines. Type L is common for interior water supply and radiant heating. Type M has the thinnest walls and is typically used for less demanding applications. We are skilled in repairing all types.

    To prevent future copper pipe leaks, consider installing a whole-house water softener if you have hard water, as mineral buildup can lead to corrosion. Maintaining appropriate water pressure, insulating pipes for cold weather and scheduling periodic plumbing inspections can also greatly extend the life of your copper pipes.
