    Gas Venting Tune-Up in Lawrence, KS

    Atmospheric gas tanks vent by draft alone, so the vent connector needs upward slope of at least a quarter inch per foot and tight joints with three screws each. Draft at the hood gets checked first. Lint on the flame arrestor of an FVIR tank can starve the burner and kill the pilot. We vacuum it clean.

    Attic and Garage Tank Checkups in Lawrence, KS

    Garage and attic tanks live through bigger temperature swings than closet units, which wears out plastic drain valves and wiring insulation faster. A pan under an attic tank is not optional. We check that the pan drain line runs to a safe spot and is not cracked or plugged, because one slow leak over a bedroom can bring down a ceiling. Gas tanks in garages need their ignition source at least 18 inches off the floor unless they are FVIR models.

    Rental and Commercial Tank Logs in Lawrence

    Landlords and property managers with a dozen units need to know which tank is next to fail. Each tank we touch goes into a log. We service 30 to 100 gallon tanks in rentals.

    Yearly Maintenance Plans in Lawrence, KS

    Electric Thermostat Service in Lawrence

    Each element on an electric tank has its own thermostat, and the upper one carries a reset button for the high-limit switch. When the reset trips, something caused it.     Maintenance Versus Replacement Costs in Lawrence, KS

    A yearly flush costs a small fraction of a new installed tank, which often runs $1,400 to $3,000 or more depending on size and venting. We tell you which one makes sense. Past 10 to 12 years, rust at the base or a seam leak means replacement. Younger tanks with a sound liner are worth maintaining.

    Sediment Flush and Descaling in Lawrence
