    Sectional Point Repair in Lawrence

    When only one section of a line is damaged, a point repair can fix it without lining the whole length. We position a short resin liner at the damaged spot using the camera and inflate it until it cures. Point repairs are a cost-effective fix for a single crack, hole or root entry. They are common on otherwise healthy lines.

    Cured-In-Place Pipe Lining in Lawrence

    Cured-in-place lining creates a new pipe inside the old one. We clean the line, then pull or invert a resin-saturated liner into place and cure it with hot water, steam or UV light. The result is a seamless, jointless pipe that roots cannot penetrate. Lining works well for cracked or root-intruded lines that still hold their shape.

    Causes of Sewer Line Damage in Lawrence, KS

    Sewer lines in Lawrence, KS can suffer damage from several factors, including shifting soils that cause pipes to crack or misalign. Much of Kansas has expansive clay soil that swells when wet and shrinks in drought, which can shift foundations and stress buried pipes. Tree root intrusion is another major culprit, as roots seek out moisture in pipes and grow, causing blockages and structural damage. Age and corrosion, especially in older cast iron or clay pipes, also contribute to deterioration. Our trenchless solutions address these common issues effectively.

    Cost Comparison of Trenchless vs Traditional in Lawrence, KS

    While the upfront cost of trenchless repair might sometimes seem higher than traditional excavation, it often becomes more cost-effective when you count the savings on landscaping, driveway or foundation repair.     Root Intrusion Repair in Lawrence, KS

    Tree and shrub roots go after moisture in sewer joints. Once inside, they grow into dense masses that cause recurring backups. We cut roots with a mechanical cutter or hydro jet, then line the pipe to seal the joints. That breaks the cycle of cleaning the line every couple of months.

    Pipe Bursting Sewer Repair in Lawrence

    Pipe bursting is a trenchless method where a new pipe is pulled through the old one while the old pipe is fractured outward. This creates a new, seamless and often larger diameter sewer line without the need for long trenching. It is best for severely damaged or collapsed pipes in Lawrence that require full replacement rather than just relining.

    Cast Iron Sewer Line Repair in Lawrence, KS

    Cast iron drains and sewer lines corrode from the inside, leaving rough scale, cracks and channel rot along the bottom of the pipe. We descale the pipe with chain knockers or cutters, then line it to restore a smooth, sealed interior. This is particularly useful for cast iron running under slabs where excavation is costly. Lining can add many years of service.
