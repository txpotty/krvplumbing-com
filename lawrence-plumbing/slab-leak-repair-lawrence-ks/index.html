    Locating and Repairing Slab Leaks in Lawrence, KS

    Direct Slab Leak Repair in Lawrence, KS

    Direct repair is often the most efficient fix for a single leak. We cut a small square. Once the pipe is exposed, we replace the bad section, test the line and backfill in compacted lifts before patching. The concrete is patched flush with the floor.

    Slab Leak Detection in Lawrence

    The first test is the water meter. Every fixture gets shut off. A meter that keeps turning tells us to isolate hot from cold, test each side and walk the losing line with a ground mic. Thermal imaging shows hot water leaks as warm areas on the floor.

    Emergency Slab Leak Services in Lawrence

    A burst line under the slab can push water through grout lines within hours. Shut the main first. A crew comes out to isolate the broken line, cap it so the rest of the house has water, and make a temporary or permanent repair depending on what we find. That goes for anywhere in Lawrence.

    PEX Repiping for Slab Leaks in Lawrence, KS

    Two or three slab leaks in a few years point to the pipe, not bad luck. That is when a repipe pays off. PEX bends with the structure, won't corrode and runs overhead, so a whole-house repipe leaves nothing under the slab. It suits many Lawrence homes.

    Electronic Slab Leak Detection in Lawrence

    Locating comes before cutting. We use a ground mic and a thermal camera. With the mic catching the hiss through the concrete and the camera showing warm water spreading, we can usually narrow the leak to about a foot. We test hot lines, cold lines and drains in your Lawrence home.

    Slab Leak Reroute in Lawrence

    Older copper under a slab tends to leak again. A reroute stops the cycle. We leave the leaking run in the ground and pull new PEX through the attic and down the walls, cutting openings where they are easiest to patch, often behind a vanity or in a closet.

    Pipe Rerouting and Repiping in Lawrence

    Old galvanized or thin copper is hard to patch reliably. Rerouting skips it. We abandon the leaking run in place and install new pipe through walls or the attic, so the problem section is gone from service and the water travels through fresh line that you can reach later. Lawrence homes with original plumbing see this often.
